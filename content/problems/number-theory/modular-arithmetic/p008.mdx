<Problem>
What are the last two digits of $13^{2024}$? (That is, what is $13^{2024} \pmod{100}$?)
</Problem>

<Solution>
We need $13^{2024} \pmod{100}$.

Since $\gcd(13, 100) = 1$, Euler's theorem applies: $\phi(100) = \phi(4)\cdot\phi(25) = 2 \cdot 20 = 40$, so $13^{40} \equiv 1 \pmod{100}$.

Reduce the exponent: $2024 = 40 \cdot 50 + 24$, so $13^{2024} \equiv 13^{24} \pmod{100}$.

Compute $13^{24}$ by repeated squaring:

$$13^2 = 169 \equiv 69 \pmod{100}$$
$$13^4 \equiv 69^2 = 4761 \equiv 61 \pmod{100}$$
$$13^8 \equiv 61^2 = 3721 \equiv 21 \pmod{100}$$
$$13^{16} \equiv 21^2 = 441 \equiv 41 \pmod{100}$$

Now combine: $13^{24} = 13^{16} \cdot 13^8 \equiv 41 \cdot 21 = 861 \equiv 61 \pmod{100}$.

The last two digits are $\mathbf{61}$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
