<Problem>
What is the remainder when $123 + 456$ is divided by $5$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Work modulo $5$. We have $123 = 5 \cdot 24 + 3$, so $123 \equiv 3 \pmod{5}$. And $456 = 5 \cdot 91 + 1$, so $456 \equiv 1 \pmod{5}$.

Therefore $123 + 456 \equiv 3 + 1 \equiv 4 \pmod{5}$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Direct calculation">
$123 + 456 = 579$. And $579 = 5 \cdot 115 + 4$, so the remainder is $4$.
</AlternateMethod>
