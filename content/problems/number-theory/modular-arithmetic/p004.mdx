<Problem>
What is the remainder when $7 \cdot 11 \cdot 13 + 5^3$ is divided by $6$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Work modulo $6$, reducing each factor first.

$$7 \equiv 1,\quad 11 \equiv 5 \equiv -1,\quad 13 \equiv 1 \pmod{6}.$$

So $7 \cdot 11 \cdot 13 \equiv 1 \cdot (-1) \cdot 1 = -1 \equiv 5 \pmod{6}$.

For the second term, $5 \equiv -1 \pmod{6}$, so

$$5^3 \equiv (-1)^3 = -1 \equiv 5 \pmod{6}.$$

Adding: $7 \cdot 11 \cdot 13 + 5^3 \equiv 5 + 5 = 10 \equiv 4 \pmod{6}$.

The remainder is $\mathbf{4}$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Direct calculation">
$7 \cdot 11 \cdot 13 = 1001$ and $5^3 = 125$, so the sum is $1126$.

Dividing: $1126 = 6 \cdot 187 + 4$, confirming the remainder is $4$.
</AlternateMethod>
