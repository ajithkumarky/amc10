<Problem>
What is the remainder when $3^{100}$ is divided by $4$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Work modulo $4$. Compute the first few powers of $3$:

$$3^1 \equiv 3 \pmod{4}, \quad 3^2 = 9 \equiv 1 \pmod{4}.$$

The residues cycle with period $2$: $3, 1, 3, 1, \ldots$

Since $100$ is even, $3^{100} \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$.

The remainder is $\mathbf{1}$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
