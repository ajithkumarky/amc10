<Problem>
What is the units digit of $3^{2025} + 7^{2025}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Work modulo $10$, finding the units digit of each term separately.

**Units digit of $3^{2025}$:** The powers of $3$ cycle through units digits $3, 9, 7, 1$ with period $4$.

$$2025 = 4 \cdot 506 + 1, \text{ so } 2025 \equiv 1 \pmod{4}.$$

Thus $3^{2025}$ has the same units digit as $3^1 = 3$.

**Units digit of $7^{2025}$:** The powers of $7$ cycle through units digits $7, 9, 3, 1$ with period $4$.

Since $2025 \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$, the units digit of $7^{2025}$ equals that of $7^1 = 7$.

**Sum:** The units digit of $3^{2025} + 7^{2025}$ is the units digit of $3 + 7 = 10$, which is $\mathbf{0}$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
