<Problem>
What is the units digit of $7^{2024}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Work modulo $10$. The units digits of $7^k$ cycle through $7, 9, 3, 1$ for $k = 1, 2, 3, 4$, then repeat with period $4$.

Since $2024 = 4 \cdot 506$, the exponent is a multiple of $4$, so $7^{2024}$ ends in the same digit as $7^4 = 2401$.

The units digit is $\mathbf{1}$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
