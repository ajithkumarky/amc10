<Problem>
Let $(x, y)$ range over all ordered pairs of positive integers satisfying $xy - 5x - 2y = 4$. What is the largest value of $y$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Apply Simon's Favorite Factoring Trick. Add $(-5)(-2) = 10$ to both sides:
$$xy - 5x - 2y + 10 = 14.$$
$$(x - 2)(y - 5) = 14.$$

Since $x$ and $y$ are positive integers, $x - 2 \geq -1$ and $y - 5 \geq -4$.

List all integer factor pairs $(a, b)$ with $a \cdot b = 14$, $a \geq -1$, and $b \geq -4$:

| $a = x-2$ | $b = y-5$ | $x$ | $y$ |
|-----------|-----------|-----|-----|
| $1$ | $14$ | $3$ | $19$ |
| $2$ | $7$ | $4$ | $12$ |
| $7$ | $2$ | $9$ | $7$ |
| $14$ | $1$ | $16$ | $6$ |

(The pairs $(-1, -14)$ and $(-2, -7)$ give $x = 1, y = -9$ and $x = 0, y = -2$ respectively — neither has both coordinates positive.)

All four pairs $(3,19)$, $(4,12)$, $(9,7)$, $(16,6)$ consist of positive integers. The largest $y$-value is $\mathbf{19}$, achieved at $(x, y) = (3, 19)$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
