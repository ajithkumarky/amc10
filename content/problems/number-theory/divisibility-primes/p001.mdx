<Problem>
Which of the following integers is divisible by $9$?

$$\text{(A)}\ 1234 \qquad \text{(B)}\ 2345 \qquad \text{(C)}\ 3456 \qquad \text{(D)}\ 4567 \qquad \text{(E)}\ 5678$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
A number is divisible by $9$ exactly when its **digit sum** is divisible by $9$.

Compute the digit sums:
- $1+2+3+4 = 10$ — not divisible by $9$
- $2+3+4+5 = 14$ — not divisible by $9$
- $3+4+5+6 = 18 = 2 \times 9$ — **divisible by $9$** ✓
- $4+5+6+7 = 22$ — not divisible by $9$
- $5+6+7+8 = 26$ — not divisible by $9$

We can verify: $3456 \div 9 = 384$. $\checkmark$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
