<Problem>
How many positive divisors of $360$ are perfect squares?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First, factor $360$:
$$360 = 8 \times 45 = 2^3 \times 3^2 \times 5^1.$$

A divisor of $360$ has the form $2^a \cdot 3^b \cdot 5^c$ where $0 \le a \le 3$, $0 \le b \le 2$, $0 \le c \le 1$.

A positive integer is a **perfect square** exactly when every prime in its factorization appears to an **even** exponent. So we need $a$, $b$, and $c$ all even:
- $a \in \{0, 2\}$ (even values in $\{0,1,2,3\}$) — $2$ choices
- $b \in \{0, 2\}$ (even values in $\{0,1,2\}$) — $2$ choices
- $c \in \{0\}$ (even values in $\{0,1\}$) — $1$ choice

Total: $2 \times 2 \times 1 = 4$.

The four divisors are $1,\ 4,\ 9,\ 36$ — all perfect squares. $\checkmark$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
