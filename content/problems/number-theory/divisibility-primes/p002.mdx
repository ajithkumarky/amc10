<Problem>
How many positive divisors does $72$ have?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First, write the prime factorization of $72$:
$$72 = 8 \times 9 = 2^3 \times 3^2.$$

The number of positive divisors of $p_1^{a_1} p_2^{a_2} \cdots$ equals $(a_1+1)(a_2+1)\cdots$, because each divisor independently chooses an exponent for each prime from $0$ up to the maximum.

$$\tau(72) = (3+1)(2+1) = 4 \times 3 = 12.$$

To double-check, list them: $1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 12, 18, 24, 36, 72$ — exactly $12$ divisors. $\checkmark$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
