<Problem>
What is the largest prime factor of $2^{2023} + 2^{2022} + 2^{2021}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Factor out the smallest power of $2$:
$$2^{2023} + 2^{2022} + 2^{2021} = 2^{2021}(2^2 + 2^1 + 2^0) = 2^{2021}(4 + 2 + 1) = 2^{2021} \times 7.$$

The prime factorization is $2^{2021} \times 7$, so the only prime factors are $2$ and $7$.

The largest prime factor is $\mathbf{7}$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Check divisibility by small primes">
The expression $2^{2023}+2^{2022}+2^{2021}$ is clearly even (divisible by $2^{2021}$). After removing all factors of $2$, we're left with $4+2+1=7$, which is prime. So the prime factors are $2$ and $7$, and the largest is $7$.
</AlternateMethod>
