<Problem>
The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is $11$, and the tens digit exceeds the units digit by $3$. What is the two-digit number?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the tens digit be $t$ and the units digit be $u$. We are given two conditions:
$$t + u = 11 \qquad \text{and} \qquad t - u = 3.$$

Adding the two equations: $2t = 14$, so $t = 7$.

Substituting back: $u = 11 - 7 = 4$.

The two-digit number is $\overline{tu} = \mathbf{74}$.

**Check:** digit sum $= 7 + 4 = 11$ ✓, and $7 - 4 = 3$ ✓.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
