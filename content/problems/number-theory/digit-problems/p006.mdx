<Problem>
What is the sum of the digits of $999{,}999 \times 7$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Compute the product directly:
$$999{,}999 \times 7 = 7{,}000{,}000 - 7 = 6{,}999{,}993.$$

Now sum the digits of $6{,}999{,}993$:
$$6 + 9 + 9 + 9 + 9 + 9 + 3 = 54.$$

The digit sum is $\mathbf{54}$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Mod 9 sanity check">
Since $999{,}999 \equiv 0 \pmod{9}$ (digit sum $= 54$, divisible by $9$), we know $999{,}999 \times 7 \equiv 0 \pmod{9}$, so its digit sum must be divisible by $9$. Among the choices, $54 = 6 \times 9$ is the only one consistent with the exact computation above.
</AlternateMethod>
