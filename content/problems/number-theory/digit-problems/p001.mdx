<Problem>
What is the units digit of $3^{100}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The units digits of powers of $3$ cycle with period $4$:
$$3^1 = 3,\quad 3^2 = 9,\quad 3^3 = 27,\quad 3^4 = 81,\quad 3^5 = 243,\ldots$$
The cycle is $3, 9, 7, 1$ and then repeats.

Since $100 = 4 \times 25$, the exponent $100$ is a multiple of $4$. Therefore $3^{100}$ has the same units digit as $3^4 = 81$, which ends in $\mathbf{1}$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
