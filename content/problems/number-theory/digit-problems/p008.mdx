<Problem>
What is the units digit of $7^{7^7}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The units digits of powers of $7$ cycle with period $4$: $7, 9, 3, 1, 7, 9, 3, 1, \ldots$

So the units digit of $7^n$ is determined by $n \pmod{4}$:
- $n \equiv 1$: units digit $7$
- $n \equiv 2$: units digit $9$
- $n \equiv 3$: units digit $3$
- $n \equiv 0$: units digit $1$

We need $7^7 \pmod{4}$. Since $7 \equiv 3 \pmod{4}$, we have $7^7 \equiv 3^7 \pmod{4}$.

The powers of $3$ mod $4$ alternate: $3^1 \equiv 3$, $3^2 \equiv 1$, $3^3 \equiv 3$, $\ldots$ The pattern is $3$ for odd exponents and $1$ for even exponents. Since $7$ is odd, $3^7 \equiv 3 \pmod{4}$.

Therefore $7^7 \equiv 3 \pmod{4}$, and so the units digit of $7^{7^7}$ equals the units digit of $7^3 = 343$, which is $\mathbf{3}$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
