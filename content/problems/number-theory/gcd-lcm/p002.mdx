<Problem>
What is $\text{lcm}(18, 30)$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Factor each number into primes:
$$18 = 2^1 \cdot 3^2, \qquad 30 = 2^1 \cdot 3^1 \cdot 5^1.$$

The LCM takes the **maximum** exponent for each prime:
$$\text{lcm}(18, 30) = 2^1 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5^1 = 2 \cdot 9 \cdot 5 = 90.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Product identity">
First find $\gcd(18, 30)$ with the Euclidean algorithm: $\gcd(18, 30) = \gcd(18, 12) = \gcd(12, 6) = 6$.

Then
$$\text{lcm}(18, 30) = \frac{18 \cdot 30}{\gcd(18, 30)} = \frac{540}{6} = 90.$$

Answer: **B**.
</AlternateMethod>
