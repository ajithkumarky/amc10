<Problem>
What is $\gcd(36, 48)$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Use the Euclidean algorithm:
$$\gcd(36, 48) = \gcd(36, 12) = \gcd(12, 0) = 12.$$

(Since $48 = 1 \cdot 36 + 12$ and $36 = 3 \cdot 12 + 0$.)

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Prime factorization">
Write $36 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2$ and $48 = 2^4 \cdot 3^1$. Take the minimum exponent for each prime:
$$\gcd(36, 48) = 2^{\min(2,4)} \cdot 3^{\min(2,1)} = 2^2 \cdot 3^1 = 4 \cdot 3 = 12.$$

Answer: **E**.
</AlternateMethod>
