<Problem>
Three buses depart from a terminal at exactly the same time. Bus $A$ returns to the terminal every $6$ minutes, Bus $B$ every $10$ minutes, and Bus $C$ every $15$ minutes. After how many minutes will all three buses first return to the terminal simultaneously?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The three buses return together when the elapsed time is a multiple of each of $6$, $10$, and $15$. The **smallest** such time is $\text{lcm}(6, 10, 15)$.

Compute the LCM using prime factorizations:
$$6 = 2 \cdot 3, \qquad 10 = 2 \cdot 5, \qquad 15 = 3 \cdot 5.$$

Taking the maximum exponent for each prime:
$$\text{lcm}(6, 10, 15) = 2^1 \cdot 3^1 \cdot 5^1 = 30.$$

**Check:** $30 / 6 = 5$ ✓, $30 / 10 = 3$ ✓, $30 / 15 = 2$ ✓. All three return at $t = 30$ minutes.

No smaller multiple of all three exists because $15$ divides $30$ and $30 < 60$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
