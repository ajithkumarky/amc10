<Problem>
Two positive integers have $\gcd$ equal to $8$ and $\text{lcm}$ equal to $120$. One of the integers is $24$. What is the other integer?
</Problem>

<Solution>
For any two positive integers $a$ and $b$,
$$\gcd(a, b) \cdot \text{lcm}(a, b) = a \cdot b.$$

With $\gcd = 8$, $\text{lcm} = 120$, and $a = 24$:
$$8 \cdot 120 = 24 \cdot b \implies b = \frac{960}{24} = 40.$$

**Verify:** $24 = 2^3 \cdot 3$ and $40 = 2^3 \cdot 5$.
- $\gcd(24, 40) = 2^3 = 8$ ✓
- $\text{lcm}(24, 40) = 2^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 = 120$ ✓

The other integer is $40$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
