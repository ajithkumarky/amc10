<Problem>
How many 3-digit base-6 numbers are divisible by 8?
</Problem>

<Solution>
A 3-digit base-6 number has the form $\overline{abc}_6$ where $a \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$ and $b, c \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$.

The smallest 3-digit base-6 number is $100_6 = 36$ and the largest is $555_6 = 5 \cdot 36 + 5 \cdot 6 + 5 = 215$.

So we count integers in $[36, 215]$ that are divisible by 8.

- Smallest multiple of 8 that is $\ge 36$: $\lceil 36/8 \rceil = 5$, so $5 \times 8 = 40$.
- Largest multiple of 8 that is $\le 215$: $\lfloor 215/8 \rfloor = 26$, so $26 \times 8 = 208$.

The multiples of 8 in this range are $8 \times 5, 8 \times 6, \ldots, 8 \times 26$, giving $26 - 5 + 1 = \mathbf{22}$ numbers.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
