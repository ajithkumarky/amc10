<Problem>
What is 2025 written in base 6?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Convert by repeatedly dividing by 6 and recording remainders:

$$2025 \div 6 = 337 \text{ remainder } 3$$
$$337 \div 6 = 56 \text{ remainder } 1$$
$$56 \div 6 = 9 \text{ remainder } 2$$
$$9 \div 6 = 1 \text{ remainder } 3$$
$$1 \div 6 = 0 \text{ remainder } 1$$

Reading the remainders from bottom to top gives $13213_6$.

**Verification:** $1 \cdot 1296 + 3 \cdot 216 + 2 \cdot 36 + 1 \cdot 6 + 3 = 1296 + 648 + 72 + 6 + 3 = 2025$. ✓

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
