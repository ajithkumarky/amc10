<Problem>
What is the value of $1034_5$ in base 10?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Expand by place value, using powers of 5:

$$1034_5 = 1 \cdot 5^3 + 0 \cdot 5^2 + 3 \cdot 5^1 + 4 \cdot 5^0 = 125 + 0 + 15 + 4 = 144.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
