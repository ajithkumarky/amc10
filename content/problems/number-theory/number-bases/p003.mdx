<Problem>
Compute $345_6 + 253_6$. Express your answer in base 6.
</Problem>

<Solution>
Add column by column from right to left, carrying whenever a column sum reaches 6.

**Ones column:** $5 + 3 = 8 = 1 \cdot 6 + 2$. Write 2, carry 1.

**Sixes column:** $4 + 5 + 1\text{ (carry)} = 10 = 1 \cdot 6 + 4$. Write 4, carry 1.

**Thirty-sixes column:** $3 + 2 + 1\text{ (carry)} = 6 = 1 \cdot 6 + 0$. Write 0, carry 1.

**New column:** Write the remaining carry 1.

Result: $1042_6$.

**Verification (base 10):** $345_6 = 3 \cdot 36 + 4 \cdot 6 + 5 = 108 + 24 + 5 = 137$ and $253_6 = 2 \cdot 36 + 5 \cdot 6 + 3 = 72 + 30 + 3 = 105$. Sum $= 242$. Check: $1042_6 = 216 + 0 + 24 + 2 = 242$. ✓

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
