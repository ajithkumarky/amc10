<Problem>
Let $f(x) = x^2 + 1$ and $g(x) = 2x - 3$. What is $f(g(2))$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Composition means we evaluate $g$ first, then feed the result to $f$.

**Step 1:** Compute $g(2)$:
$$g(2) = 2(2) - 3 = 4 - 3 = 1.$$

**Step 2:** Compute $f(g(2)) = f(1)$:
$$f(1) = 1^2 + 1 = 2.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Direct substitution">
Write $f(g(x)) = (2x-3)^2 + 1$ as a general formula, then plug in $x = 2$:
$$f(g(2)) = (2 \cdot 2 - 3)^2 + 1 = 1^2 + 1 = 2.$$
</AlternateMethod>
