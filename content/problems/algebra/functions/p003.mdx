<Problem>
Let $f(x) = \dfrac{x+1}{x-1}$ for $x \neq 1$. What is $f(f(f(3)))$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Apply $f$ three times in sequence.

**First application:** $f(3) = \dfrac{3+1}{3-1} = \dfrac{4}{2} = 2.$

**Second application:** $f(f(3)) = f(2) = \dfrac{2+1}{2-1} = \dfrac{3}{1} = 3.$

**Third application:** $f(f(f(3))) = f(3) = 2.$

Notice the pattern: $f$ maps $3 \to 2 \to 3 \to 2 \to \cdots$, so the sequence of outputs alternates between $2$ and $3$. After an odd number of applications starting from $3$, the result is $2$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
