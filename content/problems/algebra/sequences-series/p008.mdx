<Problem>
Three real numbers $4$, $4 + d$, and $4 + 3d$ (with $d > 0$) form a geometric sequence in that order. Find $d$.
</Problem>

<Solution>
In a geometric sequence, the square of the middle term equals the product of the outer terms:

$$(4+d)^2 = 4(4+3d).$$

Expand both sides:

$$16 + 8d + d^2 = 16 + 12d.$$

Simplify:

$$d^2 - 4d = 0 \implies d(d - 4) = 0.$$

So $d = 0$ or $d = 4$. Since $d > 0$, we have $d = 4$.

*(Check: the three terms are $4, 8, 16$ — each is twice the previous. ✓)*

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
