<Problem>
The first term of an arithmetic sequence is $3$ and the common difference is $4$. What is the $10$th term?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The $n$th term of an arithmetic sequence is $a_n = a_1 + (n-1)d$. With $a_1 = 3$, $d = 4$, and $n = 10$:

$$a_{10} = 3 + (10-1)(4) = 3 + 36 = 39.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Listing terms">
Write out the sequence: $3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27, 31, 35, 39$. The 10th term is $39$.
</AlternateMethod>
