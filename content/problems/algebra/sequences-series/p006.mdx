<Problem>
A sequence is defined by $a_1 = 2$ and $a_{n+1} = 3a_n - 1$ for all $n \geq 1$. What is $a_4$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Apply the recurrence three times starting from $a_1 = 2$:

$$a_2 = 3(2) - 1 = 5,$$
$$a_3 = 3(5) - 1 = 14,$$
$$a_4 = 3(14) - 1 = 41.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Closed form">
The recurrence $a_{n+1} = 3a_n - 1$ is linear. The fixed point satisfies $x = 3x - 1$, giving $x = \tfrac{1}{2}$. Define $b_n = a_n - \tfrac{1}{2}$; then $b_{n+1} = 3b_n$, so $b_n = b_1 \cdot 3^{n-1} = \tfrac{3}{2} \cdot 3^{n-1}$. Therefore $a_n = \tfrac{3^n}{2} + \tfrac{1}{2} = \tfrac{3^n + 1}{2}$. At $n = 4$: $a_4 = \tfrac{81 + 1}{2} = 41$.
</AlternateMethod>
