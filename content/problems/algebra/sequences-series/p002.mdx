<Problem>
What is the sum of the first $20$ terms of the arithmetic sequence $2, 5, 8, 11, \ldots$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The first term is $a_1 = 2$ and the common difference is $d = 3$. Using the partial-sum formula:

$$S_n = \frac{n}{2}\bigl(2a_1 + (n-1)d\bigr).$$

With $n = 20$:

$$S_{20} = \frac{20}{2}\bigl(2(2) + (19)(3)\bigr) = 10(4 + 57) = 10 \cdot 61 = 610.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="First-plus-last formula">
The 20th term is $a_{20} = 2 + 19 \cdot 3 = 59$. Then $S_{20} = \dfrac{20}{2}(2 + 59) = 10 \cdot 61 = 610$.
</AlternateMethod>
