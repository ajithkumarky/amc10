<Problem>
An infinite geometric series has first term $12$ and common ratio $\dfrac{1}{3}$. What is the sum of the series?
</Problem>

<Solution>
For an infinite geometric series with first term $a_1$ and $|r| < 1$, the sum is:

$$S = \frac{a_1}{1 - r}.$$

Here $a_1 = 12$ and $r = \dfrac{1}{3}$:

$$S = \frac{12}{1 - \tfrac{1}{3}} = \frac{12}{\tfrac{2}{3}} = 12 \cdot \frac{3}{2} = 18.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
