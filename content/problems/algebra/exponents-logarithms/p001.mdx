<Problem>
What is $2^3 \cdot 4^2$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Convert $4^2$ to a power of $2$: since $4 = 2^2$, we have $4^2 = (2^2)^2 = 2^4$.

Now apply the product rule (same base, add exponents):

$$2^3 \cdot 4^2 = 2^3 \cdot 2^4 = 2^{3+4} = 2^7 = 128.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
