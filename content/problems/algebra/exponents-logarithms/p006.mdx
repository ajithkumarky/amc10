<Problem>
What is the value of $x$ that satisfies $\log_2(x + 4) = 3$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Convert the logarithmic equation to exponential form using $\log_b k = m \iff b^m = k$:

$$\log_2(x + 4) = 3 \implies 2^3 = x + 4 \implies 8 = x + 4 \implies x = 4.$$

**Check:** $\log_2(4 + 4) = \log_2 8 = \log_2 2^3 = 3$. ✓

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
