<Problem>
If $3^x + 3^{x+2} = 90$, what is $x$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Factor the left side. Notice that $3^{x+2} = 3^x \cdot 3^2 = 9 \cdot 3^x$, so both terms share the factor $3^x$:

$$3^x + 9 \cdot 3^x = 90 \implies 3^x(1 + 9) = 90 \implies 10 \cdot 3^x = 90 \implies 3^x = 9.$$

Since $9 = 3^2$:

$$3^x = 3^2 \implies x = 2.$$

**Check:** $3^2 + 3^4 = 9 + 81 = 90$. ✓

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
