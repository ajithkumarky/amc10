<Problem>
If $2^x \cdot 8^{x-1} = 16$, what is $x$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Convert every base to a power of $2$. Since $8 = 2^3$ and $16 = 2^4$:

$$2^x \cdot \left(2^3\right)^{x-1} = 2^4.$$

Apply the power rule $(b^m)^n = b^{mn}$ to the second factor:

$$2^x \cdot 2^{3(x-1)} = 2^4.$$

Use the product rule (add exponents) on the left side:

$$2^{x + 3(x-1)} = 2^4 \implies 2^{4x - 3} = 2^4.$$

Since the bases are equal, the exponents must be equal:

$$4x - 3 = 4 \implies 4x = 7 \implies x = \frac{7}{4}.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
