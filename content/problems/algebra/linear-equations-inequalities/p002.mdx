<Problem>
The sum of two numbers is $14$ and twice the first number minus the second number equals $7$. What is the difference of the two numbers (first minus second)?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the two numbers be $x$ and $y$. We are given:
$$x + y = 14$$
$$2x - y = 7.$$

Add the two equations:
$$3x = 21 \implies x = 7.$$

Substitute back: $y = 14 - 7 = 7$.

The difference is $x - y = 7 - 7 = 0$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Elimination by inspection">
Adding the equations eliminates $y$ directly. Once we find $x = 7$ and then $y = 7$, the difference $x - y = 0$ is immediate.
</AlternateMethod>
