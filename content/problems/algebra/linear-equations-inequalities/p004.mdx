<Problem>
At a school store, 3 pencils and 2 pens cost $\$1.40$, and 5 pencils and 4 pens cost $\$2.60$. What is the cost of 2 pencils and 3 pens?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $p$ be the cost of one pencil (in cents) and $q$ be the cost of one pen (in cents). We have:
$$3p + 2q = 140$$
$$5p + 4q = 260.$$

Multiply the first equation by 2:
$$6p + 4q = 280.$$

Subtract the second equation:
$$6p + 4q - (5p + 4q) = 280 - 260 \implies p = 20.$$

Substitute $p = 20$ into the first equation:
$$60 + 2q = 140 \implies q = 40.$$

So a pencil costs $20$ cents and a pen costs $40$ cents. The cost of 2 pencils and 3 pens is:
$$2(20) + 3(40) = 40 + 120 = 160 \text{ cents} = \$1.60.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
