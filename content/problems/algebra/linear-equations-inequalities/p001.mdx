<Problem>
If $3x + 7 = 22$, what is the value of $x$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Subtract 7 from both sides:
$$3x = 15.$$

Divide both sides by 3:
$$x = 5.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
