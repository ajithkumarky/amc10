<Problem>
For what values of $x$ is the following inequality true?
$$\frac{x-1}{3} + \frac{x+2}{4} > 5$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
Multiply every term by the least common multiple of 3 and 4, which is 12:
$$4(x-1) + 3(x+2) > 60.$$

Expand:
$$4x - 4 + 3x + 6 > 60$$
$$7x + 2 > 60$$
$$7x > 58$$
$$x > \frac{58}{7}.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Verify with a test value">
Try $x = 9 > 58/7 \approx 8.29$: $\frac{8}{3} + \frac{11}{4} = \frac{32}{12} + \frac{33}{12} = \frac{65}{12} \approx 5.42 > 5$. ✓

Try $x = 8 < 58/7$: $\frac{7}{3} + \frac{10}{4} = \frac{28}{12} + \frac{30}{12} = \frac{58}{12} \approx 4.83 < 5$. ✓ (boundary confirmed)
</AlternateMethod>
