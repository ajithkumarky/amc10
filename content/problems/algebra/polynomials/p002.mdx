<Problem>
When the polynomial $p(x) = x^3 - 4x^2 + 5x - 7$ is divided by $(x - 3)$, what is the remainder?
</Problem>

<Solution>
By the Remainder Theorem, the remainder when $p(x)$ is divided by $(x - 3)$ is $p(3)$.

$$p(3) = 3^3 - 4(3^2) + 5(3) - 7 = 27 - 36 + 15 - 7 = -1.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
