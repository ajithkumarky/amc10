<Problem>
If $p(x) = 2x^2 - 3x + 1$, what is the value of $p(3)$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Substitute $x = 3$ directly:

$$p(3) = 2(3)^2 - 3(3) + 1 = 2 \cdot 9 - 9 + 1 = 18 - 9 + 1 = 10.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Horner's method">
Rewrite $p(x) = (2x - 3)x + 1$. At $x = 3$: first compute $2(3) - 3 = 3$, then $3 \cdot 3 + 1 = 10$.
</AlternateMethod>
