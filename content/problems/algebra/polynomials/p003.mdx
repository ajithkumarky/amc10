<Problem>
Let $r$, $s$, and $t$ be the three roots of $x^3 - 7x^2 + 14x - 8 = 0$. What is the value of $r^2 + s^2 + t^2$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
By Vieta's formulas for $x^3 - 7x^2 + 14x - 8 = 0$:

$$r + s + t = 7, \qquad rs + rt + st = 14, \qquad rst = 8.$$

Use the algebraic identity:

$$r^2 + s^2 + t^2 = (r+s+t)^2 - 2(rs+rt+st) = 7^2 - 2(14) = 49 - 28 = 21.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
