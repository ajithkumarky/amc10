<Problem>
The three roots of $p(x) = x^3 - ax^2 + bx - 12$ are positive integers that multiply to $12$. If the three roots are distinct and their sum is as small as possible, what is $b - a$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We need three distinct positive integers whose product is $12$. List all such triples $\{r, s, t\}$ with $rst = 12$:

- $\{1, 2, 6\}$: sum $= 9$
- $\{1, 3, 4\}$: sum $= 8$

(Other factorizations like $\{2, 2, 3\}$ repeat a value, so they are not distinct.)

The smallest sum is $8$, achieved by the roots $1, 3, 4$.

By Vieta's formulas:
$$a = r + s + t = 1 + 3 + 4 = 8,$$
$$b = rs + rt + st = (1)(3) + (1)(4) + (3)(4) = 3 + 4 + 12 = 19.$$

Therefore $b - a = 19 - 8 = 11$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
