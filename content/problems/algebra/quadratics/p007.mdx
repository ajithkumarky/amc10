<Problem>
Suppose $r$ is a root of $x^2 - 5x + 2 = 0$. What is the value of $r^3 - 5r^2 + 2r + 7$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Since $r$ satisfies $x^2 - 5x + 2 = 0$, we know $r^2 = 5r - 2$.

**Step 1 — find $r^3$.**

$$r^3 = r \cdot r^2 = r(5r - 2) = 5r^2 - 2r = 5(5r - 2) - 2r = 25r - 10 - 2r = 23r - 10.$$

**Step 2 — substitute into the expression.**

$$r^3 - 5r^2 + 2r + 7 = (23r - 10) - 5(5r - 2) + 2r + 7$$

$$= 23r - 10 - 25r + 10 + 2r + 7 = (23 - 25 + 2)r + (-10 + 10 + 7) = 0 \cdot r + 7 = 7.$$

The expression equals $7$ for **either** root of the quadratic.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
