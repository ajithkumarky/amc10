<Problem>
Let $r$ and $s$ be the two roots of $x^2 - 7x + 3 = 0$. What is $r^2 + s^2$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
By Vieta's formulas: $r + s = 7$ and $rs = 3$.

Use the identity $r^2 + s^2 = (r + s)^2 - 2rs$:

$$r^2 + s^2 = 7^2 - 2(3) = 49 - 6 = 43.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Why the identity works">
Expand $(r+s)^2 = r^2 + 2rs + s^2$, so $r^2 + s^2 = (r+s)^2 - 2rs$. This avoids solving for $r$ and $s$ individually (which would require the quadratic formula and messy square roots).
</AlternateMethod>
