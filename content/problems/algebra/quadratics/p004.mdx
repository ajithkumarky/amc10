<Problem>
What is the minimum value of $f(x) = 2x^2 - 8x + 9$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Complete the square to write $f(x)$ in vertex form.

$$f(x) = 2x^2 - 8x + 9 = 2(x^2 - 4x) + 9 = 2(x^2 - 4x + 4 - 4) + 9$$

$$= 2(x-2)^2 - 8 + 9 = 2(x-2)^2 + 1.$$

Since $2(x-2)^2 \geq 0$ for all real $x$, the minimum value of $f$ is $\mathbf{1}$, achieved when $x = 2$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Vertex formula">
For $f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c$, the vertex occurs at $x = -b/(2a)$. Here $x = 8/4 = 2$. Substituting: $f(2) = 2(4) - 8(2) + 9 = 8 - 16 + 9 = 1$.
</AlternateMethod>
