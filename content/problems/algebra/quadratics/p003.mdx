<Problem>
How many positive integer values of $k$ make both roots of $x^2 - kx + 12 = 0$ positive integers?
</Problem>

<Solution>
If both roots are positive integers $r$ and $s$, then by Vieta's formulas $r + s = k$ and $rs = 12$.

We need every pair of positive integers whose product is 12:

| $(r, s)$ | $k = r + s$ |
|---|---|
| $(1, 12)$ | $13$ |
| $(2, 6)$ | $8$ |
| $(3, 4)$ | $7$ |

(The pair $(4,3)$ is the same as $(3,4)$, and similarly for the others.)

Each pair gives a distinct value of $k$, so there are **3** valid values.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
