<Problem>
The product of the roots of $3x^2 - 15x + k = 0$ is $6$. What is the sum of the roots?
</Problem>

<Solution>
By Vieta's formulas for $3x^2 - 15x + k = 0$:

$$\text{product of roots} = \frac{k}{3}, \qquad \text{sum of roots} = \frac{15}{3} = 5.$$

The sum of the roots depends only on the coefficients of $x^2$ and $x$ — it does not depend on $k$. Regardless of the product condition, the sum is always $5$.

*(We can verify $k$: product $= k/3 = 6 \Rightarrow k = 18$, which is consistent.)*

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
