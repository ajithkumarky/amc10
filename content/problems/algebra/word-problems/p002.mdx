<Problem>
Pipe $A$ can fill a tank by itself in 12 hours. Pipe $B$ can fill the same tank by itself in 8 hours. If both pipes are opened simultaneously, how many hours does it take to fill the tank completely?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Pipe $A$ fills $\tfrac{1}{12}$ of the tank per hour and pipe $B$ fills $\tfrac{1}{8}$ of the tank per hour. Together their combined rate is

$$\frac{1}{12} + \frac{1}{8} = \frac{2}{24} + \frac{3}{24} = \frac{5}{24} \text{ tanks per hour.}$$

The time to fill one full tank is the reciprocal of this rate:

$$t = \frac{1}{5/24} = \frac{24}{5} \text{ hours.}$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Formula shortcut">
For two workers (or pipes) with individual times $a$ and $b$, the combined time is $\dfrac{ab}{a+b}$.

$$t = \frac{12 \times 8}{12 + 8} = \frac{96}{20} = \frac{24}{5}.$$
</AlternateMethod>
