<Problem>
A student takes an exam with three sections. The first section is worth 40 points, the second is worth 35 points, and the third is worth 25 points. The student scores 80% on the first section and 60% on the second section. Her overall percentage score on the entire 100-point exam is exactly 70%. What is her percentage score on the third section?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $p$ be the student's percentage score on the third section (as a number, so 100% means $p = 100$).

The points she earns on each section:
- First section: $0.80 \times 40 = 32$ points.
- Second section: $0.60 \times 35 = 21$ points.
- Third section: $\dfrac{p}{100} \times 25$ points.

Her total score on the 100-point exam must equal 70% of 100 = 70 points:

$$32 + 21 + \frac{25p}{100} = 70.$$

$$53 + \frac{p}{4} = 70.$$

$$\frac{p}{4} = 17 \implies p = 68.$$

**Check:** Points from third section = $(68/100)(25) = 17$. Total = $32 + 21 + 17 = 70$. Overall percentage = $70/100 = 70\%$. ✓

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Points deficit">
She needs 70 points total. From the first two sections she earns $32 + 21 = 53$ points, so she needs $70 - 53 = 17$ more points from the third section. The third section is worth 25 points, so her percentage on it is $17/25 = 0.68 = 68\%$.
</AlternateMethod>
