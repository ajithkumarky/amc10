<Problem>
A store sells apples for $\$0.40$ each and oranges for $\$0.60$ each. Maria buys 5 apples and 3 oranges. How much does she spend in total?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The cost of 5 apples is $5 \times \$0.40 = \$2.00$.

The cost of 3 oranges is $3 \times \$0.60 = \$1.80$.

Total spent: $\$2.00 + \$1.80 = \$3.80$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
