<Problem>
The sum of a father's age and his son's age is 56. In 8 years, the father will be exactly twice as old as the son. What is the son's current age?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $s$ = the son's current age and $f$ = the father's current age.

From the two conditions:
$$s + f = 56 \tag{1}$$
$$f + 8 = 2(s + 8) \tag{2}$$

Expand equation (2): $f + 8 = 2s + 16$, so $f = 2s + 8$.

Substitute into equation (1): $s + (2s + 8) = 56 \Rightarrow 3s = 48 \Rightarrow s = 16$.

**Check:** Son is 16, father is $56 - 16 = 40$. In 8 years: son is 24, father is 48, and $48 = 2 \times 24$. ✓

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
