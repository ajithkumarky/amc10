<Problem>
A fair six-sided die (faces labeled $1$ through $6$) is rolled twice. What is the probability that the sum of the two rolls equals $8$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
When a die is rolled twice, the sample space has $6 \times 6 = 36$ equally likely outcomes.

List all pairs $(a, b)$ with $a + b = 8$:

| First roll $a$ | Second roll $b$ |
|:-:|:-:|
| 2 | 6 |
| 3 | 5 |
| 4 | 4 |
| 5 | 3 |
| 6 | 2 |

There are $5$ favorable outcomes, so

$$P(\text{sum} = 8) = \frac{5}{36}.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
