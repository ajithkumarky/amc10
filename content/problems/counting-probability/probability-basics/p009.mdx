<Problem>
A team of $3$ is chosen at random from a group of $5$ boys and $4$ girls. Given that the team contains **at least one girl**, what is the probability that the team contains **exactly two girls**?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We need $P(\text{exactly 2 girls} \mid \text{at least 1 girl})$.

**Step 1 — Count all teams of 3 from 9 people:**

$$\binom{9}{3} = \frac{9 \cdot 8 \cdot 7}{6} = 84.$$

**Step 2 — Count teams with NO girls (all boys):**

$$\binom{5}{3} = 10.$$

**Step 3 — Count teams with at least 1 girl:**

$$84 - 10 = 74.$$

**Step 4 — Count teams with exactly 2 girls (and 1 boy):**

$$\binom{4}{2} \cdot \binom{5}{1} = 6 \cdot 5 = 30.$$

**Step 5 — Apply the conditional probability formula:**

$$P(\text{exactly 2 girls} \mid \text{at least 1 girl}) = \frac{30}{74} = \frac{15}{37}.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
