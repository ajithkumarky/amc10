<Problem>
A jar contains $3$ red marbles, $2$ blue marbles, and $5$ green marbles. One marble is drawn at random. What is the probability that the marble is red?
</Problem>

<Solution>
There are $3 + 2 + 5 = 10$ marbles in total, and each is equally likely to be drawn.

There are $3$ red marbles, so

$$P(\text{red}) = \frac{3}{10}.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
