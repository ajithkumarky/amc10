<Problem>
Cards are labeled $1$ through $10$ and placed in a box. Three cards are drawn at random without replacement. What is the probability that all three drawn cards are labeled with prime numbers?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First, identify the prime numbers in $\{1, 2, 3, \ldots, 10\}$.

Recall that $1$ is **not** prime. The primes are $2, 3, 5, 7$ — exactly $4$ prime numbers.

The total number of ways to choose $3$ cards from $10$ is

$$\binom{10}{3} = \frac{10 \cdot 9 \cdot 8}{3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = 120.$$

The number of ways to choose $3$ cards all from the $4$ primes is

$$\binom{4}{3} = 4.$$

Therefore,

$$P(\text{all three prime}) = \frac{4}{120} = \frac{1}{30}.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
