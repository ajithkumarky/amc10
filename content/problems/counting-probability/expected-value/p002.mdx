<Problem>
A fair coin is flipped twice. For each head that appears you win $\$5$, and for each tail that appears you lose $\$2$. What is the expected value of your net winnings?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $H$ be the number of heads and $T$ the number of tails after two flips. Since $H + T = 2$, we have $T = 2 - H$.

Net winnings $= 5H - 2T = 5H - 2(2 - H) = 7H - 4.$

By linearity of expectation and the fact that $E[H] = 2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 1$:

$$E[\text{winnings}] = 7 \cdot E[H] - 4 = 7 \cdot 1 - 4 = 3.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Direct enumeration">
List all four equally likely outcomes (each with probability $\frac{1}{4}$):

| Outcome | H | T | Winnings |
|---------|---|---|----------|
| HH | 2 | 0 | $10 |
| HT | 1 | 1 | $3 |
| TH | 1 | 1 | $3 |
| TT | 0 | 2 | $-4 |

$$E = \frac{1}{4}(10 + 3 + 3 + (-4)) = \frac{12}{4} = 3.$$
</AlternateMethod>
