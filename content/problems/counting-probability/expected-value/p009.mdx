<Problem>
Two fair six-sided dice are rolled independently. What is the expected value of the **larger** of the two numbers rolled? (If both dice show the same number, that number is the "larger" value.)
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $M = \max(X, Y)$ where $X$ and $Y$ are the results of the two dice.

**Find the distribution of $M$.** Since the dice are independent:

$$P(M \le k) = P(X \le k) \cdot P(Y \le k) = \left(\frac{k}{6}\right)^2 = \frac{k^2}{36}.$$

So the probability mass function of $M$ is:

$$P(M = k) = P(M \le k) - P(M \le k-1) = \frac{k^2}{36} - \frac{(k-1)^2}{36} = \frac{2k - 1}{36}, \quad k = 1, 2, \ldots, 6.$$

**Compute the expectation.**

$$E[M] = \sum_{k=1}^{6} k \cdot \frac{2k-1}{36} = \frac{1}{36} \sum_{k=1}^{6} k(2k-1).$$

Compute each term:

| $k$ | $2k-1$ | $k(2k-1)$ |
|-----|--------|-----------|
| 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 3 | 6 |
| 3 | 5 | 15 |
| 4 | 7 | 28 |
| 5 | 9 | 45 |
| 6 | 11 | 66 |

Sum $= 1 + 6 + 15 + 28 + 45 + 66 = 161$.

$$E[M] = \frac{161}{36}.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
