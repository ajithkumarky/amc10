<Problem>
A point $(x, y)$ is chosen uniformly at random inside the rectangle with $0 \le x \le 3$ and $0 \le y \le 4$. What is the probability that $x + y \le 4$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The rectangle has area $3 \times 4 = 12$.

The line $x + y = 4$ passes through $(0, 4)$ and $(4, 0)$. Inside our rectangle, it runs from $(0, 4)$ (the top-left corner) down to $(3, 1)$.

The region where $x + y > 4$ is the triangle above this line with vertices $(0, 4)$, $(3, 4)$, and $(3, 1)$. Its base is $3$ and its height is $4 - 1 = 3$, so its area is
$$\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3 \cdot 3 = \frac{9}{2}.$$

The favorable area (where $x + y \le 4$) is
$$12 - \frac{9}{2} = \frac{15}{2}.$$

$$P = \frac{15/2}{12} = \frac{15}{24} = \frac{5}{8}.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
