<Problem>
A dart is thrown uniformly at random at a circular board of radius $5$. Two additional concentric circles divide the board into three regions: an inner disk of radius $1$, a middle ring between radii $1$ and $3$, and an outer ring between radii $3$ and $5$. What is the probability that the dart lands in the middle ring?
</Problem>

<Solution>
All areas involve the same $\pi$, so we can work with $r^2$ values only.

- Area of inner disk: $\pi \cdot 1^2 = \pi$.
- Area of disk of radius $3$: $\pi \cdot 3^2 = 9\pi$.
- Area of middle ring: $9\pi - \pi = 8\pi$.
- Total area of board: $\pi \cdot 5^2 = 25\pi$.

$$P(\text{middle ring}) = \frac{8\pi}{25\pi} = \frac{8}{25}.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
