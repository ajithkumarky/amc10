<Problem>
A point $(x, y)$ is chosen uniformly at random inside the unit square $[0,1] \times [0,1]$. What is the probability that $x^2 + y^2 \le \dfrac{1}{2}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The condition $x^2 + y^2 \le \dfrac{1}{2}$ describes a disk centered at the origin with radius $r = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$.

Since $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \approx 0.707 < 1$, the entire quarter of this disk that lies in the first quadrant fits inside the unit square (the arc stays below and to the left of the square's edges).

The favorable region is that quarter-disk, with area

$$\frac{1}{4} \pi r^2 = \frac{1}{4} \cdot \pi \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \frac{\pi}{8}.$$

The unit square has area $1$, so

$$P = \frac{\pi/8}{1} = \frac{\pi}{8}.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
