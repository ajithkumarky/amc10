<Problem>
A point is chosen uniformly at random on the segment $[0, 10]$. What is the probability that the point is within $2$ units of the number $7$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
"Within $2$ units of $7$" means $|x - 7| \le 2$, which gives $x \in [5, 9]$.

This favorable segment has length $9 - 5 = 4$. The total segment has length $10$.

$$P = \frac{4}{10} = \frac{2}{5}.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
