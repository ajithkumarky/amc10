<Problem>
How many ways can the letters $A, A, B, B, C, C$ be arranged in a row so that no two identical letters are adjacent?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Total arrangements.** Without any restriction, the six letters (with two copies each of A, B, C) can be arranged in
$$\frac{6!}{2!\,2!\,2!} = \frac{720}{8} = 90 \text{ ways.}$$

**Complementary counting.** Let $\mathcal{A}$, $\mathcal{B}$, $\mathcal{C}$ be the sets of arrangements in which the two A's are adjacent, the two B's are adjacent, and the two C's are adjacent, respectively. We will compute $|\mathcal{A} \cup \mathcal{B} \cup \mathcal{C}|$ and subtract.

*Single sets.* Treat AA as one block; arrange [AA], B, B, C, C:
$$|\mathcal{A}| = \frac{5!}{2!\,2!} = 30.$$
By symmetry, $|\mathcal{B}| = |\mathcal{C}| = 30$.

*Pairs of sets.* Treat both AA and BB as blocks; arrange [AA], [BB], C, C:
$$|\mathcal{A} \cap \mathcal{B}| = \frac{4!}{2!} = 12.$$
By symmetry, $|\mathcal{A} \cap \mathcal{C}| = |\mathcal{B} \cap \mathcal{C}| = 12$.

*All three.* Treat all three pairs as blocks; arrange [AA], [BB], [CC]:
$$|\mathcal{A} \cap \mathcal{B} \cap \mathcal{C}| = 3! = 6.$$

**Inclusion-exclusion:**
$$|\mathcal{A} \cup \mathcal{B} \cup \mathcal{C}| = (30+30+30) - (12+12+12) + 6 = 90 - 36 + 6 = 60.$$

**Answer:**
$$90 - 60 = 30.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
