<Problem>
How many $4$-digit positive integers (from $1000$ to $9999$) contain the digit $7$ at least once?
</Problem>

<Solution>
It is easier to count the complement — $4$-digit integers with *no* digit equal to $7$ — and subtract from the total.

**Total 4-digit integers:** The thousands digit runs from $1$ to $9$, giving $9$ choices; each of the remaining three digits runs from $0$ to $9$, giving $10$ choices each.
$$\text{Total} = 9 \times 10 \times 10 \times 10 = 9000.$$

**4-digit integers with no 7:** The thousands digit can be $1$–$9$ except $7$: $8$ choices. Each of the remaining three digits can be $0$–$9$ except $7$: $9$ choices each.
$$\text{No 7} = 8 \times 9 \times 9 \times 9 = 5832.$$

**At least one 7:**
$$9000 - 5832 = 3168.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
