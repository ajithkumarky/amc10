<Problem>
At a party of $30$ guests, $18$ guests like pizza, $15$ guests like tacos, and $7$ guests like both pizza and tacos. How many guests like neither pizza nor tacos?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First find the number of guests who like pizza or tacos (or both) using inclusion-exclusion:

$$|\text{Pizza} \cup \text{Tacos}| = |\text{Pizza}| + |\text{Tacos}| - |\text{Pizza} \cap \text{Tacos}| = 18 + 15 - 7 = 26.$$

The guests who like *neither* are those outside this union:
$$30 - 26 = 4.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
