<Problem>
In a class of $50$ students, $24$ take math, $19$ take science, and $15$ take history. Among these, $7$ take both math and science, $5$ take both math and history, $4$ take both science and history, and $2$ take all three subjects. How many students take none of these three subjects?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Apply inclusion-exclusion to find the number of students taking at least one subject:

$$|M \cup S \cup H| = |M| + |S| + |H| - |M \cap S| - |M \cap H| - |S \cap H| + |M \cap S \cap H|.$$

Substituting the given values:
$$= 24 + 19 + 15 - 7 - 5 - 4 + 2 = 44.$$

The number of students taking *none* of the three subjects is:
$$50 - 44 = 6.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
