<Problem>
A clothing store sells $3$ styles of shirts, $4$ styles of pants, and $2$ styles of shoes. If an outfit consists of exactly one shirt, one pair of pants, and one pair of shoes, how many distinct outfits are possible?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Each choice is independent: any shirt can be paired with any pants, and any pants can be paired with any shoes. By the Multiplication Principle:

$$3 \times 4 \times 2 = 24.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
