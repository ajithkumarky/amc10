<Problem>
A sports team is to be formed with exactly $3$ men and $2$ women chosen from a pool of $6$ men and $5$ women. How many different teams are possible?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The men's selection and women's selection are **independent** choices, so we multiply.

**Choose 3 men from 6** (order doesn't matter):
$$\binom{6}{3} = \frac{6 \cdot 5 \cdot 4}{3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = 20.$$

**Choose 2 women from 5** (order doesn't matter):
$$\binom{5}{2} = \frac{5 \cdot 4}{2 \cdot 1} = 10.$$

**Total teams:**
$$20 \times 10 = 200.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
