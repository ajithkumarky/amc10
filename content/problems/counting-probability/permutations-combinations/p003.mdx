<Problem>
How many distinct arrangements of the letters in the word $\text{BOOK}$ are there?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The word BOOK has 4 letters: B, O, O, K. The letter O appears **twice**; all other letters appear once.

If all four letters were distinct, there would be $4! = 24$ arrangements. However, swapping the two O's produces the same word, so we divide by $2!$ to remove this over-counting:

$$\frac{4!}{2!} = \frac{24}{2} = 12.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Direct slot-filling">
Place the letters one position at a time. First choose which 2 of the 4 positions hold the O's: $\binom{4}{2} = 6$ ways. The remaining 2 positions are filled by B and K in $2! = 2$ ways. Total: $6 \times 2 = 12$.
</AlternateMethod>
