<Problem>
How many distinct arrangements of the letters in the word $\text{ARRANGE}$ are there?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First, identify the letters and their frequencies.

$$\text{A R R A N G E}$$

- A appears **2** times
- R appears **2** times
- N, G, E each appear **1** time

Total: $2 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 7$ letters.

If all 7 letters were distinct, there would be $7! = 5040$ arrangements. We divide by the factorials of each repeated group to remove identical rearrangements:

$$\frac{7!}{2! \cdot 2!} = \frac{5040}{2 \cdot 2} = \frac{5040}{4} = 1260.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
