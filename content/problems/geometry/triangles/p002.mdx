<Problem>
In a $30$--$60$--$90$ right triangle, the hypotenuse has length $2$. What is the length of the side opposite the $60°$ angle?
</Problem>

<Solution>
A $30$--$60$--$90$ triangle has side ratios $1 : \sqrt{3} : 2$ for the sides opposite the $30°$, $60°$, and $90°$ angles respectively.

When the hypotenuse (opposite the $90°$ angle) is $2$, the corresponding multiplier is $1$. So the side opposite the $60°$ angle is $\sqrt{3}$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
