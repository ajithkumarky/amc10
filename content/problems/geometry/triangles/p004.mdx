<Problem>
In a $30$--$60$--$90$ right triangle, the side opposite the $30°$ angle has length $5$. What is the area of the triangle?
</Problem>

<Solution>
In a $30$--$60$--$90$ triangle the sides are in ratio $1 : \sqrt{3} : 2$, for the sides opposite the $30°$, $60°$, and $90°$ angles respectively.

Since the side opposite $30°$ is $5$, the scale factor is $5$. So:

- Side opposite $30°$: $5$
- Side opposite $60°$: $5\sqrt{3}$
- Hypotenuse (opposite $90°$): $10$

The two legs are the sides opposite $30°$ and $60°$, with lengths $5$ and $5\sqrt{3}$. These are the base and height of the right triangle:

$$\text{Area} = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot 5 \cdot 5\sqrt{3} = \frac{25\sqrt{3}}{2}$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
