<Problem>
The two legs of a right triangle are in the ratio $3 : 4$, and the hypotenuse has length $20$. What is the perimeter of the triangle?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the two legs be $3k$ and $4k$ for some positive constant $k$.

By the Pythagorean theorem:

$$(3k)^2 + (4k)^2 = 20^2$$
$$9k^2 + 16k^2 = 400$$
$$25k^2 = 400 \implies k^2 = 16 \implies k = 4$$

So the legs are $3(4) = 12$ and $4(4) = 16$.

The perimeter is:

$$12 + 16 + 20 = 48$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Recognizing the Pythagorean triple">
Legs in ratio $3:4$ with hypotenuse in ratio $5$ means the triangle is a scaled $3$-$4$-$5$ right triangle. Since the hypotenuse $5k = 20$, we get $k = 4$. The perimeter is $(3 + 4 + 5) \cdot k = 12 \cdot 4 = 48$.
</AlternateMethod>
