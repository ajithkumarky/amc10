<Problem>
Triangle $ABC$ has area $60$. Point $D$ lies on $\overline{BC}$ with $BD : DC = 2 : 3$, and point $E$ lies on $\overline{AD}$ with $AE : ED = 3 : 1$. What is the area of triangle $ABE$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Step 1 — find the area of $\triangle ABD$.**

Triangles $ABD$ and $ACD$ share the same height from $A$ to line $BC$. Their areas are therefore proportional to their bases $BD$ and $DC$:

$$\frac{[\triangle ABD]}{[\triangle ABC]} = \frac{BD}{BC} = \frac{2}{5}$$

$$[\triangle ABD] = \frac{2}{5} \cdot 60 = 24$$

**Step 2 — find the area of $\triangle ABE$.**

Place $A$ at the origin. Then $E = A + \tfrac{3}{4}(D - A) = \tfrac{3}{4}\overrightarrow{D}$ (since $AE : ED = 3:1$ means $E$ is $\tfrac{3}{4}$ of the way from $A$ to $D$).

Triangles $ABE$ and $ABD$ share vertex $B$. Their areas equal $\tfrac{1}{2}|AB \times AE|$ and $\tfrac{1}{2}|AB \times AD|$ respectively. Since $AE = \tfrac{3}{4} AD$:

$$\frac{[\triangle ABE]}{[\triangle ABD]} = \frac{AE}{AD} = \frac{3}{4}$$

$$[\triangle ABE] = \frac{3}{4} \cdot 24 = 18$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
