<Problem>
What is the sum of the interior angles of a convex hexagon?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The sum of the interior angles of any convex $n$-gon is $(n-2) \times 180°$.

For a hexagon, $n = 6$:
$$(6 - 2) \times 180° = 4 \times 180° = 720°$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
