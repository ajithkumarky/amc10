<Problem>
Trapezoid $ABCD$ has $AB \parallel CD$ with $AB = 15$ and $CD = 9$. Angle $A$ is a right angle, and $BC = 10$. What is the area of the trapezoid?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Since $\angle A = 90°$, side $AD$ is perpendicular to the parallel bases $AB$ and $CD$, making $AD$ the height of the trapezoid.

Drop a perpendicular from $C$ to a point $E$ on $AB$. Because $CD \parallel AB$ and $\angle A = 90°$, the segment $AD$ is perpendicular to both bases, so $AD = CE$ and $CE$ is also the height.

Since $CD = 9$ and $AB = 15$, the horizontal distance from $D$ to $C$ along the top is $9$, and from $A$ to $E$ along the bottom is also $9$ (since $AECD$ is a rectangle with $AE = CD = 9$). Therefore the remaining portion $EB = 15 - 9 = 6$.

In right triangle $CEB$:
$$CE^2 + EB^2 = BC^2$$
$$CE^2 + 6^2 = 10^2$$
$$CE^2 = 100 - 36 = 64 \implies CE = 8$$

So the height of the trapezoid is $h = 8$. The area is:
$$\text{Area} = \frac{(AB + CD)}{2} \times h = \frac{(15 + 9)}{2} \times 8 = \frac{24}{2} \times 8 = 12 \times 8 = 96$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
