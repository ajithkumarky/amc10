<Problem>
How many diagonals does a convex decagon (10-sided polygon) have?
</Problem>

<Solution>
In an $n$-gon, each vertex can be connected to $n - 3$ other vertices by a diagonal (all vertices except itself and its two adjacent neighbors). With $n = 10$ vertices, each vertex contributes $7$ diagonals.

Since each diagonal is counted twice (once from each endpoint), the total number of diagonals is:
$$\frac{n(n-3)}{2} = \frac{10 \times 7}{2} = \frac{70}{2} = 35$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Combinations minus sides">
The total number of line segments connecting any two of the $10$ vertices is $\dbinom{10}{2} = 45$. Of these, $10$ are sides of the decagon. The remaining $45 - 10 = 35$ are diagonals.

Answer: **E**.
</AlternateMethod>
