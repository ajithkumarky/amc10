<Problem>
A rectangle has a perimeter of $44$. Its length is $4$ more than its width. What is the area of the rectangle?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the width be $w$ and the length be $\ell = w + 4$.

The perimeter of a rectangle is $2\ell + 2w$, so:
$$2(w + 4) + 2w = 44$$
$$4w + 8 = 44$$
$$4w = 36 \implies w = 9$$

Therefore $\ell = 9 + 4 = 13$, and the area is:
$$\ell \times w = 13 \times 9 = 117$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
