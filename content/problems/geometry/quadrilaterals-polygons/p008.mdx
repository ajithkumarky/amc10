<Problem>
The interior angles of a convex polygon form an arithmetic sequence. The smallest interior angle is $120°$ and the common difference between consecutive angles is $5°$. How many sides does the polygon have?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the polygon have $n$ sides. The angles are $120°, 125°, 130°, \ldots$ with common difference $5°$.

The sum of an arithmetic sequence of $n$ terms with first term $a = 120°$ and common difference $d = 5°$ is:
$$S = \frac{n}{2}\bigl(2a + (n-1)d\bigr) = \frac{n}{2}\bigl(240 + 5(n-1)\bigr) = \frac{n}{2}(235 + 5n)$$

This sum must equal the interior angle sum of an $n$-gon:
$$\frac{n}{2}(235 + 5n) = (n-2) \times 180$$

Multiply both sides by $2$:
$$n(235 + 5n) = 360n - 720$$
$$235n + 5n^2 = 360n - 720$$
$$5n^2 - 125n + 720 = 0$$
$$n^2 - 25n + 144 = 0$$
$$(n - 9)(n - 16) = 0$$

So $n = 9$ or $n = 16$.

Check convexity (all interior angles must be less than $180°$):
- For $n = 9$: largest angle $= 120 + 8(5) = 160° < 180°$ ✓
- For $n = 16$: largest angle $= 120 + 15(5) = 195° > 180°$ ✗ (not convex)

The polygon has $\boxed{9}$ sides.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
