<Problem>
In triangle $ABC$, point $D$ lies on $\overline{AB}$ and point $E$ lies on $\overline{AC}$ with $\overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}$. The area of triangle $ADE$ is $9$ and the area of trapezoid $BCED$ is $16$. What is the ratio $AD : DB$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Step 1 — relate the areas.**

Since $DE \parallel BC$, triangle $ADE$ is similar to triangle $ABC$ with linear scale factor $k = AD/AB$. The area ratio equals $k^2$, so:
$$\frac{[ADE]}{[ABC]} = k^2.$$

The total area is $[ABC] = [ADE] + [BCED] = 9 + 16 = 25$, so:
$$k^2 = \frac{[ADE]}{[ABC]} = \frac{9}{25} \implies k = \frac{3}{5}.$$

**Step 2 — find $AD : DB$.**

We have $k = AD/AB = 3/5$, so $AD = 3$ parts and $AB = 5$ parts, giving $DB = AB - AD = 2$ parts.

$$AD : DB = 3 : 2 = \frac{3}{2}.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
