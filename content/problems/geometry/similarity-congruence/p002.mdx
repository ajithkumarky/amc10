<Problem>
Triangle $PQR$ is similar to triangle $STU$ with $PQ$ corresponding to $ST$. If $PQ = 6$, $ST = 9$, and the area of triangle $PQR$ is $24$, what is the area of triangle $STU$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The linear scale factor from $\triangle PQR$ to $\triangle STU$ is
$$k = \frac{ST}{PQ} = \frac{9}{6} = \frac{3}{2}.$$

Because areas scale as the square of the linear scale factor,
$$\frac{[\triangle STU]}{[\triangle PQR]} = k^2 = \left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{9}{4}.$$

Therefore
$$[\triangle STU] = \frac{9}{4} \cdot 24 = 54.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
