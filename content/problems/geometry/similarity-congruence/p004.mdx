<Problem>
In triangle $ABC$, $AB = 10$, $AC = 6$, and $BC = 16$. The angle bisector from vertex $A$ meets side $\overline{BC}$ at point $D$. What is the length $BD$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
By the **Angle Bisector Theorem**, the bisector from $A$ divides the opposite side in the ratio of the adjacent sides:
$$\frac{BD}{DC} = \frac{AB}{AC} = \frac{10}{6} = \frac{5}{3}.$$

Since $BD + DC = BC = 16$, we can write $BD = \dfrac{5}{8} \cdot BC$:
$$BD = \frac{5}{5 + 3} \cdot 16 = \frac{5}{8} \cdot 16 = 10.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
