<Problem>
In triangle $ABC$, point $D$ lies on $\overline{AB}$ and point $E$ lies on $\overline{AC}$ such that $\overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}$. If $AD = 3$, $DB = 3$, and $BC = 10$, what is the length of $DE$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Since $DE \parallel BC$, the Basic Proportionality Theorem tells us that $\triangle ADE \sim \triangle ABC$ by AA similarity (they share angle $A$, and $\angle ADE = \angle ABC$ as corresponding angles).

The scale factor is
$$k = \frac{AD}{AB} = \frac{3}{3 + 3} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}.$$

Therefore
$$DE = k \cdot BC = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 10 = 5.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
