<Problem>
Trapezoid $ABCD$ has $\overline{AB} \parallel \overline{CD}$ with $AB = 15$ and $CD = 9$. The diagonals $\overline{AC}$ and $\overline{BD}$ intersect at point $E$. If $DE = 6$, what is the length $BE$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Since $AB \parallel CD$, angles $\angle EAB$ and $\angle ECD$ are alternate interior angles (equal), and angles $\angle EBA$ and $\angle EDC$ are alternate interior angles (equal). Together with the shared vertex angle at $E$ (vertical angles), we get $\triangle ABE \sim \triangle CDE$ by AA similarity.

The scale factor from $\triangle CDE$ to $\triangle ABE$ is
$$k = \frac{AB}{CD} = \frac{15}{9} = \frac{5}{3}.$$

Corresponding sides scale by $k$, so
$$\frac{BE}{DE} = \frac{5}{3} \implies BE = \frac{5}{3} \cdot 6 = 10.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
