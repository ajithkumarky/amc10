<Problem>
Rectangle $ABCD$ has $AB = 8$ and $BC = 10$, with vertices labeled in order so that $AB \parallel DC$. Point $M$ is the midpoint of $\overline{BC}$. Segment $\overline{AM}$ is extended beyond $M$ until it meets line $DC$ extended at point $E$ (outside the rectangle). What is the length $CE$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Set up coordinates: place $B$ at the origin, $C$ at $(10, 0)$, $A$ at $(0, 8)$, $D$ at $(10, 8)$. Then $M$, the midpoint of $\overline{BC}$, is at $(5, 0)$.

**Identify the congruent triangles.** Consider $\triangle ABM$ and $\triangle ECM$:
- $\angle ABM = \angle ECM = 90°$ (both are right angles — $AB \perp BC$ and $E$ lies on line $DC$ which is parallel to $AB$, so $EC \perp BC$).
- $BM = CM = 5$ (since $M$ is the midpoint of $BC$).
- $\angle AMB = \angle EMC$ (vertical angles at $M$).

By **ASA**, $\triangle ABM \cong \triangle ECM$.

Therefore $CE = AB = 8$.

**Coordinate check:** Line $AM$ passes through $A = (0, 8)$ and $M = (5, 0)$. Its slope is $\dfrac{0-8}{5-0} = -\dfrac{8}{5}$. Parametrically, beyond $M$: at $x = 10$, $y = 0 + (-\tfrac{8}{5})(10 - 5) = -8$. So $E = (10, -8)$, giving $CE = |0 - (-8)| = 8$ ✓.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
