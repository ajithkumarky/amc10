<Problem>
Right triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $C$, with legs $AC = 8$ and $BC = 15$. The altitude from $C$ meets the hypotenuse $\overline{AB}$ at point $D$. What is the length of the longer segment, $DB$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Step 1 — find the hypotenuse.**

$$AB = \sqrt{AC^2 + BC^2} = \sqrt{64 + 225} = \sqrt{289} = 17.$$

**Step 2 — identify the similar triangles.**

The altitude from the right angle creates $\triangle ACD \sim \triangle ACB$ and $\triangle CBD \sim \triangle CAB$. In particular, $\triangle CBD \sim \triangle CAB$ (both have a right angle and share angle $B$).

**Step 3 — set up the proportion for $DB$.**

From $\triangle CBD \sim \triangle CAB$:
$$\frac{DB}{CB} = \frac{CB}{AB} \implies DB = \frac{CB^2}{AB} = \frac{15^2}{17} = \frac{225}{17}.$$

We can verify $AD = \dfrac{AC^2}{AB} = \dfrac{64}{17}$, and indeed $AD + DB = \dfrac{64}{17} + \dfrac{225}{17} = \dfrac{289}{17} = 17$ ✓.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
