<Problem>
A circle has radius $6$. A central angle of $120°$ cuts off an arc. What is the length of that arc?
</Problem>

<Solution>
An arc is a fraction of the full circumference. The fraction equals the central angle divided by $360°$:

$$\text{Arc length} = \frac{120}{360} \cdot 2\pi r = \frac{1}{3} \cdot 2\pi(6) = \frac{1}{3} \cdot 12\pi = 4\pi$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Using radians">
Convert $120°$ to radians: $120° = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ radians. Arc length equals the radius times the central angle in radians:
$$\ell = r\theta = 6 \cdot \frac{2\pi}{3} = 4\pi$$
</AlternateMethod>
