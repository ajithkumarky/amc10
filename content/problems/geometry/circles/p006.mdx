<Problem>
A sector of a circle has radius $8$ and central angle $150°$. What is the area of the sector?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The area of a sector is the corresponding fraction of the full disk's area:

$$A_{\text{sector}} = \frac{\theta}{360°} \cdot \pi r^2 = \frac{150}{360} \cdot \pi (8)^2$$

Simplify the fraction: $\dfrac{150}{360} = \dfrac{5}{12}$.

$$A_{\text{sector}} = \frac{5}{12} \cdot 64\pi = \frac{320\pi}{12} = \frac{80\pi}{3}$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
