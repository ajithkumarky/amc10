<Problem>
A circle has a radius of $7$. What is the area of the circle?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The area of a circle with radius $r$ is $A = \pi r^2$.

Substituting $r = 7$:
$$A = \pi (7)^2 = 49\pi$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
