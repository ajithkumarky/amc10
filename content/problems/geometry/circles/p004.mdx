<Problem>
Point $P$ is outside a circle whose center is $O$ and whose radius is $5$. If $OP = 13$, what is the length of the tangent segment from $P$ to the circle?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $T$ be the point where the tangent from $P$ touches the circle. Since a tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of tangency, triangle $OTP$ is a right triangle with the right angle at $T$.

The sides are:
- $OT = 5$ (radius)
- $OP = 13$ (given)
- $PT = ?$ (tangent length we want)

By the Pythagorean theorem:
$$PT^2 + OT^2 = OP^2$$
$$PT^2 = OP^2 - OT^2 = 13^2 - 5^2 = 169 - 25 = 144$$
$$PT = 12$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
