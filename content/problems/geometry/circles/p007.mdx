<Problem>
Two circles have radii $5$ and $3$, and their centers are $10$ units apart. What is the length of a common external tangent to the two circles?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Setup.** Let $O_1$ and $O_2$ be the centers with radii $r_1 = 5$ and $r_2 = 3$, and let $d = O_1 O_2 = 10$. Let $\overline{PQ}$ be a common external tangent, tangent to the first circle at $P$ and the second at $Q$.

**Key construction.** Draw $O_1 P \perp PQ$ and $O_2 Q \perp PQ$ (tangent-radius is perpendicular). Since both radii are perpendicular to the same line, $O_1 P \parallel O_2 Q$.

Drop a perpendicular from $O_2$ to $O_1 P$, meeting $O_1 P$ at $R$. In rectangle $O_2 Q P R$ we have $PR = O_2 Q = 3$ and $O_2 R = PQ$, while $O_1 R = O_1 P - PR = 5 - 3 = 2$.

Right triangle $O_1 R O_2$ has:
- hypotenuse $O_1 O_2 = 10$
- leg $O_1 R = 2$
- leg $R O_2 = PQ$ (what we want)

By the Pythagorean theorem:
$$PQ^2 = O_1 O_2^2 - O_1 R^2 = 10^2 - 2^2 = 100 - 4 = 96$$
$$PQ = \sqrt{96} = 4\sqrt{6}$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
