<Problem>
Two chords $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{CD}$ intersect at point $P$ inside a circle, with $AP = 3$, $PB = 8$, and $CP = 4$. What is the length of $PD$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
When two chords intersect inside a circle, the products of their segments are equal. This is the intersecting-chords form of the power-of-a-point theorem:

$$AP \cdot PB = CP \cdot PD$$

Substituting the known lengths:
$$3 \cdot 8 = 4 \cdot PD$$
$$24 = 4 \cdot PD$$
$$PD = 6$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
