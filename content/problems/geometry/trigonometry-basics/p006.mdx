<Problem>
An isosceles right triangle has hypotenuse of length $8\sqrt{2}$. What is the area of the triangle?
</Problem>

<Solution>
An isosceles right triangle has two equal legs and angles $45°$-$45°$-$90°$. In a $45°$-$45°$-$90°$ triangle the hypotenuse equals $\sqrt{2}$ times a leg, so:
$$\text{leg} = \frac{\text{hypotenuse}}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{8\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2}} = 8.$$

The two legs each have length $8$. The area is:
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 8 \cdot 8 = 32.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Using the area-sine formula">
With the included angle of $90°$ between the two equal legs $\ell$:
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}\ell^2 \sin 90° = \frac{1}{2}\ell^2.$$
Since $\ell = 8$, Area $= \dfrac{1}{2}(64) = 32$.
</AlternateMethod>
