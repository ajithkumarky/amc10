<Problem>
A right triangle has one acute angle of $30°$ and a hypotenuse of length $10$. What is the area of the triangle?
</Problem>

<Solution>
In a $30°$-$60°$-$90°$ triangle the side ratios are $1 : \sqrt{3} : 2$ (opposite $30°$, $60°$, $90°$ respectively). With hypotenuse $10$:

- Leg opposite $30°$: $\dfrac{10}{2} = 5$
- Leg opposite $60°$: $\dfrac{10\sqrt{3}}{2} = 5\sqrt{3}$

The area is
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot (\text{leg}_1) \cdot (\text{leg}_2) = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 5 \cdot 5\sqrt{3} = \frac{25\sqrt{3}}{2}.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
