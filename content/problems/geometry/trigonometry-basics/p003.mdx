<Problem>
Triangle $DEF$ has $DE = 5$, $DF = 7$, and $\angle D = 60°$. What is the area of triangle $DEF$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We know two sides and the included angle, so we apply the area formula:
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot DE \cdot DF \cdot \sin(\angle D).$$

Substituting $DE = 5$, $DF = 7$, and $\sin 60° = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$:
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 5 \cdot 7 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{35}{4} \cdot \sqrt{3} = \frac{35\sqrt{3}}{4}.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Coordinate placement">
Place $D$ at the origin, $E$ at $(5, 0)$. Since $\angle D = 60°$, point $F$ lies at $(7\cos 60°, 7\sin 60°) = \bigl(\tfrac{7}{2}, \tfrac{7\sqrt{3}}{2}\bigr)$. The base is $DE = 5$ along the $x$-axis; the height is the $y$-coordinate of $F$, which is $\tfrac{7\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 5 \cdot \frac{7\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{35\sqrt{3}}{4}.$$
</AlternateMethod>
