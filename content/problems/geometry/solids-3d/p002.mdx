<Problem>
A sphere has a surface area of $100\pi$. What is the volume of the sphere?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Step 1 — find the radius.**

The surface area of a sphere is $SA = 4\pi r^2$. Setting this equal to $100\pi$:
$$4\pi r^2 = 100\pi \implies r^2 = 25 \implies r = 5$$

**Step 2 — compute the volume.**

$$V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi (5)^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi \cdot 125 = \frac{500\pi}{3}$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
