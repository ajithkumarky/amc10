<Problem>
A sphere has radius $10$. A plane cuts through the sphere at a distance of $6$ from the center. What is the area of the circular cross-section?
</Problem>

<Solution>
When a plane cuts a sphere of radius $R$ at distance $d$ from the center, the cross-section is a circle. The radius $\rho$ of that circle satisfies the Pythagorean theorem in the right triangle formed by:
- the sphere's radius $R$ (hypotenuse, from center of sphere to a point on the cross-section circle),
- the distance $d$ from the center of the sphere to the plane (one leg),
- the radius $\rho$ of the cross-section (other leg).

So $\rho^2 = R^2 - d^2$.

Substituting $R = 10$ and $d = 6$:
$$\rho^2 = 10^2 - 6^2 = 100 - 36 = 64 \implies \rho = 8$$

The area of the cross-section is:
$$A = \pi \rho^2 = 64\pi$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
