<Problem>
A right circular cylinder has a base radius of $3$ and a height of $5$. What is the volume of the cylinder?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The volume of a right circular cylinder with base radius $r$ and height $h$ is $V = \pi r^2 h$.

Substituting $r = 3$ and $h = 5$:
$$V = \pi (3)^2 (5) = \pi \cdot 9 \cdot 5 = 45\pi$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
