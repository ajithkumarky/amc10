<Problem>
A right circular cone has base radius $6$ and height $8$. What is the total surface area of the cone?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Step 1 — find the slant height.**

The slant height $l$ connects the apex to a point on the base circle. By the Pythagorean theorem:
$$l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2} = \sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{36 + 64} = \sqrt{100} = 10$$

**Step 2 — compute the total surface area.**

The total surface area is the sum of the lateral (side) area and the base area:
$$SA = \pi r l + \pi r^2 = \pi(6)(10) + \pi(6)^2 = 60\pi + 36\pi = 96\pi$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
