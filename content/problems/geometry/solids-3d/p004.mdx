<Problem>
A rectangular box has dimensions $3 \times 4 \times 12$. What is the length of the space diagonal of the box — that is, the segment connecting two opposite vertices?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The space diagonal of a rectangular box with dimensions $l \times w \times h$ has length
$$d = \sqrt{l^2 + w^2 + h^2}.$$

Substituting $l = 3$, $w = 4$, $h = 12$:
$$d = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2 + 12^2} = \sqrt{9 + 16 + 144} = \sqrt{169} = 13$$

*Alternative view:* The diagonal of the $3 \times 4$ base is $\sqrt{9+16} = 5$ (a 3-4-5 right triangle). The space diagonal is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs $5$ and $12$, which is a 5-12-13 right triangle, giving $d = 13$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
