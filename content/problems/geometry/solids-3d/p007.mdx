<Problem>
A right square pyramid has a square base of side length $10$ and a perpendicular height of $12$. What is the volume of the pyramid?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The volume of any pyramid is $V = \tfrac{1}{3} \cdot B \cdot h$, where $B$ is the base area and $h$ is the perpendicular height.

The base is a $10 \times 10$ square, so $B = 100$.

$$V = \frac{1}{3} \cdot 100 \cdot 12 = \frac{1200}{3} = 400$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Sanity check via bounding box">
The enclosing rectangular box has dimensions $10 \times 10 \times 12$ and volume $1200$. A pyramid is exactly $\tfrac{1}{3}$ of its enclosing prism, giving $V = 400$. This confirms the answer without any new computation.
</AlternateMethod>
