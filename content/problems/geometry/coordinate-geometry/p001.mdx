<Problem>
What is the distance between the points $(1, 2)$ and $(4, 6)$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Apply the distance formula with $(x_1, y_1) = (1, 2)$ and $(x_2, y_2) = (4, 6)$:

$$d = \sqrt{(4 - 1)^2 + (6 - 2)^2} = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{9 + 16} = \sqrt{25} = 5.$$

The two coordinate differences $3$ and $4$ form a Pythagorean triple $(3, 4, 5)$, so no calculator is needed.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
