<Problem>
How many lattice points $(a, b)$ with $a > 0$ and $b > 0$ lie on the circle $(x - 5)^2 + (y - 5)^2 = 25$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
A lattice point satisfies $(a - 5)^2 + (b - 5)^2 = 25$ with $a, b$ positive integers. Let $u = a - 5$ and $v = b - 5$; we need $u^2 + v^2 = 25$ where $a = u + 5 > 0$ and $b = v + 5 > 0$, i.e., $u > -5$ and $v > -5$.

**Find all integer pairs $(u, v)$ with $u^2 + v^2 = 25$:**

The ways to write $25$ as a sum of two integer squares are:

$$25 = 0^2 + 5^2 = 5^2 + 0^2 = 3^2 + 4^2 = 4^2 + 3^2,$$

with all sign combinations. The full list of pairs $(u, v)$:

$$(0, \pm 5),\quad (\pm 5, 0),\quad (\pm 3, \pm 4),\quad (\pm 4, \pm 3).$$

That is $2 + 2 + 4 + 4 = 12$ pairs total.

**Apply the constraint $u > -5$ and $v > -5$:**

We need to exclude pairs where $u \leq -5$ or $v \leq -5$.

- $u = -5$: pair $(-5, 0)$ gives $a = 0$, excluded ($a > 0$ requires $a \geq 1$, and $a = 0$ fails).
- $v = -5$: pair $(0, -5)$ gives $b = 0$, excluded.

No other pair has $|u| = 5$ or $|v| = 5$ except those two. All pairs with $|u|, |v| \in \{3, 4\}$ satisfy $u, v \in \{-4, -3, 3, 4\}$, so $u > -5$ and $v > -5$ automatically. Similarly, $(\pm 3, \pm 4)$ and $(\pm 4, \pm 3)$ all satisfy both inequalities, giving $a, b \geq 1$.

Check $(5, 0)$: $a = 10 > 0$, $b = 5 > 0$. Included. $\checkmark$
Check $(0, 5)$: $a = 5 > 0$, $b = 10 > 0$. Included. $\checkmark$

So we exclude exactly 2 pairs ($(-5, 0)$ and $(0, -5)$), leaving $12 - 2 = 10$ valid lattice points.

**The 10 points:** $(10,5),\,(5,10),\,(8,9),\,(8,1),\,(2,9),\,(2,1),\,(9,8),\,(9,2),\,(1,8),\,(1,2)$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
