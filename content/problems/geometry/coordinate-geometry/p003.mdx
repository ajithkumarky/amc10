<Problem>
Find the area of the triangle with vertices $P = (0, 0)$, $Q = (6, 0)$, and $R = (2, 5)$.
</Problem>

<Solution>
Apply the shoelace formula with the three vertices listed in order:

$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}\left|x_P(y_Q - y_R) + x_Q(y_R - y_P) + x_R(y_P - y_Q)\right|$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}\left|0(0 - 5) + 6(5 - 0) + 2(0 - 0)\right|$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}\left|0 + 30 + 0\right| = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 30 = 15.$$

*Why this works:* Since $\overline{PQ}$ lies on the $x$-axis, this is also just $\tfrac{1}{2} \cdot \text{base} \cdot \text{height} = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot 5 = 15$, confirming the shoelace answer.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
