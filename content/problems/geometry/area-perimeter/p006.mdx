<Problem>
A polygon on a coordinate grid has vertices $(0,0)$, $(6,0)$, $(6,2)$, $(4,4)$, $(2,4)$, and $(0,2)$, in order. It has $16$ lattice points on its boundary and $13$ lattice points strictly inside it. Using Pick's theorem, find the area of the polygon.
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Pick's theorem** states that for a simple polygon with vertices at lattice points,

$$A = I + \frac{B}{2} - 1,$$

where $I$ is the number of interior lattice points and $B$ is the number of boundary lattice points.

Here $I = 13$ and $B = 16$, so

$$A = 13 + \frac{16}{2} - 1 = 13 + 8 - 1 = 20.$$

**Verification by the Shoelace formula:**

$$A = \frac{1}{2}|x_1(y_2-y_6)+x_2(y_3-y_1)+x_3(y_4-y_2)+x_4(y_5-y_3)+x_5(y_6-y_4)+x_6(y_1-y_5)|$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}|0(0-2)+6(2-0)+6(4-0)+4(4-2)+2(2-4)+0(0-4)|$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}|0+12+24+8-4+0| = \frac{1}{2}(40) = 20. \checkmark$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
