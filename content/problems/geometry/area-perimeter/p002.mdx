<Problem>
An L-shaped figure is formed by taking a $10$ by $8$ rectangle and removing a $4$ by $6$ rectangle from the top-right corner. What is the perimeter of the L-shaped figure?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Place the figure with its bottom-left corner at the origin. After removing the $4 \times 6$ top-right corner, the six vertices of the L-shape are (going clockwise from the bottom-left):

$$(0,0),\; (10,0),\; (10,2),\; (6,2),\; (6,8),\; (0,8).$$

(The cut removed a $4$-wide, $6$-tall piece from the corner, leaving a $2$-tall right portion and the remaining $6$-wide left portion at full height.)

Tracing the boundary clockwise and adding each segment:

| Edge | Length |
|---|---|
| Bottom: $(0,0)$ to $(10,0)$ | $10$ |
| Right short side: $(10,0)$ to $(10,2)$ | $2$ |
| Inner horizontal: $(10,2)$ to $(6,2)$ | $4$ |
| Inner vertical: $(6,2)$ to $(6,8)$ | $6$ |
| Top: $(6,8)$ to $(0,8)$ | $6$ |
| Left side: $(0,8)$ to $(0,0)$ | $8$ |

Perimeter $= 10 + 2 + 4 + 6 + 6 + 8 = 36$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
