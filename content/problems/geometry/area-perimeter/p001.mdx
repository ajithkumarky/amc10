<Problem>
A rectangle measures $9$ units by $5$ units. A $2$ by $3$ rectangular notch is cut from one corner. What is the area of the remaining figure?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The original rectangle has area $9 \times 5 = 45$.

The removed notch has area $2 \times 3 = 6$.

The remaining area is $45 - 6 = 39$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
