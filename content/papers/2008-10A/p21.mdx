<Problem>
A cube with side length $1$ is sliced by a plane that passes through two diagonally opposite vertices $A$ and $C$ and the midpoints $B$ and $D$ of two opposite edges not containing $A$ or $C$. What is the area of quadrilateral $ABCD$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Place the cube with $A = (0,0,0)$ and $C = (1,1,1)$. Choose $B$ and $D$ as the midpoints of opposite edges that don't contain $A$ or $C$: $B = (1, 0, \tfrac{1}{2})$ and $D = (0, 1, \tfrac{1}{2})$.

**Verify $ABCD$ is a parallelogram:** $\vec{AB} = (1, 0, \tfrac{1}{2})$ and $\vec{DC} = C - D = (1, 0, \tfrac{1}{2}) = \vec{AB}$, so $AB \parallel DC$ with equal length.

**Area via cross product:**
$$\vec{AB} \times \vec{AD} = (1, 0, \tfrac{1}{2}) \times (0, 1, \tfrac{1}{2}) = \left(-\tfrac{1}{2},\,-\tfrac{1}{2},\,1\right).$$

$$\text{Area} = \left|\vec{AB} \times \vec{AD}\right| = \sqrt{\tfrac{1}{4} + \tfrac{1}{4} + 1} = \sqrt{\tfrac{3}{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
