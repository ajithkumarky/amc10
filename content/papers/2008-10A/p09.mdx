<Problem>
Suppose that
$$\frac{2x}{3}-\frac{x}{6}$$
is an integer. Which of the following statements must be true about $x$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Combine the fractions:
$$\frac{2x}{3} - \frac{x}{6} = \frac{4x}{6} - \frac{x}{6} = \frac{3x}{6} = \frac{x}{2}.$$

For $\frac{x}{2}$ to be an integer, $x$ must be even. However, $x$ need not be a multiple of any larger number (e.g., $x = 2$ works and is not a multiple of 3 or 6).

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
