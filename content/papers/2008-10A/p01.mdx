<Problem>
A bakery owner turns on his doughnut machine at $\text{8:30 AM}$. At $\text{11:10 AM}$ the machine has completed one third of the day's job. At what time will the doughnut machine complete the job?
</Problem>

<Solution>
From 8:30 AM to 11:10 AM is 2 hours 40 minutes, or $\frac{8}{3}$ hours. This is one third of the total time, so the full job takes $3 \times \frac{8}{3} = 8$ hours.

Starting at 8:30 AM and adding 8 hours gives $\text{4:30 PM}$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
