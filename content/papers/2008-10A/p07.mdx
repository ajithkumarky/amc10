<Problem>
The fraction
$$\frac{\left(3^{2008}\right)^2-\left(3^{2006}\right)^2}{\left(3^{2007}\right)^2-\left(3^{2005}\right)^2}$$
simplifies to which of the following?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Apply the difference of squares $a^2 - b^2 = (a-b)(a+b)$ to both numerator and denominator.

**Numerator:** $3^{4016} - 3^{4012} = 3^{4012}(3^4 - 1) = 3^{4012} \cdot 80$.

**Denominator:** $3^{4014} - 3^{4010} = 3^{4010}(3^4 - 1) = 3^{4010} \cdot 80$.

The ratio is:
$$\frac{3^{4012} \cdot 80}{3^{4010} \cdot 80} = 3^2 = 9.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
