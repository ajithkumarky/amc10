<Problem>
An equilateral triangle has side length 6. What is the area of the region containing all points that are outside the triangle but not more than 3 units from a point on the triangle?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The region within 3 units of the triangle's boundary (but outside) consists of:

**Three rectangles** along the sides: each is $6 \times 3$, giving total area $3 \times 18 = 54$.

**Three circular sectors** at the corners: the exterior angle at each vertex of an equilateral triangle is $180^\circ - 60^\circ = 120^\circ$. Three sectors of radius 3 and angle $120^\circ$ together form a full circle of radius 3 (since $3 \times 120^\circ = 360^\circ$), with area $\pi \cdot 3^2 = 9\pi$.

Total area $= 54 + 9\pi$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
