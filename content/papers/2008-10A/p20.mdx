<Problem>
Trapezoid $ABCD$ has bases $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{CD}$ and diagonals intersecting at $K$. Suppose that $AB = 9$, $DC = 12$, and the area of $\triangle AKD$ is $24$. What is the area of trapezoid $ABCD$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Since $AB \parallel CD$, triangles $AKB$ and $DKC$ are similar with ratio $AB:DC = 9:12 = 3:4$.

This means $KB:KD = 3:4$.

**Find all four triangle areas.**

$\triangle AKB$ and $\triangle AKD$ share vertex $A$, so their areas are in ratio $KB:KD = 3:4$:
$$[\triangle AKB] = [\triangle AKD] \cdot \frac{3}{4} = 24 \cdot \frac{3}{4} = 18.$$

$\triangle DKC$ is similar to $\triangle AKB$ with ratio $4:3$, so their areas are in ratio $16:9$:
$$[\triangle DKC] = 18 \cdot \frac{16}{9} = 32.$$

$\triangle BKC$ and $\triangle DKC$ share vertex $C$, with $KB:KD = 3:4$:
$$[\triangle BKC] = 32 \cdot \frac{3}{4} = 24.$$

**Total area:**
$$18 + 24 + 32 + 24 = 98.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
