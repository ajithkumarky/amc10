<Problem>
Which of the following is equal to the product
$$\frac{8}{4}\cdot\frac{12}{8}\cdot\frac{16}{12}\cdots\frac{4n+4}{4n}\cdots\frac{2008}{2004}?$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
This is a telescoping product. Nearly every factor in the numerator cancels with the denominator of a later fraction. Writing out the pattern:
$$\frac{8}{4}\cdot\frac{12}{8}\cdot\frac{16}{12}\cdots\frac{2008}{2004} = \frac{2008}{4} = 502.$$

The product telescopes to the last numerator over the first denominator.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
