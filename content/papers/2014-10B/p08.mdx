<Problem>
A truck travels $\dfrac{b}{6}$ feet every $t$ seconds. There are $3$ feet in a yard. How many yards does the truck travel in $3$ minutes?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First find the speed in yards per second. The truck travels $\dfrac{b}{6}$ feet per $t$ seconds, which is $\dfrac{b}{6t}$ feet per second, or $\dfrac{b}{18t}$ yards per second.

Three minutes equals $3 \times 60 = 180$ seconds. Distance in yards:
$$\frac{b}{18t} \times 180 = \frac{180b}{18t} = \frac{10b}{t}.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
