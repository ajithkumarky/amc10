<Problem>
The largest divisor of $2{,}014{,}000{,}000$ is itself. What is its fifth largest divisor?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Factorize: $2{,}014{,}000{,}000 = 2014 \times 10^6 = (2 \times 19 \times 53) \times 2^6 \times 5^6 = 2^7 \times 5^6 \times 19 \times 53$.

The largest divisors of $N = 2{,}014{,}000{,}000$ are $N/d$ for the smallest valid divisors $d$. Listing in order of increasing $d$:

| $d$ | $N/d$ |
|---|---|
| $1$ | $2{,}014{,}000{,}000$ (1st) |
| $2$ | $1{,}007{,}000{,}000$ (2nd) |
| $4$ | $503{,}500{,}000$ (3rd) |
| $5$ | $402{,}800{,}000$ (4th) |
| $8$ | $251{,}750{,}000$ (5th) |

The fifth largest divisor is $\dfrac{2{,}014{,}000{,}000}{8} = 251{,}750{,}000$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
