<Problem>
What is $\dfrac{2^3 + 2^3}{2^{-3} + 2^{-3}}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Compute numerator and denominator separately.

Numerator: $2^3 + 2^3 = 8 + 8 = 16$.

Denominator: $2^{-3} + 2^{-3} = \dfrac{1}{8} + \dfrac{1}{8} = \dfrac{1}{4}$.

So the fraction equals:
$$\frac{16}{\frac{1}{4}} = 16 \times 4 = 64.$$

Alternatively, factor: $\dfrac{2 \cdot 2^3}{2 \cdot 2^{-3}} = \dfrac{2^3}{2^{-3}} = 2^{3-(-3)} = 2^6 = 64$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
