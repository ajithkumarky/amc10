<Problem>
Leah has $13$ coins, all of which are pennies and nickels. If she had one more nickel than she has now, then she would have the same number of pennies as nickels. In cents, how much are Leah's coins worth?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $n$ be Leah's current number of nickels. Then the number of pennies is $13 - n$.

If she had one more nickel, she would have $n + 1$ nickels and still $13 - n$ pennies. The condition says these are equal:
$$n + 1 = 13 - n \implies 2n = 12 \implies n = 6.$$

So Leah has $6$ nickels and $7$ pennies. Her total is:
$$6 \times 5 + 7 \times 1 = 30 + 7 = 37 \text{ cents.}$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
