<Problem>
Camden constructs a square window using $8$ equal-size panes of glass, as shown. The ratio of the height to width for each pane is $5 : 2$, and the borders around and between the panes are $2$ inches wide. In inches, what is the side length of the square window?

The $8$ panes are arranged in a $4 \times 2$ grid (4 columns, 2 rows). Each pane has height-to-width ratio $5:2$.

<Solution>
Let the width of each pane be $w$ inches. Since the height-to-width ratio is $5:2$, the height of each pane is $\dfrac{5w}{2}$.

The window has 4 panes across: 5 borders (left edge, 3 between panes, right edge) of 2 inches each, plus 4 pane widths:
$$\text{Side} = 5 \cdot 2 + 4w = 10 + 4w.$$

The window has 2 panes tall: 3 borders of 2 inches each, plus 2 pane heights:
$$\text{Side} = 3 \cdot 2 + 2 \cdot \frac{5w}{2} = 6 + 5w.$$

Since the window is square, set these equal:
$$10 + 4w = 6 + 5w \implies w = 4.$$

Side length $= 10 + 4(4) = 10 + 16 = 26$ inches.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
