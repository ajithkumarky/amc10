<Problem>
Suppose $A > B > 0$ and $A$ is $x\%$ greater than $B$. What is $x$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
"$A$ is $x\%$ greater than $B$" means $A = B + \dfrac{x}{100} \cdot B = B\!\left(1 + \dfrac{x}{100}\right)$.

Solving for $x$:
$$\frac{A}{B} = 1 + \frac{x}{100} \implies \frac{x}{100} = \frac{A}{B} - 1 = \frac{A - B}{B} \implies x = 100\!\left(\frac{A-B}{B}\right).$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
