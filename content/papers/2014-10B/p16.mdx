<Problem>
Four fair six-sided dice are rolled. What is the probability that at least three of the four dice show the same value?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We count two cases: exactly three dice match, or all four dice match.

**Case 1: All four dice show the same value.**

Choose the value: 6 ways. All four dice show it: $\frac{6}{6^4} = \frac{6}{1296}$.

**Case 2: Exactly three dice show the same value (and the fourth is different).**

Choose which value appears three times: 6 ways.
Choose which 3 of the 4 dice show that value: $\binom{4}{3} = 4$ ways.
The remaining die shows a different value: $5$ choices.

Count: $6 \times 4 \times 5 = 120$.

Total favorable outcomes: $6 + 120 = 126$.

Total outcomes: $6^4 = 1296$.

Probability: $\dfrac{126}{1296} = \dfrac{7}{72}$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
