<Problem>
Real numbers $a$, $b$, and $c$ have arithmetic mean $0$. The arithmetic mean of $a^2$, $b^2$, and $c^2$ is $10$. What is the arithmetic mean of $ab$, $ac$, and $bc$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
From the given information:

$$\frac{a+b+c}{3} = 0 \implies a+b+c = 0,$$

$$\frac{a^2+b^2+c^2}{3} = 10 \implies a^2+b^2+c^2 = 30.$$

Expand $(a+b+c)^2$:

$$(a+b+c)^2 = a^2+b^2+c^2 + 2(ab+ac+bc).$$

$$0 = 30 + 2(ab+ac+bc).$$

$$ab+ac+bc = -15.$$

The arithmetic mean of $ab$, $ac$, and $bc$ is:

$$\frac{ab+ac+bc}{3} = \frac{-15}{3} = -5.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
