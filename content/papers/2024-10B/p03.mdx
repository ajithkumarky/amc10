<Problem>
For how many integer values of $x$ is

$$|2x| \le 7\pi?$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
Since $7\pi \approx 21.99$, the inequality $|2x| \le 7\pi$ means $|x| \le \dfrac{7\pi}{2} \approx 10.996$.

The integers satisfying $|x| \le 10.996$ are $x = -10, -9, -8, \ldots, 0, \ldots, 8, 9, 10$, which is $10 + 1 + 10 = 21$ integers.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
