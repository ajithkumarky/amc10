<Problem>
In a long line of people, the 1013th person from the left is also the 1010th person from the right. How many people are in the line?
</Problem>

<Solution>
If someone is the 1013th from the left, there are 1012 people to their left. If that same person is the 1010th from the right, there are 1009 people to their right. The total number of people is:

$$1012 + 1 + 1009 = 2022.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
