<Problem>
A rectangle has integer side lengths and an area of $2024$. What is the least possible perimeter of the rectangle?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We need to factor $2024$ as $\ell \times w$ with $\ell, w$ positive integers, and minimize the perimeter $2(\ell + w)$, which means maximizing $\min(\ell, w)$.

First, factor $2024$:

$$2024 = 2^3 \times 11 \times 23.$$

The factor pairs $(\ell, w)$ with $\ell \le w$ and $\ell \cdot w = 2024$ include: $(1, 2024)$, $(2, 1012)$, $(4, 506)$, $(8, 253)$, $(11, 184)$, $(22, 92)$, $(23, 88)$, $(44, 46)$.

The pair closest to $\sqrt{2024} \approx 44.99$ is $(44, 46)$.

Check: $44 \times 46 = 2024$. ✓

Perimeter $= 2(44+46) = 2 \times 90 = 180$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
