<Problem>
What is the remainder when $7^{2024} + 7^{2025} + 7^{2026}$ is divided by $19$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Factor out the smallest power:

$$7^{2024} + 7^{2025} + 7^{2026} = 7^{2024}(1 + 7 + 49) = 7^{2024} \cdot 57.$$

Notice that $57 = 3 \times 19$, so $57$ is divisible by $19$. Therefore the entire expression is divisible by $19$, and the remainder is $0$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
