<Problem>
In the figure below $WXYZ$ is a rectangle with $WX = 4$ and $WZ = 8$. Point $M$ lies on $\overline{XY}$, point $A$ lies on $\overline{YZ}$, and $\angle WMA$ is a right angle. The areas of $\triangle WXM$ and $\triangle WAZ$ are equal. What is the area of $\triangle WMA$?

The rectangle has $W$ at top-left, $Z$ at top-right, $X$ at bottom-left, $Y$ at bottom-right; $M$ is on the bottom side $\overline{XY}$ and $A$ is on the right side $\overline{YZ}$.
</Problem>

<Solution>
Set up coordinates: $W=(0,4)$, $X=(0,0)$, $Y=(8,0)$, $Z=(8,4)$.

Let $M=(m,0)$ on $\overline{XY}$ and $A=(8,a)$ on $\overline{YZ}$.

**Equal areas condition:**

$$[\triangle WXM] = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot WX \cdot XM = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot 4 \cdot m = 2m.$$

$$[\triangle WAZ] = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot WZ \cdot AZ = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot 8\cdot(4-a) = 4(4-a).$$

Setting equal: $2m = 4(4-a)$, so $m = 8-2a$.

**Right angle condition** ($\angle WMA = 90°$):

$$\overrightarrow{MW} = (0-m,\; 4-0) = (-m,4), \qquad \overrightarrow{MA} = (8-m,\; a).$$

Dot product equals zero:

$$-m(8-m) + 4a = 0 \implies m^2 - 8m + 4a = 0.$$

Substitute $m = 8-2a$:

$$(8-2a)^2 - 8(8-2a) + 4a = 0$$
$$64-32a+4a^2-64+16a+4a = 0$$
$$4a^2 - 12a = 0 \implies 4a(a-3) = 0.$$

So $a=0$ (degenerate, $A=Y$) or $a=3$. Take $a=3$, giving $m = 8-6=2$.

Thus $M=(2,0)$, $A=(8,3)$, $W=(0,4)$.

**Area of $\triangle WMA$** by the shoelace formula:

$$[\triangle WMA] = \tfrac{1}{2}|x_W(y_M-y_A)+x_M(y_A-y_W)+x_A(y_W-y_M)|$$
$$= \tfrac{1}{2}|0(0-3)+2(3-4)+8(4-0)|$$
$$= \tfrac{1}{2}|0-2+32| = \tfrac{30}{2} = 15.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
