<Problem>
What is $10! - 7! \cdot 6!$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Compute each factorial:

$$10! = 3{,}628{,}800, \quad 7! = 5040, \quad 6! = 720.$$

$$7! \cdot 6! = 5040 \times 720 = 3{,}628{,}800.$$

Therefore:

$$10! - 7! \cdot 6! = 3{,}628{,}800 - 3{,}628{,}800 = 0.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
