<Problem>
The harmonic mean of a collection of numbers is the reciprocal of the arithmetic mean of the reciprocals of the numbers in the collection. For example, the harmonic mean of $4, 4, 5$ is
$$\frac{1}{\dfrac{1}{3}\!\left(\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{5}\right)} = \frac{30}{7}.$$

What is the harmonic mean of all the real roots of the $4050^{\text{th}}$ degree polynomial
$$\prod_{k=1}^{2025}(kx^2 - 4x - 3) = (x^2-4x-3)(2x^2-4x-3)(3x^2-4x-3)\cdots(2025x^2-4x-3)\,?$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
For each factor $kx^2 - 4x - 3$, let its real roots be $r_k$ and $s_k$ (if they exist). By Vieta's formulas:

$$r_k + s_k = \frac{4}{k}, \qquad r_k s_k = \frac{-3}{k}.$$

The sum of reciprocals of the two roots is:
$$\frac{1}{r_k} + \frac{1}{s_k} = \frac{r_k + s_k}{r_k s_k} = \frac{4/k}{-3/k} = -\frac{4}{3}.$$

This is the same for every $k$! Now we need to check which quadratics have real (non-zero) roots. The discriminant of $kx^2 - 4x - 3$ is $16 + 12k > 0$ for all $k \geq 1$, so every factor has two distinct real roots. Neither root is $0$ (since the constant term $-3 \neq 0$).

So all $2025$ quadratics contribute real roots, giving $2025 \times 2 = 4050$ real roots total.

The harmonic mean $H$ of a collection $\{x_i\}$ of $n$ numbers is:
$$H = \frac{n}{\sum_i \frac{1}{x_i}}.$$

Total number of real roots: $n = 4050$.

Sum of reciprocals of all roots:
$$\sum_{k=1}^{2025}\left(\frac{1}{r_k}+\frac{1}{s_k}\right) = \sum_{k=1}^{2025}\left(-\frac{4}{3}\right) = 2025 \times \left(-\frac{4}{3}\right) = -2700.$$

Harmonic mean:
$$H = \frac{4050}{-2700} = -\frac{4050}{2700} = -\frac{3}{2}.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
