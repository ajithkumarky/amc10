<Problem>
Let $N$ be the unique positive integer such that dividing $273436$ by $N$ leaves a remainder of $16$ and dividing $272760$ by $N$ leaves a remainder of $15$. What is the tens digit of $N$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The conditions give $N \mid 273436 - 16 = 273420$ and $N \mid 272760 - 15 = 272745$, so $N$ divides $\gcd(273420, 272745)$.

$$\gcd(273420, 272745) = \gcd(273420 - 272745,\, 272745) = \gcd(675,\, 272745).$$

Since $675 = 3^3 \times 5^2$ and $272745 = 675 \times 404 - 45$... more directly: $272745 \div 675$ leaves remainder $45$ (since $675 \times 404 = 272700$ and $272745 - 272700 = 45$), so $\gcd(675, 272745) = \gcd(675, 45) = 45$ (because $675 = 45 \times 15$).

The divisors of $45$ are $1, 3, 5, 9, 15, 45$. Since the remainder $16$ requires $N > 16$, the only possibility is $N = 45$.

**Verification:** $273436 = 45 \times 6076 + 16$ and $272760 = 45 \times 6061 + 15$. $\checkmark$

The tens digit of $45$ is $\mathbf{4}$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
