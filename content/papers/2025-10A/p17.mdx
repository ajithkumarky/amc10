<Problem>
Let $N$ be the unique positive integer such that dividing $273436$ by $N$ leaves a remainder of $16$ and dividing $272760$ by $N$ leaves a remainder of $15$. What is the tens digit of $N$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The conditions say:
$$273436 \equiv 16 \pmod{N} \implies N \mid 273436 - 16 = 273420,$$
$$272760 \equiv 15 \pmod{N} \implies N \mid 272760 - 15 = 272745.$$

So $N$ is a common divisor of $273420$ and $272745$. We want their GCD.

$$\gcd(273420,\, 272745) = \gcd(273420 - 272745,\, 272745) = \gcd(675,\, 272745).$$

Factor $675$: $675 = 27 \times 25 = 3^3 \times 5^2$.

Divide: $272745 \div 675 = 403.99...$, so check $675 \times 404 = 272700$; $272745 - 272700 = 45$.

$\gcd(675, 272745) = \gcd(675, 45) = 45$.

Wait, let me redo: $272745 = 675 \times 403 + r$. $675 \times 403 = 675 \times 400 + 675\times 3 = 270000 + 2025 = 272025$. $272745 - 272025 = 720$. Hmm, $720 > 675$.

$675 \times 404 = 272025 + 675 = 272700$. $272745 - 272700 = 45$.

$\gcd(675, 272745) = \gcd(675, 45)$.

$675 = 45 \times 15$, so $\gcd(675, 45) = 45$.

So all common divisors of $273420$ and $272745$ are divisors of $45 = 3^2 \times 5$.

Divisors of $45$: $1, 3, 5, 9, 15, 45$.

The remainder when dividing by $N$ must be less than $N$ (i.e., $N > 16$ since the remainder is 16, and $N > 15$ since the remainder is 15). So $N > 16$, leaving only $N = 45$.

**Verification:** $273420 \div 45 = 6076$, so $273436 = 45 \times 6076 + 16$ ✓. $272745 \div 45 = 6061 - $ wait: $45 \times 6061 = 272745$ exactly, so $272760 = 272745 + 15 = 45 \times 6061 + 15$ ✓.

$N = 45$. The tens digit of $45$ is $\mathbf{4}$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
