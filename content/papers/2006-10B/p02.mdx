<Problem>
For real numbers $x$ and $y$, define $x \mathop{\spadesuit} y = (x+y)(x-y)$. What is $3 \mathop{\spadesuit} (4 \mathop{\spadesuit} 5)$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First compute the inner operation: $4 \mathop{\spadesuit} 5 = (4+5)(4-5) = 9 \cdot (-1) = -9$.

Then compute $3 \mathop{\spadesuit} (-9) = (3 + (-9))(3 - (-9)) = (-6)(12) = -72$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
