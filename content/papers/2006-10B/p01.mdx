<Problem>
What is $(-1)^{1} + (-1)^{2} + \cdots + (-1)^{2006}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Since $2006$ is even, the exponents run from $1$ to $2006$. The terms alternate between $-1$ (odd exponents) and $+1$ (even exponents). There are $1003$ odd exponents and $1003$ even exponents, so the sum is $1003 \cdot (-1) + 1003 \cdot 1 = 0$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
