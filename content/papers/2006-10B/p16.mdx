<Problem>
Leap Day, February 29, 2004, occurred on a Sunday. On what day of the week will Leap Day, February 29, 2020, occur?
</Problem>

<Solution>
From 2004 to 2020 is 16 years. We count the number of days in this span to find the day-of-week shift.

In these 16 years, there are 4 leap years: 2004, 2008, 2012, 2016. (2020 itself is the target date, so we count leap days between the two dates: in 2008, 2012, 2016 — that's 3 extra days beyond the "normal" Feb 28 leap days... let me count more carefully.)

From Feb 29, 2004 to Feb 29, 2020: this is exactly 16 years.

Years 2004–2019 (16 years): count ordinary years and leap years.
- Leap years in this range: 2004, 2008, 2012, 2016 — four leap years, each 366 days.
- Ordinary years: 12 years, each 365 days.

Total days $= 4 \times 366 + 12 \times 365 = 1464 + 4380 = 5844$.

Day shift $= 5844 \mod 7$. We compute: $5844 = 7 \times 834 + 6$. So the shift is 6 days.

Sunday $+ 6$ days $=$ Saturday.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
