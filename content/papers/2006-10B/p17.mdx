<Problem>
Bob and Alice each have a bag that contains one ball of each of the colors blue, green, orange, red, and violet. Alice randomly selects one ball from her bag and puts it into Bob's bag. Bob then randomly selects one ball from his bag and puts it into Alice's bag. What is the probability that after this process the contents of the two bags are the same?
</Problem>

<Solution>
After Alice puts a ball of some color $X$ into Bob's bag, Bob has 6 balls: his original 5 (one of each color) plus Alice's ball of color $X$. So Bob now has 2 balls of color $X$ and 1 ball of each of the other 4 colors.

For the two bags to end up with the same contents (each having one of every color), Bob must give Alice a ball of color $X$. Bob's bag contains 2 balls of color $X$ out of 6 total, so the probability he selects a color-$X$ ball is $\dfrac{2}{6} = \dfrac{1}{3}$.

This holds regardless of which color Alice picked initially, so the answer is $\dfrac{1}{3}$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
