<Problem>
What is the tens digit in the sum $7! + 8! + 9! + \cdots + 2006!$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
For $n \ge 10$, $n!$ is divisible by $100$ (since $n!$ contains factors of both $4$ and $25$, giving at least two factors of 10). So all terms from $10!$ onward contribute $00$ to the last two digits.

We only need the tens digit of $7! + 8! + 9!$.

- $7! = 5040$
- $8! = 8 \times 5040 = 40320$
- $9! = 9 \times 40320 = 362880$

Sum of last two digits: $40 + 20 + 80 = 140$. The tens digit of this sum (ignoring hundreds) is $4$.

More carefully: $7! + 8! + 9! = 5040 + 40320 + 362880 = 408240$. The tens digit is $4$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
