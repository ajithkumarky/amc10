<Problem>
A region is bounded by semicircular arcs constructed on the sides of a square whose sides measure $\dfrac{2}{\pi}$. What is the perimeter of this region?

Each side of the square serves as the diameter of a semicircle; the four semicircles bow outward, forming a four-petaled region. The perimeter consists of the four semicircular arcs on the outside of the square.
</Problem>

<Solution>
Each side of the square has length $\dfrac{2}{\pi}$, which serves as the diameter of one semicircle. The radius of each semicircle is $\dfrac{1}{\pi}$.

The circumference of a full circle with radius $\dfrac{1}{\pi}$ is $2\pi \cdot \dfrac{1}{\pi} = 2$. Each semicircle contributes half of this: $1$.

There are $4$ semicircles on the $4$ sides, so the total perimeter is $4 \times 1 = 4$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
