<Problem>
Let $X$ and $Y$ be the following sums of arithmetic sequences:
$$X = 10 + 12 + 14 + \cdots + 100,$$
$$Y = 12 + 14 + 16 + \cdots + 102.$$
What is the value of $Y - X$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Notice that $Y$ contains all the terms of $X$ except $10$, plus the extra term $102$.

Specifically, $Y - X = 102 - 10 = 92$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
