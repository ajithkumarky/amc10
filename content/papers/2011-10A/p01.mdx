<Problem>
A cell phone plan costs $\$20$ each month, plus $5$ cents per text message sent, plus $10$ cents for each minute used over $30$ hours. In January Michelle sent $100$ text messages and talked for $30.5$ hours. How much did she pay?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The base cost is $\textdollar 20$.

Text messages: $100 \times \$0.05 = \$5.00$.

Overage minutes: $30.5$ hours $= 1830$ minutes, and $30$ hours $= 1800$ minutes, so she used $30$ minutes over the limit. That costs $30 \times \$0.10 = \$3.00$.

Total: $\$20 + \$5 + \$3 = \$28.00$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
