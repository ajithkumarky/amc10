<Problem>
Square $EFGH$ has one vertex on each side of square $ABCD$. Point $E$ is on $\overline{AB}$ with $AE = 7 \cdot EB$. What is the ratio of the area of $EFGH$ to the area of $ABCD$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the side length of $ABCD$ be $8$ (chosen so $AE$ and $EB$ are integers). Since $AE = 7 \cdot EB$ and $AE + EB = 8$, we get $EB = 1$ and $AE = 7$.

By the symmetry of how $EFGH$ sits inside $ABCD$, each vertex of $EFGH$ cuts its side of $ABCD$ in the ratio $7:1$. So the small right triangle cut off at each corner has legs $7$ and $1$.

The side length of $EFGH$ equals the hypotenuse of such a triangle:
$$EF = \sqrt{7^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{49 + 1} = \sqrt{50} = 5\sqrt{2}.$$

The area of $ABCD$ is $8^2 = 64$, and the area of $EFGH$ is $(5\sqrt{2})^2 = 50$.

$$\frac{\text{Area of }EFGH}{\text{Area of }ABCD} = \frac{50}{64} = \frac{25}{32}.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
