<Problem>
Each vertex of convex pentagon $ABCDE$ is to be assigned a color. There are $6$ colors to choose from, and the ends of each diagonal must have different colors. How many different colorings are possible?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Label the five vertices $A,B,C,D,E$. The constraint applies only to diagonal endpoints (not to adjacent vertices connected by sides). The five diagonals of the pentagon are $AC, CE, EB, BD, DA$, which form a 5-cycle $A$–$C$–$E$–$B$–$D$–$A$. The number of proper colorings of a cycle $C_5$ with $k$ colors is given by the chromatic polynomial:

$$P(C_5,k) = (k-1)^5 + (-1)^5(k-1) = (k-1)\bigl[(k-1)^4 - 1\bigr].$$

With $k=6$:

$$P(C_5,6) = 5\cdot(5^4-1) = 5\cdot 624 = 3120.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
