<Problem>
Seven students count from $1$ to $1000$ as follows:

- Alice says all the numbers, except she skips the middle number in each consecutive group of three numbers. That is, Alice says $1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, \ldots, 997, 999, 1000$.
- Barbara says all of the numbers that Alice does not say, except she also skips the middle number in each consecutive group of three numbers.
- Candice says all of the numbers that neither Alice nor Barbara says, except she also skips the middle number in each consecutive group of three numbers.
- Debbie, Eliza, and Fatima say all of the numbers that none of the students with the first names beginning before theirs in the alphabet say, except each also skips the middle number in each of her consecutive groups of three numbers.
- Finally, George says the only number that no one else says.

What number does George say?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Each student receives a list and discards its middle element in every group of three, passing those discarded elements to the next student. Tracking which number survives all six rounds of discarding to reach George:

- Alice skips the 2nd of every group of three from $\{1,2,3,\ldots\}$: she skips $2, 5, 8, 11, \ldots$ (numbers $\equiv 2\pmod{3}$). Barbara receives this list.
- Barbara skips the 2nd of every group of three from $\{2,5,8,11,14,\ldots\}$: she skips $5, 14, 23, \ldots$ (numbers $\equiv 5\pmod{9}$). Candice receives this list.
- Candice skips the 2nd from $\{5,14,23,32,\ldots\}$: she skips $14, 41, 68, \ldots$ (numbers $\equiv 14\pmod{27}$). Debbie receives this list.
- Debbie skips the 2nd from $\{14,41,68,\ldots\}$: she skips $41, 122, 203, \ldots$ (numbers $\equiv 41\pmod{81}$). Eliza receives this list.
- Eliza skips the 2nd from $\{41,122,203,\ldots\}$: she skips $122, 365, 608, \ldots$ (numbers $\equiv 122\pmod{243}$). Fatima receives this list.
- Fatima receives $\{122, 365, 608\}$ (the only three members $\le 1000$) and skips the middle one: $365$. George receives only $365$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
