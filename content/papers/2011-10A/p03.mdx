<Problem>
Suppose $[a\ b]$ denotes the average of $a$ and $b$, and $\{a\ b\ c\}$ denotes the average of $a$, $b$, and $c$. What is $\{\{1\ 1\ 0\}\ [0\ 1]\ 0\}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Work from the inside out.

$\{1\ 1\ 0\} = \dfrac{1+1+0}{3} = \dfrac{2}{3}$.

$[0\ 1] = \dfrac{0+1}{2} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

Now compute the outer average:
$$\left\{\frac{2}{3}\ \frac{1}{2}\ 0\right\} = \frac{\frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{2} + 0}{3} = \frac{\frac{4}{6} + \frac{3}{6}}{3} = \frac{\frac{7}{6}}{3} = \frac{7}{18}.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
