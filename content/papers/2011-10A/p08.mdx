<Problem>
Last summer $30\%$ of the birds living on Town Lake were geese, $25\%$ were swans, $10\%$ were herons, and $35\%$ were ducks. What percent of the birds that were not swans were geese?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The birds that were not swans make up $100\% - 25\% = 75\%$ of all birds.

Of all birds, $30\%$ were geese. As a fraction of non-swans:
$$\frac{30\%}{75\%} = \frac{30}{75} = \frac{2}{5} = 40\%.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
