<Problem>
Each row of the Misty Moon Amphitheater has $33$ seats. Rows $12$ through $22$ are reserved for a youth club. How many seats are reserved for this club?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Rows 12 through 22 inclusive: the number of rows is $22 - 12 + 1 = 11$.

Each row has 33 seats, so the total is $11 \times 33 = 363$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
