<Problem>
Minneapolis-St. Paul International Airport is $8$ miles southwest of downtown St. Paul and $10$ miles southeast of downtown Minneapolis. Which of the following is closest to the number of miles between downtown St. Paul and downtown Minneapolis?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $A$ = Airport, $P$ = St. Paul, $M$ = Minneapolis.

The airport is 8 miles southwest of St. Paul, so $AP = 8$ with direction southwest from $P$ (i.e., the bearing from $A$ to $P$ is northeast).

The airport is 10 miles southeast of Minneapolis, so $AM = 10$ with direction southeast from $M$ (i.e., the bearing from $A$ to $M$ is northwest).

"Southwest" and "southeast" are perpendicular directions (they differ by 90°). So the angle at $A$ between the directions to $P$ and to $M$ is $90°$.

By the Pythagorean theorem:
$$MP = \sqrt{AP^2 + AM^2} = \sqrt{8^2 + 10^2} = \sqrt{64 + 100} = \sqrt{164} \approx 12.8.$$

The closest answer choice is $13$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
