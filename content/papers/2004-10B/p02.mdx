<Problem>
How many two-digit positive integers have at least one $7$ as a digit?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Count the two-digit numbers (10–99) with at least one digit equal to 7.

**Numbers with 7 in the tens place:** 70, 71, 72, 73, 74, 75, 76, 77, 78, 79 — that is 10 numbers.

**Numbers with 7 in the units place:** 17, 27, 37, 47, 57, 67, 77, 87, 97 — that is 9 numbers.

The number 77 is counted in both groups. By inclusion-exclusion: $10 + 9 - 1 = 18$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
