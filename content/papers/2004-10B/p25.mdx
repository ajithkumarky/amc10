<Problem>
A circle of radius $1$ is internally tangent to two circles of radius $2$ at points $A$ and $B$, where $AB$ is a diameter of the smaller circle. What is the area of the region, shaded in the figure, that is outside the smaller circle and inside each of the two larger circles?

The small circle has center $O$ at the origin with radius $1$, so $A = (0,1)$ and $B = (0,-1)$. The two large circles of radius $2$ are centered at $A = (0,1)$ and $B = (0,-1)$ respectively.
</Problem>

<Solution>
Set up coordinates: small circle has center $O = (0,0)$, radius $1$. The two large circles have centers $A = (0,1)$ and $B = (0,-1)$, each with radius $2$.

**Intersection of the two large circles:** Find where $|P - A| = 2$ and $|P - B| = 2$.
$x^2 + (y-1)^2 = 4$ and $x^2 + (y+1)^2 = 4$.
Subtracting: $(y-1)^2 - (y+1)^2 = 0 \Rightarrow -4y = 0 \Rightarrow y = 0$.
Then $x^2 + 1 = 4 \Rightarrow x = \pm\sqrt{3}$.
Intersection points: $P_1 = (\sqrt{3}, 0)$ and $P_2 = (-\sqrt{3}, 0)$.

**Shaded region** = (intersection of two large circles) $-$ (small circle).

**Area of intersection of the two large circles:**
Each large circle contributes a circular segment. For the large circle centered at $A = (0,1)$, the chord is $P_1 P_2$ with $|P_1 P_2| = 2\sqrt{3}$. The distance from $A$ to this chord (which lies on $y=0$) is $1$. Half-chord $= \sqrt{3}$, radius $= 2$, so $\cos\theta = 1/2$, giving $\theta = \pi/3$ and the full angle subtended $= 2\pi/3$.

Area of one circular segment $= \frac{1}{2}r^2(\theta - \sin\theta) = \frac{1}{2}(4)\left(\frac{2\pi}{3} - \sin\frac{2\pi}{3}\right) = 2\left(\frac{2\pi}{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = \frac{4\pi}{3} - \sqrt{3}$.

The intersection of the two large circles consists of two such segments, so:
$$\text{Area(intersection of large circles)} = 2\left(\frac{4\pi}{3} - \sqrt{3}\right) = \frac{8\pi}{3} - 2\sqrt{3}.$$

**Area of small circle** $= \pi(1)^2 = \pi$.

**Shaded area** $= \frac{8\pi}{3} - 2\sqrt{3} - \pi = \frac{8\pi}{3} - \frac{3\pi}{3} - 2\sqrt{3} = \frac{5\pi}{3} - 2\sqrt{3}$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
