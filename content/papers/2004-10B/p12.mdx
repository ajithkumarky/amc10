<Problem>
An annulus is the region between two concentric circles. The concentric circles have radii $b$ and $c$, with $b > c$. Let $OX$ be a radius of the larger circle, let $XZ$ be tangent to the smaller circle at $Z$, and let $OY$ be the radius of the larger circle that contains $Z$. Let $a = XZ$, $d = YZ$, and $e = XY$. What is the area of the annulus?

In the figure: $O$ is the common center, $X$ is on the larger circle, $Z$ is on the smaller circle with $OZ \perp XZ$, and $Y$ is on the larger circle with $O$, $Z$, $Y$ collinear.
</Problem>

<Solution>
The area of the annulus is $\pi b^2 - \pi c^2$.

Since $XZ$ is tangent to the smaller circle at $Z$, we have $OZ \perp XZ$, so triangle $OXZ$ is a right triangle with:
- hypotenuse $OX = b$ (radius of larger circle),
- leg $OZ = c$ (radius of smaller circle),
- leg $XZ = a$.

By the Pythagorean theorem: $a^2 + c^2 = b^2$, so $b^2 - c^2 = a^2$.

Therefore the area of the annulus is:
$$\pi b^2 - \pi c^2 = \pi(b^2 - c^2) = \pi a^2.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
