<Problem>
At each basketball practice last week, Jenny made twice as many free throws as she made at the previous practice. At her fifth practice she made $48$ free throws. How many free throws did she make at the first practice?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $f$ be the number of free throws at the first practice. Since each practice she makes twice as many as the previous one, the counts are $f, 2f, 4f, 8f, 16f$.

At the fifth practice: $16f = 48$, so $f = 3$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
