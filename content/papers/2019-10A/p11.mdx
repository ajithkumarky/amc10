<Problem>
How many positive integer divisors of $201^9$ are perfect squares or perfect cubes (or both)?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First, factor $201^9$. Since $201 = 3 \times 67$:
$$201^9 = 3^9 \times 67^9.$$

A divisor of $3^9 \times 67^9$ has the form $3^a \times 67^b$ where $0 \leq a \leq 9$ and $0 \leq b \leq 9$.

We use inclusion-exclusion: count divisors that are perfect squares **plus** perfect cubes **minus** those that are both (perfect sixth powers).

**Perfect square divisors** ($a$ and $b$ both even): $a \in \{0,2,4,6,8\}$ — 5 choices; $b \in \{0,2,4,6,8\}$ — 5 choices. Total: $5 \times 5 = 25$.

**Perfect cube divisors** ($a$ and $b$ both multiples of 3): $a \in \{0,3,6,9\}$ — 4 choices; $b \in \{0,3,6,9\}$ — 4 choices. Total: $4 \times 4 = 16$.

**Perfect sixth power divisors** ($a$ and $b$ both multiples of 6): $a \in \{0,6\}$ — 2 choices; $b \in \{0,6\}$ — 2 choices. Total: $2 \times 2 = 4$.

By inclusion-exclusion:
$$25 + 16 - 4 = \boxed{37}.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
