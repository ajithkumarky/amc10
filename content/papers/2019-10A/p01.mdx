<Problem>
What is the value of $(2^0 - 1 + 5^2 - 0)^{-1} \times 5$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Evaluate the parenthesised expression first:

$$2^0 - 1 + 5^2 - 0 = 1 - 1 + 25 - 0 = 25.$$

Then $(25)^{-1} \times 5 = \dfrac{1}{25} \times 5 = \dfrac{1}{5}$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>

<AlternateMethod title="Simplifying before evaluating">
$(2^0 - 1 + 5^2 - 0)^{-1} \times 5 = \dfrac{5}{25} = \dfrac{1}{5}$ — once you notice $2^0 - 1 = 0$, only the $5^2$ remains in the parentheses.
</AlternateMethod>
