<Problem>
A child builds towers using identically shaped cubes of different colors. How many different towers with a height $8$ cubes can the child build with $2$ red cubes, $3$ blue cubes, and $4$ green cubes? (One cube will be left out.)
</Problem>

<Solution>
We have $2 + 3 + 4 = 9$ cubes total and must choose 8 to build a tower (one is left out), then arrange the chosen 8 in a vertical sequence.

The cube left out is one of the three colors:

**Case 1: Leave out a red cube.**
Remaining cubes: 1 red, 3 blue, 4 green — 8 cubes total.
Number of arrangements: $\dfrac{8!}{1! \cdot 3! \cdot 4!} = \dfrac{40320}{6 \cdot 24} = \dfrac{40320}{144} = 280$.

**Case 2: Leave out a blue cube.**
Remaining cubes: 2 red, 2 blue, 4 green — 8 cubes total.
Number of arrangements: $\dfrac{8!}{2! \cdot 2! \cdot 4!} = \dfrac{40320}{2 \cdot 2 \cdot 24} = \dfrac{40320}{96} = 420$.

**Case 3: Leave out a green cube.**
Remaining cubes: 2 red, 3 blue, 3 green — 8 cubes total.
Number of arrangements: $\dfrac{8!}{2! \cdot 3! \cdot 3!} = \dfrac{40320}{2 \cdot 6 \cdot 6} = \dfrac{40320}{72} = 560$.

**Total:**
$$280 + 420 + 560 = \boxed{1260}.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
