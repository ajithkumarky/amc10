<Problem>
Isaac has written down one integer two times and another integer three times. The sum of the five numbers is $100$, and one of the numbers is $28$. What is the other number?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the two integers be $28$ and $y$. There are two cases depending on how many times $28$ appears.

**Case 1:** $28$ appears twice and $y$ appears three times.
$$2(28) + 3y = 100 \implies 56 + 3y = 100 \implies 3y = 44.$$
This gives $y = 44/3$, which is not an integer. No solution here.

**Case 2:** $28$ appears three times and $y$ appears twice.
$$3(28) + 2y = 100 \implies 84 + 2y = 100 \implies 2y = 16 \implies y = 8.$$

The other number is $8$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
