<Problem>
Johann has $64$ fair coins. He flips all the coins. Any coin that lands on tails is tossed again. Coins that land on tails on the second toss are tossed a third time. What is the expected number of coins that are now heads?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Consider any single coin. It ends up as heads if:
- It lands heads on the **first** toss (probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$), OR
- It lands tails on the first toss, then heads on the **second** (probability $\tfrac{1}{2} \cdot \tfrac{1}{2} = \tfrac{1}{4}$), OR
- It lands tails on both the first and second tosses, then heads on the **third** (probability $\tfrac{1}{4} \cdot \tfrac{1}{2} = \tfrac{1}{8}$), OR
- It lands tails on all three tosses (probability $\tfrac{1}{8}$) — in this case it ends up tails.

Wait — re-reading: coins landing tails the third time are NOT tossed a fourth time. So a coin ends up heads if it gets heads on toss 1, toss 2 (given tails on 1), or toss 3 (given tails on 1 and 2), and ends up tails only if it gets tails all three times.

$$P(\text{heads}) = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{8} = \frac{7}{8}.$$

By linearity of expectation:
$$E[\text{heads}] = 64 \times \frac{7}{8} = 56.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
