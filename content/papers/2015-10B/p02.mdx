<Problem>
Marie does three equally time-consuming tasks in a row without taking breaks. She begins the first task at 1:00 PM and finishes the second task at 2:40 PM. When does she finish the third task?
</Problem>

<Solution>
From 1:00 PM to 2:40 PM is 100 minutes. Since this covers exactly two equally long tasks, each task takes $100 \div 2 = 50$ minutes.

She finishes the second task at 2:40 PM, so she finishes the third task 50 minutes later at **3:30 PM**.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
