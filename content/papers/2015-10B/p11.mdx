<Problem>
Among the positive integers less than $100$, each of whose digits is a prime number, one is selected at random. What is the probability that the selected number is prime?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The prime digits are $2, 3, 5, 7$.

**Single-digit numbers with all prime digits:** $2, 3, 5, 7$ — all 4 are prime. That's 4 numbers, all prime.

**Two-digit numbers with all prime digits:** Each digit is chosen from $\{2, 3, 5, 7\}$, giving $4 \times 4 = 16$ two-digit numbers: $22, 23, 25, 27, 32, 33, 35, 37, 52, 53, 55, 57, 72, 73, 75, 77$.

Which of these are prime?
- $22 = 2 \times 11$: No
- $23$: Prime
- $25 = 5^2$: No
- $27 = 3^3$: No
- $32 = 2^5$: No
- $33 = 3 \times 11$: No
- $35 = 5 \times 7$: No
- $37$: Prime
- $52 = 4 \times 13$: No
- $53$: Prime
- $55 = 5 \times 11$: No
- $57 = 3 \times 19$: No
- $72 = 8 \times 9$: No
- $73$: Prime
- $75 = 3 \times 25$: No
- $77 = 7 \times 11$: No

Primes among two-digit numbers: $23, 37, 53, 73$ — that's 4 primes out of 16.

**Total numbers:** $4 + 16 = 20$.
**Total primes:** $4 + 4 = 8$.

$$P = \frac{8}{20} = \frac{2}{5}.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
