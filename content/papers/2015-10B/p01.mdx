<Problem>
What is the value of $2-(-2)^{-2}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We evaluate $(-2)^{-2}$ first. Since $(-2)^2 = 4$, we get $(-2)^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{4}$.

Therefore:
$$2 - (-2)^{-2} = 2 - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{8}{4} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{7}{4}.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
