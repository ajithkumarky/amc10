<Problem>
Four siblings ordered an extra large pizza. Alex ate $\dfrac{1}{5}$, Beth $\dfrac{1}{3}$, and Cyril $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of the pizza. Dan got the leftovers. What is the sequence of the siblings in decreasing order of the part of pizza they consumed?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First, find what Dan ate. The total eaten by Alex, Beth, and Cyril is:
$$\frac{1}{5} + \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{12}{60} + \frac{20}{60} + \frac{15}{60} = \frac{47}{60}.$$

So Dan ate $1 - \dfrac{47}{60} = \dfrac{13}{60}$.

Now compare the four fractions using a common denominator of 60:
- Beth: $\dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{20}{60}$
- Cyril: $\dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{15}{60}$
- Dan: $\dfrac{13}{60}$
- Alex: $\dfrac{1}{5} = \dfrac{12}{60}$

Decreasing order: Beth ($20$), Cyril ($15$), Dan ($13$), Alex ($12$).

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
