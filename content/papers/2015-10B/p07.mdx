<Problem>
Consider the operation "minus the reciprocal of," defined by $a \diamond b = a - \dfrac{1}{b}$. What is $((1 \diamond 2) \diamond 3) - (1 \diamond (2 \diamond 3))$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Compute each piece step by step.

**Left side:** $((1 \diamond 2) \diamond 3)$

First: $1 \diamond 2 = 1 - \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

Then: $\dfrac{1}{2} \diamond 3 = \dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{3}{6} - \dfrac{2}{6} = \dfrac{1}{6}$.

**Right side:** $1 \diamond (2 \diamond 3)$

First: $2 \diamond 3 = 2 - \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{5}{3}$.

Then: $1 \diamond \dfrac{5}{3} = 1 - \dfrac{1}{5/3} = 1 - \dfrac{3}{5} = \dfrac{2}{5}$.

**Difference:**
$$\frac{1}{6} - \frac{2}{5} = \frac{5}{30} - \frac{12}{30} = -\frac{7}{30}.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
