<Problem>

<Solution>
Set up coordinates: $A = (0,10)$, $B = (0,0)$, $C = (10,0)$, $D = (10,10)$ (a square with side $10$).

$F$ lies on $\overline{AD}$ (the top side), so $F = (f, 10)$ for some $0 \le f \le 10$.

**Finding the direction of $DE$:** From $D$, the ray $DC$ points straight down (direction $270°$). Since $E$ is on the opposite half-plane from $A$ with respect to line $CD$ (i.e., to the right of $CD$) and $\angle CDE = 110°$, ray $DE$ is $110°$ counterclockwise from ray $DC$, giving direction $270° + 110° = 380° = 20°$.

So ray $DE$ points in direction $20°$ from $D$.

**Finding $\angle FDE$:** Ray $DF$ points from $D$ toward $F$ (leftward along the top side), which is direction $180°$.

$$\angle FDE = 180° - 20° = 160°.$$

**Isoceles triangle $DEF$:** Since $DE = DF$, triangle $DEF$ is isoceles with vertex angle $\angle FDE = 160°$. The two base angles are equal:

$$\angle DEF = \angle DFE = \frac{180° - 160°}{2} = 10°.$$

**Finding $\angle AFE$:** Since $A$, $F$, $D$ are collinear (all on the top side of the square), $\angle AFE$ and $\angle DFE$ are supplementary:

$$\angle AFE = 180° - \angle DFE = 180° - 10° = 170°.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
