<Problem>
Menkara has a $4 \times 6$ index card. If she shortens the length of one side of this card by $1$ inch, the card would have area $18$ square inches. What would the area of the card be in square inches if instead she shortens the length of the other side by $1$ inch?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The card has dimensions $4$ and $6$, so its area is $24$ square inches.

If the $4$-inch side is shortened by $1$, the new dimensions are $3 \times 6 = 18$. That matches the given condition, so it is the $4$-inch side that is shortened.

If instead the $6$-inch side is shortened by $1$, the new dimensions are $4 \times 5 = 20$ square inches.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
