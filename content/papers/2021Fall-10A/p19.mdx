<Problem>
A disk of radius $1$ rolls all the way around the inside of a square of side length $s > 4$ and sweeps out a region of area $A$. A second disk of radius $1$ rolls all the way around the outside of the same square and sweeps out a region of area $2A$. The value of $s$ can be written as $a + \dfrac{b\pi}{c}$, where $a$, $b$, and $c$ are positive integers and $b$ and $c$ are relatively prime. What is $a + b + c$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Inside sweep.**

Place the square as $[0,s]^2$. The disk's center stays distance $1$ from each wall, tracing the boundary of the inner square $[1,s{-}1]^2$ (side $s-2$, with sharp corners since the disk fits exactly tangent to both walls at each inside corner).

The swept region is $\{P \in [0,s]^2 : \mathrm{dist}(P,\,\partial([1,s{-}1]^2)) \le 1\}$. We split it into two parts.

**Part 1 — interior band** (points inside $[1,s{-}1]^2$ within distance $1$ of its boundary): these are the points in $[1,s{-}1]^2 \setminus [2,s{-}2]^2$, with area $(s-2)^2-(s-4)^2 = 4s-12$.

**Part 2 — outer layer** (points in $[0,s]^2$ outside $[1,s{-}1]^2$, within distance $1$ of the path). Along each of the 4 sides the strip has dimensions $1 \times (s-2)$, area $s-2$. At each of the 4 outer corners of the path (e.g., the point $(1,1)$ inside the original square), the nearest path point is that corner itself; the reachable set in the adjacent corner of $[0,s]^2$ is a quarter-disk of radius $1$, area $\pi/4$.

Total outer-layer area: $4(s-2) + 4 \cdot \tfrac{\pi}{4} = 4s - 8 + \pi$.

So the full inside swept area is:
$$A = (4s-12)+(4s-8+\pi) = 8s-20+\pi.$$

**Outside sweep.**

The disk's center travels at distance $1$ outside the square: straight segments of length $s$ along each side, and quarter-circles of radius $1$ around each convex corner. The disk (radius $1$) therefore covers the band from distance $0$ to $2$ outside the square.

- 4 rectangular strips, one per side: $s \times 2$ each, total area $8s$.
- 4 corner pieces: at each convex corner the center sweeps a quarter-circle of radius $1$, so the disk sweeps a full quarter-disk of radius $2$ (area $\pi$ each), total $4\pi$. These pieces do not overlap with the rectangular strips.

$$2A = 8s + 4\pi.$$

**Solving for $s$.**

$$2(8s - 20 + \pi) = 8s + 4\pi$$
$$16s - 40 + 2\pi = 8s + 4\pi$$
$$8s = 40 + 2\pi$$
$$s = 5 + \frac{\pi}{4}.$$

Check: $s \approx 5.79 > 4$ ✓.

Writing $s = 5 + \dfrac{1 \cdot \pi}{4}$ gives $a = 5$, $b = 1$, $c = 4$ with $\gcd(1,4) = 1$, so

$$a + b + c = 5 + 1 + 4 = \boxed{10}.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
