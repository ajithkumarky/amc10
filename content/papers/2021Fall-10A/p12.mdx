<Problem>
The base-nine representation of the number $N$ is $27{,}006{,}000{,}052_{\text{nine}}$. What is the remainder when $N$ is divided by $5$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We need $N \pmod{5}$. Since $9 \equiv 4 \pmod{5}$, we note that $9^2 = 81 \equiv 1 \pmod{5}$.

So powers of $9$ alternate mod $5$: $9^k \equiv 4$ when $k$ is odd, and $9^k \equiv 1$ when $k$ is even.

The digits of $N$ in base nine are (from most to least significant, so $9^{10}$ down to $9^0$):

$$2,\ 7,\ 0,\ 0,\ 6,\ 0,\ 0,\ 0,\ 0,\ 5,\ 2$$

Compute $d \cdot 9^k \pmod 5$ for each nonzero digit:

| Digit $d$ | Power $k$ | $9^k \bmod 5$ | $d \cdot 9^k \bmod 5$ |
|-----------|-----------|----------------|------------------------|
| $2$ | $10$ | $1$ | $2$ |
| $7$ | $9$ | $4$ | $28 \equiv 3$ |
| $6$ | $6$ | $1$ | $6 \equiv 1$ |
| $5$ | $1$ | $4$ | $20 \equiv 0$ |
| $2$ | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ |

Sum: $2 + 3 + 1 + 0 + 2 = 8 \equiv 3 \pmod{5}$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
