<Problem>
What is the value of $\dfrac{(2112-2021)^2}{169}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First compute the numerator: $2112 - 2021 = 91$, so the expression becomes $\dfrac{91^2}{169}$.

Notice that $91 = 7 \times 13$ and $169 = 13^2$, so

$$\frac{91^2}{169} = \frac{(7 \times 13)^2}{13^2} = 7^2 = 49.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
