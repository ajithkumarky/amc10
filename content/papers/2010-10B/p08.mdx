<Problem>
A ticket to a school play cost $x$ dollars, where $x$ is a whole number. A group of 9th graders buys tickets costing a total of $\textdollar 48$, and a group of 10th graders buys tickets costing a total of $\textdollar 64$. How many values for $x$ are possible?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Since $x$ divides both $48$ and $64$, $x$ must be a common divisor of $48$ and $64$.

$$\gcd(48, 64) = 16.$$

The positive divisors of $16$ are $1, 2, 4, 8, 16$ — that is $5$ values.

Each is a valid whole-number ticket price that divides both $48$ and $64$, so all $5$ values are possible.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
