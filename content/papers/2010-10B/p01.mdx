<Problem>
What is $100(100-3)-(100\cdot100-3)$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Expand each part:

$$100(100-3) = 100 \cdot 97 = 9700$$

$$100 \cdot 100 - 3 = 10000 - 3 = 9997$$

So the expression equals $9700 - 9997 = -297$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
