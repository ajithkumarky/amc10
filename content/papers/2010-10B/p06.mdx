<Problem>
A circle is centered at $O$, $\overline{AB}$ is a diameter and $C$ is a point on the circle with $\angle COB = 50^\circ$. What is the degree measure of $\angle CAB$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The central angle $\angle COB = 50^\circ$ subtends arc $CB$. By the Inscribed Angle Theorem, the inscribed angle $\angle CAB$ subtends the same arc $CB$ from point $A$ on the circle, so:

$$\angle CAB = \frac{1}{2}\angle COB = \frac{50^\circ}{2} = 25^\circ.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
