<Problem>
Shelby drives her scooter at a speed of $30$ miles per hour if it is not raining, and $20$ miles per hour if it is raining. Today she drove in the sun in the morning and in the rain in the evening, for a total of $16$ miles in $40$ minutes. How many minutes did she drive in the rain?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $t$ be the time (in hours) she drove in the rain. She drove in the sun for $\dfrac{40}{60} - t = \dfrac{2}{3} - t$ hours.

Total distance:
$$30\!\left(\frac{2}{3} - t\right) + 20t = 16$$
$$20 - 30t + 20t = 16$$
$$20 - 10t = 16$$
$$t = \frac{4}{10} = \frac{2}{5} \text{ hours} = \frac{2}{5} \times 60 = 24 \text{ minutes.}$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
