<Problem>
A triangle has side lengths $10$, $10$, and $12$. A rectangle has width $4$ and area equal to the area of the triangle. What is the perimeter of this rectangle?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The triangle is isosceles with base $12$ and equal sides $10$. Drop the altitude from the apex to the base; it bisects the base into two segments of $6$. By the Pythagorean theorem, the altitude has length $\sqrt{10^2 - 6^2} = \sqrt{64} = 8$.

Area of triangle $= \dfrac{1}{2} \times 12 \times 8 = 48$.

The rectangle has width $4$ and area $48$, so its length is $48 \div 4 = 12$.

Perimeter $= 2(4 + 12) = 32$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
