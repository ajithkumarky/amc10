<Problem>
For a real number $x$, define $\heartsuit(x)$ to be the average of $x$ and $x^2$. What is $\heartsuit(1)+\heartsuit(2)+\heartsuit(3)$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
By definition, $\heartsuit(x) = \dfrac{x + x^2}{2}$.

$$\heartsuit(1) = \frac{1+1}{2} = 1, \quad \heartsuit(2) = \frac{2+4}{2} = 3, \quad \heartsuit(3) = \frac{3+9}{2} = 6.$$

$$\heartsuit(1)+\heartsuit(2)+\heartsuit(3) = 1 + 3 + 6 = 10.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
