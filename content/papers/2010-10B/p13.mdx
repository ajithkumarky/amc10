<Problem>
What is the sum of all the solutions of $x = \left|2x-|60-2x|\right|$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Case 1: $x \ge 30$**, so $60 - 2x \le 0$ and $|60 - 2x| = 2x - 60$.

The equation becomes $x = |2x - (2x-60)| = |60| = 60$.

Check: $x = 60 \ge 30$. Valid. Solution: $x = 60$.

**Case 2: $x < 30$**, so $|60 - 2x| = 60 - 2x$.

The equation becomes $x = |2x - (60-2x)| = |4x - 60|$.

**Sub-case 2a: $x \ge 15$** (so $4x - 60 \ge 0$):
$$x = 4x - 60 \implies 60 = 3x \implies x = 20.$$
Check: $15 \le 20 < 30$. Valid. Solution: $x = 20$.

**Sub-case 2b: $x < 15$** (so $4x - 60 < 0$):
$$x = -(4x - 60) = 60 - 4x \implies 5x = 60 \implies x = 12.$$
Check: $12 < 15$. Valid. Solution: $x = 12$.

The three solutions are $60, 20, 12$, with sum $60 + 20 + 12 = 92$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
