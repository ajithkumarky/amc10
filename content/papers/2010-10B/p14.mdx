<Problem>
The average of the numbers $1, 2, 3,\cdots, 98, 99,$ and $x$ is $100x$. What is $x$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The sum of $1$ through $99$ is $\dfrac{99 \cdot 100}{2} = 4950$.

There are $100$ numbers total (the integers $1$–$99$ plus $x$), so the average is:
$$\frac{4950 + x}{100} = 100x.$$

Solving:
$$4950 + x = 10000x \implies 4950 = 9999x \implies x = \frac{4950}{9999} = \frac{50}{101}.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
