<Problem>
Makayla attended two meetings during her $9$-hour work day. The first meeting took $45$ minutes and the second meeting took twice as long. What percent of her work day was spent attending meetings?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The first meeting took $45$ minutes and the second took $2 \times 45 = 90$ minutes, for a total of $135$ minutes in meetings.

Her work day is $9 \times 60 = 540$ minutes.

The fraction spent in meetings is $\dfrac{135}{540} = \dfrac{1}{4} = 25\%$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
