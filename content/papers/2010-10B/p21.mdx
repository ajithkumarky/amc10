<Problem>
A palindrome between $1000$ and $10{,}000$ is chosen at random. What is the probability that it is divisible by $7$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
A 4-digit palindrome has the form $\overline{abba}$ where $a \in \{1,\dots,9\}$ and $b \in \{0,\dots,9\}$.

Its value is $1001a + 110b$.

Since $1001 = 7 \times 143$, we have $7 \mid 1001a$ for all $a$. So the palindrome is divisible by $7$ if and only if $7 \mid 110b$.

Since $\gcd(110, 7) = 1$, we need $7 \mid b$. Thus $b \in \{0, 7\}$.

There are $9 \times 10 = 90$ total palindromes (9 choices for $a$, 10 for $b$). Those divisible by 7 have $b = 0$ or $b = 7$: that's $9 \times 2 = 18$ palindromes.

$$P = \frac{18}{90} = \frac{1}{5}.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
