<Problem>
Minnie rides on a flat road at $20$ kilometers per hour (kph), downhill at $30$ kph, and uphill at $5$ kph. Penny rides on a flat road at $30$ kph, downhill at $40$ kph, and uphill at $10$ kph. Minnie goes from town $A$ to town $B$, a distance of $10$ km all uphill, then from town $B$ to town $C$, a distance of $15$ km all downhill, and then back to town $A$, a distance of $20$ km on the flat. Penny goes the other way around using the same route. How many more minutes does it take Minnie to complete the $45$-km ride than it takes Penny?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Minnie's route: A to B (10 km uphill), B to C (15 km downhill), C to A (20 km flat).

Penny goes the other way: A to C (20 km flat), C to B (15 km uphill), B to A (10 km downhill).

**Minnie's time:**
- A to B (uphill at 5 kph): $\dfrac{10}{5} = 2$ hours.
- B to C (downhill at 30 kph): $\dfrac{15}{30} = 0.5$ hours.
- C to A (flat at 20 kph): $\dfrac{20}{20} = 1$ hour.
- Total: $3.5$ hours $= 210$ minutes.

**Penny's time:**
- A to C (flat at 30 kph): $\dfrac{20}{30} = \dfrac{2}{3}$ hours.
- C to B (uphill at 10 kph): $\dfrac{15}{10} = 1.5$ hours.
- B to A (downhill at 40 kph): $\dfrac{10}{40} = 0.25$ hours.
- Total: $\dfrac{2}{3} + 1.5 + 0.25 = \dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{7}{4} = \dfrac{8}{12} + \dfrac{21}{12} = \dfrac{29}{12}$ hours $= \dfrac{29 \times 60}{12} = 145$ minutes.

Difference: $210 - 145 = 65$ minutes.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
