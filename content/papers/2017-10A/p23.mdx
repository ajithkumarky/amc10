<Problem>
How many triangles with positive area have all their vertices at points $(i,j)$ in the coordinate plane, where $i$ and $j$ are integers between $1$ and $5$, inclusive?
</Problem>

<Solution>
There are $5 \times 5 = 25$ lattice points. Total ways to choose 3 points: $\dbinom{25}{3} = 2300$.

We subtract the number of collinear triples (which give degenerate zero-area triangles).

**Count collinear triples:**

*Horizontal lines:* 5 rows, each with 5 points: $5 \times \dbinom{5}{3} = 5 \times 10 = 50$.

*Vertical lines:* Same: $50$.

*Diagonal lines (slope 1):* Diagonals of length $k$ contain $\dbinom{k}{3}$ collinear triples.
- $k=5$: 1 diagonal (main): $\binom{5}{3}=10$.
- $k=4$: 2 diagonals: $2\times\binom{4}{3}=8$.
- $k=3$: 2 diagonals: $2\times\binom{3}{3}=2$.
- $k \le 2$: 0.
Total slope-1: $10+8+2=20$.

*Diagonal lines (slope $-1$):* Same by symmetry: $20$.

*Other slopes:* We need to find all lines through at least 3 of the 25 points with slopes other than $0, \pm1, \infty$.

Slope $\pm 2$: Lines with slope 2 through the grid. E.g., $y = 2x + b$. Points $(1,1),(2,3),(3,5)$: 3 collinear. $(1,2),(2,4)$: only 2. Also $(1,3),(2,5)$: 2. $(3,1),(4,3),(5,5)$: 3. $(2,1),(3,3),(4,5)$: 3. For slope $-2$: similarly. Let me count: slope 2 lines with 3+ points: $(1,1),(2,3),(3,5)$; $(2,1),(3,3),(4,5)$; $(3,1),(4,3),(5,5)$ — that's 3 lines, each giving $\binom{3}{3}=1$ triple. Total: 3.
Slope $-2$: by symmetry: 3 triples.
Slope $1/2$ (i.e., $\Delta y = 1$ per $\Delta x = 2$): $(1,1),(3,2),(5,3)$; $(1,2),(3,3),(5,4)$; $(1,3),(3,4),(5,5)$; also $(1,4),(3,5)$ only 2 pts. So 3 lines with 3 points. 3 triples.
Slope $-1/2$: 3 triples.
Slope $2/3$, $3/2$, etc.: Check if any line gives 3+ points. Slope $3/2$: $(1,1),(3,4)$: $\Delta y=3,\Delta x=2$; next would be $(5,7)$ out of range. Only 2 points. Slope $2/1$: counted above. Slope $1/3$: $(1,1),(4,2)$: next is $(7,3)$ out of range. Only 2 points. Slope $4$: $(1,1),(2,5)$: only 2. Slope $1/4$: $(1,1),(5,2)$: only 2.

So other slopes contribute: $3+3+3+3 = 12$ collinear triples.

Total collinear triples: $50 + 50 + 20 + 20 + 12 = 152$.

Valid triangles: $2300 - 152 = 2148$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
