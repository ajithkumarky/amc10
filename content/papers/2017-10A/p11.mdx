<Problem>
The region consisting of all points in three-dimensional space within $3$ units of line segment $\overline{AB}$ has volume $216\pi$. What is the length $AB$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The region within distance $r = 3$ of a line segment of length $L$ is a "capsule": a cylinder of radius $3$ and length $L$, capped on each end by a hemisphere of radius $3$.

Volume of the capsule:
$$V = \pi r^2 L + \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = \pi (3)^2 L + \frac{4}{3}\pi (3)^3 = 9\pi L + 36\pi.$$

Set equal to $216\pi$:
$$9\pi L + 36\pi = 216\pi$$
$$9L + 36 = 216$$
$$9L = 180$$
$$L = 20.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
