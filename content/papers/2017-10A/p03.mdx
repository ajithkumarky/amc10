<Problem>
Tamara has three rows of two $6$-feet by $2$-feet flower beds in her garden. The beds are separated and also surrounded by $1$-foot-wide walkways. What is the total area of the walkways, in square feet?

The garden has 2 columns and 3 rows of flower beds. Each bed is 6 ft wide and 2 ft tall. Beds are separated and surrounded by 1-foot walkways on all sides.
</Problem>

<Solution>
First find the total garden dimensions.

Horizontally: 1 (border) + 6 (bed) + 1 (gap) + 6 (bed) + 1 (border) = 15 ft.

Vertically: 1 (border) + 2 (bed) + 1 (gap) + 2 (bed) + 1 (gap) + 2 (bed) + 1 (border) = 10 ft.

Total garden area: $15 \times 10 = 150$ sq ft.

Total flower bed area: $6 \times 2 \times 6 = 72$ sq ft (6 beds, each $6 \times 2$).

Walkway area: $150 - 72 = 78$ sq ft.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
