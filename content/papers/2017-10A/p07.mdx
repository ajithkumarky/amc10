<Problem>
Jerry and Silvia wanted to go from the southwest corner of a square field to the northeast corner. Jerry walked due east and then due north to reach the goal, but Silvia headed northeast and reached the goal walking in a straight line. Which of the following is closest to how much shorter Silvia's trip was, compared to Jerry's trip?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the side of the square be $1$.

Jerry's path: east $1$ unit then north $1$ unit, total distance $= 2$.

Silvia's path: the diagonal, distance $= \sqrt{1^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{2} \approx 1.414$.

Silvia's trip is shorter by $2 - \sqrt{2} \approx 0.586$.

As a fraction of Jerry's trip: $\dfrac{2 - \sqrt{2}}{2} = 1 - \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \approx 1 - 0.707 = 0.293 \approx 30\%$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
