<Problem>
The sum of two nonzero real numbers is $4$ times their product. What is the sum of the reciprocals of the two numbers?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the two numbers be $a$ and $b$. We are told $a + b = 4ab$.

Divide both sides by $ab$ (valid since both are nonzero):
$$\frac{a+b}{ab} = 4.$$

But $\dfrac{a+b}{ab} = \dfrac{1}{b} + \dfrac{1}{a}$, which is exactly the sum of the reciprocals.

So the sum of the reciprocals equals $4$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
