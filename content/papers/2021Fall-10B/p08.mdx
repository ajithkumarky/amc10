<Problem>
The greatest prime number that is a divisor of $16{,}384$ is $2$ because $16{,}384 = 2^{14}$. What is the sum of the digits of the greatest prime number that is a divisor of $16{,}383$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We need to factor $16{,}383 = 2^{14} - 1$.

Factor using difference of squares and sum of cubes:

$$2^{14} - 1 = (2^7-1)(2^7+1) = 127 \times 129.$$

Now factor each:
- $127$: check divisibility by primes up to $\sqrt{127} \approx 11.3$. Not divisible by 2, 3, 5, 7, 11. So 127 is prime.
- $129 = 3 \times 43$, and 43 is prime.

So $16{,}383 = 3 \times 43 \times 127$.

The greatest prime factor is $127$. The sum of its digits is $1 + 2 + 7 = 10$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
