<Solution>
Use the Shoelace formula on the vertices in order: $(1,0) \to (3,5) \to (5,0) \to (3,2)$.

$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}|x_1(y_2-y_4)+x_2(y_3-y_1)+x_3(y_4-y_2)+x_4(y_1-y_3)|$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}|1(5-2)+3(0-0)+5(2-5)+3(0-0)|$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}|1\cdot3 + 3\cdot0 + 5\cdot(-3) + 3\cdot0|$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}|3 + 0 - 15 + 0| = \frac{1}{2}\cdot 12 = 6.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
