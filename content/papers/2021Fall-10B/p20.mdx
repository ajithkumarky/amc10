<Problem>
In a particular game, each of $4$ players rolls a standard $6$-sided die. The winner is the player who rolls the highest number. If there is a tie for the highest roll, those involved in the tie will roll again and this process will continue until one player wins. Hugo is one of the players in this game. What is the probability that Hugo's first roll was a $5$, given that he won the game?
</Problem>

<Solution>
By Bayes' theorem:
$$P(\text{first roll}=5 \mid \text{won}) = \frac{P(\text{won} \mid \text{first roll}=5)\cdot P(\text{first roll}=5)}{P(\text{won})}.$$

By symmetry among the 4 players, $P(\text{won}) = \tfrac{1}{4}$, and $P(\text{first roll}=5) = \tfrac{1}{6}$.

**Computing $P(\text{won} \mid \text{first roll}=5)$.**

If Hugo rolls a 5, he is immediately eliminated if any other player rolls a 6. Otherwise he either wins outright (all others roll $\le 4$) or enters a tiebreak with whoever else rolled a 5.

**Case A: All 3 others roll $\le 4$.**

Hugo wins immediately.
$$P_A = \left(\frac{4}{6}\right)^3 = \frac{8}{27}.$$

**Case B: Exactly $k \ge 1$ others roll 5, none roll 6.**

Hugo enters a tiebreak with $k$ opponents and wins with probability $\tfrac{1}{k+1}$.

$$P_B = \sum_{k=1}^{3} \binom{3}{k}\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)^k\!\left(\frac{4}{6}\right)^{3-k}\cdot\frac{1}{k+1}.$$

- $k=1$: $\displaystyle 3\cdot\frac{1}{6}\cdot\frac{16}{36}\cdot\frac{1}{2} = \frac{48}{432} = \frac{1}{9}$.

- $k=2$: $\displaystyle 3\cdot\frac{1}{36}\cdot\frac{4}{6}\cdot\frac{1}{3} = \frac{12}{648} = \frac{1}{54}$.

- $k=3$: $\displaystyle \frac{1}{216}\cdot\frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{864}$.

$$P(\text{won} \mid \text{first roll}=5) = \frac{8}{27} + \frac{1}{9} + \frac{1}{54} + \frac{1}{864}.$$

Converting to denominator 864:
$$= \frac{256}{864} + \frac{96}{864} + \frac{16}{864} + \frac{1}{864} = \frac{369}{864} = \frac{41}{96}.$$

**Applying Bayes:**

$$P(\text{first roll}=5 \mid \text{won}) = \frac{\tfrac{41}{96}\cdot\tfrac{1}{6}}{\tfrac{1}{4}} = \frac{41}{96}\cdot\frac{4}{6} = \frac{164}{576} = \frac{41}{144}.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
