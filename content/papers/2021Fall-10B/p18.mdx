<Problem>
Three identical square sheets of paper each with side length $6$ are stacked on top of each other. The middle sheet is rotated clockwise $30^\circ$ about its center and the top sheet is rotated clockwise $60^\circ$ about its center, resulting in a $24$-sided polygon. The area of this polygon can be expressed in the form $a - b\sqrt{c}$, where $a$, $b$, and $c$ are positive integers, and $c$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. What is $a + b + c$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Place the common center at the origin. The three squares $S_0$, $S_{30}$, $S_{60}$ have side length 6 and are rotated clockwise by $0^\circ$, $30^\circ$, $60^\circ$ respectively. The 24-sided polygon is their **union**, so by inclusion-exclusion:

$$\text{Area(union)} = 3\cdot\text{Area}(S_i) - 3\cdot\text{Area}(S_i \cap S_j) + \text{Area}(S_0 \cap S_{30} \cap S_{60}).$$

Each square has area $6^2 = 36$.

**Pairwise intersection $S_0 \cap S_{30}$.**

The constraints are $|x|\le 3$, $|y|\le 3$, $\bigl|\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}x - \tfrac{1}{2}y\bigr|\le 3$, and $\bigl|\tfrac{1}{2}x + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}y\bigr|\le 3$. This intersection is an octagon. Two adjacent vertices in the first quadrant are found by intersecting edges of $S_0$ and $S_{30}$:

- $x = 3$ meets $\tfrac{1}{2}x + \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}y = 3$: gives $V_1 = (3,\,\sqrt{3})$.
- $\tfrac{1}{2}x + \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}y = 3$ meets $y = 3$: gives $V_2 = (6 - 3\sqrt{3},\,3)$.

The octagon has 8 such triangular sectors from the origin. One sector has area:
$$[OV_1 V_2] = \tfrac{1}{2}|x_1 y_2 - x_2 y_1| = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl|3\cdot 3 - (6-3\sqrt{3})\cdot\sqrt{3}\bigr| = \tfrac{1}{2}|9 - 6\sqrt{3}+9| = 9 - 3\sqrt{3}.$$

So $\text{Area}(S_0 \cap S_{30}) = 8(9 - 3\sqrt{3}) = 72 - 24\sqrt{3}$.

By symmetry (all pairs are rotated $30^\circ$ apart), all three pairwise intersections have the same area.

**Triple intersection $S_0 \cap S_{30} \cap S_{60}$.**

Adding the constraints from $S_{60}$ ($\bigl|\tfrac{1}{2}x - \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}y\bigr|\le 3$ and $\bigl|\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}x + \tfrac{1}{2}y\bigr|\le 3$) cuts the octagon further into a 24-gon. Two adjacent vertices near the positive $x$-axis are:

- $x = 3$ meets $\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}x - \tfrac{1}{2}y = 3$ (from $S_{30}$): gives $V_1 = (3,\,3\sqrt{3}-6)$.
- $\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}x - \tfrac{1}{2}y = 3$ meets $\tfrac{1}{2}x - \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}y = 3$ (from $S_{60}$): solving gives $V_2 = (3\sqrt{3}-3,\,3-3\sqrt{3})$.

The triple intersection has 12-fold symmetry with 2 vertices per $30^\circ$ sector. One sector area:
$$[OV_1 V_2] = \tfrac{1}{2}|3(3-3\sqrt{3}) - (3\sqrt{3}-3)(3\sqrt{3}-6)|.$$

Computing: $3(3-3\sqrt{3}) = 9-9\sqrt{3}$ and $(3\sqrt{3}-3)(3\sqrt{3}-6) = 27-18\sqrt{3}-9\sqrt{3}+18 = 45-27\sqrt{3}$.

$$[OV_1 V_2] = \tfrac{1}{2}|9-9\sqrt{3}-45+27\sqrt{3}| = \tfrac{1}{2}(36-18\sqrt{3}) = 18-9\sqrt{3}.$$

So $\text{Area}(S_0 \cap S_{30} \cap S_{60}) = 12(18-9\sqrt{3}) = 216 - 108\sqrt{3}$.

**Inclusion-exclusion:**

$$\text{Area(union)} = 3(36) - 3(72-24\sqrt{3}) + (216-108\sqrt{3})$$
$$= 108 - 216 + 72\sqrt{3} + 216 - 108\sqrt{3} = 108 - 36\sqrt{3}.$$

So $a = 108$, $b = 36$, $c = 3$, giving:

$$a + b + c = 108 + 36 + 3 = 147.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
