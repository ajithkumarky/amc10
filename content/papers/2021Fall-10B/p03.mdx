<Problem>
The expression $\dfrac{2021}{2020} - \dfrac{2020}{2021}$ is equal to the fraction $\dfrac{p}{q}$ in which $p$ and $q$ are positive integers whose greatest common divisor is $1$. What is $p$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Subtract the two fractions over a common denominator of $2020 \cdot 2021$:

$$\frac{2021}{2020} - \frac{2020}{2021} = \frac{2021^2 - 2020^2}{2020 \cdot 2021}.$$

The numerator factors as a difference of squares:

$$2021^2 - 2020^2 = (2021+2020)(2021-2020) = 4041 \cdot 1 = 4041.$$

So the fraction equals $\dfrac{4041}{2020 \cdot 2021}$.

Now check whether $\gcd(4041, 2020 \cdot 2021) = 1$.
$4041 = 3 \times 1347 = 3 \times 3 \times 449$. Check that 449 is prime (not divisible by 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19 — and $21^2 > 449$). Indeed 449 is prime.

$2020 = 4 \times 5 \times 101$ and $2021 = 43 \times 47$. Neither contains 3 or 449 as a factor, so $\gcd(4041, 2020 \cdot 2021) = 1$.

Therefore $p = 4041$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
