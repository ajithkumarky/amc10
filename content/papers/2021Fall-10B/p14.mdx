<Problem>
Una rolls $6$ standard $6$-sided dice simultaneously and calculates the product of the $6$ numbers obtained. What is the probability that the product is divisible by $4$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We use complementary counting. The product of the six dice is divisible by $4$ unless it fails to contain two factors of $2$. There are two "bad" cases:

**Case 1: The product contains no factor of $2$ at all** (i.e., all six dice show odd numbers).

The odd faces of a die are $1, 3, 5$ — that's $3$ out of $6$ faces. The probability all six dice are odd is
$$\left(\frac{3}{6}\right)^6 = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^6 = \frac{1}{64}.$$

**Case 2: The product contains exactly one factor of $2$** (i.e., exactly one die shows a number with exactly one factor of $2$, and all others are odd).

A die face contributes exactly one factor of $2$ only if it equals $2$ or $6$ (both are $\equiv 2 \pmod{4}$). That's $2$ out of $6$ faces, with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$.

Exactly one die lands on such a face and the other five are all odd:
$$\binom{6}{1} \cdot \frac{1}{3} \cdot \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^5 = 6 \cdot \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{32} = \frac{6}{96} = \frac{1}{16}.$$

**Probability the product is NOT divisible by $4$:**
$$\frac{1}{64} + \frac{1}{16} = \frac{1}{64} + \frac{4}{64} = \frac{5}{64}.$$

**Probability the product IS divisible by $4$:**
$$1 - \frac{5}{64} = \frac{59}{64}.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
