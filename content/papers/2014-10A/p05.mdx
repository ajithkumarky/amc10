<Problem>
On an algebra quiz, $10\%$ of the students scored $70$ points, $35\%$ scored $80$ points, $30\%$ scored $90$ points, and the rest scored $100$ points. What is the difference between the mean and median score of the students' scores on this quiz?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The remaining percentage is $100\% - 10\% - 35\% - 30\% = 25\%$ scored 100 points.

**Mean:** Use the percentages as weights.
$$\text{Mean} = 0.10(70) + 0.35(80) + 0.30(90) + 0.25(100)$$
$$= 7 + 28 + 27 + 25 = 87.$$

**Median:** Order all students from lowest to highest score. The 50th percentile falls in the range of scores. The cumulative percentages are:
- Up to 70: 10%
- Up to 80: 10% + 35% = 45%
- Up to 90: 45% + 30% = 75%

The 50th percentile lands in the 90-point group (between 45% and 75%), so the **median is 90**.

**Difference:** $|90 - 87| = 3$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
