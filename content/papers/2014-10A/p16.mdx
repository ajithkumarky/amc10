<Problem>
In rectangle $ABCD$, $AB=1$, $BC=2$, and points $E$, $F$, and $G$ are midpoints of $\overline{BC}$, $\overline{CD}$, and $\overline{AD}$, respectively. Point $H$ is the midpoint of $\overline{GE}$. What is the area of the shaded quadrilateral region?

Place $D=(0,0)$, $C=(1,0)$, $B=(1,2)$, $A=(0,2)$. Then $F=(1/2,0)$ is the midpoint of $\overline{CD}$, $G=(0,1)$ is the midpoint of $\overline{AD}$, $E=(1,1)$ is the midpoint of $\overline{BC}$, and $H=(1/2,1)$ is the midpoint of $\overline{GE}$. The shaded region is the quadrilateral formed by the intersection of triangles $AFB$ and $DHC$.
</Problem>

<Solution>
Set up coordinates: $D=(0,0)$, $C=(1,0)$, $B=(1,2)$, $A=(0,2)$, $F=(1/2,0)$, $G=(0,1)$, $E=(1,1)$, $H=(1/2,1)$.

The two diagonals drawn are $\overline{AF}$ (from $A=(0,2)$ to $F=(1/2,0)$) and $\overline{BF}$ (from $B=(1,2)$ to $F=(1/2,0)$), forming triangle $AFB$. Similarly, $\overline{DH}$ (from $D=(0,0)$ to $H=(1/2,1)$) and $\overline{CH}$ (from $C=(1,0)$ to $H=(1/2,1)$), forming triangle $DHC$.

The shaded quadrilateral is the intersection of the interior of triangle $AFB$ with the interior of triangle $DHC$. 

**Line $AF$:** from $(0,2)$ to $(1/2,0)$: slope $= \dfrac{0-2}{1/2-0} = -4$. Equation: $y = -4x + 2$.

**Line $BF$:** from $(1,2)$ to $(1/2,0)$: slope $= \dfrac{0-2}{1/2-1} = 4$. Equation: $y = 4x - 2$.

**Line $DH$:** from $(0,0)$ to $(1/2,1)$: slope $= 2$. Equation: $y = 2x$.

**Line $CH$:** from $(1,0)$ to $(1/2,1)$: slope $= \dfrac{1-0}{1/2-1} = -2$. Equation: $y = -2(x-1) = -2x+2$.

Find the four vertices of the shaded quadrilateral (intersections of the four lines pairwise):

- $AF \cap DH$: $-4x+2 = 2x \Rightarrow x=1/3, y=2/3$. Point $X=(1/3,\,2/3)$.
- $BF \cap CH$: $4x-2 = -2x+2 \Rightarrow 6x=4 \Rightarrow x=2/3, y=2/3$. Point $Y=(2/3,\,2/3)$.
- $AF \cap CH$: $-4x+2 = -2x+2 \Rightarrow -2x=0 \Rightarrow x=0, y=2$. This is point $A$, not inside the quadrilateral.
- $DH \cap BF$: $2x = 4x-2 \Rightarrow x=1, y=2$. This is point $B$, not inside.
- $F=(1/2,0)$ and $H=(1/2,1)$: check if on boundary.

The shaded region has four vertices: $F=(1/2,0)$, $X=(1/3,2/3)$, $H=(1/2,1)$, $Y=(2/3,2/3)$.

**Area by Shoelace** with vertices in order $(1/2,0),(1/3,2/3),(1/2,1),(2/3,2/3)$:

$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}|x_1(y_2-y_4)+x_2(y_3-y_1)+x_3(y_4-y_2)+x_4(y_1-y_3)|$$
$$= \frac{1}{2}\left|\frac{1}{2}\!\left(\frac{2}{3}-\frac{2}{3}\right)+\frac{1}{3}(1-0)+\frac{1}{2}\!\left(\frac{2}{3}-\frac{2}{3}\right)+\frac{2}{3}(0-1)\right|$$
$$= \frac{1}{2}\left|0+\frac{1}{3}+0-\frac{2}{3}\right| = \frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{6}.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
