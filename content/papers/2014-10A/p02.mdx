<Problem>
Roy's cat eats $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of a can of cat food every morning and $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of a can of cat food every evening. Before feeding his cat on Monday morning, Roy opened a box containing $6$ cans of cat food. On what day of the week did the cat finish eating all the cat food in the box?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Each day the cat eats $\dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{4}{12} + \dfrac{3}{12} = \dfrac{7}{12}$ of a can.

Total food: 6 cans. Number of full daily rations: $6 \div \dfrac{7}{12} = 6 \cdot \dfrac{12}{7} = \dfrac{72}{7} \approx 10.28$ days.

After 10 full days (Monday through Wednesday of the second week), the cat has eaten $10 \cdot \dfrac{7}{12} = \dfrac{70}{12} = \dfrac{35}{6}$ cans. That leaves $6 - \dfrac{35}{6} = \dfrac{36-35}{6} = \dfrac{1}{6}$ of a can.

Day 11 starts on Thursday morning. The cat eats $\dfrac{1}{3}$ in the morning, but only $\dfrac{1}{6}$ remains, which is less than $\dfrac{1}{3}$. So the cat finishes the food on Thursday morning.

Counting from Monday (Day 1): Day 11 is Thursday.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
