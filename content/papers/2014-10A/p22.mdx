<Problem>
In rectangle $ABCD$, $AB=20$ and $BC=10$. Let $E$ be a point on $\overline{CD}$ such that $\angle CBE=15^\circ$. What is $AE$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Place $B$ at the origin, $C=(10,0)$, $D=(10,20)$, $A=(0,20)$ — wait, $AB=20$ is the longer side and $BC=10$.

Actually: $AB$ and $CD$ are the longer sides (length 20), $BC$ and $AD$ are the shorter sides (length 10).

Place $B=(0,0)$, $C=(10,0)$, $D=(10,20)$, $A=(0,20)$.

$E$ is on $\overline{CD}$, so $E = (10, e)$ for some $0 \le e \le 20$.

$\angle CBE = 15°$. $\overrightarrow{BC} = (10,0)$ (pointing right). $\overrightarrow{BE} = (10, e)$.

$$\tan(\angle CBE) = \frac{e}{10} = \tan 15°.$$

$\tan 15° = \tan(45° - 30°) = \dfrac{1 - 1/\sqrt{3}}{1 + 1/\sqrt{3}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}-1}{\sqrt{3}+1} = \dfrac{(\sqrt{3}-1)^2}{2} = \dfrac{4-2\sqrt{3}}{2} = 2 - \sqrt{3}.$

So $e = 10\tan 15° = 10(2-\sqrt{3}) = 20 - 10\sqrt{3}$.

Thus $E = (10,\, 20-10\sqrt{3})$.

$A = (0, 20)$.

$$AE = \sqrt{(10-0)^2 + (20-10\sqrt{3}-20)^2} = \sqrt{100 + (10\sqrt{3})^2} = \sqrt{100 + 300} = \sqrt{400} = 20.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
