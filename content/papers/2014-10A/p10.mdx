<Problem>
Five positive consecutive integers starting with $a$ have average $b$. What is the average of $5$ consecutive integers that start with $b$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The five consecutive integers starting with $a$ are $a, a+1, a+2, a+3, a+4$.

Their average is $b = \dfrac{5a + 10}{5} = a + 2$.

Now the five consecutive integers starting with $b = a+2$ are: $b, b+1, b+2, b+3, b+4$.

Their average is $b + 2 = (a+2) + 2 = a + 4$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
