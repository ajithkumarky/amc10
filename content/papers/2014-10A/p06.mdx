<Problem>
Suppose that $a$ cows give $b$ gallons of milk in $c$ days. At this rate, how many gallons of milk will $d$ cows give in $e$ days?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Find the rate per cow per day. If $a$ cows produce $b$ gallons in $c$ days, then one cow produces $\dfrac{b}{ac}$ gallons per day.

For $d$ cows over $e$ days:
$$d \cdot e \cdot \frac{b}{ac} = \frac{bde}{ac}.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
