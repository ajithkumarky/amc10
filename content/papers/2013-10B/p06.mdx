<Problem>
The average age of $33$ fifth-graders is $11$. The average age of $55$ of their parents is $33$. What is the average age of all of these parents and fifth-graders?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The total age of the $33$ fifth-graders is $33 \times 11 = 363$.

The total age of the $55$ parents is $55 \times 33 = 1815$.

Combined total age: $363 + 1815 = 2178$.

Combined number of people: $33 + 55 = 88$.

Average age: $\dfrac{2178}{88} = 24.75$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
