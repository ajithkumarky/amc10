<Problem>
Real numbers $x$ and $y$ satisfy the equation $x^2 + y^2 = 10x - 6y - 34$. What is $x+y$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Rearrange to recognize a circle equation. Move all terms to the left:
$$x^2 - 10x + y^2 + 6y + 34 = 0.$$

Complete the square for $x$ and $y$:
$$(x^2 - 10x + 25) + (y^2 + 6y + 9) + 34 - 25 - 9 = 0$$
$$(x-5)^2 + (y+3)^2 = 0.$$

A sum of two squares equals zero only when each square is zero. So:
$$x - 5 = 0 \implies x = 5, \quad y + 3 = 0 \implies y = -3.$$

Therefore $x + y = 5 + (-3) = 2$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
