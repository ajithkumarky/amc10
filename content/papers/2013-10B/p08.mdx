<Problem>
Ray's car averages $40$ miles per gallon of gasoline, and Tom's car averages $10$ miles per gallon of gasoline. Ray and Tom each drive the same number of miles. What is the cars' combined rate of miles per gallon of gasoline?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let each person drive $d$ miles.

- Ray uses $\dfrac{d}{40}$ gallons.
- Tom uses $\dfrac{d}{10}$ gallons.

Total miles driven: $2d$. Total gas used: $\dfrac{d}{40} + \dfrac{d}{10} = \dfrac{d}{40} + \dfrac{4d}{40} = \dfrac{5d}{40} = \dfrac{d}{8}$.

Combined rate $= \dfrac{2d}{d/8} = 2d \cdot \dfrac{8}{d} = 16$ miles per gallon.

This is the harmonic mean of $40$ and $10$: $\dfrac{2 \cdot 40 \cdot 10}{40+10} = \dfrac{800}{50} = 16$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
