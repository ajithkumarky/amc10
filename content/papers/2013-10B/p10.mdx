<Problem>
A basketball team's players were successful on $50\%$ of their two-point shots and $40\%$ of their three-point shots, which resulted in $54$ points. They attempted $50\%$ more two-point shots than three-point shots. How many three-point shots did they attempt?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $t$ = number of three-point shots attempted. Then the number of two-point shots attempted is $1.5t$ (50% more).

Points from two-point shots: $0.50 \times 1.5t \times 2 = 0.75t \times 2 = 1.5t$.

Points from three-point shots: $0.40 \times t \times 3 = 1.2t$.

Total points: $1.5t + 1.2t = 2.7t = 54$.

Solving: $t = \dfrac{54}{2.7} = 20$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
