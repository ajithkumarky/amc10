<Problem>
What is $\dfrac{2+4+6}{1+3+5} - \dfrac{1+3+5}{2+4+6}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Compute each sum: $2+4+6 = 12$ and $1+3+5 = 9$.

So the expression becomes:
$$\frac{12}{9} - \frac{9}{12} = \frac{4}{3} - \frac{3}{4}.$$

Finding a common denominator of 12:
$$\frac{16}{12} - \frac{9}{12} = \frac{7}{12}.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
