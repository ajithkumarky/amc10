<Problem>
Let $S$ be the set of sides and diagonals of a regular pentagon. A pair of elements of $S$ are selected at random without replacement. What is the probability that the two chosen segments have the same length?
</Problem>

<Solution>
A regular pentagon has $5$ sides and $5$ diagonals, for a total of $\lvert S \rvert = 10$ segments. All sides have the same length, and all diagonals have the same (different) length.

Total ways to choose a pair from 10: $\dbinom{10}{2} = 45$.

Favorable pairs (both sides or both diagonals):
- Both sides: $\dbinom{5}{2} = 10$
- Both diagonals: $\dbinom{5}{2} = 10$

Total favorable: $10 + 10 = 20$.

Probability: $\dfrac{20}{45} = \dfrac{4}{9}$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
