<Problem>
When counting from $3$ to $201$, $53$ is the $51^\text{st}$ number counted. When counting backwards from $201$ to $3$, $53$ is the $n^\text{th}$ number counted. What is $n$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Counting from $3$ to $201$ gives $201 - 3 + 1 = 199$ total numbers.

The number $53$ is the $51^\text{st}$ when counting forward, so it is the $(199 - 51 + 1) = 149^\text{th}$ when counting backward.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
