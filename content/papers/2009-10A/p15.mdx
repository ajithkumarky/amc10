<Problem>
The figures $F_1$, $F_2$, $F_3$, and $F_4$ are the first in a sequence of figures. For $n \geq 3$, $F_n$ is constructed from $F_{n-1}$ by surrounding it with a square and placing one more diamond on each side of the new square than $F_{n-1}$ had on each side of its outside square. For example, figure $F_3$ has $13$ diamonds. How many diamonds are there in figure $F_{20}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First identify the pattern. We know $F_3$ has $13$ diamonds.

$F_1$ has $1$ diamond. $F_2$ has $1 + 4 = 5$ diamonds (1 center plus 1 on each of 4 sides). $F_3$ has $5 + 8 = 13$ diamonds (adding 2 per side on the new outer square, giving $4 \times 2 = 8$ more).

The number added when going from $F_{n-1}$ to $F_n$ is $4(n-1)$ diamonds (there are $n-1$ diamonds per side on the outer ring of $F_n$, and 4 sides).

So the total in $F_n$ is:
$$F_n = 1 + \sum_{k=1}^{n-1} 4k = 1 + 4 \cdot \frac{(n-1)n}{2} = 1 + 2n(n-1).$$

Check: $F_3 = 1 + 2(3)(2) = 1 + 12 = 13$. $\checkmark$

For $n = 20$:
$$F_{20} = 1 + 2(20)(19) = 1 + 760 = 761.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
