<Problem>
Suppose that $P = 2^m$ and $Q = 3^n$. Which of the following is equal to $12^{mn}$ for every pair of integers $(m, n)$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Write $12^{mn}$ in terms of its prime factors: $12 = 2^2 \cdot 3$, so
$$12^{mn} = 2^{2mn} \cdot 3^{mn}.$$

Now express each answer choice in terms of 2s and 3s, using $P = 2^m$ and $Q = 3^n$:

- $P^{2n}Q^m = (2^m)^{2n}(3^n)^m = 2^{2mn} \cdot 3^{mn}$. $\checkmark$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
