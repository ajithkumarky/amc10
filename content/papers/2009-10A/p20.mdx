<Problem>
Andrea and Lauren are $20$ kilometers apart. They bike toward one another with Andrea traveling three times as fast as Lauren, and the distance between them decreasing at a rate of $1$ kilometer per minute. After $5$ minutes, Andrea stops biking because of a flat tire and waits for Lauren. After how many minutes from the time they started to bike does Lauren reach Andrea?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The combined closing speed is $1$ km/min. Andrea travels at $3$ times Lauren's speed, so Lauren's speed is $\frac{1}{4}$ km/min and Andrea's is $\frac{3}{4}$ km/min.

**First 5 minutes:** They close by $5$ km, leaving $15$ km between them. Andrea has traveled $\frac{3}{4} \times 5 = \frac{15}{4}$ km from her start; Lauren has traveled $\frac{1}{4} \times 5 = \frac{5}{4}$ km from her start.

**After minute 5:** Andrea stops. Lauren is still $15$ km away from Andrea and continues at $\frac{1}{4}$ km/min. Time for Lauren to cover $15$ km: $\frac{15}{1/4} = 60$ minutes.

Total time: $5 + 60 = 65$ minutes.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
