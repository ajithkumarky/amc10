<Problem>
At Jefferson Summer Camp, $60\%$ of the children play soccer, $30\%$ of the children swim, and $40\%$ of the soccer players swim. To the nearest whole percent, what percent of the non-swimmers play soccer?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the total number of children be $100$.

- Soccer players: $60$.
- Swimmers: $30$.
- Soccer players who also swim: $40\% \times 60 = 24$.

Soccer players who do NOT swim: $60 - 24 = 36$.

Non-swimmers total: $100 - 30 = 70$.

Percent of non-swimmers who play soccer: $\dfrac{36}{70} \approx 0.5143 \approx 51\%$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
