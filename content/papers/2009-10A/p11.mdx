<Problem>
One dimension of a cube is increased by $1$, another is decreased by $1$, and the third is left unchanged. The volume of the new rectangular solid is $5$ less than that of the cube. What was the volume of the cube?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the side length of the cube be $s$. The new rectangular solid has dimensions $s+1$, $s-1$, and $s$, so its volume is $(s+1)(s-1)(s) = (s^2-1)(s) = s^3 - s$.

Setting the volume decrease equal to 5:
$$s^3 - (s^3 - s) = 5 \implies s = 5.$$

The volume of the cube is $5^3 = 125$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
