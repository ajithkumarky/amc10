<Problem>
What is the sum of the digits of the square of $111{,}111{,}111$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The number $111{,}111{,}111$ has 9 ones. Squaring repunit numbers follows a pattern:

$$11^2 = 121, \quad 111^2 = 12321, \quad 1111^2 = 1234321, \ldots$$

The square of the $n$-digit repunit (all 1s) is the palindrome $123\ldots n\ldots321$. For $n=9$:

$$111{,}111{,}111^2 = 12{,}345{,}678{,}987{,}654{,}321.$$

The digit sum is $1+2+3+4+5+6+7+8+9+8+7+6+5+4+3+2+1 = 2(1+2+\cdots+8)+9 = 2(36)+9 = 81$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
