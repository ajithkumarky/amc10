<Problem>
Which of the following is equal to $1 + \dfrac{1}{1+\dfrac{1}{1+1}}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Work from the innermost expression outward.

The innermost part: $1 + 1 = 2$.

Next level: $1 + \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{2}$.

Outermost: $1 + \dfrac{1}{3/2} = 1 + \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{5}{3}$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
