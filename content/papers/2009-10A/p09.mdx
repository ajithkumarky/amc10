<Problem>
Positive integers $a$, $b$, and $2009$, with $a < b < 2009$, form a geometric sequence with an integer ratio. What is $a$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the common ratio be the integer $r \geq 2$. Then $b = ar$ and $2009 = ar^2$, so $a = 2009/r^2$.

Factor $2009$: $2009 = 7 \times 287 = 7 \times 7 \times 41 = 7^2 \times 41$.

For $a$ to be a positive integer, $r^2$ must divide $2009 = 7^2 \times 41$. The perfect square divisors of $2009$ are $1$ and $49$.

- $r^2 = 1$: $r = 1$, but then $a = b = 2009$, violating $a < b < 2009$.
- $r^2 = 49$: $r = 7$, giving $a = 2009/49 = 41$, $b = 41 \times 7 = 287$, and $287 \times 7 = 2009$. Check: $41 < 287 < 2009$. $\checkmark$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
