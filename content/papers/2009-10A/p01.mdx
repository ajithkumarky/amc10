<Problem>
One can holds $12$ ounces of soda. What is the minimum number of cans needed to provide a gallon ($128$ ounces) of soda?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We need at least $\lceil 128/12 \rceil$ cans. Since $128 \div 12 = 10.67\ldots$, we need $11$ cans (10 cans give only 120 ounces, which is not enough).

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
