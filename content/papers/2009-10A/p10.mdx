<Problem>
Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $B$. Point $D$ is the foot of the altitude from $B$, $AD = 3$, and $DC = 4$. What is the area of $\triangle ABC$?

The altitude from $B$ meets $AC$ at $D$, with $A$, $D$, $C$ collinear and $BD \perp AC$.
</Problem>

<Solution>
By the geometric mean relation for a right triangle, the altitude to the hypotenuse satisfies $BD^2 = AD \cdot DC = 3 \cdot 4 = 12$, so $BD = 2\sqrt{3}$.

The hypotenuse is $AC = AD + DC = 3 + 4 = 7$.

Area of $\triangle ABC = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot AC \cdot BD = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 7 \cdot 2\sqrt{3} = 7\sqrt{3}$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
