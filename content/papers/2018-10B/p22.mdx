<Problem>
Real numbers $x$ and $y$ are chosen independently and uniformly at random from the interval $[0,1]$. Which of the following numbers is closest to the probability that $x$, $y$, and $1$ are the side lengths of an obtuse triangle?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Triangle inequality.** For $x$, $y$, $1$ to form a triangle, all three inequalities must hold. Since $x, y \in [0,1]$, the conditions $x + 1 > y$ and $y + 1 > x$ are automatic. The only binding constraint is $x + y > 1$.

**Obtuseness.** By the law of cosines, a triangle is obtuse when the square of its longest side exceeds the sum of squares of the other two. Since $x, y \leq 1$, neither $x$ nor $y$ can be the longest side in a way that makes obtuseness possible: $x^2 > y^2 + 1$ requires $x > 1$, and similarly for $y$. So the only way the triangle can be obtuse is if the angle opposite the side of length $1$ exceeds $90°$, which requires $1^2 > x^2 + y^2$, i.e., $x^2 + y^2 < 1$.

**The target region** is therefore: $x + y > 1$ and $x^2 + y^2 < 1$, with $x, y \in [0,1]$.

This is the region inside the unit quarter-disk but above the chord $x + y = 1$. The chord connects $(1, 0)$ and $(0, 1)$, both on the unit circle, and the arc between them bulges outward (e.g., at $\theta = 45°$, $\cos 45° + \sin 45° = \sqrt{2} > 1$). So the chord cuts a circular segment off the arc side of the quarter-disk.

- Area of the quarter-disk: $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
- Area of the quarter-disk with $x + y \leq 1$: this is the triangle with vertices $(0,0)$, $(1,0)$, $(0,1)$, area $= \dfrac{1}{2}$ (the entire triangle lies inside the unit circle since all its points satisfy $x^2 + y^2 \leq \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot (x+y)^2 \leq \tfrac{1}{2}$... more simply, $x^2+y^2 \leq (x+y)^2/1$ is not needed — the triangle's farthest points from the origin are $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$, both exactly on the circle, so the whole triangle is inside the disk).

Thus area of the target region $= \dfrac{\pi}{4} - \dfrac{1}{2} \approx 0.7854 - 0.5000 = 0.2854$.

Since the sample space is the unit square with area $1$, the probability is $\dfrac{\pi}{4} - \dfrac{1}{2} \approx 0.285$, which is closest to $0.29$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
