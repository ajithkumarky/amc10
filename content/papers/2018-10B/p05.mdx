<Problem>
How many subsets of $\{2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9\}$ contain at least one prime number?
</Problem>

<Solution>
It's easier to count the complement: subsets containing NO prime number.

The primes in $\{2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9\}$ are $2, 3, 5, 7$. The non-primes (composites and 1, but here just composites) are $4, 6, 8, 9$.

So the subsets with no prime are exactly the subsets of $\{4, 6, 8, 9\}$. There are $2^4 = 16$ such subsets.

The total number of subsets of an 8-element set is $2^8 = 256$.

Subsets containing at least one prime $= 256 - 16 = 240$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
