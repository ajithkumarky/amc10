<Problem>
A three-dimensional rectangular box with dimensions $X$, $Y$, and $Z$ has faces whose surface areas are $24, 24, 48, 48, 72,$ and $72$ square units. What is $X+Y+Z$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
A rectangular box has three pairs of congruent faces. The face areas are:
$$XY = 24, \quad XZ = 48, \quad YZ = 72.$$

To find $X$, $Y$, $Z$, multiply all three equations:
$$(XY)(XZ)(YZ) = 24 \cdot 48 \cdot 72 = 82944$$
$$(XYZ)^2 = 82944 \implies XYZ = 288.$$

Then:
$$X = \frac{XYZ}{YZ} = \frac{288}{72} = 4, \quad Y = \frac{XYZ}{XZ} = \frac{288}{48} = 6, \quad Z = \frac{XYZ}{XY} = \frac{288}{24} = 12.$$

Check: $XY = 24$ ✓, $XZ = 48$ ✓, $YZ = 72$ ✓.

Therefore $X + Y + Z = 4 + 6 + 12 = 22$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
