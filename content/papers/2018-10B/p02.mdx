<Problem>
Sam drove $96$ miles in $90$ minutes. His average speed during the first $30$ minutes was $60$ mph (miles per hour), and his average speed during the second $30$ minutes was $65$ mph. What was his average speed, in mph, during the last $30$ minutes?
</Problem>

<Solution>
In the first 30 minutes (= $\frac{1}{2}$ hour), Sam drove $60 \times \frac{1}{2} = 30$ miles.

In the second 30 minutes, he drove $65 \times \frac{1}{2} = 32.5$ miles.

The total for the first two segments is $30 + 32.5 = 62.5$ miles. Since the whole trip is $96$ miles, the last segment covers $96 - 62.5 = 33.5$ miles in $\frac{1}{2}$ hour.

Average speed in the last segment $= 33.5 \div \frac{1}{2} = 67$ mph.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
