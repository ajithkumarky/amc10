<Problem>
Kate bakes a $20$-inch by $18$-inch pan of cornbread. The cornbread is cut into pieces that measure $2$ inches by $2$ inches. How many pieces of cornbread does the pan contain?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Each piece is a $2 \times 2$ square. Along the 20-inch side we fit $20 \div 2 = 10$ pieces, and along the 18-inch side we fit $18 \div 2 = 9$ pieces. The total number of pieces is $10 \times 9 = 90$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
