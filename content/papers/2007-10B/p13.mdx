<Problem>
Two circles of radius $2$ are centered at $(2,0)$ and at $(0,2).$ What is the area of the intersection of the interiors of the two circles?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The two circles have centers $O_1 = (2,0)$ and $O_2 = (0,2)$, each with radius $2$. The distance between centers is $|O_1 O_2| = \sqrt{4 + 4} = 2\sqrt{2}$.

The intersection area of two circles of radius $r$ whose centers are distance $d$ apart equals $2r^2 \cos^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{d}{2r}\right) - \dfrac{d}{2}\sqrt{4r^2 - d^2}$.

With $r = 2$ and $d = 2\sqrt{2}$:
$$\frac{d}{2r} = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{4} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, \quad \cos^{-1}\!\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right) = \frac{\pi}{4}.$$
$$\sqrt{4r^2 - d^2} = \sqrt{16 - 8} = \sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2}.$$

$$\text{Area} = 2(4)\cdot\frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{2}\cdot 2\sqrt{2} = 2\pi - \sqrt{2}\cdot 2\sqrt{2} = 2\pi - 4 = 2(\pi - 2).$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
