<Problem>
Tom's age is $T$ years, which is also the sum of the ages of his three children. His age $N$ years ago was twice the sum of their ages then. What is $T/N?$
</Problem>

<Solution>
Currently, Tom's age is $T$ and the sum of his children's ages is also $T$.

$N$ years ago:
- Tom's age was $T - N$.
- Each child was $N$ years younger, so the sum of the three children's ages was $T - 3N$.

The condition states:
$$T - N = 2(T - 3N) = 2T - 6N.$$

Solving: $T - N = 2T - 6N \implies 5N = T \implies T/N = 5.$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
