<Problem>
Point $P$ is inside equilateral $\triangle ABC.$ Points $Q, R,$ and $S$ are the feet of the perpendiculars from $P$ to $\overline{AB}, \overline{BC},$ and $\overline{CA},$ respectively. Given that $PQ = 1, PR = 2,$ and $PS = 3,$ what is $AB?$
</Problem>

<Solution>
By Viviani's Theorem, for any point inside an equilateral triangle, the sum of the perpendicular distances to the three sides equals the altitude of the triangle.

Let the side length be $s$. The altitude of an equilateral triangle with side $s$ is $\dfrac{s\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

The three perpendicular distances are $PQ = 1$, $PR = 2$, $PS = 3$, summing to $6$. So:
$$\frac{s\sqrt{3}}{2} = 6 \implies s = \frac{12}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{12\sqrt{3}}{3} = 4\sqrt{3}.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
