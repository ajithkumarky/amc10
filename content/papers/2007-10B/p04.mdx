<Problem>
The point $O$ is the center of the circle circumscribed about $\triangle ABC,$ with $\angle BOC = 120^\circ$ and $\angle AOB = 140^\circ.$ What is the degree measure of $\angle ABC?$
</Problem>

<Solution>
The three central angles must sum to $360^\circ$, so:
$$\angle AOC = 360^\circ - 120^\circ - 140^\circ = 100^\circ.$$

By the Inscribed Angle Theorem, an inscribed angle is half the central angle subtending the same arc. The angle $\angle ABC$ is inscribed in the circle and intercepts arc $AC$ (not containing $B$). The central angle for that arc is $\angle AOC = 100^\circ$, so:
$$\angle ABC = \frac{100^\circ}{2} = 50^\circ.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
