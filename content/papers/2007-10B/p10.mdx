<Problem>
Two points $B$ and $C$ are in a plane. Let $S$ be the set of all points $A$ in the plane for which $\triangle ABC$ has area $1.$ Which of the following describes $S?$
</Problem>

<Solution>
The area of $\triangle ABC$ equals $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot |BC| \cdot h$, where $h$ is the perpendicular distance from $A$ to line $BC$. For the area to equal $1$, we need:
$$h = \frac{2}{|BC|}.$$

This is a fixed positive distance. The set of all points at a fixed distance $h$ from the line $BC$ consists of two lines, one on each side of $BC$, both parallel to $BC$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
