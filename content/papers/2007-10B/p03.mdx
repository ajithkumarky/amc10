<Problem>
A college student drove his compact car $120$ miles home for the weekend and averaged $30$ miles per gallon. On the return trip the student drove his parents' SUV and averaged only $20$ miles per gallon. What was the average gas mileage, in miles per gallon, for the round trip?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Average fuel economy for a round trip is total miles divided by total gallons used.

- Trip home: $120$ miles at $30$ mpg uses $\dfrac{120}{30} = 4$ gallons.
- Return trip: $120$ miles at $20$ mpg uses $\dfrac{120}{20} = 6$ gallons.

Total: $240$ miles on $10$ gallons, giving $\dfrac{240}{10} = 24$ miles per gallon.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
