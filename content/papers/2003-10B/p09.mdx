<Problem>
Find the value of $x$ that satisfies the equation
$$25^{-2} = \frac{5^{48/x}}{5^{26/x} \cdot 25^{17/x}}.$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
Rewrite everything as a power of $5$. Since $25 = 5^2$:

Left side: $25^{-2} = 5^{-4}$.

Right side: $25^{17/x} = 5^{34/x}$, so
$$\frac{5^{48/x}}{5^{26/x} \cdot 5^{34/x}} = 5^{48/x - 26/x - 34/x} = 5^{(48-26-34)/x} = 5^{-12/x}.$$

Setting exponents equal:
$$-4 = \frac{-12}{x} \implies x = \frac{-12}{-4} = 3.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
