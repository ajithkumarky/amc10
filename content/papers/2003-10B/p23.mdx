<Problem>
A regular octagon $ABCDEFGH$ has an area of one square unit. What is the area of the rectangle $ABEF$?

The vertices are labeled consecutively: $A$ at top-left, $B$ at top-right, $C$ at right-upper, $D$ at right-lower, $E$ at bottom-right, $F$ at bottom-left, $G$ at left-lower, $H$ at left-upper.
</Problem>

<Solution>
Inscribe the octagon in a unit circle (circumradius $R = 1$). The vertices are equally spaced at $45°$ intervals. Using the labeling given, assign angles so that $A$ is at $112.5°$, $B$ at $67.5°$, $E$ at $292.5°$, and $F$ at $247.5°$.

Each side of the octagon subtends $45°$ at the center, so the side length is $s = 2\sin 22.5°$.

The rectangle $ABEF$ has two pairs of opposite sides:
- $AB = EF = s = 2\sin 22.5°$ (sides of the octagon).
- $AE$: $A$ is at $112.5°$ and $E$ is at $292.5°$, which are exactly $180°$ apart, so $AE$ is a diameter — but checking the rectangle more carefully, the relevant side is $AF$.

$A$ is at $112.5°$ and $F$ is at $247.5°$, separated by $135°$, so $AF = 2\sin(67.5°) = 2\cos 22.5°$.

Area of rectangle $ABEF$ with circumradius $1$:
$$AB \times AF = 2\sin 22.5° \times 2\cos 22.5° = 2\sin 45° = \sqrt{2}.$$

Area of the regular octagon with circumradius $1$ is $2\sqrt{2}$ (using $A = 2R^2\sqrt{2}$).

Therefore the rectangle occupies fraction $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2\sqrt{2}} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ of the octagon's area.

Since the total octagon area is $1$, the area of rectangle $ABEF$ is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
