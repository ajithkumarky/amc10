<Problem>
Which of the following is the same as

$$\frac{2-4+6-8+10-12+14}{3-6+9-12+15-18+21}?$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
Factor $2$ from every term in the numerator and $3$ from every term in the denominator:

$$\frac{2(1-2+3-4+5-6+7)}{3(1-2+3-4+5-6+7)} = \frac{2}{3}.$$

The alternating sum $1-2+3-4+5-6+7 = 4$ appears in both numerator and denominator and cancels.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
