<Problem>
A clock chimes once at $30$ minutes past each hour and chimes on the hour according to the hour. For example, at $1\text{ PM}$ there is one chime and at noon and midnight there are twelve chimes. Starting at $11\text{:}15\text{ AM}$ on February $26, 2003$, on what date will the $2003\text{rd}$ chime occur?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Chimes in a 12-hour period:** The clock chimes $1+2+3+\cdots+12 = 78$ times on the hours, plus $12$ half-hour chimes (one per hour), for a total of $78 + 12 = 90$ chimes per $12$-hour period.

**Chimes in a full day:** $90 \times 2 = 180$ chimes per day.

**Starting point:** $11\text{:}15$ AM on Feb 26. The next chimes are at $11\text{:}30$ AM (1 chime) and $12\text{:}00$ PM (12 chimes), etc.

**Chimes from 11:15 AM onward on Feb 26:**
- $11\text{:}30$ AM: $1$ chime
- $12\text{:}00$ PM: $12$ chimes
- $12\text{:}30$ PM: $1$ chime
- $1\text{:}00$ PM: $1$ chime
- $\vdots$
- $11\text{:}00$ PM: $11$ chimes
- $11\text{:}30$ PM: $1$ chime
- $12\text{:}00$ AM (midnight): $12$ chimes

From $11\text{:}30$ AM to midnight (end of Feb 26), we have a partial $12$-hour block: from $11\text{:}30$ AM to $12\text{:}00$ noon is the $11\text{:}30$ half-chime, then the full $12$-hour PM block, then the midnight chimes.

More cleanly: from $11\text{:}15$ AM, the remaining chimes on Feb 26 (up to and including midnight):
- Remaining in the $12\text{:}00$ AM–$11\text{:}59$ AM block: the $11\text{:}30$ AM chime ($1$) = $1$
- Full $12\text{:}00$ PM–$11\text{:}59$ PM block: $90$ chimes
- Midnight ($12\text{:}00$ AM): $12$ chimes

Actually, let me count from $11\text{:}30$ AM Feb 26 directly. The number of complete days from $11\text{:}30$ AM onward is cleaner.

**From 11:30 AM on Feb 26 (first chime):**

Chimes in each $12$-hour cycle = $90$. The $2003$rd chime from the start happens at chime number $2003$.

Since we start at $11\text{:}30$ AM, the first two chimes at $11\text{:}30$ AM and $12\text{:}00$ PM give $1 + 12 = 13$ chimes. Then each full $24$-hour day gives $180$ chimes.

We want when cumulative chimes reach $2003$.

$2003 \div 180 = 11$ remainder $23$. So after $11$ full days ($11 \times 180 = 1980$ chimes), we need $2003 - 1980 = 23$ more chimes.

$11$ days after $11\text{:}30$ AM Feb $26$ = $11\text{:}30$ AM March $9$.

Now we count $23$ chimes starting from $11\text{:}30$ AM March $9$:
- $11\text{:}30$ AM: $1$ chime (total: $1$)
- $12\text{:}00$ PM: $12$ chimes (total: $13$)
- $12\text{:}30$ PM: $1$ chime (total: $14$)
- $1\text{:}00$ PM: $1$ chime (total: $15$)
- $1\text{:}30$ PM: $1$ chime (total: $16$)
- $2\text{:}00$ PM: $2$ chimes (total: $18$)
- $2\text{:}30$ PM: $1$ chime (total: $19$)
- $3\text{:}00$ PM: $3$ chimes (total: $22$)
- $3\text{:}30$ PM: $1$ chime (total: $23$) ✓

The $2003$rd chime occurs at $3\text{:}30$ PM on **March 9**.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
