<Problem>
Moe uses a mower to cut his rectangular $90$-foot by $150$-foot lawn. The swath he cuts is $28$ inches wide, but he overlaps each cut by $4$ inches to make sure that no grass is missed. He walks at the rate of $5000$ feet per hour while pushing the mower. Which of the following is closest to the number of hours it will take Moe to mow the lawn?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Each pass effectively covers $28 - 4 = 24$ inches $= 2$ feet of new width. Moe mows along the $150$-foot direction, so the number of passes needed is:
$$\frac{90 \text{ ft}}{2 \text{ ft/pass}} = 45 \text{ passes}.$$

Each pass is $150$ feet long, so the total distance walked is:
$$45 \times 150 = 6750 \text{ feet}.$$

At $5000$ feet per hour:
$$\frac{6750}{5000} = 1.35 \text{ hours}.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
