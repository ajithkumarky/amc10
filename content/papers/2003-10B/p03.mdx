<Problem>
The sum of $5$ consecutive even integers is $4$ less than the sum of the first $8$ consecutive odd counting numbers. What is the smallest of the even integers?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The first $8$ consecutive odd counting numbers are $1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15$. Their sum is $8^2 = 64$.

Let the five consecutive even integers be $n, n+2, n+4, n+6, n+8$. Their sum is $5n + 20$.

Setting up the equation:
$$5n + 20 = 64 - 4 = 60$$
$$5n = 40$$
$$n = 8.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
