<Problem>
In rectangle $ABCD$, $AB=5$ and $BC=3$. Points $F$ and $G$ are on $\overline{CD}$ so that $DF=1$ and $GC=2$. Lines $AF$ and $BG$ intersect at $E$. Find the area of $\triangle AEB$.

Place $A=(0,0)$, $B=(5,0)$, $C=(5,3)$, $D=(0,3)$, so $F=(1,3)$ and $G=(3,3)$.
</Problem>

<Solution>
Using coordinates: $A=(0,0)$, $B=(5,0)$, $F=(1,3)$, $G=(3,3)$.

**Line $AF$:** passes through $(0,0)$ and $(1,3)$, so slope $= 3$. Equation: $y = 3x$.

**Line $BG$:** passes through $(5,0)$ and $(3,3)$, so slope $= \dfrac{3-0}{3-5} = -\dfrac{3}{2}$. Equation: $y = -\dfrac{3}{2}(x-5) = -\dfrac{3}{2}x + \dfrac{15}{2}$.

**Intersection $E$:** Set $3x = -\dfrac{3}{2}x + \dfrac{15}{2}$:
$$3x + \frac{3}{2}x = \frac{15}{2} \implies \frac{9}{2}x = \frac{15}{2} \implies x = \frac{5}{3}.$$
$$y = 3 \cdot \frac{5}{3} = 5.$$

So $E = \left(\dfrac{5}{3}, 5\right)$.

**Area of $\triangle AEB$** with $A=(0,0)$, $E=\left(\dfrac{5}{3},5\right)$, $B=(5,0)$:

Base $AB = 5$, height $=$ the $y$-coordinate of $E = 5$ (since $AB$ lies along the $x$-axis).

$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 5 = \frac{25}{2}.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
