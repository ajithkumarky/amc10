<Problem>
A line with slope $3$ intersects a line with slope $5$ at point $(10, 15)$. What is the distance between the $x$-intercepts of these two lines?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Line 1** (slope $3$ through $(10,15)$): $y - 15 = 3(x - 10)$, so $y = 3x - 15$. Setting $y = 0$: $x = 5$.

**Line 2** (slope $5$ through $(10,15)$): $y - 15 = 5(x - 10)$, so $y = 5x - 35$. Setting $y = 0$: $x = 7$.

Distance between $x$-intercepts $= |7 - 5| = 2$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
