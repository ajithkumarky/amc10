<Problem>
Al gets the disease algebritis and must take one green pill and one pink pill each day for two weeks. A green pill costs $\textdollar 1$ more than a pink pill, and Al's pills cost a total of $\textdollar 546$ for the two weeks. How much does one green pill cost?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Two weeks is $14$ days. Each day Al takes one green pill and one pink pill, so over $14$ days he takes $14$ green pills and $14$ pink pills.

Let the cost of one pink pill be $p$ dollars. Then a green pill costs $p + 1$ dollars, and the total cost is:
$$14(p+1) + 14p = 546$$
$$14p + 14 + 14p = 546$$
$$28p = 532$$
$$p = 19.$$

One green pill costs $19 + 1 = \textdollar 20$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
