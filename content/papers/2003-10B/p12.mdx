<Problem>
Al, Betty, and Clare split $\textdollar 1000$ among them to be invested in different ways. Each begins with a different amount. At the end of one year, they have a total of $\textdollar 1500$. Betty and Clare have both doubled their money, whereas Al has managed to lose $\textdollar 100$. What was Al's original portion?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let Al's original amount be $a$, Betty's be $b$, and Clare's be $c$. We know:
$$a + b + c = 1000.$$

After one year: Al has $a - 100$, Betty has $2b$, Clare has $2c$, and the total is $1500$:
$$(a - 100) + 2b + 2c = 1500.$$

Substituting $b + c = 1000 - a$:
$$(a - 100) + 2(1000 - a) = 1500$$
$$a - 100 + 2000 - 2a = 1500$$
$$-a + 1900 = 1500$$
$$a = 400.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
