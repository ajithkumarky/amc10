<Problem>
Three circles with radius 2 are mutually tangent. What is the total area of the circles and the region bounded by them?

The three circles each have radius 2 and are arranged so that each pair is externally tangent. The region bounded by them is the curved triangle enclosed between the three circles.
</Problem>

<Solution>
The centers of the three mutually tangent circles form an equilateral triangle with side length $2 + 2 = 4$ (since each pair of circles is externally tangent, the distance between centers equals the sum of radii).

**Area of the equilateral triangle** with side 4:
$$A_{\triangle} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \cdot 4^2 = 4\sqrt{3}.$$

**Area of the three circular sectors** inside the triangle: each angle of the equilateral triangle is $60°$, so each sector has central angle $60° = \frac{\pi}{3}$ radians and radius 2.
$$3 \times \frac{1}{6}\pi r^2 = 3 \times \frac{1}{6}\pi(4) = 2\pi.$$

**Area of the curved triangular region** between the circles:
$$A_{\text{gap}} = A_{\triangle} - 3 \times \text{(sector)} = 4\sqrt{3} - 2\pi.$$

**Total area** = area of 3 full circles + area of gap:
$$3\pi r^2 + (4\sqrt{3} - 2\pi) = 12\pi + 4\sqrt{3} - 2\pi = 10\pi + 4\sqrt{3}.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
