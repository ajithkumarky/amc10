<Problem>
The point in the $xy$-plane with coordinates $(1000, 2012)$ is reflected across the line $y = 2000$. What are the coordinates of the reflected point?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Reflecting across a horizontal line $y = k$ leaves the $x$-coordinate unchanged and maps $y$ to $2k - y$.

Here $k = 2000$ and $y = 2012$, so the reflected $y$-coordinate is $2(2000) - 2012 = 4000 - 2012 = 1988$.

The reflected point is $(1000, 1988)$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
