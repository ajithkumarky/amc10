<Problem>
In rectangle $ABCD$, $AB = 6$, $AD = 30$, and $G$ is the midpoint of $\overline{AD}$. Segment $AB$ is extended 2 units beyond $B$ to point $E$, and $F$ is the intersection of $\overline{ED}$ and $\overline{BC}$. What is the area of $BFDG$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Set up coordinates: $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (6, 0)$, $C = (6, 30)$, $D = (0, 30)$.

$G$ is the midpoint of $\overline{AD}$: $G = (0, 15)$.

$E$ is 2 units beyond $B$ from $A$: $E = (8, 0)$.

**Find $F$, the intersection of $\overline{ED}$ and $\overline{BC}$:**

Line $ED$: from $E = (8, 0)$ to $D = (0, 30)$.

Direction: $(-8, 30)$. Parametric: $(8 - 8t, 30t)$.

$\overline{BC}$ is the segment $x = 6$, $0 \leq y \leq 30$.

Setting $8 - 8t = 6$: $t = \frac{1}{4}$, giving $y = 30 \cdot \frac{1}{4} = \frac{15}{2}$.

So $F = \left(6, \frac{15}{2}\right)$.

**Area of quadrilateral $BFDG$** with vertices $B = (6, 0)$, $F = (6, \frac{15}{2})$, $D = (0, 30)$, $G = (0, 15)$:

Using the shoelace formula:
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} |x_B(y_F - y_G) + x_F(y_D - y_B) + x_D(y_G - y_F) + x_G(y_B - y_D)|$$
$$= \frac{1}{2} \left|6\!\left(\frac{15}{2} - 15\right) + 6(30 - 0) + 0\!\left(15 - \frac{15}{2}\right) + 0(0 - 30)\right|$$
$$= \frac{1}{2} \left|6 \cdot \left(-\frac{15}{2}\right) + 6 \cdot 30\right|$$
$$= \frac{1}{2} \left|-45 + 180\right| = \frac{135}{2}.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
