<Problem>
Jesse cuts a circular paper disk of radius 12 along two radii to form two sectors, the smaller having a central angle of 120 degrees. He makes two circular cones, using each sector to form the lateral surface of a cone. What is the ratio of the volume of the smaller cone to that of the larger?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Smaller cone** (from the 120° sector): The sector's arc length becomes the circumference of the cone's base, and the sector's radius (12) becomes the slant height.

Arc length of 120° sector: $\frac{120}{360} \cdot 2\pi(12) = 8\pi$.

Base circumference $= 8\pi$, so base radius $r_1 = 4$.

Slant height $\ell = 12$, so height $h_1 = \sqrt{144 - 16} = \sqrt{128} = 8\sqrt{2}$.

**Larger cone** (from the 240° sector): Arc length $= \frac{240}{360} \cdot 2\pi(12) = 16\pi$.

Base radius $r_2 = 8$, height $h_2 = \sqrt{144 - 64} = \sqrt{80} = 4\sqrt{5}$.

**Ratio of volumes:**
$$\frac{V_1}{V_2} = \frac{\frac{1}{3}\pi r_1^2 h_1}{\frac{1}{3}\pi r_2^2 h_2} = \frac{r_1^2 h_1}{r_2^2 h_2} = \frac{16 \cdot 8\sqrt{2}}{64 \cdot 4\sqrt{5}} = \frac{128\sqrt{2}}{256\sqrt{5}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2\sqrt{5}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2\sqrt{5}} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{5}}{\sqrt{5}} = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
