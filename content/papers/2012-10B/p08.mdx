<Problem>
What is the sum of all integer solutions to $1 < (x-2)^2 < 25$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The inequality $1 < (x-2)^2 < 25$ means $(x-2)^2 > 1$ and $(x-2)^2 < 25$.

From $(x-2)^2 < 25$: $|x - 2| < 5$, so $-3 < x < 7$.

From $(x-2)^2 > 1$: $|x - 2| > 1$, so $x < 1$ or $x > 3$.

Combining: integer solutions in $(-3, 7)$ with $x < 1$ or $x > 3$ are:
- $x \in \{-2, -1, 0\}$ (from $-3 < x < 1$, excluding $-3$ and $1$)
- $x \in \{4, 5, 6\}$ (from $3 < x < 7$, excluding $3$ and $7$)

Sum $= (-2) + (-1) + 0 + 4 + 5 + 6 = 12$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
