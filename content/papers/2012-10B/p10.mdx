<Problem>
How many ordered pairs of positive integers $(M, N)$ satisfy the equation $\dfrac{M}{6} = \dfrac{6}{N}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The equation gives $MN = 36$. We need ordered pairs of positive integers $(M, N)$ with $MN = 36$.

The number of such pairs equals the number of positive divisors of 36. Since $36 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2$, it has $(2+1)(2+1) = 9$ divisors.

Each divisor $d$ of 36 gives the pair $(M, N) = (d, 36/d)$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
