<Problem>
The figure shown is called a trefoil and is constructed by drawing circular sectors about sides of congruent equilateral triangles. What is the area of a trefoil whose horizontal base has length $2$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The trefoil with horizontal base $2$ is built from two congruent equilateral triangles sharing a side, with four circular sectors (each of radius $1$ and central angle $60°$) drawn around the triangles' sides.

The horizontal base $= 2$ means each equilateral triangle has side length $1$. The trefoil consists of four $60°$ sectors, each of radius $1$:

$$\text{Area} = 4 \times \frac{60°}{360°} \times \pi(1)^2 = 4 \times \frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{2\pi}{3}.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
