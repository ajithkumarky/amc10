<Problem>
How many positive cubes divide $3! \cdot 5! \cdot 7!$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First find the prime factorization of $3! \cdot 5! \cdot 7!$:
- $3! = 2 \cdot 3$
- $5! = 2^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$
- $7! = 2^4 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5 \cdot 7$

Product: $2^{1+3+4} \cdot 3^{1+1+2} \cdot 5^{1+1} \cdot 7^1 = 2^8 \cdot 3^4 \cdot 5^2 \cdot 7^1$.

A perfect cube $d$ divides this product if and only if $d = 2^a \cdot 3^b \cdot 5^c \cdot 7^e$ where $a$, $b$, $c$, $e$ are multiples of $3$ with $a \leq 8$, $b \leq 4$, $c \leq 2$, $e \leq 1$.

- $a \in \{0, 3, 6\}$: 3 choices
- $b \in \{0, 3\}$: 2 choices
- $c \in \{0\}$: 1 choice ($c \leq 2$ and $c$ must be a multiple of $3$, so $c=0$ only)
- $e \in \{0\}$: 1 choice

Total: $3 \times 2 \times 1 \times 1 = 6$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
