<Problem>
Josh and Mike live $13$ miles apart. Yesterday Josh started to ride his bicycle toward Mike's house. A little later Mike started to ride his bicycle toward Josh's house. When they met, Josh had ridden for twice the length of time as Mike and at four-fifths of Mike's rate. How many miles had Mike ridden when they met?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let Mike's speed be $v$ and his riding time be $t$. Then Josh's speed is $\tfrac{4}{5}v$ and his riding time is $2t$.

Distance covered: Mike rides $vt$ miles, Josh rides $\tfrac{4}{5}v \cdot 2t = \tfrac{8}{5}vt$ miles. Together they cover the full 13 miles:
$$vt + \frac{8}{5}vt = 13 \implies \frac{13}{5}vt = 13 \implies vt = 5.$$

Mike rode $vt = 5$ miles.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
