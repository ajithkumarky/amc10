<Problem>
How many positive integers $n$ satisfy the following condition:
$$\left(130n\right)^{50} > n^{100} > 2^{200} \text{ ?}$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
Take the $50$th root throughout (all quantities are positive):
$$130n > n^2 > 2^4 = 16.$$

**Right inequality:** $n^2 > 16 \Rightarrow n > 4$, so $n \geq 5$.

**Left inequality:** $130n > n^2 \Rightarrow 130 > n$, so $n \leq 129$.

Together: $5 \leq n \leq 129$, giving $129 - 5 + 1 = 125$ positive integers.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
