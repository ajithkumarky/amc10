<Problem>
In $\triangle ABC$ we have $AB = 25$, $BC = 39$, and $AC = 42$. Points $D$ and $E$ are on $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{AC}$ respectively, with $AD = 19$ and $AE = 14$. What is the ratio of the area of triangle $ADE$ to the area of the quadrilateral $BCED$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Triangles $ADE$ and $ABC$ share angle $A$, so their areas are in the ratio:

$$\frac{[ADE]}{[ABC]} = \frac{AD \cdot AE}{AB \cdot AC} = \frac{19 \cdot 14}{25 \cdot 42} = \frac{266}{1050} = \frac{19}{75}.$$

The quadrilateral $BCED$ has area $[ABC] - [ADE]$, so:

$$\frac{[ADE]}{[BCED]} = \frac{[ADE]}{[ABC] - [ADE]} = \frac{\frac{19}{75}[ABC]}{[ABC] - \frac{19}{75}[ABC]} = \frac{19/75}{1 - 19/75} = \frac{19/75}{56/75} = \frac{19}{56}.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
