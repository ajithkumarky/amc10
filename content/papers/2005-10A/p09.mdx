<Problem>
Three tiles are marked $X$ and two other tiles are marked $O$. The five tiles are randomly arranged in a row. What is the probability that the arrangement reads $XOXOX$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The total number of ways to arrange 5 tiles (3 identical $X$'s and 2 identical $O$'s) is $\dbinom{5}{2} = 10$ (choosing which 2 positions get $O$).

Only one arrangement is $XOXOX$, so the probability is $\dfrac{1}{10}$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
