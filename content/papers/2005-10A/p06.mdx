<Problem>
The average (mean) of $20$ numbers is $30$, and the average of $30$ other numbers is $20$. What is the average of all $50$ numbers?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The sum of the first group is $20 \times 30 = 600$. The sum of the second group is $30 \times 20 = 600$. The combined sum is $1200$, and there are $50$ numbers total.

$$\text{Average} = \frac{1200}{50} = 24.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
