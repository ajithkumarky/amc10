<Problem>
A store normally sells windows at $\textdollar 100$ each. This week the store is offering one free window for each purchase of four. Dave needs seven windows and Doug needs eight windows. How many dollars will they save if they purchase the windows together rather than separately?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The deal gives 1 free window per 4 purchased, meaning every group of 5 costs 4 windows' price.

**Separately:** Dave buys 7 windows: with 4 he gets 1 free (pays for 4, gets 5), then buys 2 more. He pays for $4 + 2 = 6$ windows = $\textdollar 600$. Doug buys 8 windows: buys 4, gets 1 free (pays 4, gets 5), then needs 3 more. He pays for $4 + 3 = 7$ windows = $\textdollar 700$. Total separately: $\textdollar 1300$.

**Together:** Dave and Doug together need $7 + 8 = 15$ windows. With 12 purchased they receive 3 free (three groups of 4), needing to buy $15 - 3 = 12$ windows. Cost: $\textdollar 1200$.

Savings: $1300 - 1200 = \textdollar 100$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
