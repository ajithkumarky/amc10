<Problem>
The equations $2x + 7 = 3$ and $bx - 10 = -2$ have the same solution $x$. What is the value of $b$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
From $2x + 7 = 3$ we get $2x = -4$, so $x = -2$.

Substituting into $bx - 10 = -2$: $b(-2) - 10 = -2 \Rightarrow -2b = 8 \Rightarrow b = -4$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
