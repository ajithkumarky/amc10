<Problem>
Let $S$ be the set of the $2005$ smallest positive multiples of $4$, and let $T$ be the set of the $2005$ smallest positive multiples of $6$. How many elements are common to $S$ and $T$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
$S = \{4, 8, 12, \ldots, 4\cdot 2005\} = \{4, 8, \ldots, 8020\}$ and $T = \{6, 12, 18, \ldots, 6\cdot 2005\} = \{6, 12, \ldots, 12030\}$.

An integer is in both sets if and only if it is a multiple of $\text{lcm}(4,6) = 12$ that is also at most $\min(8020, 12030) = 8020$ (since it must be in $S$, the smaller-topped set).

The common elements are the positive multiples of $12$ up to $8020$:
$$\left\lfloor \frac{8020}{12} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor 668.33\ldots \right\rfloor = 668.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
