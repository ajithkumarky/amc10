<Problem>
An equiangular octagon has four sides of length $1$ and four sides of length $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$, arranged so that no two consecutive sides have the same length. What is the area of the octagon?
</Problem>

<Solution>
An equiangular octagon has all interior angles equal to $135°$, so consecutive sides meet at $45°$ turns. The sides alternate between length $1$ and $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$, and the eight directions (in $45°$ steps) are $0°, 45°, 90°, 135°, 180°, 225°, 270°, 315°$.

Assign the length-$1$ sides to the $0°$, $90°$, $180°$, $270°$ directions and the $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ sides to the $45°$, $135°$, $225°$, $315°$ directions. Each diagonal side contributes $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \cdot \cos 45° = \dfrac{1}{2}$ in each coordinate direction. Tracing vertices from $(0,0)$:

$$V_0=(0,0),\quad V_1=(1,0),\quad V_2=\!\left(\tfrac{3}{2},\tfrac{1}{2}\right),\quad V_3=\!\left(\tfrac{3}{2},\tfrac{3}{2}\right),\quad V_4=\!\left(1,2\right),$$
$$V_5=(0,2),\quad V_6=\!\left(-\tfrac{1}{2},\tfrac{3}{2}\right),\quad V_7=\!\left(-\tfrac{1}{2},\tfrac{1}{2}\right).$$

Applying the Shoelace formula $\text{Area} = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl|\sum_i x_i(y_{i+1}-y_{i-1})\bigr|$:

$$= \tfrac{1}{2}\!\left|0 + 1\!\cdot\!\tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{3}{2}\!\cdot\!\tfrac{3}{2} + \tfrac{3}{2}\!\cdot\!\tfrac{3}{2} + 1\!\cdot\!\tfrac{1}{2} + 0 + \left(-\tfrac{1}{2}\right)\!\cdot\!\left(-\tfrac{3}{2}\right) + \left(-\tfrac{1}{2}\right)\!\cdot\!\left(-\tfrac{3}{2}\right)\right|$$
$$= \tfrac{1}{2}\!\left(\tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{9}{4} + \tfrac{9}{4} + \tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{3}{4} + \tfrac{3}{4}\right) = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot 7 = \tfrac{7}{2}.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
