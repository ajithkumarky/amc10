<Problem>
While eating out, Mike and Joe each tipped their server $\textdollar 2$. Mike tipped $10\%$ of his bill and Joe tipped $20\%$ of his bill. What was the difference, in dollars, between their bills?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Mike's bill satisfies $0.10 \times M = 2$, so $M = 20$. Joe's bill satisfies $0.20 \times J = 2$, so $J = 10$. The difference is $20 - 10 = 10$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
