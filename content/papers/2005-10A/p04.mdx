<Problem>
A rectangle with a diagonal of length $x$ is twice as long as it is wide. What is the area of the rectangle?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the width be $w$ and the length be $2w$. By the Pythagorean theorem on the diagonal:
$$w^2 + (2w)^2 = x^2 \implies 5w^2 = x^2 \implies w^2 = \frac{x^2}{5}.$$

The area is $w \cdot 2w = 2w^2 = \dfrac{2}{5}x^2$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
