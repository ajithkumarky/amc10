<Problem>
A wooden cube $n$ units on a side is painted red on all six faces and then cut into $n^3$ unit cubes. Exactly one-fourth of the total number of faces of the unit cubes are red. What is $n$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The $n^3$ unit cubes have $6n^3$ faces in total. The number of red faces equals the number of painted faces on the big cube, which is $6n^2$ (six faces each of area $n^2$).

Setting one-fourth of all faces equal to the red faces:
$$\frac{1}{4}(6n^3) = 6n^2 \implies \frac{6n^3}{4} = 6n^2 \implies n = 4.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
