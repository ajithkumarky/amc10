<Problem>
Farmer Pythagoras has a field in the shape of a right triangle. The right triangle's legs have lengths $3$ and $4$ units. In the corner where those sides meet at a right angle, he leaves a small unplanted square $S$ so that from the air it looks like the right angle symbol. The rest of the field is planted. The shortest distance from $S$ to the hypotenuse is $2$ units. What fraction of the field is planted?

<Solution>
Place the right angle at the origin, with legs along the positive $x$-axis (length $4$) and positive $y$-axis (length $3$). So the vertices are $C = (0,0)$, $B = (4, 0)$, and $A = (0, 3)$.

**The hypotenuse line $AB$:**

The line through $A = (0,3)$ and $B = (4,0)$: $\frac{x}{4} + \frac{y}{3} = 1$, or $3x + 4y = 12$.

**Finding the side length $s$ of square $S$:**

The square $S$ has one corner at the origin, sides along the positive $x$- and $y$-axes, and the opposite corner at $(s, s)$.

The shortest distance from $S$ to the hypotenuse equals the distance from the corner $(s, s)$ to the line $3x + 4y = 12$:
$$\text{distance} = \frac{|3s + 4s - 12|}{5} = \frac{|7s - 12|}{5} = \frac{12 - 7s}{5}$$
(since $s$ is small, $7s < 12$).

Set this equal to $2$:
$$\frac{12 - 7s}{5} = 2 \implies 12 - 7s = 10 \implies s = \frac{2}{7}.$$

**Areas:**

- Total field area: $\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3 \cdot 4 = 6$.
- Area of square $S$: $s^2 = \left(\frac{2}{7}\right)^2 = \frac{4}{49}$.
- Planted area: $6 - \frac{4}{49} = \frac{294 - 4}{49} = \frac{290}{49}$.
- Fraction planted: $\dfrac{290/49}{6} = \dfrac{290}{294} = \dfrac{145}{147}$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
