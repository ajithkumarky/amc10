<Problem>
A unit of blood expires after $10! = 10 \cdot 9 \cdot 8 \cdots 1$ seconds. Yasin donates a unit of blood at noon of January 1. On what day does his unit of blood expire?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First compute $10!$:
$$10! = 3{,}628{,}800 \text{ seconds}$$

Convert to days. There are $60 \times 60 \times 24 = 86{,}400$ seconds per day.

$$\frac{3{,}628{,}800}{86{,}400} = 42 \text{ days}$$

Starting from noon on January 1 and adding exactly 42 days brings us to noon on February 12 (January has 31 days, so $31 - 1 = 30$ days remain in January after January 1, and we need $42 - 30 = 12$ more days into February).

The blood expires on February 12.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
