<Problem>
What is the greatest integer less than or equal to $$\frac{3^{100}+2^{100}}{3^{96}+2^{96}}?$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
Divide numerator and denominator by $3^{96}$:

$$\frac{3^{100}+2^{100}}{3^{96}+2^{96}} = \frac{3^4 + 2^{100}/3^{96}}{1 + 2^{96}/3^{96}} = \frac{81 + (2/3)^{96} \cdot 2^4}{1 + (2/3)^{96}}.$$

Let $t = \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{96}$. Since $\frac{2}{3} < 1$, we have $0 < t \ll 1$ (extremely small).

$$\frac{81 + 16t}{1 + t}.$$

Since $t$ is positive and tiny:
$$\frac{81 + 16t}{1 + t} = 81 \cdot \frac{1 + \frac{16t}{81}}{1 + t}.$$

Since $\frac{16}{81} < 1$, we have $\frac{16t}{81} < t$, so the numerator grows slower than the denominator as $t$ increases, meaning the fraction is $< 81$.

More precisely:
$$\frac{81 + 16t}{1 + t} = 81 - \frac{65t}{1 + t}.$$

Since $t > 0$, the expression is strictly less than $81$ but very close to $81$.

Therefore $\left\lfloor \frac{81 + 16t}{1 + t} \right\rfloor = 80$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
