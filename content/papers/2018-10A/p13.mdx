<Problem>
A paper triangle with sides of lengths $3$, $4$, and $5$ inches is folded so that point $A$ falls on point $B$. What is the length in inches of the crease?

The triangle has $A$ at the lower-left (the right angle is at $C$, lower-right), $B$ at the upper-right, $C$ at the lower-right, with $BC = 3$, $AC = 4$, and $AB = 5$.

<Solution>
When we fold $A$ onto $B$, the crease is the perpendicular bisector of segment $\overline{AB}$ restricted to the triangle.

**Setup coordinates:** Place $C = (0, 0)$, $A = (-4, 0)$, $B = (0, 3)$.

**Midpoint of $AB$:** $M = \left(-2, \frac{3}{2}\right)$.

**Slope of $AB$:** $\frac{3 - 0}{0 - (-4)} = \frac{3}{4}$.

**Slope of crease (perpendicular to $AB$):** $-\frac{4}{3}$.

**Equation of crease:** Through $M = \left(-2, \frac{3}{2}\right)$ with slope $-\frac{4}{3}$:
$$y - \frac{3}{2} = -\frac{4}{3}(x + 2) \implies y = -\frac{4}{3}x - \frac{8}{3} + \frac{3}{2} = -\frac{4}{3}x - \frac{7}{6}.$$

**Find where the crease intersects the triangle's sides.**

The crease must intersect two sides of $\triangle ABC$.

*Intersection with $\overline{AC}$ (the segment from $A = (-4, 0)$ to $C = (0, 0)$, along $y = 0$):*
$$0 = -\frac{4}{3}x - \frac{7}{6} \implies x = -\frac{7}{8}.$$
Point $P = \left(-\frac{7}{8}, 0\right)$. Check: $-4 \leq -\frac{7}{8} \leq 0$. $\checkmark$

*Intersection with $\overline{BC}$ (from $B = (0, 3)$ to $C = (0, 0)$, along $x = 0$):*
$$y = -\frac{4}{3}(0) - \frac{7}{6} = -\frac{7}{6} < 0.$$
Not on $\overline{BC}$ (which has $y \geq 0$). 

*Intersection with $\overline{AB}$ (from $A = (-4, 0)$ to $B = (0, 3)$):*
Line $AB$: $y = \frac{3}{4}(x + 4)$. Set equal to crease:
$$\frac{3}{4}(x+4) = -\frac{4}{3}x - \frac{7}{6}.$$
$$\frac{3x}{4} + 3 = -\frac{4x}{3} - \frac{7}{6}.$$
Multiply through by $12$:
$$9x + 36 = -16x - 14 \implies 25x = -50 \implies x = -2.$$
$$y = \frac{3}{4}(-2 + 4) = \frac{3}{2}.$$
Point $Q = \left(-2, \frac{3}{2}\right) = M$. This is the midpoint of $AB$, which makes sense.

So the crease runs from $P = \left(-\frac{7}{8}, 0\right)$ on $\overline{AC}$ to $Q = \left(-2, \frac{3}{2}\right)$ on $\overline{AB}$.

**Length of crease $PQ$:**
$$PQ = \sqrt{\left(-2 - \left(-\frac{7}{8}\right)\right)^2 + \left(\frac{3}{2} - 0\right)^2} = \sqrt{\left(-\frac{9}{8}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^2}$$
$$= \sqrt{\frac{81}{64} + \frac{9}{4}} = \sqrt{\frac{81}{64} + \frac{144}{64}} = \sqrt{\frac{225}{64}} = \frac{15}{8}.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
