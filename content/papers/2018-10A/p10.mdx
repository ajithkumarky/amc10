<Problem>
Suppose that real number $x$ satisfies $$\sqrt{49-x^2}-\sqrt{25-x^2}=3.$$ What is the value of $\sqrt{49-x^2}+\sqrt{25-x^2}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $u = \sqrt{49 - x^2}$ and $v = \sqrt{25 - x^2}$.

We're told $u - v = 3$.

Notice that:
$$u^2 - v^2 = (49 - x^2) - (25 - x^2) = 24.$$

But also $u^2 - v^2 = (u-v)(u+v) = 3(u+v)$.

Therefore:
$$3(u + v) = 24 \implies u + v = 8.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
