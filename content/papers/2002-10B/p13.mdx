<Problem>
Find the value(s) of $x$ such that $8xy - 12y + 2x - 3 = 0$ is true for all values of $y$.
</Problem>

<Solution>
Rearrange by grouping terms with $y$ and terms without $y$:

$$8xy - 12y + 2x - 3 = y(8x - 12) + (2x - 3) = 0.$$

For this to hold for **all** values of $y$, the coefficient of $y$ must be zero and the constant term must also be zero:

$$8x - 12 = 0 \quad \text{and} \quad 2x - 3 = 0.$$

Both give $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$, which is consistent.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
