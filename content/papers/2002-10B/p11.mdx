<Problem>
The product of three consecutive positive integers is $8$ times their sum. What is the sum of their squares?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the three consecutive integers be $n-1$, $n$, $n+1$.

Their product: $(n-1)n(n+1) = n(n^2-1) = n^3 - n$.

Their sum: $(n-1)+n+(n+1) = 3n$.

Setting product equal to $8$ times the sum:
$$n^3 - n = 8 \cdot 3n = 24n.$$
$$n^3 = 25n \implies n^2 = 25 \implies n = 5 \quad (\text{since } n > 0).$$

The three integers are $4$, $5$, $6$.

Sum of squares: $4^2 + 5^2 + 6^2 = 16 + 25 + 36 = 77$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
