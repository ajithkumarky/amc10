<Problem>
Suppose July of year $N$ has five Mondays. Which of the following must occur five times in the August of year $N$? (Note: Both months have $31$ days.)
</Problem>

<Solution>
July has $31 = 4 \times 7 + 3$ days, so it has five occurrences of exactly three consecutive days of the week. For July to have five Mondays, Monday must be among those three days.

July's $31$ days fill exactly $4$ complete weeks plus $3$ extra days. The extra days are the first three days of the week that July starts on. For five Mondays to occur, Monday must fall in those first three days of July — meaning July starts on Saturday, Sunday, or Monday.

August starts $31$ days after July starts. Since $31 = 4 \times 7 + 3$, August starts exactly $3$ days of the week after July.

- If July starts on Saturday, August starts on Tuesday. August's extra three days are Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday — giving five Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays.
- If July starts on Sunday, August starts on Wednesday. August's extra three days are Wednesday, Thursday, Friday — giving five Wednesdays, Thursdays, and Fridays.
- If July starts on Monday, August starts on Thursday. August's extra three days are Thursday, Friday, Saturday — giving five Thursdays, Fridays, and Saturdays.

In all three cases, **Thursday** appears among the five-occurrence days.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
