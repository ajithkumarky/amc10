<Problem>
The ratio $\dfrac{2^{2001} \cdot 3^{2003}}{6^{2002}}$ is:
</Problem>

<Solution>
Write $6^{2002} = 2^{2002} \cdot 3^{2002}$, so:

$$\frac{2^{2001} \cdot 3^{2003}}{6^{2002}} = \frac{2^{2001} \cdot 3^{2003}}{2^{2002} \cdot 3^{2002}} = 2^{2001-2002} \cdot 3^{2003-2002} = 2^{-1} \cdot 3^{1} = \frac{3}{2}.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
