<Problem>
For the nonzero numbers $a$, $b$, and $c$, define

$$(a,b,c)=\frac{abc}{a+b+c}.$$

Find $(2,4,6)$.
</Problem>

<Solution>
Apply the definition directly with $a=2$, $b=4$, $c=6$:

$$(2,4,6) = \frac{2 \cdot 4 \cdot 6}{2+4+6} = \frac{48}{12} = 4.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
