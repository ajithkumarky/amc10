<Problem>
Suppose that $a$ and $b$ are nonzero real numbers, and that the equation $x^2+ax+b=0$ has solutions $a$ and $b$. What is the pair $(a,b)$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
By Vieta's formulas, since $a$ and $b$ are the two roots of $x^2 + ax + b = 0$:
$$a + b = -a \quad \text{and} \quad ab = b.$$

From $ab = b$ and $b \neq 0$: divide both sides by $b$ to get $a = 1$.

From $a + b = -a$: $1 + b = -1$, so $b = -2$.

Check: $x^2 + x - 2 = (x+2)(x-1) = 0$ has roots $x = 1 = a$ and $x = -2 = b$. ✓

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
