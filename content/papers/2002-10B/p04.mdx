<Problem>
What is the value of

$$(3x-2)(4x+1)-(3x-2)4x+1$$

when $x=4$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The expression is $(3x-2)(4x+1) - (3x-2)(4x) + 1$. Factor out $(3x-2)$:

$$(3x-2)\bigl[(4x+1) - 4x\bigr] + 1 = (3x-2)(1) + 1 = 3x - 2 + 1 = 3x - 1.$$

At $x = 4$: $3(4) - 1 = 12 - 1 = 11$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
