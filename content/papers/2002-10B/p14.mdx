<Problem>
The number $25^{64} \cdot 64^{25}$ is the square of a positive integer $N$. In decimal representation, the sum of the digits of $N$ is
</Problem>

<Solution>
Write $25 = 5^2$ and $64 = 2^6$:

$$25^{64} \cdot 64^{25} = 5^{128} \cdot 2^{150}.$$

Since this equals $N^2$:

$$N^2 = 2^{150} \cdot 5^{128} = 2^{22} \cdot (2 \cdot 5)^{128} \cdot 5^0 \cdot \ldots$$

More precisely: $N^2 = 2^{150} \cdot 5^{128} = 2^{22} \cdot (10)^{128}$.

So $N = 2^{11} \cdot 10^{64} = 2048 \cdot 10^{64}$.

In decimal, $N = 2048\underbrace{00\cdots0}_{64}$.

The sum of digits of $N$ is $2 + 0 + 4 + 8 = 14$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
