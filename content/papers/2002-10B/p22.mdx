<Problem>
Let $\triangle XOY$ be a right-angled triangle with $\angle XOY = 90°$. Let $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of the legs $OX$ and $OY$, respectively. Given $XN = 19$ and $YM = 22$, find $XY$.
</Problem>

<Solution>
Place $O$ at the origin, $X = (a, 0)$, $Y = (0, b)$.

Then $M$ is the midpoint of $OX$, so $M = (a/2, 0)$, and $N$ is the midpoint of $OY$, so $N = (0, b/2)$.

Using the given lengths:
$$XN^2 = a^2 + \frac{b^2}{4} = 19^2 = 361.$$
$$YM^2 = \frac{a^2}{4} + b^2 = 22^2 = 484.$$

Add these two equations:
$$\frac{5a^2}{4} + \frac{5b^2}{4} = 845 \implies a^2 + b^2 = \frac{845 \times 4}{5} = 676.$$

Therefore $XY = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} = \sqrt{676} = 26$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
