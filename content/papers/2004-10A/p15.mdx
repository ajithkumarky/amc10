<Problem>
Given that $-4 \leq x \leq -2$ and $2 \leq y \leq 4$, what is the largest possible value of $\dfrac{x+y}{x}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Rewrite: $\dfrac{x+y}{x} = 1 + \dfrac{y}{x}$.

To maximize this, we need to maximize $\dfrac{y}{x}$. Since $x < 0$, a larger ratio $y/x$ (less negative) comes from making $y/x$ as close to $0$ as possible, i.e., minimizing $|y/x| = |y|/|x|$. This means minimizing $y$ and maximizing $|x|$ (making $x$ most negative).

Take $y = 2$ (smallest) and $x = -4$ (most negative, largest $|x|$):
$$\frac{x+y}{x} = \frac{-4+2}{-4} = \frac{-2}{-4} = \frac{1}{2}.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
