<Problem>
Alicia earns $20$ dollars per hour, of which $1.45\%$ is deducted to pay local taxes. How many cents per hour of Alicia's wages are used to pay local taxes?
</Problem>

<Solution>
$1.45\%$ of $20$ dollars is $0.0145 \times 20 = 0.29$ dollars per hour. Converting to cents: $0.29 \times 100 = 29$ cents per hour.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
