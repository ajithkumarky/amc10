<Problem>
At a party, each man danced with exactly three women and each woman danced with exactly two men. Twelve men attended the party. How many women attended the party?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Count the total number of man-woman dance pairs in two ways.

From the men's side: $12$ men each danced with $3$ women, giving $12 \times 3 = 36$ pairs.

From the women's side: if $w$ women attended, each danced with $2$ men, giving $2w$ pairs.

Setting these equal: $2w = 36$, so $w = 18$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
