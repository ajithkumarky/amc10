<Problem>
Henry's Hamburger Heaven offers its hamburgers with the following condiments: ketchup, mustard, mayonnaise, tomato, lettuce, pickles, cheese, and onions. A customer can choose one, two, or three meat patties, and any collection of condiments. How many different kinds of hamburgers can be ordered?
</Problem>

<Solution>
There are $3$ choices for the number of patties (one, two, or three).

There are $8$ condiments, and any subset is allowed (including none), giving $2^8 = 256$ condiment combinations.

By the multiplication principle, the total number of different hamburgers is $3 \times 256 = 768$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
