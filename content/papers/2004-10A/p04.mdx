<Problem>
What is the value of $x$ if $|x-1| = |x-2|$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The equation $|x-1| = |x-2|$ means $x$ is equidistant from $1$ and $2$ on the number line, so $x$ is the midpoint: $x = \dfrac{1+2}{2} = \dfrac{3}{2}$.

We can verify algebraically: squaring both sides gives $(x-1)^2 = (x-2)^2$, so $x^2 - 2x + 1 = x^2 - 4x + 4$, giving $2x = 3$, thus $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
