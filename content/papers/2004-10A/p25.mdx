<Problem>
Three mutually tangent spheres of radius $1$ rest on a horizontal plane. A sphere of radius $2$ rests on them. What is the distance from the plane to the top of the larger sphere?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Each small sphere of radius $1$ rests on the plane, so its center is at height $1$. The centers of the three small spheres form an equilateral triangle (since all three are mutually tangent with radius $1$, the distance between any two centers is $1+1=2$). So the triangle has side length $2$.

The centroid of an equilateral triangle with side $s$ is at distance $\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{3}}$ from each vertex. With $s = 2$: centroid is at distance $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{3}}$ from each small sphere's center.

The large sphere has radius $2$. It rests on the three small spheres, so its center is at distance $2 + 1 = 3$ from each small sphere's center.

Let $h$ be the height of the large sphere's center above the plane. The distance from the large sphere's center to a small sphere's center is:
$$\sqrt{\left(\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}\right)^2 + (h - 1)^2} = 3.$$
$$\frac{4}{3} + (h-1)^2 = 9.$$
$$(h-1)^2 = 9 - \frac{4}{3} = \frac{23}{3}.$$
$$h - 1 = \sqrt{\frac{23}{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{69}}{3}.$$
$$h = 1 + \frac{\sqrt{69}}{3}.$$

The top of the large sphere is at height $h + 2 = 3 + \dfrac{\sqrt{69}}{3}$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
