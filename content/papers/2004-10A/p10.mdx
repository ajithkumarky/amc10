<Problem>
Coin $A$ is flipped three times and coin $B$ is flipped four times. What is the probability that the number of heads obtained from flipping the two fair coins is the same?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We need $P(\text{heads from } A = \text{heads from } B)$. The number of heads from $A$ ranges from $0$ to $3$, and from $B$ from $0$ to $4$. We sum over equal counts $k = 0, 1, 2, 3$:

$$P = \sum_{k=0}^{3} \binom{3}{k}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^3 \cdot \binom{4}{k}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^4 = \frac{1}{128}\sum_{k=0}^{3}\binom{3}{k}\binom{4}{k}.$$

Computing each term:
- $k=0$: $\binom{3}{0}\binom{4}{0} = 1$
- $k=1$: $\binom{3}{1}\binom{4}{1} = 3 \cdot 4 = 12$
- $k=2$: $\binom{3}{2}\binom{4}{2} = 3 \cdot 6 = 18$
- $k=3$: $\binom{3}{3}\binom{4}{3} = 1 \cdot 4 = 4$

Sum $= 1 + 12 + 18 + 4 = 35$.

$$P = \frac{35}{128}.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
