<Problem>
Let $f$ be a function with the following properties:

(i) $f(1) = 1$, and

(ii) $f(2n) = n \cdot f(n)$ for any positive integer $n$.

What is the value of $f(2^{100})$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Apply the recurrence repeatedly:
$$f(2^{100}) = 2^{99} \cdot f(2^{99}) = 2^{99} \cdot 2^{98} \cdot f(2^{98}) = 2^{99} \cdot 2^{98} \cdot 2^{97} \cdots 2^1 \cdot f(2^1).$$

And $f(2) = f(2 \cdot 1) = 1 \cdot f(1) = 1$.

So $f(2^{100}) = 2^{99} \cdot 2^{98} \cdots 2^1 \cdot f(2) = 2^{99+98+\cdots+1} \cdot 1 = 2^{99 \cdot 100/2} = 2^{4950}$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
