<Problem>
A company sells peanut butter in cylindrical jars. Marketing research suggests that using wider jars will increase sales. If the diameter of the jars is increased by $25\%$ without altering the volume, by what percent must the height be decreased?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The volume of a cylinder is $V = \pi r^2 h$. If the diameter increases by $25\%$, the radius also increases by $25\%$, so the new radius is $1.25r$.

For constant volume:
$$\pi (1.25r)^2 h' = \pi r^2 h \implies 1.5625 \cdot h' = h \implies h' = \frac{h}{1.5625} = \frac{h}{(5/4)^2} = \frac{16h}{25}.$$

The height decreases from $h$ to $\dfrac{16h}{25}$, a decrease of $h - \dfrac{16h}{25} = \dfrac{9h}{25}$, which is $\dfrac{9}{25} = 36\%$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
