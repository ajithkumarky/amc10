<Problem>
Bertha has $6$ daughters and no sons. Some of her daughters have $6$ daughters, and the rest have none. Bertha has a total of $30$ daughters and granddaughters, and no great-granddaughters. How many of Bertha's daughters and granddaughters have no daughters?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $k$ be the number of Bertha's daughters who each have $6$ daughters. The total number of daughters and granddaughters is $6 + 6k = 30$, so $6k = 24$ and $k = 4$.

Bertha has $4$ daughters who each have $6$ daughters, and $6 - 4 = 2$ daughters who have no daughters.

The number of people with no daughters:
- The $2$ daughters of Bertha who have no daughters,
- All $24$ granddaughters (who have no daughters, since there are no great-granddaughters).

Total: $2 + 24 = 26$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
