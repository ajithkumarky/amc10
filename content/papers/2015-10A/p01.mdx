<Problem>
What is the value of $(2^0-1+5^2+0)^{-1}\times5?$
</Problem>

<Solution>
First simplify the expression inside the parentheses. We have $2^0 = 1$, so:
$$2^0 - 1 + 5^2 + 0 = 1 - 1 + 25 + 0 = 25.$$

Now compute $(25)^{-1} \times 5 = \dfrac{1}{25} \times 5 = \dfrac{5}{25} = \dfrac{1}{5}$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
