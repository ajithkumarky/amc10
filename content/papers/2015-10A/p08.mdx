<Problem>
Two years ago Pete was three times as old as his cousin Claire. 2 years before that, Pete was four times as old as Claire. In how many years will the ratio of their ages be $2 : 1$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let Pete's current age be $P$ and Claire's current age be $C$.

Two years ago: $P - 2 = 3(C - 2)$, so $P - 2 = 3C - 6$, giving $P = 3C - 4$.

Four years ago (i.e., 2 years before that): $P - 4 = 4(C - 4)$, so $P - 4 = 4C - 16$, giving $P = 4C - 12$.

Setting equal: $3C - 4 = 4C - 12$, so $C = 8$. Then $P = 3(8) - 4 = 20$.

We want $P + t = 2(C + t)$ for some $t > 0$:
$$20 + t = 2(8 + t) = 16 + 2t$$
$$4 = t.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
