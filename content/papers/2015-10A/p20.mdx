<Problem>
A rectangle with positive integer side lengths in $\text{cm}$ has area $A$ $\text{cm}^2$ and perimeter $P$ $\text{cm}$. Which of the following numbers cannot equal $A+P$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the sides be positive integers $a$ and $b$. Then $A = ab$ and $P = 2a + 2b$, so:
$$A + P = ab + 2a + 2b.$$

Add $4$ to both sides:
$$A + P + 4 = ab + 2a + 2b + 4 = (a+2)(b+2).$$

Let $N = A + P$. Then $N + 4 = (a+2)(b+2)$. We need $N + 4$ to be expressible as a product of two integers, each at least $3$ (since $a, b \ge 1$ means $a+2, b+2 \ge 3$).

Check each choice:
- $N = 100$: $N + 4 = 104 = 8 \times 13$. Since $8 \ge 3$ and $13 \ge 3$: valid (e.g., $a=6, b=11$).
- $N = 102$: $N + 4 = 106 = 2 \times 53$. The only factorizations of $106$ with both factors $\ge 3$ require checking: $106 = 1 \times 106 = 2 \times 53$. Neither $1$ nor $2$ is $\ge 3$, and $2 \times 53$ has factor $2 < 3$. So $106$ cannot be written as $(a+2)(b+2)$ with $a,b \ge 1$. This means $N = 102$ is impossible!
- $N = 104$: $N + 4 = 108 = 4 \times 27$. Both $\ge 3$: valid (e.g., $a=2, b=25$).
- $N = 106$: $N + 4 = 110 = 5 \times 22$. Both $\ge 3$: valid.
- $N = 108$: $N + 4 = 112 = 4 \times 28$. Both $\ge 3$: valid.

So the answer that cannot equal $A + P$ is $102$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
