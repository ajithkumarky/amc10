<Problem>
How many terms are there in the arithmetic sequence $13$, $16$, $19$, $\ldots$, $70$, $73$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The sequence starts at $13$, ends at $73$, and has common difference $3$. The $n$-th term is $13 + 3(n-1)$.

Setting this equal to $73$:
$$13 + 3(n-1) = 73$$
$$3(n-1) = 60$$
$$n - 1 = 20$$
$$n = 21.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
