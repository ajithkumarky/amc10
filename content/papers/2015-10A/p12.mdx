<Problem>
Points $(\sqrt{\pi}, a)$ and $(\sqrt{\pi}, b)$ are distinct points on the graph of $y^2+x^4=2x^2y+1$. What is $|a-b|$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Rearrange the equation $y^2 + x^4 = 2x^2 y + 1$:
$$y^2 - 2x^2 y + x^4 = 1$$
$$(y - x^2)^2 = 1$$
$$y - x^2 = \pm 1$$
$$y = x^2 + 1 \quad \text{or} \quad y = x^2 - 1.$$

At $x = \sqrt{\pi}$, we have $x^2 = \pi$, so:
$$a = \pi + 1, \quad b = \pi - 1 \quad (\text{or vice versa}).$$

Therefore $|a - b| = |(\pi+1) - (\pi-1)| = 2$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
