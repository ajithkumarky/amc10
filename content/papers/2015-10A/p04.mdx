<Problem>
Pablo, Sofia, and Mia got some candy eggs at a party. Pablo had three times as many eggs as Sofia, and Sofia had twice as many eggs as Mia. Pablo decides to give some of his eggs to Sofia and Mia so that all three will have the same number of eggs. What fraction of his eggs should Pablo give to Sofia?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let Mia have $m$ eggs. Then Sofia has $2m$ and Pablo has $3 \times 2m = 6m$ eggs. The total is $m + 2m + 6m = 9m$. After redistribution, each person has $3m$ eggs.

Sofia needs to end up with $3m$ eggs. She starts with $2m$, so Pablo gives Sofia $3m - 2m = m$ eggs.

Pablo starts with $6m$ eggs, so the fraction he gives to Sofia is $\dfrac{m}{6m} = \dfrac{1}{6}$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
