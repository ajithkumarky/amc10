<Problem>
The sum of two positive numbers is $5$ times their difference. What is the ratio of the larger number to the smaller number?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the larger number be $L$ and the smaller be $S$. The condition states:
$$L + S = 5(L - S).$$

Expanding: $L + S = 5L - 5S$, so $6S = 4L$, giving $\dfrac{L}{S} = \dfrac{6}{4} = \dfrac{3}{2}$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
