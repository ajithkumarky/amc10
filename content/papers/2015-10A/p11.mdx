<Problem>
The ratio of the length to the width of a rectangle is $4 : 3$. If the rectangle has diagonal of length $d$, then the area may be expressed as $kd^2$ for some constant $k$. What is $k$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the length be $4x$ and width be $3x$ for some positive real $x$.

The diagonal satisfies $d^2 = (4x)^2 + (3x)^2 = 16x^2 + 9x^2 = 25x^2$, so $x^2 = \dfrac{d^2}{25}$.

The area is $A = 4x \cdot 3x = 12x^2 = 12 \cdot \dfrac{d^2}{25} = \dfrac{12}{25}d^2$.

Therefore $k = \dfrac{12}{25}$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
