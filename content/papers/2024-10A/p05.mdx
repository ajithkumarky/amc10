<Problem>
What is the least value of $n$ such that $n!$ is a multiple of $2024$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First, factor $2024$:

$$2024 = 8 \times 253 = 8 \times 11 \times 23 = 2^3 \times 11 \times 23$$

For $n!$ to be a multiple of $2024$, it must contain the prime factors $2^3$, $11$, and $23$.

- $2^3$ is present in $n!$ for all $n \geq 4$ (in fact, $4! = 24 = 2^3 \times 3$ already has it).
- The factor of $11$ first appears when $n \geq 11$.
- The factor of $23$ first appears when $n \geq 23$.

The most demanding requirement is $n \geq 23$. Once $n = 23$, we have $23!$ which includes $2, 3, \ldots, 23$, so it contains $2^3$, $11$, and $23$ as factors. Therefore the least $n$ is $23$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
