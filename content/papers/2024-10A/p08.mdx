<Problem>
Amy, Bomani, Charlie, and Daria work in a chocolate factory. On Monday Amy, Bomani, and Charlie started working at $1{:}00$ PM and were able to pack $4$, $3$, and $3$ packages, respectively, every $3$ minutes. At some later time, Daria joined the group, and Daria was able to pack $5$ packages every $4$ minutes. Together, they finished packing $450$ packages at exactly $2{:}45$ PM. At what time did Daria join the group?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The total working time from $1{:}00$ PM to $2{:}45$ PM is $105$ minutes.

Amy, Bomani, and Charlie together pack $4 + 3 + 3 = 10$ packages every $3$ minutes, which is $\frac{10}{3}$ packages per minute.

Daria packs $5$ packages every $4$ minutes, which is $\frac{5}{4}$ packages per minute.

Let $d$ be the number of minutes after $1{:}00$ PM when Daria joins. She works for $105 - d$ minutes.

Total packages packed:

$$\frac{10}{3} \cdot 105 + \frac{5}{4} \cdot (105 - d) = 450$$

$$350 + \frac{5}{4}(105 - d) = 450$$

$$\frac{5}{4}(105 - d) = 100$$

$$105 - d = 80$$

$$d = 25$$

Daria joined $25$ minutes after $1{:}00$ PM, which is $1{:}25$ PM.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
