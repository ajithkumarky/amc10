<Problem>
What is the value of $9901 \cdot 101 - 99 \cdot 10101$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Notice that $9901 = 9900 + 1$ and $10101 = 10100 + 1$.

Let's expand both products:

$$9901 \cdot 101 = (9900 + 1) \cdot 101 = 9900 \cdot 101 + 101 = 999900 + 101 = 1000001$$

$$99 \cdot 10101 = 99 \cdot (10100 + 1) = 99 \cdot 10100 + 99 = 999900 + 99 = 999999$$

Therefore:

$$9901 \cdot 101 - 99 \cdot 10101 = 1000001 - 999999 = 2$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
