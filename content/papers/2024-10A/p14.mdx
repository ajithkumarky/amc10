<Problem>
One side of an equilateral triangle of height $24$ lies on line $\ell$. A circle of radius $12$ is tangent to line $\ell$ and is externally tangent to the triangle. The area of the region exterior to the triangle and the circle and bounded by the triangle, the circle, and line $\ell$ can be written as $a\sqrt{b} - c\pi$, where $a$, $b$, and $c$ are positive integers and $b$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. What is $a + b + c$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Set up coordinates.**

An equilateral triangle of height $24$ has side length $s = \dfrac{24}{\sqrt{3}/2} = 16\sqrt{3}$. Place the base on line $\ell$ with the left base vertex at $V = (-8\sqrt{3},\, 0)$ and right base vertex at $(8\sqrt{3},\, 0)$.

The circle of radius $12$ sits above $\ell$ tangent to it, so its center is at $C = (x_0,\, 12)$. The left slant side runs from $(-8\sqrt{3}, 0)$ to $(0, 24)$ with equation $\sqrt{3}\,x - y + 24 = 0$. For the circle to be externally tangent to this side (touching from outside the triangle):

$$\frac{|\sqrt{3}\,x_0 - 12 + 24|}{2} = 12 \implies \sqrt{3}\,x_0 + 12 = -24 \implies x_0 = -12\sqrt{3}.$$

So $C = (-12\sqrt{3},\, 12)$.

**Identify the bounded region.**

The region lies in the wedge between $\ell$ and the left slant side, exterior to both the triangle and the circle. Let $P = (-12\sqrt{3},\, 0)$ be where the circle touches $\ell$ and $Q$ be where it touches the slant side. The region is bounded by segment $VP$ on $\ell$, the near arc $PQ$ of the circle, and segment $QV$ on the slant side.

**Compute key lengths and angles.**

$$VP = |-12\sqrt{3} - (-8\sqrt{3})| = 4\sqrt{3}, \qquad VC = \sqrt{(4\sqrt{3})^2 + 12^2} = \sqrt{192} = 8\sqrt{3}.$$

In right triangle $VPC$ (right angle at $P$ since $CP \perp \ell$):

$$\cos(\angle PVC) = \frac{VP}{VC} = \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{8\sqrt{3}} = \frac{1}{2}, \quad \text{so } \angle PVC = 60°.$$

The exterior corner at $V$ between $\ell$ and the slant side has measure $120°$, and $VC$ bisects it, so $\angle QVC = 60°$ as well, giving $VQ = VP = 4\sqrt{3}$ by symmetry.

**Area of the kite $VPCQ$.**

$$\text{Area}(\triangle VPC) = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot VP \cdot CP = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot 4\sqrt{3} \cdot 12 = 24\sqrt{3}.$$

By symmetry $\text{Area}(\triangle VQC) = 24\sqrt{3}$, so $\text{Area(kite)} = 48\sqrt{3}$.

**Central angle of arc $PQ$.**

In right triangle $VPC$: $\angle VCP = 180° - 90° - 60° = 30°$. By symmetry $\angle VCQ = 30°$, so the arc $PQ$ on the side toward $V$ (the arc bounding the region) subtends $\angle PCV + \angle VCQ = 60°$ at center $C$.

**Area of sector $CPQ$.**

$$\text{Area(sector)} = \frac{60°}{360°}\cdot\pi\cdot 12^2 = \frac{1}{6}\cdot 144\pi = 24\pi.$$

**Area of the bounded region.**

$$\text{Area} = \text{Area(kite)} - \text{Area(sector)} = 48\sqrt{3} - 24\pi.$$

So $a = 48$, $b = 3$, $c = 24$, and $a + b + c = 48 + 3 + 24 = 75$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
