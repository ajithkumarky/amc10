<Problem>
A rectangular box has integer side lengths in the ratio $1:3:4$. Which of the following could be the volume of the box?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The side lengths are $k$, $3k$, and $4k$ for some positive integer $k$. The volume is:
$$V = k \cdot 3k \cdot 4k = 12k^3.$$

We need to check which answer choice is a multiple of $12$ and equals $12k^3$ for some positive integer $k$:

- $48 = 12 \cdot 4 = 12 \cdot 2^2$. For $12k^3 = 48$, we need $k^3 = 4$, which is not a perfect cube.
- $56$: not divisible by $12$.
- $64$: not divisible by $12$.
- $96 = 12 \cdot 8 = 12 \cdot 2^3$. For $12k^3 = 96$, we need $k^3 = 8$, so $k = 2$. This works!
- $144 = 12 \cdot 12$. For $12k^3 = 144$, we need $k^3 = 12$, which is not a perfect cube.

With $k = 2$, the box has dimensions $2 \times 6 \times 8$ and volume $96$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
