<Problem>
The remainder can be defined for all real numbers $x$ and $y$ with $y \neq 0$ by $$\text{rem}(x, y) = x - y\left\lfloor \frac{x}{y} \right\rfloor$$ where $\left\lfloor \dfrac{x}{y} \right\rfloor$ denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to $\dfrac{x}{y}$. What is the value of $\text{rem}\!\left(\dfrac{3}{8}, -\dfrac{2}{5}\right)$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We need $\text{rem}\!\left(\tfrac{3}{8}, -\tfrac{2}{5}\right) = \tfrac{3}{8} - \left(-\tfrac{2}{5}\right)\left\lfloor \dfrac{3/8}{-2/5} \right\rfloor$.

First compute the ratio:
$$\frac{3/8}{-2/5} = \frac{3}{8} \cdot \frac{-5}{2} = -\frac{15}{16}.$$

Since $-\dfrac{15}{16} \approx -0.9375$, we have $\left\lfloor -\dfrac{15}{16} \right\rfloor = -1$.

Therefore:
$$\text{rem} = \frac{3}{8} - \left(-\frac{2}{5}\right)(-1) = \frac{3}{8} - \frac{2}{5} = \frac{15}{40} - \frac{16}{40} = -\frac{1}{40}.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
