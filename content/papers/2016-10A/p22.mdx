<Problem>
For some positive integer $n$, the number $110n^3$ has $110$ positive integer divisors, including $1$ and the number $110n^3$. How many positive integer divisors does the number $81n^4$ have?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First, $110 = 2 \cdot 5 \cdot 11$.

The number of divisors of $110n^3$ is $110$. Since $110 = 2 \cdot 5 \cdot 11$, the divisor count factorization must equal $110$.

Write $n = 2^a \cdot 5^b \cdot 11^c \cdot m^3$ where $\gcd(m, 2 \cdot 5 \cdot 11) = 1$ (i.e., $m$ contributes only primes other than $2, 5, 11$).

Then $110n^3 = 2^{1+3a} \cdot 5^{1+3b} \cdot 11^{1+3c} \cdot m^3$.

The number of divisors: $(2+3a)(1+3b)(1+3c) \cdot \tau(m^3) = 110$, where the exponents of $2$, $5$, $11$ in $110n^3$ are $1+3a$, $1+3b$, $1+3c$ respectively, contributing factors $(2+3a)$, $(2+3b)$... wait let me redo.

$110 = 2^1 \cdot 5^1 \cdot 11^1$. So $110n^3 = 2^{1+3a} \cdot 5^{1+3b} \cdot 11^{1+3c} \cdot (\text{other primes from } m^3)$.

Number of divisors $= (1+3a+1)(1+3b+1)(1+3c+1) \cdot \tau(m^3)$... I need to be more careful.

Let $n = 2^a \cdot 5^b \cdot 11^c \cdot k$ where $\gcd(k, 110) = 1$. Then:
$$110n^3 = 2^{1+3a} \cdot 5^{1+3b} \cdot 11^{1+3c} \cdot k^3.$$

Number of divisors:
$$(1+3a+1)(1+3b+1)(1+3c+1)\cdot\tau(k^3) = 110.$$

That's $(2+3a)(2+3b)(2+3c)\cdot\tau(k^3) = 110$.

Since $110 = 2 \cdot 5 \cdot 11$, and each factor $(2+3a), (2+3b), (2+3c) \geq 2$ (since $a,b,c \geq 0$):

Try $a = b = c = 0$ and $\tau(k^3) = ?$: $(2)(2)(2)\tau(k^3) = 110 \Rightarrow \tau(k^3) = 110/8$, not integer.

Try to factor $110 = 2 \cdot 5 \cdot 11$ as a product where each factor $\equiv 2 \pmod{3}$ (since $2 + 3a \equiv 2 \pmod 3$):

$2 \equiv 2, 5 \equiv 2, 11 \equiv 2 \pmod{3}$ — all three factors work!

If $(2+3a, 2+3b, 2+3c) = (2, 5, 11)$ in some order and $\tau(k^3) = 1$ (meaning $k=1$):

$2+3a = 2 \Rightarrow a=0$; $2+3b = 5 \Rightarrow b=1$; $2+3c = 11 \Rightarrow c=3$.

Check: $2 \cdot 5 \cdot 11 \cdot 1 = 110$. ✓

So $n = 2^0 \cdot 5^1 \cdot 11^3 \cdot 1 = 5 \cdot 11^3$ (or any permutation of which prime gets which exponent).

**Number of divisors of $81n^4$:**

$81 = 3^4$. With $n = 5^1 \cdot 11^3$ (taking $a=0, b=1, c=3$):

$n^4 = 5^4 \cdot 11^{12}$.

$81n^4 = 3^4 \cdot 5^4 \cdot 11^{12}$.

Number of divisors $= (4+1)(4+1)(12+1) = 5 \cdot 5 \cdot 13 = 325$.

Let's verify with another valid assignment: $n = 2^0 \cdot 5^3 \cdot 11^1$:
$n^4 = 5^{12} \cdot 11^4$; $81n^4 = 3^4 \cdot 5^{12} \cdot 11^4$; $\tau = 5 \cdot 13 \cdot 5 = 325$. Same answer.

And $n = 2^0 \cdot 5^0 \cdot 11^0 \cdot k$ cases with $\tau(k^3) > 1$ would give different factorizations, but they all lead to $\tau(81n^4) = 325$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
