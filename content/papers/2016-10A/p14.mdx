<Problem>
How many ways are there to write $2016$ as the sum of twos and threes, ignoring order? (For example, $1008 \cdot 2 + 0 \cdot 3$ and $402 \cdot 2 + 404 \cdot 3$ are two such ways.)
</Problem>

<Solution>
We need non-negative integer solutions to $2a + 3b = 2016$.

**When $b = 0$:** $2a = 2016 \Rightarrow a = 1008$. Valid.

**When $b > 0$:** We need $2016 - 3b$ to be non-negative and even. Since $3b$ has the same parity as $b$, and $2016$ is even, we need $b$ to be even.

Let $b = 2k$ where $k \geq 0$: then $2a = 2016 - 6k \Rightarrow a = 1008 - 3k$. We need $a \geq 0$, so $k \leq 336$.

The values of $k$ are $0, 1, 2, \ldots, 336$, giving $\mathbf{337}$ solutions.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
