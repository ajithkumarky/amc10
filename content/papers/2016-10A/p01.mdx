<Problem>
What is the value of $\dfrac{11!-10!}{9!}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Factor out $10!$ from the numerator:

$$\frac{11! - 10!}{9!} = \frac{10!(11 - 1)}{9!} = \frac{10! \cdot 10}{9!} = 10 \cdot 10 = 100.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
