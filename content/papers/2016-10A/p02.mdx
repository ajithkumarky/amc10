<Problem>
For what value $x$ does $10^{x} \cdot 100^{2x} = 1000^{5}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Write everything as a power of $10$:

$$10^x \cdot (10^2)^{2x} = (10^3)^5$$
$$10^x \cdot 10^{4x} = 10^{15}$$
$$10^{5x} = 10^{15}.$$

So $5x = 15$, giving $x = 3$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
