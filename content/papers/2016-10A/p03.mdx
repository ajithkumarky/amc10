<Problem>
For every dollar Ben spent on bagels, David spent $25$ cents less. Ben paid $\$12.50$ more than David. How much did they spend in the bagel store together?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let Ben spend $b$ dollars. David spends $0.75b$ dollars (25 cents less per dollar means David pays $75\%$ of what Ben pays).

Ben paid $\$12.50$ more than David:
$$b - 0.75b = 12.50 \implies 0.25b = 12.50 \implies b = 50.$$

David spent $0.75 \times 50 = 37.50$ dollars.

Together they spent $50 + 37.50 = \$87.50$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
