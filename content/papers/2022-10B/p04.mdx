<Problem>
A donkey suffers an attack of hiccups and the first hiccup happens at $4{:}00$ one afternoon. Suppose that the donkey hiccups regularly every $5$ seconds. At what time does the donkey's $700$th hiccup occur?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The first hiccup is at time $0$ seconds (i.e., 4:00:00). The $n$th hiccup occurs at time $5(n-1)$ seconds after 4:00.

The 700th hiccup occurs at:
$$5 \times (700 - 1) = 5 \times 699 = 3495 \text{ seconds after } 4{:}00.$$

Convert 3495 seconds to minutes and seconds:
$$3495 = 58 \times 60 + 15,$$
so 3495 seconds is 58 minutes and 15 seconds.

The 700th hiccup occurs at $4{:}58$ and $15$ seconds, which is 15 seconds after $4{:}58$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
