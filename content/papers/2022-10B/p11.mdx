<Problem>
All the high schools in a large school district are involved in a fundraiser selling T-shirts. Which of the choices below is logically equivalent to the statement "No school bigger than Euclid HS sold more T-shirts than Euclid HS"?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The original statement is: "No school bigger than Euclid HS sold more T-shirts than Euclid HS."

In logic, "No A is B" is equivalent to "No B is A" (contrapositive via universal negation). Formally, "No A is B" means: for all schools $X$, if $X$ is bigger than Euclid, then $X$ did NOT sell more T-shirts than Euclid.

The contrapositive of "if bigger, then not more" is "if more, then not bigger." In plain English: no school that sold more T-shirts than Euclid HS is bigger than Euclid HS.

Check each option:
- **(A)** is the converse (reverse direction) — not logically equivalent.
- **(B)** "No school that sold more T-shirts than Euclid HS is bigger than Euclid HS." This is exactly the contrapositive form. ✓
- **(C)** says all bigger schools sold fewer — stronger than what we know (the original says bigger schools didn't sell MORE, which allows selling the same amount too). Not equivalent.
- **(D)** is the inverse, not equivalent.
- **(E)** contradicts the original idea.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
