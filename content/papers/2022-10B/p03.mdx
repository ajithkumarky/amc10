<Problem>
How many three-digit positive integers have an odd number of even digits?
</Problem>

<Solution>
A three-digit number has digits in the hundreds, tens, and ones places. An odd number of even digits means exactly 1 or exactly 3 even digits.

The even digits are $\{0, 2, 4, 6, 8\}$ (5 choices) and the odd digits are $\{1, 3, 5, 7, 9\}$ (5 choices). The hundreds digit cannot be 0.

**Case 1: Exactly 1 even digit.**

Sub-case 1a: The even digit is in the hundreds place.
The hundreds digit has 4 choices (2, 4, 6, 8 — not 0), and each of the other two digits is odd: $5 \times 5 = 25$ choices for tens and ones.
Count: $4 \times 25 = 100$.

Sub-case 1b: The even digit is in the tens place.
Hundreds digit is odd (5 choices), tens digit is even (5 choices), ones digit is odd (5 choices).
Count: $5 \times 5 \times 5 = 125$.

Sub-case 1c: The even digit is in the ones place.
Hundreds digit is odd (5 choices), tens digit is odd (5 choices), ones digit is even (5 choices).
Count: $5 \times 5 \times 5 = 125$.

Case 1 total: $100 + 125 + 125 = 350$.

**Case 2: Exactly 3 even digits.**

Hundreds digit is even and nonzero (4 choices), tens digit is even (5 choices), ones digit is even (5 choices).
Count: $4 \times 5 \times 5 = 100$.

**Grand total:** $350 + 100 = 450$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
