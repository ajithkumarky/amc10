<Problem>
The diagram below shows a rectangle with side lengths $4$ and $8$ and a square with side length $5$. Three vertices of the square lie on three different sides of the rectangle as shown. What is the area of the region inside both the square and the rectangle?

(The square has vertex $A=(1,0)$ on the bottom side, vertex $B=(4,4)$ on the top side, and vertex $C=(8,1)$ on the right side, with the fourth vertex $D=(5,-3)$ outside the rectangle below the bottom edge. A right-angle mark is shown at $A$.)
</Problem>

<Solution>
Set up coordinates with the rectangle having corners at $(0,0)$, $(8,0)$, $(8,4)$, $(0,4)$.

**Finding the square's vertices.** The square has vertices $A=(1,0)$, $B=(4,4)$, $C=(8,1)$, $D=(5,-3)$.

**Verify it's a square with side 5:**
- $AB$: $(4-1, 4-0) = (3,4)$, length $= \sqrt{9+16} = 5$ ✓
- $BC$: $(8-4, 1-4) = (4,-3)$, length $= \sqrt{16+9} = 5$ ✓
- $(3,4) \cdot (4,-3) = 12 - 12 = 0$ ✓ (right angle)

The three vertices on the rectangle are: $A=(1,0)$ on the bottom side ($y=0$), $B=(4,4)$ on the top side ($y=4$), and $C=(8,1)$ on the right side ($x=8$). Vertex $D=(5,-3)$ is outside the rectangle below the bottom edge.

**Finding the overlap region.** The intersection of the square with the rectangle is the square minus the triangular piece that dips below $y=0$.

The side $CD$: from $(8,1)$ to $(5,-3)$. Parameterize: $(8,1)+t(-3,-4)$. It crosses $y=0$ when $1-4t=0$, so $t=1/4$, giving $x = 8 - 3/4 = 29/4$.

The side $DA$: from $(5,-3)$ to $(1,0)$. It ends at $A=(1,0)$, which lies exactly on $y=0$.

So the part of the square below $y=0$ is the triangle with vertices $D=(5,-3)$, $A=(1,0)$, and $(29/4,\, 0)$.

**Area of this triangle:**
Base along $y=0$: from $(1,0)$ to $(29/4,0)$, length $= 29/4 - 1 = 25/4$.
Height: from $y=0$ down to $D=(5,-3)$, height $= 3$.

$$\text{Area of triangle} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{25}{4} \cdot 3 = \frac{75}{8}.$$

**Area of overlap:**
$$\text{Area} = \text{Area of square} - \text{Area below rectangle} = 25 - \frac{75}{8} = \frac{200}{8} - \frac{75}{8} = \frac{125}{8} = 15\frac{5}{8}.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
