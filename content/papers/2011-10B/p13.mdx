<Problem>
Two real numbers are selected independently at random from the interval $[-20, 10]$. What is the probability that the product of those numbers is greater than zero?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The product of two numbers is positive when both are positive or both are negative.

The interval $[-20, 10]$ has total length $30$. Of this:
- Negative portion: $[-20, 0)$, length $20$, so probability $\frac{20}{30} = \frac{2}{3}$.
- Positive portion: $(0, 10]$, length $10$, so probability $\frac{10}{30} = \frac{1}{3}$.

The probability that both numbers are negative: $\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{4}{9}$.

The probability that both numbers are positive: $\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{9}$.

The probability that the product is positive:
$$\frac{4}{9} + \frac{1}{9} = \frac{5}{9}.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
