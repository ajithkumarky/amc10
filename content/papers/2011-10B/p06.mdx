<Problem>
On Halloween Casper ate $1/3$ of his candies and then gave $2$ candies to his brother. The next day he ate $1/3$ of his remaining candies and then gave $4$ candies to his sister. On the third day he ate his final $8$ candies. How many candies did Casper have at the beginning?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Work backwards from day 3. Before day 3, Casper had $8$ candies.

Before giving 4 to his sister on day 2, he had $8 + 4 = 12$ candies. That was after eating $1/3$ of what he had at the start of day 2, so $12$ was $2/3$ of day 2's starting amount. Day 2 started with $12 \div \frac{2}{3} = 18$ candies.

Before giving 2 to his brother on day 1, he had $18 + 2 = 20$ candies. That was after eating $1/3$ of the original amount, so $20$ was $2/3$ of the beginning amount. The beginning amount was $20 \div \frac{2}{3} = 30$ candies.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
