<Problem>
At a certain beach if it is at least $80^{\circ}$ F and sunny, then the beach will be crowded. On June 10 the beach was not crowded. What can be concluded about the weather conditions on June 10?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The given rule is: (at least $80^\circ$ F) AND (sunny) $\Rightarrow$ (crowded).

The contrapositive is logically equivalent: NOT (crowded) $\Rightarrow$ NOT (at least $80^\circ$ F) OR NOT (sunny), which simplifies to: (cooler than $80^\circ$ F) OR (not sunny).

Since the beach was not crowded on June 10, we can conclude that it was either cooler than $80^\circ$ F or it was not sunny (or both). This matches choice B.

Choice A is too strong — it requires both conditions, but the contrapositive only guarantees at least one.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
