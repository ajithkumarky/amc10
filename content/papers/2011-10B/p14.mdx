<Problem>
A rectangular parking lot has a diagonal of $25$ meters and an area of $168$ square meters. In meters, what is the perimeter of the parking lot?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the side lengths be $l$ and $w$. We have two equations:
$$l^2 + w^2 = 25^2 = 625 \quad \text{and} \quad lw = 168.$$

The perimeter is $2(l + w)$. Use the identity $(l + w)^2 = l^2 + 2lw + w^2$:
$$(l + w)^2 = 625 + 2(168) = 625 + 336 = 961.$$

So $l + w = \sqrt{961} = 31$, and the perimeter is $2 \times 31 = 62$ meters.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
