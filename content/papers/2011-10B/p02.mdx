<Problem>
Josanna's test scores to date are $90, 80, 70, 60,$ and $85$. Her goal is to raise her test average at least $3$ points with her next test. What is the minimum test score she would need to accomplish this goal?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Her current average is $\dfrac{90+80+70+60+85}{5} = \dfrac{385}{5} = 77$. She wants her new average to be at least $77+3=80$.

If her next score is $s$, the new average over 6 tests must be at least $80$:
$$\frac{385 + s}{6} \ge 80 \implies 385 + s \ge 480 \implies s \ge 95.$$

The minimum score needed is $95$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
