<Problem>
At a store, when a length is reported as $x$ inches that means the length is at least $x - 0.5$ inches and at most $x + 0.5$ inches. Suppose the dimensions of a rectangular tile are reported as $2$ inches by $3$ inches. In square inches, what is the minimum area for the rectangle?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The reported dimensions are 2 inches and 3 inches. By the store's convention, the actual length could be as small as $2 - 0.5 = 1.5$ inches and the actual width as small as $3 - 0.5 = 2.5$ inches.

The minimum area is therefore $1.5 \times 2.5 = 3.75$ square inches.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
