<Problem>
The sum of two angles of a triangle is $6/5$ of a right angle, and one of these two angles is $30^{\circ}$ larger than the other. What is the degree measure of the largest angle in the triangle?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The sum of the two angles is $\frac{6}{5} \times 90^\circ = 108^\circ$.

Let the smaller of the two angles be $\alpha$, so the larger is $\alpha + 30^\circ$. Then:
$$\alpha + (\alpha + 30^\circ) = 108^\circ \implies 2\alpha = 78^\circ \implies \alpha = 39^\circ.$$

The two angles are $39^\circ$ and $69^\circ$. The third angle of the triangle is $180^\circ - 108^\circ = 72^\circ$.

The largest angle is $\max(39^\circ, 69^\circ, 72^\circ) = 72^\circ$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
