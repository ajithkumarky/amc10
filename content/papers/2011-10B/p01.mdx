<Problem>
What is $$\dfrac{2+4+6}{1+3+5} - \dfrac{1+3+5}{2+4+6}$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
The two sums are $2+4+6=12$ and $1+3+5=9$, so the expression equals:
$$\frac{12}{9} - \frac{9}{12} = \frac{4}{3} - \frac{3}{4} = \frac{16}{12} - \frac{9}{12} = \frac{7}{12}.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
