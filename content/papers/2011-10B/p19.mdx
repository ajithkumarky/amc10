<Problem>
What is the product of all the roots of the equation $$\sqrt{5|x|+8} = \sqrt{x^2-16}.$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
Square both sides (both sides must be non-negative, so squaring is valid when solutions exist):
$$5|x| + 8 = x^2 - 16 \implies x^2 - 5|x| - 24 = 0.$$

Let $u = |x| \ge 0$:
$$u^2 - 5u - 24 = 0 \implies (u-8)(u+3) = 0.$$

So $u = 8$ or $u = -3$. Since $u = |x| \ge 0$, only $u = 8$ works, giving $|x| = 8$, so $x = 8$ or $x = -8$.

**Verify both solutions exist (the right side must be non-negative):**
- $x = 8$: $\sqrt{5(8)+8} = \sqrt{48}$ and $\sqrt{64-16} = \sqrt{48}$. Valid.
- $x = -8$: $\sqrt{5(8)+8} = \sqrt{48}$ and $\sqrt{64-16} = \sqrt{48}$. Valid.

The product of all roots is $8 \times (-8) = -64$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
