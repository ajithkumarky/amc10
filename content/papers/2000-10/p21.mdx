<Problem>
If all alligators are ferocious creatures and some creepy crawlers are alligators, which statement(s) must be true?

$$\text{I. All alligators are creepy crawlers.}$$
$$\text{II. Some ferocious creatures are creepy crawlers.}$$
$$\text{III. Some alligators are not creepy crawlers.}$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
We are given:
- All alligators are ferocious creatures.
- Some creepy crawlers are alligators (i.e., there exists at least one thing that is both a creepy crawler and an alligator).

**Statement I:** "All alligators are creepy crawlers." — We only know *some* creepy crawlers are alligators (the set of alligators and the set of creepy crawlers overlap), but this does not mean *all* alligators are creepy crawlers. False in general.

**Statement II:** "Some ferocious creatures are creepy crawlers." — Since some creepy crawlers are alligators, there exists at least one alligator that is also a creepy crawler. Since all alligators are ferocious, that alligator is also a ferocious creature. Therefore there exists a ferocious creature (namely that alligator) that is also a creepy crawler. **This must be true.**

**Statement III:** "Some alligators are not creepy crawlers." — We only know some creepy crawlers are alligators; it's possible that all alligators are creepy crawlers (making III false). So III need not be true.

Only Statement II must be true.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
