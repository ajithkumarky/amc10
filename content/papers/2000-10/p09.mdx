<Problem>
If $|x - 2| = p$, where $x < 2$, then $x - p = $
</Problem>

<Solution>
Since $x < 2$, we have $x - 2 < 0$, so $|x - 2| = -(x - 2) = 2 - x$.

Therefore $2 - x = p$, which gives $x = 2 - p$.

Then:
$$x - p = (2 - p) - p = 2 - 2p.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
