<Problem>
At Olympic High School, $\frac{2}{5}$ of the freshmen and $\frac{4}{5}$ of the sophomores took the AMC 10. Given that the number of freshman and sophomore contestants was the same, which of the following must be true?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $F$ = number of freshmen and $S$ = number of sophomores.

The number of freshman contestants is $\frac{2}{5}F$ and the number of sophomore contestants is $\frac{4}{5}S$.

Setting these equal:
$$\frac{2}{5}F = \frac{4}{5}S \implies 2F = 4S \implies F = 2S.$$

So there are twice as many freshmen as sophomores.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
