<Problem>
Chandra pays an on-line service provider a fixed monthly fee plus an hourly charge for connect time. Her December bill was $\$12.48$, but in January her bill was $\$17.54$ because she used twice as much connect time as in December. What is the fixed monthly fee?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $f$ be the fixed fee and $h$ be the hourly charge times the number of hours in December.

December: $f + h = 12.48$

January: $f + 2h = 17.54$

Subtracting the first from the second: $h = 17.54 - 12.48 = 5.06$.

Therefore $f = 12.48 - 5.06 = 7.42$.

The fixed monthly fee is $\$7.42$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
