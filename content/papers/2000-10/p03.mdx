<Problem>
Each day, Jenny ate $20\%$ of the jellybeans that were in her jar at the beginning of that day. At the end of the second day, $32$ remained. How many jellybeans were in the jar originally?
</Problem>

<Solution>
If Jenny ate $20\%$ each day, then $80\% = \frac{4}{5}$ of the jellybeans remained at the end of each day.

Let $n$ be the original number. After day 1: $\frac{4}{5}n$. After day 2: $\frac{4}{5} \cdot \frac{4}{5} n = \frac{16}{25} n$.

Setting this equal to $32$:
$$\frac{16}{25} n = 32 \implies n = 32 \cdot \frac{25}{16} = 2 \cdot 25 = 50.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
