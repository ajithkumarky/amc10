<Problem>
The diagram shows $28$ lattice points, each one unit from its nearest neighbors. Segment $AB$ meets segment $CD$ at $E$. Find the length of segment $AE$.

The coordinates are: $A = (0, 3)$, $B = (6, 0)$, $C = (4, 2)$, $D = (2, 0)$. Point $E$ is the intersection of segments $AB$ and $CD$.
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Line through $A = (0, 3)$ and $B = (6, 0)$:**

Slope $= \frac{0 - 3}{6 - 0} = -\frac{1}{2}$.

Equation: $y = 3 - \frac{x}{2}$, or $x + 2y = 6$.

**Line through $C = (4, 2)$ and $D = (2, 0)$:**

Slope $= \frac{2 - 0}{4 - 2} = 1$.

Equation: $y - 0 = 1(x - 2)$, so $y = x - 2$, or $x - y = 2$.

**Intersection $E$:**

From the two equations:
$$x + 2y = 6 \quad \text{and} \quad x - y = 2.$$

Subtract the second from the first: $3y = 4 \implies y = \frac{4}{3}$.

Then $x = 2 + y = 2 + \frac{4}{3} = \frac{10}{3}$.

So $E = \left(\frac{10}{3}, \frac{4}{3}\right)$.

**Length $AE$:** $A = (0, 3)$, $E = \left(\frac{10}{3}, \frac{4}{3}\right)$.

$$AE = \sqrt{\left(\frac{10}{3} - 0\right)^2 + \left(\frac{4}{3} - 3\right)^2} = \sqrt{\frac{100}{9} + \left(-\frac{5}{3}\right)^2} = \sqrt{\frac{100}{9} + \frac{25}{9}} = \sqrt{\frac{125}{9}} = \frac{5\sqrt{5}}{3}.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
