<Problem>
$2000 \cdot ({2000}^{2000}) = $
</Problem>

<Solution>
Using the rule $a \cdot a^n = a^{n+1}$:

$$2000 \cdot 2000^{2000} = 2000^1 \cdot 2000^{2000} = 2000^{2001}.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
