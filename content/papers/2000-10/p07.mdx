<Problem>
In rectangle $ABCD$, $AD = 1$, $P$ is on $\overline{AB}$, and $\overline{DB}$ and $\overline{DP}$ trisect $\angle ADC$. What is the perimeter of $\triangle BDP$?

The rectangle has $A$ at the top-left, $B$ at the top-right, $C$ at the bottom-right, $D$ at the bottom-left. $AD = 1$ is the left side (height). $P$ is a point on the top side $AB$ between $A$ and $B$. Lines $DP$ and $DB$ divide the right angle $\angle ADC$ into three equal $30°$ angles.
</Problem>

<Solution>
Since $\angle ADC = 90°$ is trisected by $DP$ and $DB$, we have $\angle ADP = 30°$, $\angle PDB = 30°$, and $\angle BDC = 30°$.

**Finding $AB$ (width of rectangle):** In right triangle $ADC$, $\tan(\angle ADC) = \infty$ — but $\angle BDC = 30°$, so in right triangle $BDC$ with $DC = AB$ and $BC = AD = 1$:
$$\tan(30°) = \frac{BC}{DC} = \frac{1}{DC} \implies DC = \frac{1}{\tan 30°} = \sqrt{3}.$$

So $AB = DC = \sqrt{3}$.

**Finding $AP$:** $\angle ADP = 30°$. In right triangle $ADP$ with $AD = 1$:
$$\tan(30°) = \frac{AP}{AD} = \frac{AP}{1} \implies AP = \tan(30°) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}.$$

**Finding $BP$:** $BP = AB - AP = \sqrt{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} = \frac{3\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{3}}{3} = \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$.

**Lengths of triangle sides:**

$DB$: In right triangle $BDC$, $DB = \frac{BC}{\sin(30°)} = \frac{1}{1/2} = 2$.

$DP$: In right triangle $ADP$, $DP = \frac{AD}{\cos(30°)} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}/2} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$.

**Perimeter of $\triangle BDP$:**
$$BP + DP + DB = \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3} + \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3} + 2 = \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{3} + 2 = 2 + \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{3}.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
