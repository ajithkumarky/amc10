<Problem>
Suppose that $4^a = 5$, $5^b = 6$, $6^c = 7$, and $7^d = 8$. What is $a \cdot b \cdot c \cdot d$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Chain the equations by substitution:

$$4^a = 5 \implies (4^a)^b = 5^b = 6 \implies 4^{ab} = 6.$$
$$4^{ab} = 6 \implies (4^{ab})^c = 6^c = 7 \implies 4^{abc} = 7.$$
$$4^{abc} = 7 \implies (4^{abc})^d = 7^d = 8 \implies 4^{abcd} = 8.$$

Now $4^{abcd} = 8$ means $2^{2abcd} = 2^3$, so $2abcd = 3$ and $abcd = \dfrac{3}{2}$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
