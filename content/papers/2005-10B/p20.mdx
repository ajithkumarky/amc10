<Problem>
What is the average (mean) of all $5$-digit numbers that can be formed by using each of the digits $1$, $3$, $5$, $7$, and $8$ exactly once?
</Problem>

<Solution>
There are $5! = 120$ such numbers. Each digit appears in each position exactly $\frac{120}{5} = 24$ times.

The sum of all digits: $1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 8 = 24$.

The sum of all $120$ numbers:
$$24 \times (10000 + 1000 + 100 + 10 + 1) \times 24 = 24 \times 11111 \times 24.$$

Wait — the contribution of each digit to a given position is: (digit appears $24$ times) $\times$ (place value). Summing over all digits and all positions:

$$\text{Total sum} = 24 \times (1+3+5+7+8) \times (10000 + 1000 + 100 + 10 + 1) = 24 \times 24 \times 11111.$$

$$= 576 \times 11111 = 6{,}399{,}936.$$

**Mean:** $\dfrac{6{,}399{,}936}{120} = 53{,}332.8$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
