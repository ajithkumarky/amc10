<Problem>
An $8$-foot by $10$-foot floor is tiled with square tiles of size $1$ foot by $1$ foot. Each tile has a pattern consisting of four white quarter circles of radius $\frac{1}{2}$ foot centered at each corner of the tile. The remaining portion of the tile is shaded. How many square feet of the floor are shaded?
</Problem>

<Solution>
There are $8 \times 10 = 80$ tiles total. On each tile, the four quarter-circle cutouts together form one full circle of radius $\frac{1}{2}$, with area $\pi \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{\pi}{4}$.

The shaded area per tile is $1 - \frac{\pi}{4}$.

Total shaded area: $80\left(1 - \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = 80 - 20\pi$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
