<Problem>
How many numbers between $1$ and $2005$ are integer multiples of $3$ or $4$ but not $12$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We want multiples of $3$ or $4$, but excluding multiples of $12$ (which are multiples of both $3$ and $4$).

**Multiples of $3$** in $[1, 2005]$: $\lfloor 2005/3 \rfloor = 668$.

**Multiples of $4$** in $[1, 2005]$: $\lfloor 2005/4 \rfloor = 501$.

**Multiples of $12$** in $[1, 2005]$: $\lfloor 2005/12 \rfloor = 167$.

By inclusion-exclusion, multiples of $3$ or $4$: $668 + 501 - 167 = 1002$.

Subtracting those that are multiples of $12$: $1002 - 167 = 835$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
