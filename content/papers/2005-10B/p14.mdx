<Problem>
Equilateral $\triangle ABC$ has side length $2$, $M$ is the midpoint of $\overline{AC}$, and $C$ is the midpoint of $\overline{BD}$. What is the area of $\triangle CDM$?

The figure shows equilateral triangle $ABC$ with $B$ at the left, $A$ at the top-left, $C$ at the right, and $D$ further right of $C$ on the base line, with $M$ on $AC$.
</Problem>

<Solution>
Set up coordinates: $B = (0, 0)$, $C = (2, 0)$, $A = (1, \sqrt{3})$ (equilateral with side $2$).

Since $C$ is the midpoint of $\overline{BD}$, $D = 2C - B = (4, 0)$.

$M$ is the midpoint of $\overline{AC}$: $M = \left(\frac{1+2}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{3}+0}{2}\right) = \left(\frac{3}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$.

Triangle $CDM$ has vertices $C = (2, 0)$, $D = (4, 0)$, $M = \left(\frac{3}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$.

Base $CD = 2$ (along the $x$-axis). Height = the $y$-coordinate of $M = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times 2 \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
