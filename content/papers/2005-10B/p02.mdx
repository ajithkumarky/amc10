<Problem>
A positive number $x$ has the property that $x\%$ of $x$ is $4$. What is $x$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
$x\%$ of $x$ means $\frac{x}{100} \cdot x = \frac{x^2}{100} = 4$, so $x^2 = 400$, giving $x = 20$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
