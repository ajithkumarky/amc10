<Problem>
A scout troop buys $1000$ candy bars at a price of five for $\textdollar 2$. They sell all the candy bars at a price of two for $\textdollar 1$. What was the profit, in dollars?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Cost:** At five bars for $\textdollar 2$, the cost per bar is $\frac{2}{5}$ dollars. For $1000$ bars: $1000 \times \frac{2}{5} = 400$ dollars.

**Revenue:** At two bars for $\textdollar 1$, the price per bar is $\frac{1}{2}$ dollar. For $1000$ bars: $1000 \times \frac{1}{2} = 500$ dollars.

**Profit:** $500 - 400 = 100$ dollars.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
