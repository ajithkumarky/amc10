<Problem>
One fair die has faces $1$, $1$, $2$, $2$, $3$, $3$ and another has faces $4$, $4$, $5$, $5$, $6$, $6$. The dice are rolled and the numbers on the top faces are added. What is the probability that the sum will be odd?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The sum is odd when one addend is even and the other is odd.

**Die 1** shows an odd number ($1$ or $3$) with probability $\frac{4}{6} = \frac{2}{3}$, and an even number ($2$) with probability $\frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3}$.

**Die 2** shows an odd number ($5$) with probability $\frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3}$, and an even number ($4$ or $6$) with probability $\frac{4}{6} = \frac{2}{3}$.

For an odd sum:
- Die 1 odd AND Die 2 even: $\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{4}{9}$.
- Die 1 even AND Die 2 odd: $\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{9}$.

Total probability: $\frac{4}{9} + \frac{1}{9} = \frac{5}{9}$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
