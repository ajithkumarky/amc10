<Problem>
A circle is inscribed in a square, then a square is inscribed in this circle, and finally, a circle is inscribed in this square. What is the ratio of the area of the smaller circle to the area of the larger square?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the larger square have side length $2$. The inscribed circle has radius $1$ (diameter equals the side). A square inscribed in that circle has its diagonal equal to the diameter $2$, so its side is $\frac{2}{\sqrt{2}} = \sqrt{2}$. The circle inscribed in this inner square has radius $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.

Area of smaller circle: $\pi \left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{\pi}{2}$.

Area of larger square: $2^2 = 4$.

Ratio: $\dfrac{\pi/2}{4} = \dfrac{\pi}{8}$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
