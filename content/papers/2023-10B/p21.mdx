<Problem>
Each of $2023$ balls is randomly placed into one of $3$ bins. Which of the following is closest to the probability that each of the bins will contain an odd number of balls?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Each ball independently goes to bin 1, 2, or 3 with probability $\tfrac{1}{3}$ each. Let $X_1, X_2, X_3$ be the counts. We want $P(X_1 \text{ odd}, X_2 \text{ odd}, X_3 \text{ odd})$.

**Parity indicator trick.** For any integer $k$, the indicator $[k \text{ is odd}] = \dfrac{1 - (-1)^k}{2}$. Therefore:

$$P(X_1 \text{ odd},\, X_2 \text{ odd},\, X_3 \text{ odd}) = E\!\left[\prod_{i=1}^{3} \frac{1-(-1)^{X_i}}{2}\right] = \frac{1}{8}\sum_{S \subseteq \{1,2,3\}} (-1)^{|S|} E\!\left[(-1)^{\sum_{i \in S} X_i}\right].$$

**Computing $E\bigl[(-1)^{\sum_{i \in S} X_i}\bigr]$.**

For a single ball going to bin $j$ with probability $\tfrac{1}{3}$, its contribution to $(-1)^{\sum_{i \in S} X_i}$ is $(-1)^{[j \in S]}$. Thus for $n = 2023$ independent balls:
$$E\!\left[(-1)^{\sum_{i\in S}X_i}\right] = \left(\frac{1}{3}\sum_{j=1}^{3}(-1)^{[j\in S]}\right)^{2023}.$$

- $|S| = 0$: $\left(\frac{1+1+1}{3}\right)^{2023} = 1$.
- $|S| = 1$ (say $S = \{1\}$): $\left(\frac{-1+1+1}{3}\right)^{2023} = \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{2023} \approx 0$.
- $|S| = 2$ (say $S = \{1,2\}$): $\left(\frac{(-1)(-1)+(-1)(1)+(1)(-1)}{3}\right)$ — wait, we evaluate each bin: bin 1 gives $(-1)^{[1\in S]}(-1)^{[2\in S]}$... Actually it's $\left(\frac{(-1)+(-1)+(1)}{3}\right)^{2023} = \left(\frac{-1}{3}\right)^{2023} \approx 0$.
- $|S| = 3$: $\left(\frac{-1-1-1}{3}\right)^{2023} = (-1)^{2023} = -1$.

Since $n = 2023$ is large and odd, all terms with $0 < |S| < 3$ are essentially $0$, and the $|S|=3$ term gives $(-1)^{2023} = -1$.

$$P(\text{all odd}) = \frac{1}{8}\Bigl[1 - 3\cdot\tfrac{1}{3^{2023}} + 3\cdot\tfrac{(-1)^{2023}}{3^{2023}} - (-1)^{2023}\Bigr] \approx \frac{1}{8}[1 - 0 + 0 - (-1)] = \frac{1}{8}\cdot 2 = \frac{1}{4}.$$

(The terms involving $3^{-2023}$ are negligibly small.)

The probability is very close to $\dfrac{1}{4}$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
