<Problem>
What is the area of the region in the coordinate plane defined by
$$\big| |x| - 1 \big| + \big| |y| - 1 \big| \le 1?$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
By symmetry across both axes, the region is symmetric about both coordinate axes. We work in the first quadrant ($x \ge 0$, $y \ge 0$) where $|x| = x$ and $|y| = y$, then multiply by $4$.

In the first quadrant, the condition becomes $|x - 1| + |y - 1| \le 1$. This is a square (diamond) centered at $(1, 1)$ with "radius" $1$ in the $L^1$ (taxicab) metric. Its vertices are at $(0,1)$, $(2,1)$, $(1,0)$, $(1,2)$, forming a square with diagonal $2$, so area $= \frac{1}{2}(2)(2) = 2$.

However, we must consider the first quadrant only (the region $x \ge 0$, $y \ge 0$). The diamond is centered at $(1,1)$ and its leftmost vertex is $(0,1)$ and lowest vertex is $(1,0)$, both on the boundary of the first quadrant. So the entire diamond lies within the first quadrant ($x \ge 0$, $y \ge 0$). The first-quadrant area is $2$.

Multiplying by $4$ for all four quadrants: total area $= 4 \times 2 = 8$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
