<Problem>
The numbers $16$ and $25$ are a pair of consecutive positive perfect squares whose difference is $9$. How many pairs of consecutive positive perfect squares have a difference of less than or equal to $2023$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Consecutive perfect squares $(n)^2$ and $(n+1)^2$ have difference:
$$(n+1)^2 - n^2 = 2n + 1.$$

We need $2n + 1 \le 2023$, which gives $2n \le 2022$, so $n \le 1011$.

Since $n$ must be a positive integer ($n \ge 1$), the valid values are $n = 1, 2, 3, \ldots, 1011$, giving $1011$ pairs.

(Check: the pair $16, 25$ corresponds to $n = 4$, and $2(4) + 1 = 9$ ✓.)

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
