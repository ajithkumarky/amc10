<Problem>
A rectangular box $P$ has distinct edge lengths $a$, $b$, and $c$. The sum of the lengths of all $12$ edges of $P$ is $13$, the sum of the areas of all $6$ faces of $P$ is $\dfrac{11}{2}$, and the volume of $P$ is $\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is the length of the longest interior diagonal connecting two vertices of $P$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
A rectangular box with edge lengths $a$, $b$, $c$ has:
- $12$ edges: four of each length, so total edge length $= 4(a + b + c)$.
- $6$ faces: two of each pair, so total face area $= 2(ab + bc + ca)$.
- Volume $= abc$.
- Space diagonal $= \sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}$.

**Setting up the equations.**

$$4(a + b + c) = 13 \implies a + b + c = \frac{13}{4}.$$

$$2(ab + bc + ca) = \frac{11}{2} \implies ab + bc + ca = \frac{11}{4}.$$

$$abc = \frac{1}{2}.$$

**Finding the diagonal.**

We use the identity:
$$a^2 + b^2 + c^2 = (a + b + c)^2 - 2(ab + bc + ca).$$

$$a^2 + b^2 + c^2 = \left(\frac{13}{4}\right)^2 - 2 \cdot \frac{11}{4} = \frac{169}{16} - \frac{22}{4} = \frac{169}{16} - \frac{88}{16} = \frac{81}{16}.$$

The space diagonal is:
$$\sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2} = \sqrt{\frac{81}{16}} = \frac{9}{4}.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
