<Problem>
Square $ABCD$ is rotated $20^\circ$ clockwise about its center to obtain square $EFGH$, as shown below. What is the degree measure of $\angle EAB$?

<Solution>
Let $O$ be the shared center of both squares. All four vertices of each square lie on the same circumscribed circle of radius $r$ centered at $O$.

**Step 1: Locate the key points.** With $A$ at angle $180°$ from the positive $x$-axis and $B$ at $90°$ (adjacent vertices of $ABCD$), rotating $ABCD$ clockwise by $20°$ places $E$ at $180° - 20° = 160°$. Going counterclockwise: $B$ is at $90°$, $E$ at $160°$, $A$ at $180°$, so $E$ lies between $B$ and $A$ on the circumcircle.

**Step 2: Find $\angle OAE$.** Triangle $OAE$ is isosceles with $OA = OE = r$ and central angle $\angle AOE = 20°$. Therefore:
$$\angle OAE = \frac{180° - 20°}{2} = 80°.$$

**Step 3: Find $\angle OAB$.** Triangle $OAB$ is isosceles with $OA = OB = r$ and central angle $\angle AOB = 90°$ (adjacent vertices of a square). Therefore:
$$\angle OAB = \frac{180° - 90°}{2} = 45°.$$

**Step 4: Compute $\angle EAB$.** Ray $AO$, ray $AE$, and ray $AB$ all emanate from $A$. Since $E$ is between $B$ and $A$ on the arc, ray $AE$ lies between rays $AO$ and $AB$, and:
$$\angle EAB = \angle OAE - \angle OAB = 80° - 45° = 35°.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
