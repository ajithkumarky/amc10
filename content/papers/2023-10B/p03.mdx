<Problem>
A $3$-$4$-$5$ right triangle is inscribed in circle $A$, and a $5$-$12$-$13$ right triangle is inscribed in circle $B$. What is the ratio of the area of circle $A$ to the area of circle $B$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
A right triangle inscribed in a circle has its hypotenuse as the diameter of that circle (by Thales' theorem).

For circle $A$: the hypotenuse of the $3$-$4$-$5$ triangle is $5$, so the diameter is $5$ and the radius is $r_A = \frac{5}{2}$.

For circle $B$: the hypotenuse of the $5$-$12$-$13$ triangle is $13$, so the diameter is $13$ and the radius is $r_B = \frac{13}{2}$.

The ratio of the areas is:
$$\frac{\pi r_A^2}{\pi r_B^2} = \frac{r_A^2}{r_B^2} = \frac{(5/2)^2}{(13/2)^2} = \frac{25}{169}.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
