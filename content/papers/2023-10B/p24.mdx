<Problem>
What is the perimeter of the boundary of the region consisting of all points which can be expressed as $(2u - 3w,\; v + 4w)$ with $0 \leq u \leq 1$, $0 \leq v \leq 1$, and $0 \leq w \leq 1$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The set of points is the image of the unit cube $[0,1]^3$ under the linear map
$$(u, v, w) \mapsto (2u - 3w,\; v + 4w).$$

This can be written as the Minkowski sum of three line segments (one for each variable):
$$R = \{\mathbf{0}\} + u\,\mathbf{e}_1 + v\,\mathbf{e}_2 + w\,\mathbf{e}_3,$$
where $\mathbf{e}_1 = (2, 0)$, $\mathbf{e}_2 = (0, 1)$, $\mathbf{e}_3 = (-3, 4)$ are the image vectors of the three unit directions. This is a **zonotope** — the Minkowski sum of three line segments in $\mathbb{R}^2$.

**Perimeter of a 2D zonotope.** For a zonotope that is the Minkowski sum of $k$ segments with direction vectors $\mathbf{v}_1, \ldots, \mathbf{v}_k$ (no two parallel), the perimeter equals $2\sum_{i=1}^k |\mathbf{v}_i|$. This is because each segment $[0,\mathbf{v}_i]$ contributes one edge in direction $\mathbf{v}_i$ and one edge in direction $-\mathbf{v}_i$ to the boundary.

**Check no two vectors are parallel:**
- $\mathbf{e}_1 = (2,0)$: direction $0°$
- $\mathbf{e}_2 = (0,1)$: direction $90°$
- $\mathbf{e}_3 = (-3,4)$: direction $\arctan(-4/3) \approx 127°$

All three have distinct directions, so the formula applies.

**Computing lengths:**
$$|\mathbf{e}_1| = \sqrt{4+0} = 2, \quad |\mathbf{e}_2| = \sqrt{0+1} = 1, \quad |\mathbf{e}_3| = \sqrt{9+16} = 5.$$

**Perimeter:**
$$P = 2(|\mathbf{e}_1| + |\mathbf{e}_2| + |\mathbf{e}_3|) = 2(2 + 1 + 5) = 16.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
