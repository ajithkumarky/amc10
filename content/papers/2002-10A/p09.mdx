<Problem>
There are 3 numbers $A$, $B$, and $C$, such that $1001C - 2002A = 4004$, and $1001B + 3003A = 5005$. What is the average of $A$, $B$, and $C$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Divide the first equation by $1001$: $C - 2A = 4$, so $C = 2A + 4$.

Divide the second equation by $1001$: $B + 3A = 5$, so $B = 5 - 3A$.

The average of $A$, $B$, $C$ is:
$$\frac{A + B + C}{3} = \frac{A + (5 - 3A) + (2A + 4)}{3} = \frac{9}{3} = 3.$$

The $A$ terms cancel, so the average is always $3$ regardless of the value of $A$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
