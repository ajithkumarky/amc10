<Problem>
Both roots of the quadratic equation $x^2 - 63x + k = 0$ are prime numbers. The number of possible values of $k$ is
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the two prime roots be $p$ and $q$. By Vieta's formulas:
- $p + q = 63$
- $pq = k$

Since $63$ is odd, one of $p, q$ must be even and the other odd. The only even prime is $2$, so one root is $2$ and the other is $61$.

Check: $61$ is prime (not divisible by $2, 3, 5, 7$; $\sqrt{61} < 8$). Yes, $61$ is prime.

So $k = 2 \times 61 = 122$ is the only possibility. There is exactly $1$ value of $k$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
