<Problem>
Each of the small circles in the figure has radius $1$. The innermost circle is tangent to the six circles that surround it, and each of those circles is tangent to the large circle and to its small-circle neighbors. Find the area of the shaded region.

The figure shows a large circle filled with gray, with one small white circle at the center and six small white circles arranged around it (each tangent to the center circle and to the large circle).
</Problem>

<Solution>
First, find the radius of the large circle. The center circle has radius $1$. Each surrounding circle also has radius $1$, and its center is at distance $1 + 1 = 2$ from the origin (center-to-center distance between tangent circles of radius $1$). The surrounding circle's far edge is at distance $2 + 1 = 3$ from the origin, so the large circle has radius $3$.

Count the areas:
- Large circle: $\pi(3)^2 = 9\pi$
- Inner small circle: $\pi(1)^2 = \pi$
- Six surrounding small circles: $6 \cdot \pi(1)^2 = 6\pi$

Shaded area $= 9\pi - \pi - 6\pi = 2\pi$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
