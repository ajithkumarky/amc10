<Problem>
Compute the sum of all the roots of $(2x + 3)(x - 4) + (2x + 3)(x - 6) = 0$.
</Problem>

<Solution>
Factor out the common factor $(2x + 3)$:

$$(2x + 3)\bigl[(x - 4) + (x - 6)\bigr] = 0$$
$$(2x + 3)(2x - 10) = 0.$$

The roots are $2x + 3 = 0 \Rightarrow x = -\dfrac{3}{2}$ and $2x - 10 = 0 \Rightarrow x = 5$.

Sum of roots $= -\dfrac{3}{2} + 5 = \dfrac{7}{2}$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
