<Problem>
If an arc of $45^\circ$ on circle $A$ has the same length as an arc of $30^\circ$ on circle $B$, then the ratio of the area of circle $A$ to the area of circle $B$ is
</Problem>

<Solution>
Arc length equals $\dfrac{\theta}{360^\circ} \cdot 2\pi r$. Let $r_A$ and $r_B$ be the radii.

Setting arc lengths equal:
$$\frac{45}{360} \cdot 2\pi r_A = \frac{30}{360} \cdot 2\pi r_B$$
$$\frac{1}{8} r_A = \frac{1}{12} r_B$$
$$r_A = \frac{8}{12} r_B = \frac{2}{3} r_B.$$

The ratio of areas is:
$$\frac{\pi r_A^2}{\pi r_B^2} = \left(\frac{r_A}{r_B}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{4}{9}.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
