<Problem>
The sides of a triangle have lengths $15$, $20$, and $25$. Find the length of the shortest altitude.
</Problem>

<Solution>
Check: $15^2 + 20^2 = 225 + 400 = 625 = 25^2$, so this is a right triangle with legs $15$ and $20$ and hypotenuse $25$.

Area $= \dfrac{1}{2}(15)(20) = 150$.

The altitude to each side has length $h = \dfrac{2 \cdot \text{Area}}{\text{side}}$:
- Altitude to side $15$: $h = \dfrac{300}{15} = 20$
- Altitude to side $20$: $h = \dfrac{300}{20} = 15$
- Altitude to side $25$: $h = \dfrac{300}{25} = 12$

The shortest altitude is $12$, drawn to the longest side (the hypotenuse).

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
