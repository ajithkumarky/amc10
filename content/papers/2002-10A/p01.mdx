<Problem>
The ratio $\dfrac{10^{2000}+10^{2002}}{10^{2001}+10^{2001}}$ is closest to which of the following numbers?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Factor $10^{2000}$ from the numerator and $10^{2001}$ from the denominator:

$$\frac{10^{2000}+10^{2002}}{10^{2001}+10^{2001}} = \frac{10^{2000}(1+10^{2})}{10^{2001}(1+1)} = \frac{101}{10 \cdot 2} = \frac{101}{20} = 5.05.$$

This is closest to $5$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
