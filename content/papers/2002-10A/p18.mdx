<Problem>
A $3 \times 3 \times 3$ cube is formed by gluing together $27$ standard cubical dice. (On a standard die, the sum of the numbers on any pair of opposite faces is $7$.) The smallest possible sum of all the numbers showing on the surface of the $3 \times 3 \times 3$ cube is
</Problem>

<Solution>
Classify the 27 small dice by how many faces are visible:
- **8 corner dice:** 3 faces visible each
- **12 edge dice:** 2 faces visible each
- **6 face-center dice:** 1 face visible each
- **1 center die:** 0 faces visible

**Corner dice (3 faces visible):** The three visible faces share a vertex. On a standard die, opposite face pairs sum to 7: $(1,6),(2,5),(3,4)$. Three faces meeting at a vertex come one from each pair. The minimum sum of three such faces is $1+2+3 = 6$.

**Edge dice (2 faces visible):** Two visible faces come from two different opposite pairs. Minimum sum is $1+2 = 3$.

**Face-center dice (1 face visible):** Minimum showing face is $1$.

**Total minimum:**
$$8 \times 6 + 12 \times 3 + 6 \times 1 = 48 + 36 + 6 = 90.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
