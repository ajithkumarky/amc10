<Problem>
How many digits are in the base-ten representation of $8^5 \cdot 5^{10} \cdot 15^5$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First simplify the expression by factoring everything into primes.

$8^5 = 2^{15}$, $\quad 5^{10} = 5^{10}$, $\quad 15^5 = (3 \cdot 5)^5 = 3^5 \cdot 5^5$.

So the product equals:
$$2^{15} \cdot 5^{10} \cdot 3^5 \cdot 5^5 = 2^{15} \cdot 5^{15} \cdot 3^5 = (2 \cdot 5)^{15} \cdot 3^5 = 10^{15} \cdot 243.$$

$10^{15}$ is a $1$ followed by fifteen zeros (16 digits), so $243 \times 10^{15}$ is $243$ followed by fifteen zeros.

That number has $3 + 15 = 18$ digits.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
