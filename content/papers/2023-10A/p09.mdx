<Problem>
A digital display shows the current date as an $8$-digit integer consisting of a $4$-digit year, followed by a $2$-digit month, followed by a $2$-digit date within the month. For example, Arbor Day this year is displayed as $20230428.$ For how many dates in $2023$ does each digit appear an even number of times in the $8$-digit display for that date?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Every date in 2023 displays as $2023MMDD$. The prefix $2023$ contributes digits $\{0,2,2,3\}$ — one $0$, two $2$s, one $3$. For all eight digits to have even total counts, the four digits of $MMDD$ must supply an **odd** count of $0$s, an **odd** count of $3$s, and **even** counts of every other digit. With only four positions available, this forces $MMDD$ to contain exactly one $0$, one $3$, and two copies of some digit $d \notin \{0,3\}$ (since having three $0$s or three $3$s is impossible in a valid date, and any digit appearing once is odd-counted).

We now enumerate valid calendar dates $(MM, DD)$ whose four digits form the multiset $\{0, 3, d, d\}$:

**$d = 1$:** digits $\{0,1,1,3\}$.
- $MM=01$: remaining $\{1,3\}$ gives $DD=13$ (Jan 13) or $DD=31$ (Jan 31). Both valid.
- $MM=03$: remaining $\{1,1\}$ gives $DD=11$ (Mar 11). Valid.
- $MM=10$: remaining $\{1,3\}$ gives $DD=13$ (Oct 13) or $DD=31$ (Oct 31). Both valid.
- $MM=11$: remaining $\{0,3\}$ gives $DD=03$ (Nov 3) or $DD=30$ (Nov 30). Both valid.
Subtotal: **7 dates**.

**$d = 2$:** digits $\{0,2,2,3\}$.
- $MM=02$: remaining $\{2,3\}$ gives $DD=23$ (Feb 23) ✓ or $DD=32$ ✗.
- $MM=03$: remaining $\{2,2\}$ gives $DD=22$ (Mar 22) ✓.
Subtotal: **2 dates**.

**$d \ge 4$:** For any $d \in \{4,5,6,7,8,9\}$, the valid months from $\{0,3,d,d\}$ are only $03$ or $0d$. In both cases the remaining two digits form a day exceeding 31 or an impossible value ($dd \ge 44$, or $3d \ge 34$, or $d3 \ge 43$). **0 dates** for each.

**Total:** $7 + 2 = 9$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
