<Problem>
Cities $A$ and $B$ are $45$ miles apart. Alicia lives in $A$ and Beth lives in $B$. Alicia bikes toward $B$ at 18 miles per hour. Leaving at the same time, Beth bikes toward $A$ at 12 miles per hour. How many miles from City $A$ will they be when they meet?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Alicia and Beth ride toward each other, so they close the 45-mile gap at a combined rate of $18 + 12 = 30$ miles per hour.

Time until they meet: $\dfrac{45}{30} = 1.5$ hours.

Alicia travels $18 \times 1.5 = 27$ miles from City $A$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
