<Problem>
Henry decides one morning to do a workout, and he walks $\tfrac{3}{4}$ of the way from his home to his gym. The gym is $2$ kilometers away from Henry's home. At that point, he changes his mind and walks $\tfrac{3}{4}$ of the way from where he is back toward home. When he reaches that point, he changes his mind again and walks $\tfrac{3}{4}$ of the distance from there back toward the gym. If Henry keeps changing his mind when he has walked $\tfrac{3}{4}$ of the distance toward either the gym or home from the point where he last changed his mind, he will get very close to walking back and forth between a point $A$ kilometers from home and a point $B$ kilometers from home. What is $|A-B|$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Place home at $0$ and gym at $2$. In the limit, Henry oscillates between two fixed points $A$ (a turning point closer to the gym, reached on a walk toward the gym) and $B$ (a turning point closer to home, reached on a walk toward home).

The limiting behavior: from $B$, he walks $\tfrac34$ of the way toward the gym ($2$) to reach $A$; from $A$, he walks $\tfrac34$ of the way toward home ($0$) to reach $B$. This gives a self-consistent pair of equations:

$$A = B + \tfrac34(2 - B), \qquad B = A - \tfrac34 A = \tfrac14 A.$$

Substitute $B = \tfrac14 A$ into the first:

$$A = \tfrac14 A + \tfrac34\left(2 - \tfrac14 A\right) = \tfrac14 A + \tfrac32 - \tfrac{3}{16}A.$$

So $A - \tfrac14 A + \tfrac{3}{16}A = \tfrac32$, i.e. $\tfrac{16 - 4 + 3}{16}A = \tfrac{15}{16}A = \tfrac32$, giving $A = \tfrac{8}{5}$. Then $B = \tfrac14\cdot\tfrac85 = \tfrac25$.

$$|A - B| = \frac85 - \frac25 = \frac65 = 1\tfrac15.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
