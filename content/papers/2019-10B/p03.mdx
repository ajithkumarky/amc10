<Problem>
In a high school with $500$ students, $40\%$ of the seniors play a musical instrument, while $30\%$ of the non-seniors do not play a musical instrument. In all, $46.8\%$ of the students do not play a musical instrument. How many non-seniors play a musical instrument?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $s$ be the number of seniors and $n$ the number of non-seniors, so $s + n = 500$.

Among seniors, $40\%$ play, so $60\%$ do **not** play: that is $0.6s$ seniors who don't play. Among non-seniors, $30\%$ do not play: that is $0.3n$.

Total students not playing is $46.8\%$ of $500 = 234$. So

$$0.6s + 0.3n = 234.$$

Substitute $s = 500 - n$:

$$0.6(500 - n) + 0.3n = 234 \implies 300 - 0.6n + 0.3n = 234 \implies -0.3n = -66 \implies n = 220.$$

Non-seniors who play make up $70\%$ of the non-seniors: $0.7 \cdot 220 = 154$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
