<Problem>
All lines with equation $ax+by=c$ such that $a,b,c$ form an arithmetic progression pass through a common point. What are the coordinates of that point?
</Problem>

<Solution>
If $a, b, c$ are in arithmetic progression, then the middle term is the average of the outer two:

$$b = \frac{a+c}{2}, \quad \text{i.e.} \quad 2b = a + c.$$

We want a point $(x,y)$ that satisfies $ax + by = c$ for **every** such triple. Rewrite the line equation and use $c = 2b - a$:

$$ax + by = 2b - a \implies a(x+1) + b(y-2) = 0.$$

For this to hold for all valid $a$ and $b$, both coefficients must vanish:

$$x + 1 = 0 \quad\text{and}\quad y - 2 = 0,$$

giving $x = -1$, $y = 2$. The common point is $(-1, 2)$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
