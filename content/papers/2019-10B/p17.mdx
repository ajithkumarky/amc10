<Problem>
A red ball and a green ball are randomly and independently tossed into bins numbered with positive integers so that for each ball, the probability that it is tossed into bin $k$ is $2^{-k}$ for $k=1,2,3,\ldots$. What is the probability that the red ball is tossed into a higher-numbered bin than the green ball?
</Problem>

<Solution>
By symmetry, the events "red higher than green" and "green higher than red" have equal probability. Call this common value $p$. Let $q$ be the probability they land in the **same** bin. Then

$$2p + q = 1.$$

Compute $q$, the probability of a tie:

$$q = \sum_{k=1}^\infty \big(2^{-k}\big)^2 = \sum_{k=1}^\infty 4^{-k} = \frac{1/4}{1 - 1/4} = \frac13.$$

Therefore

$$p = \frac{1 - q}{2} = \frac{1 - \tfrac13}{2} = \frac{2/3}{2} = \frac13.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
