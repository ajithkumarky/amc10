<Problem>
The faces of a cubical die are marked with the numbers $1$, $2$, $2$, $3$, $3$, and $4$. The faces of another die are marked with the numbers $1$, $3$, $4$, $5$, $6$, and $8$. Both dice are thrown. What is the probability that the sum of the top two numbers will be $5$, $7$, or $9$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Die A has faces: 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4 (6 faces). Die B has faces: 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 (6 faces). Total outcomes: 36.

Count pairs $(a, b)$ with $a + b \in \{5, 7, 9\}$:

**Sum = 5:** $(a, b)$ pairs: $(1,4), (2,3), (3,2), (4,1)$.
- $(1,4)$: A shows 1 (1 way), B shows 4 (1 way): $1 \times 1 = 1$
- $(2,3)$: A shows 2 (2 ways), B shows 3 (1 way): $2 \times 1 = 2$
- $(3,2)$: A shows 3 (2 ways), B shows 2 (0 ways — B has no 2): $0$
- $(4,1)$: A shows 4 (1 way), B shows 1 (1 way): $1 \times 1 = 1$
Subtotal: $1 + 2 + 0 + 1 = 4$

**Sum = 7:** $(a, b)$ pairs: $(1,6), (2,5), (3,4), (4,3)$.
- $(1,6)$: $1 \times 1 = 1$
- $(2,5)$: $2 \times 1 = 2$
- $(3,4)$: $2 \times 1 = 2$
- $(4,3)$: $1 \times 1 = 1$
Subtotal: $1 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 6$

**Sum = 9:** $(a, b)$ pairs: $(1,8), (2,7), (3,6), (4,5)$.
- $(1,8)$: $1 \times 1 = 1$
- $(2,7)$: B has no 7: $0$
- $(3,6)$: $2 \times 1 = 2$
- $(4,5)$: $1 \times 1 = 1$
Subtotal: $1 + 0 + 2 + 1 = 4$

Total favorable outcomes: $4 + 6 + 4 = 14$.

Probability: $\frac{14}{36} = \frac{7}{18}$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
