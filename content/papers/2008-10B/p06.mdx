<Problem>
Points $B$ and $C$ lie on $\overline{AD}$. The length of $AB$ is $4$ times the length of $BD$, and the length of $AC$ is $9$ times the length of $CD$. The length of $BC$ is what fraction of the length of $AD$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $AD = 1$. Since $AB = 4 \cdot BD$ and $AB + BD = AD = 1$, we get $5 \cdot BD = 1$, so $BD = 1/5$ and $AB = 4/5$.

Since $AC = 9 \cdot CD$ and $AC + CD = 1$, we get $10 \cdot CD = 1$, so $CD = 1/10$ and $AC = 9/10$.

Then $BC = AC - AB = 9/10 - 4/5 = 9/10 - 8/10 = 1/10$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
