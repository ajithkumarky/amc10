<Problem>
A semipro baseball league has teams with $21$ players each. League rules state that a player must be paid at least $\textdollar 15{,}000$ and that the total of all players' salaries for each team cannot exceed $\textdollar 700{,}000$. What is the maximum possible salary, in dollars, for a single player?
</Problem>

<Solution>
To maximize one player's salary, minimize the other 20 players' salaries. Each of the 20 others earns the minimum $\textdollar 15{,}000$, totaling $20 \times 15{,}000 = \textdollar 300{,}000$.

The top player can then earn at most $\textdollar 700{,}000 - \textdollar 300{,}000 = \textdollar 400{,}000$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
