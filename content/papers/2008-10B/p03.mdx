<Problem>
Assume that $x$ is a positive real number. Which of the following is equivalent to $\sqrt[3]{x\sqrt{x}}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Rewrite the inner expression: $x\sqrt{x} = x \cdot x^{1/2} = x^{3/2}$.

Then $\sqrt[3]{x^{3/2}} = \left(x^{3/2}\right)^{1/3} = x^{1/2}$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
