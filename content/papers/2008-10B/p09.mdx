<Problem>
A quadratic equation $ax^2 - 2ax + b = 0$ has two real solutions. What is the average of these two solutions?
</Problem>

<Solution>
By Vieta's formulas, the sum of the two roots equals $\frac{2a}{a} = 2$. The average is therefore $\frac{2}{2} = 1$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
