<Problem>
An equilateral triangle of side length $10$ is completely filled in by non-overlapping equilateral triangles of side length $1$. How many small triangles are required?
</Problem>

<Solution>
When an equilateral triangle of side $n$ is tiled by unit equilateral triangles, the count is $n^2$. This is because you get $n^2$ small triangles total (some pointing up, some pointing down).

For $n = 10$: the number of small triangles is $10^2 = 100$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
