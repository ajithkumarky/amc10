<Problem>
For each positive integer $n$, the mean of the first $n$ terms of a sequence is $n$. What is the 2008th term of the sequence?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $S_n$ denote the sum of the first $n$ terms. We are told $S_n / n = n$, so $S_n = n^2$.

The $n$th term is $a_n = S_n - S_{n-1} = n^2 - (n-1)^2 = 2n - 1$.

Therefore $a_{2008} = 2(2008) - 1 = 4015$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
