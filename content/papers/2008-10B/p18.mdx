<Problem>
Bricklayer Brenda would take nine hours to build a chimney alone, and Bricklayer Brandon would take $10$ hours to build it alone. When they work together, they talk a lot, and their combined output decreases by $10$ bricks per hour. Working together, they build the chimney in $5$ hours. How many bricks are in the chimney?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $N$ be the total number of bricks. Brenda's rate is $N/9$ bricks/hour, Brandon's rate is $N/10$ bricks/hour. Together with the 10-brick-per-hour penalty, their effective rate is $\frac{N}{9} + \frac{N}{10} - 10$.

They finish in 5 hours:
$$5\left(\frac{N}{9} + \frac{N}{10} - 10\right) = N.$$
$$\frac{N}{9} + \frac{N}{10} - 10 = \frac{N}{5}.$$

Multiply through by 90:
$$10N + 9N - 900 = 18N.$$
$$19N - 900 = 18N.$$
$$N = 900.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
