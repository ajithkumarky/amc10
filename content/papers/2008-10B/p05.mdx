<Problem>
For real numbers $a$ and $b$, define $a \, \$ \, b = (a - b)^2$. What is $(x - y)^2 \, \$ \, (y - x)^2$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Note that $(x - y)^2 = (y - x)^2$, so let $t = (x-y)^2$. Then:
$$(x-y)^2 \, \$ \, (y-x)^2 = t \, \$ \, t = (t - t)^2 = 0.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
