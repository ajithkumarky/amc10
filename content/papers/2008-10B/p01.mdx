<Problem>
A basketball player made $5$ baskets during a game. Each basket was worth either $2$ or $3$ points. How many different numbers could represent the total points scored by the player?
</Problem>

<Solution>
If the player makes $k$ three-point baskets (and $5-k$ two-point baskets), the total is $3k + 2(5-k) = k + 10$. As $k$ ranges from $0$ to $5$, the totals are $10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15$ — six distinct values.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
