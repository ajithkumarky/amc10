<Problem>
Michael walks at the rate of $5$ feet per second on a long straight path. Trash pails are located every $200$ feet along the path. A garbage truck travels at $10$ feet per second in the same direction as Michael and stops for $30$ seconds at each pail. As Michael passes a pail, he notices the truck ahead of him just leaving the next pail. How many times will Michael and the truck meet (counting the initial observation as meeting 0 — i.e., how many subsequent meetings)?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Set $t = 0$ when Michael is at pail 0 and the truck just leaves pail 1 (200 feet ahead). Michael's position: $x_M(t) = 5t$. The truck drives 200 feet in $200/10 = 20$ seconds, then stops 30 seconds — a 50-second cycle per pail. Starting from pail 1 at $t=0$: the truck arrives at pail $k+1$ at $t = 20k$ and departs at $t = 50k$ (for $k = 0, 1, 2, \ldots$), so it stops at pail $k+2$ (position $200(k+2)$) during $t \in [50k+20,\, 50k+50]$.

**Meetings at stops:** Michael reaches pail $k+2$ at $t = 40(k+2)$. For this to fall in $[50k+20,\, 50k+50]$:
- Left: $50k+20 \leq 40k+80 \Rightarrow k \leq 6$.
- Right: $40k+80 \leq 50k+50 \Rightarrow k \geq 3$.

So $k \in \{3,4,5,6\}$, giving meetings at pails 5, 6, 7, 8 at times $t = 200, 240, 280, 320$.

**Meetings while moving:** During $[50k, 50k+20]$ the truck moves from position $200(k+1)$ at speed 10. Setting $5t = 200(k+1)+10(t-50k)$ gives $t = 60k-40$. For this to lie in $[50k, 50k+20]$:
- Left: $50k \leq 60k-40 \Rightarrow k \geq 4$.
- Right: $60k-40 \leq 50k+20 \Rightarrow k \leq 6$.

So $k \in \{4,5,6\}$, giving meetings at $t = 200, 260, 320$.

**Deduplication:** At $t=200$, the truck is exactly at pail 5 (position 1000), which is simultaneously the end of the $k=3$ stop and the start of the $k=4$ driving phase — one meeting. At $t=320$, similarly one meeting at pail 8. The full set of distinct meeting times is $\{200, 240, 260, 280, 320\}$ — exactly 5 meetings.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
