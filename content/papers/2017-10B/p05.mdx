<Problem>
Camilla had twice as many blueberry jelly beans as cherry jelly beans. After eating $10$ pieces of each kind, she now has three times as many blueberry jelly beans as cherry jelly beans. How many blueberry jelly beans did she originally have?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $c$ be the original number of cherry jelly beans. Then Camilla originally had $2c$ blueberry jelly beans.

After eating $10$ of each:
- Cherry: $c - 10$
- Blueberry: $2c - 10$

The new ratio is $3:1$:
$$2c - 10 = 3(c - 10)$$
$$2c - 10 = 3c - 30$$
$$20 = c$$

So the original number of blueberry jelly beans was $2c = 2(20) = 40$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
