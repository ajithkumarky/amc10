<Problem>
In the figure below, $3$ of the $6$ disks are to be painted blue, $2$ are to be painted red, and $1$ is to be painted green. Two paintings that can be obtained from one another by a rotation or a reflection of the entire figure are considered the same. How many different paintings are possible?

The figure shows $6$ disks arranged in a triangular grid: one disk at the top, two in the middle row, and three at the bottom row (a triangular arrangement of $3$ rows).
</Problem>

<Solution>
The 6 disks form a triangular grid: 3 corner positions (vertices of the triangle) and 3 edge-midpoint positions. The symmetry group is the dihedral group $D_3$ with 6 elements: rotations by $0°, 120°, 240°$ and 3 reflections. Apply Burnside's lemma:

$$\text{distinct colorings} = \frac{1}{6}\sum_{g \in G}|\text{Fix}(g)|.$$

The color palette is 3 blue (B), 2 red (R), 1 green (G).

**Identity:** All $\dfrac{6!}{3!\,2!\,1!} = 60$ colorings are fixed.

**Rotations by $120°$ and $240°$:** Each rotation cycles the 3 corners in one orbit and the 3 edge-midpoints in another. A fixed coloring would require all 3 corners to share one color and all 3 edge-midpoints to share one color — needing counts of $3$ each from our palette. Since we have only $2R$ and $1G$ (neither is $3$), and the $3B$ can fill at most one orbit, the remaining orbit has no valid monochromatic assignment. So $|\text{Fix}(120°)| = |\text{Fix}(240°)| = 0$.

**Each reflection:** A reflection fixes 1 corner and 1 edge-midpoint, and swaps the other 2 corners with each other and the other 2 edge-midpoints with each other. This creates 4 orbits: a corner pair (size 2), an edge pair (size 2), a corner singleton (size 1), and an edge singleton (size 1). For a coloring to be fixed, both disks in each pair must share a color.

We must distribute $3B, 2R, 1G$ across these orbits. Each pair contributes an even count; the two singletons each contribute 1. To get 3 blues (odd), we need exactly one pair colored blue and exactly one singleton colored blue. Enumerating all valid assignments of $({\rm corner\text{-}pair}, {\rm edge\text{-}pair}, {\rm corner\text{-}singleton}, {\rm edge\text{-}singleton})$:

| Corner pair | Edge pair | Corner singleton | Edge singleton |
|:-----------:|:---------:|:----------------:|:--------------:|
| B | R | B | G |
| B | R | G | B |
| R | B | B | G |
| R | B | G | B |

Each reflection fixes exactly $4$ colorings. With 3 reflections: $3 \times 4 = 12$ fixed colorings.

**Burnside's count:**
$$\frac{1}{6}(60 + 0 + 0 + 4 + 4 + 4) = \frac{72}{6} = 12.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
