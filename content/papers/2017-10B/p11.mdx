<Problem>
At Typico High School, $60\%$ of the students like dancing, and the rest dislike it. Of those who like dancing, $80\%$ say that they like it, and the rest say that they dislike it. Of those who dislike dancing, $90\%$ say that they dislike it, and the rest say that they like it. What fraction of students who say they dislike dancing actually like it?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Use $100$ students as a convenient total.

- $60$ like dancing, $40$ dislike dancing.

Of the $60$ who like dancing:
- $80\%$ say they like it: $48$ students.
- $20\%$ say they dislike it: $12$ students.

Of the $40$ who dislike dancing:
- $90\%$ say they dislike it: $36$ students.
- $10\%$ say they like it: $4$ students.

Students who say they dislike dancing: $12 + 36 = 48$.

Of these $48$, the ones who actually like dancing: $12$.

Fraction: $\dfrac{12}{48} = \dfrac{1}{4} = 25\%$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
