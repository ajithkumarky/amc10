<Problem>
Suppose that $x$ and $y$ are nonzero real numbers such that $\dfrac{3x+y}{x-3y}=-2$. What is the value of $\dfrac{x+3y}{3x-y}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
From the given equation: $3x + y = -2(x - 3y) = -2x + 6y$, so $5x = 5y$, meaning $x = y$.

Now substitute $x = y$ into the expression we want:
$$\frac{x + 3y}{3x - y} = \frac{x + 3x}{3x - x} = \frac{4x}{2x} = 2.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
