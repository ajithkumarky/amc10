<Problem>
Samia set off on her bicycle to visit her friend, traveling at an average speed of $17$ kilometers per hour. When she had gone half the distance to her friend's house, a tire went flat, and she walked the rest of the way at $5$ kilometers per hour. In all it took her $44$ minutes to reach her friend's house. In kilometers rounded to the nearest tenth, how far did Samia walk?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the total distance be $2d$ km (so Samia bikes $d$ km and walks $d$ km).

Time biking: $\dfrac{d}{17}$ hours.

Time walking: $\dfrac{d}{5}$ hours.

Total time is $44$ minutes $= \dfrac{44}{60} = \dfrac{11}{15}$ hours:
$$\frac{d}{17} + \frac{d}{5} = \frac{11}{15}.$$

Finding a common denominator for the left side (LCD of $17$ and $5$ is $85$):
$$\frac{5d}{85} + \frac{17d}{85} = \frac{22d}{85} = \frac{11}{15}.$$

Solving: $d = \dfrac{11}{15} \cdot \dfrac{85}{22} = \dfrac{11 \cdot 85}{15 \cdot 22} = \dfrac{935}{330} = \dfrac{85}{30} = \dfrac{17}{6} \approx 2.833\ldots$ km.

Rounded to the nearest tenth: $d \approx 2.8$ km.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
