<Problem>
Mr. Zhou places all the integers from $1$ to $225$ into a $15$ by $15$ grid. He places $1$ in the middle square (eighth row and eighth column) and places other numbers one by one clockwise in a spiral pattern outward from the center. What is the sum of the greatest number and the least number that appear in the second row from the top?

(The diagram in the original problem shows a partial $7 \times 7$ view of the spiral: starting at the center, the spiral first steps right, then proceeds clockwise — down, left, up, right, and so on, in increasing segment lengths.)
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Setting up coordinates.** Place the center cell at $(7, 7)$ using $(x, y)$ where $x$ is the column (0–14, left to right) and $y$ is the row (0–14, bottom to top). The second row from the top is $y = 13$.

**Ring structure.** The spiral fills the grid in concentric square rings. Ring $k$ ($k \ge 1$) is the perimeter of the $(2k+1) \times (2k+1)$ square centered at $(7,7)$, containing $8k$ cells. Ring $k$ holds the numbers from $(2k-1)^2 + 1$ to $(2k+1)^2$.

- Ring 6: numbers 122–169 (48 cells).
- Ring 7: numbers 170–225 (56 cells).

**Tracing the spiral.** Ring $k$ starts one step to the right of its top-right corner, at position $(7+k,\; 7+k-1)$.

*Ring 6* starts at $(13, 12) = 122$ and proceeds:
- Down 11 steps: $(13, 1) = 133$.
- Left 12 steps: $(1, 1) = 145$.
- Up 12 steps: $(1, 13) = 157$.
- Right 12 steps: $(13, 13) = 169$.

So cells $(1,13)$ through $(13,13)$ hold values $157$ through $169$.

*Ring 7* starts at $(14, 13) = 170$ and proceeds:
- Down 13 steps: $(14, 0) = 183$.
- Left 14 steps: $(0, 0) = 197$.
- Up 14 steps: $(0, 13) = 210$, and one more up to $(0, 14) = 211$.
- Right 14 steps: $(14, 14) = 225$.

So cell $(14, 13) = 170$ and cell $(0, 13) = 210$.

**Values in row $y = 13$ (second from top):**

| $x$ | 0 | 1 | 2 | $\cdots$ | 13 | 14 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| value | 210 | 157 | 158 | $\cdots$ | 169 | 170 |

The **least** value in this row is $157$ (at $x=1$) and the **greatest** is $210$ (at $x=0$).

$$157 + 210 = 367.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
