<Problem>
What is the value of $\sqrt{\left(3-2\sqrt{3}\right)^2}+\sqrt{\left(3+2\sqrt{3}\right)^2}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Recall that $\sqrt{t^2} = |t|$ for any real $t$.

First, check the sign of $3 - 2\sqrt{3}$. Since $\sqrt{3} \approx 1.732$, we get $2\sqrt{3} \approx 3.464 > 3$, so $3 - 2\sqrt{3} < 0$.

Therefore:
$$\sqrt{\left(3-2\sqrt{3}\right)^2} = |3-2\sqrt{3}| = 2\sqrt{3}-3.$$

The second term has $3 + 2\sqrt{3} > 0$, so:
$$\sqrt{\left(3+2\sqrt{3}\right)^2} = 3+2\sqrt{3}.$$

Adding the two results:
$$(2\sqrt{3}-3) + (3+2\sqrt{3}) = 4\sqrt{3}.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
