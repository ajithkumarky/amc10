<Problem>
A square piece of paper has side length $1$ and vertices $A, B, C,$ and $D$ in that order. As shown in the figure, the paper is folded so that vertex $C$ meets edge $\overline{AD}$ at point $C'$, and edge $\overline{BC}$ intersects edge $\overline{AB}$ at point $E$. Suppose that $C'D = \dfrac{1}{3}$. What is the perimeter of triangle $\triangle AEC'$?

(The square has $A$ at upper-left, $B$ at lower-left, $C$ at lower-right, $D$ at upper-right. After folding, $C$ lands on $AD$ at $C'$ with $C'D = 1/3$, so $AC' = 2/3$. The fold brings edge $BC$ to a new position; the image of $B$ under the fold is $E$ on edge $AB$.)
</Problem>

<Solution>
Set coordinates $A=(0,1)$, $B=(0,0)$, $C=(1,0)$, $D=(1,1)$. Since $C'D=\tfrac{1}{3}$, we have $C'=(2/3,1)$ on $\overline{AD}$.

The fold is the perpendicular bisector of $\overline{CC'}$. The midpoint of $CC'$ is $M=(5/6,\,1/2)$ and the direction of $CC'$ is $(-1/3,1)$, so the fold line has direction $(3,1)$ and passes through $M$.

To find $E$, reflect $C=(1,0)$'s preimage $B=(0,0)$ across the fold line, then find where the image segment crosses $\overline{AB}$ (the edge $x=0$). Reflecting $B$ across the fold line: parameterize the line as $(5/6+3t,\,1/2+t)$; the foot of the perpendicular from $B=(0,0)$ satisfies

$$3(5/6+3t)+(1/2+t)=0 \implies 3+10t=0 \implies t=-\tfrac{3}{10},$$

giving foot $=(-1/15,\,1/5)$ and reflected image $B'=(-2/15,\,2/5)$.

The folded edge is the segment $B'C'$. Parameterize $B'C'$: direction $(2/3-(-2/15),\,1-2/5)=(4/5,\,3/5)$. Setting $x=0$ gives parameter $t=1/6$, so $E=(0,\,1/2)$.

Now compute the side lengths of $\triangle AEC'$ with $A=(0,1)$, $E=(0,1/2)$, $C'=(2/3,1)$:

$$AE = \tfrac{1}{2}, \quad AC' = \tfrac{2}{3}, \quad EC' = \sqrt{\left(\tfrac{2}{3}\right)^2+\left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right)^2} = \sqrt{\tfrac{16}{36}+\tfrac{9}{36}} = \tfrac{5}{6}.$$

$$\text{Perimeter} = \tfrac{1}{2}+\tfrac{2}{3}+\tfrac{5}{6} = \tfrac{3+4+5}{6} = \tfrac{12}{6} = 2.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
