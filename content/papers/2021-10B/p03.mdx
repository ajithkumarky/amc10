<Problem>
In an after-school program for juniors and seniors, there is a debate team with an equal number of students from each class on the team. Among the $28$ students in the program, $25\%$ of the juniors and $10\%$ of the seniors are on the debate team. How many juniors are in the program?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $j$ be the number of juniors and $s$ the number of seniors. We are told:

$$j + s = 28$$

The number of juniors on the debate team is $0.25j$ and the number of seniors is $0.10s$. Since these are equal:

$$0.25j = 0.10s \implies s = \frac{0.25}{0.10}j = 2.5j.$$

Substituting into the first equation:

$$j + 2.5j = 28 \implies 3.5j = 28 \implies j = 8.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
