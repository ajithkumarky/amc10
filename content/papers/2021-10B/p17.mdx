<Problem>
Ravon, Oscar, Aditi, Tyrone, and Kim play a card game. Each person is given $2$ cards out of a set of $10$ cards numbered $1, 2, 3, \ldots, 10.$ The score of a player is the sum of the numbers of their cards. The scores of the players are as follows: Ravon — $11$, Oscar — $4$, Aditi — $7$, Tyrone — $16$, Kim — $17$. Which of the following statements is true?

$\textbf{(A)}\ \text{Ravon was given card 3.}$

$\textbf{(B)}\ \text{Aditi was given card 3.}$

$\textbf{(C)}\ \text{Ravon was given card 4.}$

$\textbf{(D)}\ \text{Aditi was given card 4.}$

$\textbf{(E)}\ \text{Tyrone was given card 7.}$
</Problem>

<Solution>
The five scores sum to $11 + 4 + 7 + 16 + 17 = 55 = 1 + 2 + \cdots + 10$ ✓, confirming each card is used exactly once.

**Determine Oscar's cards.** Oscar scores $4$; the only pair from $\{1,\ldots,10\}$ summing to $4$ is $\{1, 3\}$.

So Oscar has cards $1$ and $3$.

**Determine Aditi's cards.** Aditi scores $7$; possible pairs from the remaining $\{2,4,5,6,7,8,9,10\}$ summing to $7$: $\{2,5\}$ or $\{3,4\}$ — but $3$ is taken, so $\{2, 5\}$.

So Aditi has cards $2$ and $5$.

**Determine Ravon's cards.** Ravon scores $11$; remaining cards: $\{4,6,7,8,9,10\}$. Pairs summing to $11$: $\{4,7\}$, $\{\mathbf{3},8\}$ (taken), $\{2,9\}$ (taken), $\{1,10\}$ (taken). Only $\{4, 7\}$ works.

So Ravon has cards $4$ and $7$.

**Verify Tyrone and Kim.** Remaining: $\{6, 8, 9, 10\}$. Tyrone scores $16$: pairs from these summing to $16$: $\{6,10\}$ or $\{7,9\}$ (taken). So $\{6,10\}$. Kim gets $\{8,9\}$, sum $= 17$ ✓.

**Check the answer choices:**
- (A) Ravon was given card 3. — False (Ravon has 4 and 7).
- (B) Aditi was given card 3. — False (Aditi has 2 and 5).
- (C) Ravon was given card 4. — **True** ✓.
- (D) Aditi was given card 4. — False.
- (E) Tyrone was given card 7. — False (Tyrone has 6 and 10).

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
