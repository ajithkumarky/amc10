<Problem>
The ages of Jonie's four cousins are distinct single-digit positive integers. Two of the cousins' ages multiplied together give $24$, while the other two multiply to $30$. What is the sum of the ages of Jonie's four cousins?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We need two pairs of distinct single-digit positive integers, one pair with product $24$ and the other with product $30$, with all four values distinct.

Pairs of distinct single-digit positive integers with product $24$:
- $3 \times 8 = 24$
- $4 \times 6 = 24$

Pairs of distinct single-digit positive integers with product $30$:
- $5 \times 6 = 30$

Now we need all four ages to be distinct:

- $\{3, 8\}$ and $\{5, 6\}$: all four values $3, 8, 5, 6$ are distinct. Sum $= 22$.
- $\{4, 6\}$ and $\{5, 6\}$: the value $6$ repeats, so this is invalid.

The only valid combination is $\{3, 8, 5, 6\}$, with sum $3 + 5 + 6 + 8 = 22$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
