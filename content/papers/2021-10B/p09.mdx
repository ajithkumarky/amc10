<Problem>
The point $P(a,b)$ in the $xy$-plane is first rotated counterclockwise by $90^\circ$ around the point $(1,5)$ and then reflected about the line $y = -x$. The image of $P$ after these two transformations is at $(-6,3)$. What is $b - a$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We work backwards from the final image to recover $P$.

**Step 1: Undo the reflection.**
Reflection about the line $y = -x$ maps $(x, y) \mapsto (-y, -x)$, and this map is its own inverse. The image after reflection is $(-6, 3)$, so the point before reflection was:
$$(-6, 3) \xrightarrow{\text{undo reflection}} (-3,\;-(-6)) \cdot \text{ [apply } (x,y)\mapsto(-y,-x)] = (-3, 6).$$
So after the rotation (but before the reflection), the point was $P' = (-3, 6)$.

**Step 2: Undo the CCW $90°$ rotation about $(1,5)$.**
A CCW $90°$ rotation about $(c_x, c_y)$ sends $(x,y)$ to:
$$(c_x - (y - c_y),\; c_y + (x - c_x)).$$
Its inverse is a CW $90°$ rotation about the same center:
$$(x, y) \mapsto (c_x + (y - c_y),\; c_y - (x - c_x)).$$

Applying the inverse rotation to $P' = (-3, 6)$ with $(c_x, c_y) = (1, 5)$:
$$a = 1 + (6 - 5) = 2, \qquad b = 5 - (-3 - 1) = 9.$$

**Verification:** Rotate $P = (2, 9)$ by $90°$ CCW about $(1,5)$:
$(1 - (9-5),\; 5 + (2-1)) = (1-4,\; 5+1) = (-3, 6). \checkmark$
Reflect $(-3, 6)$ about $y = -x$: $(-6, 3). \checkmark$

Therefore $b - a = 9 - 2 = 7$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
