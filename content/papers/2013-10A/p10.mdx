<Problem>
A flower bouquet contains pink roses, red roses, pink carnations, and red carnations. One third of the pink flowers are roses, three fourths of the red flowers are carnations, and six tenths of the flowers are pink. What percent of the flowers are carnations?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the total number of flowers be $100$.

- Pink flowers: $60\%$ of $100 = 60$.
- Red flowers: $40\%$ of $100 = 40$.

Pink flowers breakdown:
- Pink roses: $\frac{1}{3} \times 60 = 20$.
- Pink carnations: $60 - 20 = 40$.

Red flowers breakdown:
- Red carnations: $\frac{3}{4} \times 40 = 30$.
- Red roses: $40 - 30 = 10$.

Total carnations $= 40 + 30 = 70$.

So $70\%$ of the flowers are carnations.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
