<Problem>
Kris and his five brothers are ages 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, and 13. One afternoon two of his brothers whose ages sum to 16 went to the movies, two brothers younger than 10 went to play baseball, and Kris and the 5-year-old stayed home. How old is Kris?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We have six ages: 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13. Each person goes to exactly one activity.

**Movies:** Two brothers whose ages sum to 16. Looking at pairs: $3+13=16$. So the 3- and 13-year-olds went to the movies.

**Baseball:** Two brothers younger than 10. The remaining ages younger than 10 (after removing 3 and 13) are 5, 7, and 9. But Kris and the 5-year-old stayed home, so 5 is taken. The two who went to baseball must be from 7 and 9.

**Home:** Kris and the 5-year-old. The only remaining age after assigning 3, 13, 7, and 9 is 11. So Kris is 11.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
