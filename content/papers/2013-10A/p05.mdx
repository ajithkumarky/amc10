<Problem>
Tom, Dorothy, and Hammy went on a vacation and agreed to split the costs evenly. During their trip Tom paid $\$105$, Dorothy paid $\$125$, and Hammy paid $\$175$. In order to share costs equally, Tom gave Hammy $t$ dollars, and Dorothy gave Hammy $d$ dollars. What is $t - d$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Total cost $= 105 + 125 + 175 = \$405$. Each person's fair share is $\$405 / 3 = \$135$.

- Tom paid $\$105$, so he owes $135 - 105 = \$30$ more. Thus $t = 30$.
- Dorothy paid $\$125$, so she owes $135 - 125 = \$10$ more. Thus $d = 10$.

Therefore $t - d = 30 - 10 = 20$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
