<Problem>
In a recent basketball game, Shenille attempted only three-point shots and two-point shots. She was successful on $20\%$ of her three-point shots and $30\%$ of her two-point shots. Shenille attempted $30$ shots. How many points did she score?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $x$ = number of three-point shots attempted, and $30 - x$ = number of two-point shots attempted.

Points scored $= 3 \cdot (0.20 \cdot x) + 2 \cdot (0.30 \cdot (30-x))$
$= 0.6x + 0.6(30 - x)$
$= 0.6x + 18 - 0.6x$
$= 18$.

Remarkably, the $x$ terms cancel! No matter how she splits her attempts between two-point and three-point shots, she always scores $18$ points.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
