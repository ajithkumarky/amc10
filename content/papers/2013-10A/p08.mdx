<Problem>
What is the value of $\dfrac{2^{2014}+2^{2012}}{2^{2014}-2^{2012}}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Factor $2^{2012}$ from both numerator and denominator:

$$\frac{2^{2014}+2^{2012}}{2^{2014}-2^{2012}} = \frac{2^{2012}(2^{2}+1)}{2^{2012}(2^{2}-1)} = \frac{4+1}{4-1} = \frac{5}{3}.$$

The key insight is that $2^{2012}$ cancels completely, leaving a simple arithmetic fraction.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
