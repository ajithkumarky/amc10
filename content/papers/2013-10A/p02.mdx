<Problem>
Alice is making a batch of cookies and needs $2\dfrac{1}{2}$ cups of sugar. Unfortunately, her measuring cup holds only $\dfrac{1}{4}$ cup of sugar. How many times must she fill that cup to get the correct amount of sugar?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Alice needs $2\frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{2}$ cups of sugar. Each fill of her measuring cup gives $\frac{1}{4}$ cup.

Number of fills $= \dfrac{5/2}{1/4} = \dfrac{5}{2} \times 4 = 10$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
