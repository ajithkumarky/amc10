<Problem>
A taxi ride costs $\$1.50$ plus $\$0.25$ per mile traveled. How much does a 5-mile taxi ride cost?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The base fare is $\$1.50$. For 5 miles at $\$0.25$ per mile, the mileage charge is $5 \times 0.25 = \$1.25$.

Total cost: $1.50 + 1.25 = \$2.75$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
