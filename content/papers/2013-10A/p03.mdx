<Problem>
Square $ABCD$ has side length $10$. Point $E$ is on $\overline{BC}$, and the area of $\triangle ABE$ is $40$. What is $BE$?

Square $ABCD$ with $A$ at lower-left, $B$ at upper-left, $C$ at upper-right, $D$ at lower-right. Point $E$ is on $\overline{BC}$ between $B$ and $C$.

<Solution>
Triangle $ABE$ has base $AB = 10$ (one side of the square) and height $BE$ (the horizontal distance from $B$ to $E$ along $\overline{BC}$).

Area $= \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot AB \cdot BE = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 10 \cdot BE = 5 \cdot BE$.

Setting this equal to $40$: $5 \cdot BE = 40$, so $BE = 8$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
