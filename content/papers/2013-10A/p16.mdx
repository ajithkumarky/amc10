<Problem>
A triangle with vertices $(6, 5)$, $(8, -3)$, and $(9, 1)$ is reflected about the line $x = 8$ to create a second triangle. What is the area of the union of the two triangles?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Setup.** Label $A = (6,5)$, $B = (8,-3)$, $C = (9,1)$. Reflecting $(x,y)$ across $x=8$ gives $(16-x,y)$, so the reflected triangle has vertices $A'=(10,5)$, $B'=(8,-3)$, $C'=(7,1)$. Note $B' = B$ since $B$ lies on the line of reflection.

**Area of each triangle.** By the cross-product formula with $\vec{AB}=(2,-8)$ and $\vec{AC}=(3,-4)$:
$$\text{Area} = \tfrac{1}{2}|2(-4)-(-8)(3)| = \tfrac{1}{2}|-8+24| = 8.$$
The reflected triangle has the same area $8$.

**Intersection of the two triangles.** We identify where the boundaries cross.

- Parameterize edge $AB$: $(6+2t,\,5-8t)$. Setting $x=7$ gives $t=\tfrac{1}{2}$, $y=1$, so $C'=(7,1)$ lies on edge $AB$.
- Parameterize edge $A'B'$: $(10-2t,\,5-8t)$. Setting $x=9$ gives $t=\tfrac{1}{2}$, $y=1$, so $C=(9,1)$ lies on edge $A'B'$.
- Parameterize edge $CA$: from $C=(9,1)$ to $A=(6,5)$, direction $(-3,4)$. Setting $x=8$ gives $t=\tfrac{1}{3}$, $y=\tfrac{7}{3}$, so $P=\bigl(8,\tfrac{7}{3}\bigr)$ lies on $CA$.
- Parameterize edge $C'A'$: from $C'=(7,1)$ to $A'=(10,5)$. Setting $x=8$ gives $t=\tfrac{1}{3}$, $y=\tfrac{7}{3}$, so $P$ also lies on $C'A'$.

The intersection region is the quadrilateral with vertices $B=(8,-3)$, $C=(9,1)$, $P=\bigl(8,\tfrac{7}{3}\bigr)$, $C'=(7,1)$, bounded by edges of both triangles.

**Area of intersection** (shoelace on $B,C,P,C'$):
$$\text{Area} = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl|x_B(y_C - y_{C'}) + x_C(y_P - y_B) + x_P(y_{C'} - y_C) + x_{C'}(y_B - y_P)\bigr|$$
$$= \tfrac{1}{2}\left|8(1-1) + 9\!\left(\tfrac{7}{3}+3\right) + 8(1-1) + 7\!\left(-3-\tfrac{7}{3}\right)\right|$$
$$= \tfrac{1}{2}\left|9\cdot\tfrac{16}{3} + 7\cdot\left(-\tfrac{16}{3}\right)\right| = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfrac{(9-7)\cdot 16}{3} = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfrac{32}{3} = \tfrac{16}{3}.$$

**Area of union:**
$$8 + 8 - \frac{16}{3} = \frac{48-16}{3} = \boxed{\dfrac{32}{3}}.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
