<Problem>
Kiana has two older twin brothers. The product of their three ages is $128$. What is the sum of their three ages?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Since $128 = 2^7$, all three ages must be powers of $2$. Let the twins' age be $t$ and Kiana's age be $k$, with $t > k$ (the brothers are older). We need $t^2 \cdot k = 128$.

Testing powers of 2:
- $t = 8, k = 2$: $64 \cdot 2 = 128$. ✓ Sum $= 8 + 8 + 2 = 18$.
- $t = 4, k = 8$: gives $k > t$, so Kiana would be older — not valid.
- $t = 16, k = \tfrac{1}{2}$: not a positive integer age.

The only valid factoring is $t = 8, k = 2$, giving a sum of $18$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
