<Problem>
A flagpole is originally $5$ meters tall. A hurricane snaps the flagpole at a point $x$ meters above the ground so that the upper part, still attached to the stump, touches the ground $1$ meter away from the base. What is $x$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The stump has height $x$, the fallen upper part has length $5 - x$, and it reaches the ground $1$ meter from the base. This forms a right triangle with legs $x$ (vertical) and $1$ (horizontal), and hypotenuse $5 - x$.

By the Pythagorean theorem:
$$x^2 + 1^2 = (5-x)^2.$$
$$x^2 + 1 = 25 - 10x + x^2.$$
$$1 = 25 - 10x.$$
$$10x = 24.$$
$$x = 2.4.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
