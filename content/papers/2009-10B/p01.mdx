<Problem>
Each morning of her five-day workweek, Jane bought either a $50$-cent muffin or a $75$-cent bagel. Her total cost for the week was a whole number of dollars. How many bagels did she buy?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $b$ be the number of bagels. Then she bought $5 - b$ muffins. Total cost in cents:
$$75b + 50(5 - b) = 25b + 250.$$

For the total to be a whole number of dollars, it must be divisible by $100$. So we need $25b + 250 \equiv 0 \pmod{100}$, i.e., $25b \equiv -250 \equiv 50 \pmod{100}$, i.e., $b \equiv 2 \pmod{4}$.

With $0 \le b \le 5$, the only solution is $b = 2$. Check: $25(2) + 250 = 300$ cents $= \$3.00$. ✓

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
