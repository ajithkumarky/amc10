<Problem>
As shown below, convex pentagon $ABCDE$ has sides $AB=3$, $BC=4$, $CD=6$, $DE=3$, and $EA=7$. The pentagon is originally positioned in the plane with vertex $A$ at the origin and vertex $B$ on the positive $x$-axis. The pentagon is then rolled clockwise to the right along the $x$-axis. Which side will touch the point $x=2009$ on the $x$-axis?
</Problem>

<Solution>
As the pentagon rolls along the $x$-axis, each side touches the $x$-axis in turn. Starting with $A$ at the origin and $B$ on the positive $x$-axis, the sequence of contact sides as the pentagon rolls right is $AB, BC, CD, DE, EA$, then repeats.

The perimeter is $3 + 4 + 6 + 3 + 7 = 23$.

After one full revolution, the pentagon has moved $23$ units. The point $x = 2009$ lies at position $2009 \pmod{23}$.

$2009 = 23 \times 87 + 8$, since $23 \times 87 = 2001$ and $2009 - 2001 = 8$.

So $x = 2009$ is equivalent to $x = 8$ in the first revolution (starting from $x = 0$).

The sides touch the $x$-axis in these intervals:
- $AB$: $x \in [0, 3]$
- $BC$: $x \in [3, 7]$
- $CD$: $x \in [7, 13]$
- $DE$: $x \in [13, 16]$
- $EA$: $x \in [16, 23]$

Since $x = 8$ falls in $[7, 13]$, the side $\overline{CD}$ is touching the $x$-axis at $x = 2009$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
