<Problem>
Paula the painter had just enough paint for $30$ identically sized rooms. Unfortunately, on the way to work, three cans of paint fell off her truck, so she had only enough paint for $25$ rooms. How many cans of paint did she use for the $25$ rooms?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $n$ be the original number of cans. Since all rooms are identical, each room requires $\dfrac{n}{30}$ cans of paint.

After losing 3 cans, she has $n - 3$ cans, enough for 25 rooms:
$$n - 3 = 25 \cdot \frac{n}{30} = \frac{5n}{6}.$$

Solving: $n - \dfrac{5n}{6} = 3$, so $\dfrac{n}{6} = 3$, giving $n = 18$.

She used $n - 3 = 18 - 3 = 15$ cans for the 25 rooms.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
