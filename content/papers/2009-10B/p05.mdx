<Problem>
Twenty percent less than $60$ is one-third more than what number?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Twenty percent less than $60$ is $60 \times 0.80 = 48$.

"One-third more than a number $x$" means $x + \tfrac{1}{3}x = \tfrac{4}{3}x$.

Setting $\tfrac{4}{3}x = 48$ gives $x = 48 \times \tfrac{3}{4} = 36$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
