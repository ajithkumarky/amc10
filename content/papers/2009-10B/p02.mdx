<Problem>
Which of the following is equal to $\dfrac{\frac{1}{3}-\frac{1}{4}}{\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{3}}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Compute numerator and denominator separately.

Numerator: $\dfrac{1}{3} - \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{4-3}{12} = \dfrac{1}{12}$.

Denominator: $\dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{3-2}{6} = \dfrac{1}{6}$.

Dividing: $\dfrac{1/12}{1/6} = \dfrac{1}{12} \times 6 = \dfrac{6}{12} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
