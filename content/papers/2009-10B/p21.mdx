<Problem>
What is the remainder when $3^0 + 3^1 + 3^2 + \cdots + 3^{2009}$ is divided by $8$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First, find $3^k \pmod{8}$ for small $k$:
- $3^0 = 1$
- $3^1 = 3$
- $3^2 = 9 \equiv 1 \pmod{8}$
- $3^3 \equiv 3 \pmod{8}$

The pattern repeats with period $2$: odd powers of $3$ are $\equiv 3 \pmod 8$, even powers are $\equiv 1 \pmod 8$.

The sum $S = 3^0 + 3^1 + \cdots + 3^{2009}$ has $2010$ terms. Group them in pairs:
$$(3^0 + 3^1) + (3^2 + 3^3) + \cdots + (3^{2008} + 3^{2009}).$$

There are $1005$ pairs, each congruent to $1 + 3 = 4 \pmod{8}$.

$$S \equiv 1005 \times 4 \pmod{8}.$$

Since $1005 = 125 \times 8 + 5$, we have $1005 \equiv 5 \pmod{8}$, so $1005 \times 4 \equiv 5 \times 4 = 20 \equiv 4 \pmod{8}$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
