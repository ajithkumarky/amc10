<Problem>
Members of the Rockham Soccer League buy socks and T-shirts. Socks cost $\textdollar 4$ per pair and each T-shirt costs $\textdollar 5$ more than a pair of socks. Each member needs one pair of socks and a shirt for home games and another pair of socks and a shirt for away games. If the total cost is $\textdollar 2366$, how many members are in the League?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Each T-shirt costs $4 + 5 = \textdollar 9$. Each member needs two pairs of socks and two T-shirts, so the cost per member is:
$$2(4) + 2(9) = 8 + 18 = \textdollar 26.$$

The number of members is $\frac{2366}{26} = 91$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
