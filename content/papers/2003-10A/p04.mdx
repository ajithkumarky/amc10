<Problem>
It takes Mary $30$ minutes to walk uphill $1$ km from her home to school, but it takes her only $10$ minutes to walk from school to her home along the same route. What is her average speed, in km/hr, for the round trip?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Average speed equals total distance divided by total time. Mary walks $1$ km each way, so the total distance is $2$ km. The total time is $30 + 10 = 40$ minutes $= \frac{40}{60} = \frac{2}{3}$ hours.

$$\text{Average speed} = \frac{2}{\frac{2}{3}} = 2 \times \frac{3}{2} = 3 \text{ km/hr}.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
