<Problem>
Pat is to select six cookies from a tray containing only chocolate chip, oatmeal, and peanut butter cookies. There are at least six of each of these three kinds of cookies on the tray. How many different assortments of six cookies can be selected?
</Problem>

<Solution>
This is a stars-and-bars problem. Let $c$, $o$, $p$ be the number of chocolate chip, oatmeal, and peanut butter cookies chosen. We need:
$$c + o + p = 6, \quad c, o, p \geq 0.$$

The number of non-negative integer solutions is $\dbinom{6 + 3 - 1}{3 - 1} = \dbinom{8}{2} = 28$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
