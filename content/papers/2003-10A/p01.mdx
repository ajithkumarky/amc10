<Problem>
What is the difference between the sum of the first $2003$ even counting numbers and the sum of the first $2003$ odd counting numbers?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The first $2003$ even counting numbers are $2, 4, 6, \ldots, 4006$, with sum $\sum_{k=1}^{2003} 2k = 2 \cdot \frac{2003 \cdot 2004}{2} = 2003 \cdot 2004$.

The first $2003$ odd counting numbers are $1, 3, 5, \ldots, 4005$, with sum $\sum_{k=1}^{2003}(2k-1) = 2003^2$.

The difference is:
$$2003 \cdot 2004 - 2003^2 = 2003(2004 - 2003) = 2003 \cdot 1 = 2003.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
