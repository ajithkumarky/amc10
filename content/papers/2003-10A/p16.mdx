<Problem>
What is the units digit of $13^{2003}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The units digit of a power of $13$ depends only on the units digit of $3$, since the units digit of a product depends only on the units digits of the factors.

The units digits of powers of $3$ cycle with period $4$:
$$3^1 \equiv 3,\quad 3^2 \equiv 9,\quad 3^3 \equiv 7,\quad 3^4 \equiv 1 \pmod{10}, \quad 3^5 \equiv 3, \ldots$$

To find the units digit of $13^{2003}$, find $2003 \pmod{4}$:
$$2003 = 4 \times 500 + 3, \quad \text{so } 2003 \equiv 3 \pmod{4}.$$

Therefore $13^{2003}$ has the same units digit as $3^3 = 27$, which is $7$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
