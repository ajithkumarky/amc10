<Problem>
A solid box is $15$ cm by $10$ cm by $8$ cm. A new solid is formed by removing a cube $3$ cm on a side from each corner of this box. What percent of the original volume is removed?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The original volume is $15 \times 10 \times 8 = 1200$ cubic cm.

The box has $8$ corners, and a $3 \times 3 \times 3$ cube is removed from each. However, each cube is entirely within the corner and the cubes do not overlap (since the smallest dimension is $8$ and $3 + 3 = 6 < 8$, so cubes on opposite faces along any direction don't overlap). The total removed volume is:
$$8 \times 3^3 = 8 \times 27 = 216 \text{ cubic cm}.$$

The percent removed is:
$$\frac{216}{1200} \times 100 = 18\%.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
