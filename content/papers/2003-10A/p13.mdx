<Problem>
The sum of three numbers is $20$. The first is four times the sum of the other two. The second is seven times the third. What is the product of all three?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the three numbers be $a$, $b$, $c$.

From the given conditions:
1. $a + b + c = 20$
2. $a = 4(b + c)$
3. $b = 7c$

From (1) and (2): $4(b + c) + b + c = 20$, so $5(b + c) = 20$, giving $b + c = 4$.

Then $a = 4 \cdot 4 = 16$.

From (3) and $b + c = 4$: $7c + c = 4$, so $8c = 4$, giving $c = \frac{1}{2}$ and $b = \frac{7}{2}$.

The product is:
$$a \cdot b \cdot c = 16 \cdot \frac{7}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 16 \cdot \frac{7}{4} = 28.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
