<Problem>
Simplify $\sqrt[3]{x\sqrt[3]{x\sqrt[3]{x\sqrt{x}}}}$.
</Problem>

<Solution>
Work from the inside out, writing everything as a power of $x$.

**Innermost:** $\sqrt{x} = x^{1/2}$.

**Next layer:** $x \cdot \sqrt{x} = x \cdot x^{1/2} = x^{3/2}$, then $\sqrt[3]{x^{3/2}} = x^{1/2}$.

**Next layer:** $x \cdot x^{1/2} = x^{3/2}$, then $\sqrt[3]{x^{3/2}} = x^{1/2}$.

**Outermost:** $x \cdot x^{1/2} = x^{3/2}$, then $\sqrt[3]{x^{3/2}} = x^{1/2} = \sqrt{x}$.

More carefully, let's track the exponent. Define the expression from inside out:
- Level 0: $x^{1/2}$
- Level 1: $\bigl(x \cdot x^{1/2}\bigr)^{1/3} = x^{(3/2)/3} = x^{1/2}$
- Level 2: $\bigl(x \cdot x^{1/2}\bigr)^{1/3} = x^{1/2}$
- Level 3 (outermost): $\bigl(x \cdot x^{1/2}\bigr)^{1/3} = x^{1/2}$

The result is $x^{1/2} = \sqrt{x}$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
