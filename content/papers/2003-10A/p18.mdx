<Problem>
What is the sum of the reciprocals of the roots of the equation
$$\frac{2003}{2004}x+1+\frac{1}{x}=0?$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
Multiply through by $x$ to clear the denominator (note $x \neq 0$):
$$\frac{2003}{2004}x^2 + x + 1 = 0.$$

Let the roots be $r$ and $s$. By Vieta's formulas:
$$r + s = -\frac{1}{2003/2004} = -\frac{2004}{2003}, \quad rs = \frac{1}{2003/2004} = \frac{2004}{2003}.$$

The sum of the reciprocals is:
$$\frac{1}{r} + \frac{1}{s} = \frac{r+s}{rs} = \frac{-2004/2003}{2004/2003} = -1.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
