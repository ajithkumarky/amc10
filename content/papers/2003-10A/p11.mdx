<Problem>
The sum of the two 5-digit numbers $AMC10$ and $AMC12$ is $123422$. What is $A+M+C$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Here $AMC10$ and $AMC12$ represent 5-digit numbers where $A$, $M$, $C$ are digits. In place-value notation:
$$AMC10 = 10000A + 1000M + 100C + 10, \quad AMC12 = 10000A + 1000M + 100C + 12.$$

Their sum is:
$$2(10000A + 1000M + 100C) + 22 = 123422.$$

So:
$$2(10000A + 1000M + 100C) = 123400,$$
$$10000A + 1000M + 100C = 61700.$$

Therefore $A = 6$, $M = 1$, $C = 7$, and $A + M + C = 6 + 1 + 7 = 14$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
