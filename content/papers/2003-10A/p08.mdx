<Problem>
What is the probability that a randomly drawn positive factor of $60$ is less than $7$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First find all positive factors of $60 = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$. The number of factors is $(2+1)(1+1)(1+1) = 12$.

The factors of $60$ are: $1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 12, 15, 20, 30, 60$.

Those less than $7$ are: $1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6$ — that's $6$ factors.

The probability is $\dfrac{6}{12} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
