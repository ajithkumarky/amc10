<Problem>
The number of inches in the perimeter of an equilateral triangle equals the number of square inches in the area of its circumscribed circle. What is the radius, in inches, of the circle?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the side length of the equilateral triangle be $s$ and the circumradius be $R$.

**Circumradius of an equilateral triangle:** For an equilateral triangle with side $s$, the circumradius is $R = \dfrac{s}{\sqrt{3}}$.

**Perimeter:** $P = 3s$.

**Area of circumscribed circle:** $\pi R^2$.

Setting perimeter equal to circle area:
$$3s = \pi R^2.$$

Since $s = R\sqrt{3}$:
$$3R\sqrt{3} = \pi R^2.$$

Divide both sides by $R$ (assuming $R \neq 0$):
$$3\sqrt{3} = \pi R.$$

$$R = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{\pi}.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
