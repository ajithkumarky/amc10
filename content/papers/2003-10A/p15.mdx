<Problem>
What is the probability that an integer in the set $\{1,2,3,\ldots,100\}$ is divisible by $2$ and not divisible by $3$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Count integers from $1$ to $100$ that are divisible by $2$ but not by $3$.

- Divisible by $2$: $\lfloor 100/2 \rfloor = 50$ numbers.
- Divisible by both $2$ and $3$ (i.e., by $6$): $\lfloor 100/6 \rfloor = 16$ numbers.

Divisible by $2$ but not $3$: $50 - 16 = 34$.

The probability is $\dfrac{34}{100} = \dfrac{17}{50}$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
