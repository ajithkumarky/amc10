<Problem>
A $252^\circ$ sector of a circle of radius $10$ is rolled up by aligning the two straight sides to form a cone. Which cone is formed?
</Problem>

<Solution>
When the sector is rolled into a cone, the radius of the sector ($10$) becomes the slant height of the cone. So the slant height is $l = 10$.

The arc length of the $252^\circ$ sector equals the circumference of the base of the cone.

Arc length $= \dfrac{252}{360} \times 2\pi(10) = \dfrac{7}{10} \times 20\pi = 14\pi$.

This equals $2\pi r$ where $r$ is the base radius:
$$2\pi r = 14\pi \implies r = 7.$$

So the cone has slant height $10$ and base radius $7$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
