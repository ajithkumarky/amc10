<Problem>
A number $x$ is $2$ more than the product of its reciprocal and its additive inverse. In which interval does the number lie?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The reciprocal of $x$ is $\frac{1}{x}$ and the additive inverse of $x$ is $-x$. Their product is $\frac{1}{x} \cdot (-x) = -1$.

So the condition says:
$$x = -1 + 2 = 1.$$

Since $x = 1$, and $0 < 1 \leq 2$, the number lies in the interval $0 < x \leq 2$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
