<Problem>
A regular octagon is formed by cutting an isosceles right triangle from each of the four corners of a square with sides of length $2000$. What is the length of each side of the octagon?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the leg of each removed right triangle be $x$. The hypotenuse of such an isosceles right triangle is $x\sqrt{2}$.

The regular octagon has all sides equal. The sides of the octagon alternate between:
- The hypotenuse of a removed triangle: length $x\sqrt{2}$.
- The remaining middle portion of the square's side: length $2000 - 2x$.

For a regular octagon, all sides are equal:
$$x\sqrt{2} = 2000 - 2x.$$
$$x\sqrt{2} + 2x = 2000.$$
$$x(\sqrt{2} + 2) = 2000.$$
$$x = \frac{2000}{\sqrt{2} + 2} = \frac{2000}{2 + \sqrt{2}} \cdot \frac{2 - \sqrt{2}}{2 - \sqrt{2}} = \frac{2000(2 - \sqrt{2})}{4 - 2} = \frac{2000(2 - \sqrt{2})}{2} = 1000(2 - \sqrt{2}).$$

The side length of the octagon is:
$$x\sqrt{2} = 1000(2 - \sqrt{2})\sqrt{2} = 1000(2\sqrt{2} - 2) = 2000(\sqrt{2} - 1).$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
