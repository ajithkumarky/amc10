<Problem>
A right circular cylinder with its diameter equal to its height is inscribed in a right circular cone. The cone has diameter $10$ and altitude $12$, and the axes of the cylinder and cone coincide. Find the radius of the cylinder.
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $r$ be the radius of the cylinder. Since the diameter equals the height, the height of the cylinder is $2r$.

The cone has base radius $5$ and height $12$. Taking a cross-section through the axis, the cone appears as a triangle: the apex is at height $12$ directly above the center of the base.

The slant side of the cone goes from the apex $(0, 12)$ down to the base edge $(5, 0)$. The equation of this slant edge is:
$$\frac{x}{5} + \frac{y}{12} = 1 \implies y = 12\left(1 - \frac{x}{5}\right).$$

The top rim of the inscribed cylinder is at radius $r$ and height $2r$ (the cylinder's top face). This rim must touch the cone's slant side:
$$2r = 12\left(1 - \frac{r}{5}\right).$$
$$2r = 12 - \frac{12r}{5}.$$
$$2r + \frac{12r}{5} = 12.$$
$$\frac{10r + 12r}{5} = 12.$$
$$\frac{22r}{5} = 12.$$
$$r = \frac{60}{22} = \frac{30}{11}.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
