<Problem>
The state income tax where Kristin lives is levied at the rate of $p\%$ of the first $\textdollar 28000$ of annual income plus $(p + 2)\%$ of any amount above $\textdollar 28000$. Kristin noticed that the state income tax she paid amounted to $(p + 0.25)\%$ of her annual income. What was her annual income?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let Kristin's annual income be $I > 28000$. Her tax is:
$$\text{Tax} = \frac{p}{100}(28000) + \frac{p+2}{100}(I - 28000).$$

This equals $(p + 0.25)\%$ of $I$:
$$\frac{p+0.25}{100} \cdot I = \frac{p \cdot 28000 + (p+2)(I-28000)}{100}.$$

Multiply through by 100:
$$(p + 0.25)I = 28000p + (p+2)(I - 28000).$$

Expand the right side:
$$28000p + pI - 28000p + 2I - 56000 = pI + 2I - 56000.$$

So:
$$(p + 0.25)I = pI + 2I - 56000.$$
$$0.25I = 2I - 56000.$$
$$56000 = 1.75I.$$
$$I = \frac{56000}{1.75} = 32000.$$

Kristin's annual income was $\textdollar 32{,}000$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
