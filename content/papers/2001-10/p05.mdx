<Problem>
The twelve pentominoes are the twelve distinct shapes made by joining five unit squares edge-to-edge. How many of the twelve pentominoes have at least one line of reflectional symmetry?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The twelve pentominoes are traditionally labeled F, I, L, N, P, T, U, V, W, X, Y, Z.

Checking each for reflectional symmetry (a line that maps the shape onto itself):

- **I**: symmetric (vertical axis through the middle). ✓
- **T**: symmetric (vertical axis through the top square). ✓
- **U**: symmetric (vertical axis through the middle column). ✓
- **V**: symmetric (diagonal axis at 45°). ✓
- **W**: symmetric (diagonal axis). ✓
- **X**: symmetric (multiple axes). ✓

The asymmetric ones (no line of symmetry) are: F, L, N, P, Y, Z — that is 6 pentominoes without symmetry.

So the number with at least one line of reflectional symmetry is $12 - 6 = 6$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
