<Problem>
What is the maximum number of possible points of intersection of a circle and a triangle?
</Problem>

<Solution>
A triangle has 3 sides, each of which is a line segment. A circle can intersect any line in at most 2 points, so a circle can intersect any single side of the triangle in at most 2 points.

With 3 sides, the maximum number of intersections is $3 \times 2 = 6$.

This maximum is achievable: imagine a circle large enough to cross each of the three sides twice (entering and exiting through each side). For example, a circle centered at the centroid with just the right radius will cross all three sides twice.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
