<Problem>
The sum of two numbers is $S$. Suppose $3$ is added to each number and then each of the resulting numbers is doubled. What is the sum of the final two numbers?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the two numbers be $a$ and $b$, so $a + b = S$.

After adding 3 to each: the numbers become $a + 3$ and $b + 3$.

After doubling each: the numbers become $2(a+3)$ and $2(b+3)$.

Their sum is:
$$2(a+3) + 2(b+3) = 2a + 6 + 2b + 6 = 2(a+b) + 12 = 2S + 12.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
