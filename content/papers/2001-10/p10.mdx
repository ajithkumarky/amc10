<Problem>
If $x$, $y$, and $z$ are positive with $xy = 24$, $xz = 48$, and $yz = 72$, then $x + y + z$ is
</Problem>

<Solution>
Multiply all three equations together:
$$(xy)(xz)(yz) = 24 \cdot 48 \cdot 72.$$
$$(xyz)^2 = 24 \cdot 48 \cdot 72.$$

Compute: $24 \cdot 48 = 1152$ and $1152 \cdot 72 = 82944$.

So $(xyz)^2 = 82944$, giving $xyz = \sqrt{82944} = 288$.

Now solve for each variable:
$$z = \frac{xyz}{xy} = \frac{288}{24} = 12, \quad y = \frac{xyz}{xz} = \frac{288}{48} = 6, \quad x = \frac{xyz}{yz} = \frac{288}{72} = 4.$$

Therefore $x + y + z = 4 + 6 + 12 = 22$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
