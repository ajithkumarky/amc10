<Problem>
When the decimal point of a certain positive decimal number is moved four places to the right, the new number is four times the reciprocal of the original number. What is the original number?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the original number be $x$. Moving the decimal point four places to the right multiplies by $10^4 = 10000$, giving $10000x$.

The condition says $10000x = \dfrac{4}{x}$.

Multiplying both sides by $x$:
$$10000x^2 = 4 \implies x^2 = \frac{4}{10000} = \frac{1}{2500} \implies x = \frac{1}{50} = 0.02.$$

(We take the positive root since $x > 0$.)

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
