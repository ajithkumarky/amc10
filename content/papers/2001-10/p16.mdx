<Problem>
The mean of three numbers is $10$ more than the least of the numbers and $15$ less than the greatest. The median of the three numbers is $5$. What is their sum?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the three numbers in increasing order be $a \leq b \leq c$, so the median is $b = 5$.

Let the mean be $m$. Then:
- $m = a + 10$ (mean is 10 more than least), so $a = m - 10$.
- $m = c - 15$ (mean is 15 less than greatest), so $c = m + 15$.

The sum of the three numbers is $3m$:
$$a + b + c = 3m$$
$$(m - 10) + 5 + (m + 15) = 3m$$
$$2m + 10 = 3m$$
$$m = 10.$$

So $a = 0$, $b = 5$, $c = 25$, and the sum is $3m = 3 \times 10 = 30$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
