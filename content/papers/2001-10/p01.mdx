<Problem>
The median of the list $n, n + 3, n + 4, n + 5, n + 6, n + 8, n + 10, n + 12, n + 15$ is $10$. What is the mean?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The list has 9 terms, so the median is the 5th term when arranged in order. The list is already in increasing order, so the 5th term is $n + 6 = 10$, giving $n = 4$.

The mean is the sum of all terms divided by 9. The sum is:
$$9n + (3 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 8 + 10 + 12 + 15) = 9n + 63.$$

With $n = 4$: $9(4) + 63 = 36 + 63 = 99$, so the mean is $99 \div 9 = 11$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
