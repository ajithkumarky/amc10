<Problem>
In the magic square shown, the sums of the numbers in each row, column, and diagonal are the same. Five of these numbers are represented by $v$, $w$, $x$, $y$, and $z$. Find $y + z$.

The $3 \times 3$ magic square has the following entries (row by row, top to bottom):
- Row 3 (top): $v$, $24$, $w$
- Row 2 (middle): $18$, $x$, $y$
- Row 1 (bottom): $25$, $z$, $21$
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Find the magic sum.**

The bottom row gives: $25 + z + 21 = 46 + z$.

We need another equation. The known entries are $25$, $24$, $21$, $18$, and the magic sum $M$ satisfies $3M = $ (sum of all 9 entries).

From the bottom row: $M = 46 + z$.
From the middle row: $18 + x + y = M$.
From the top row: $v + 24 + w = M$.

**Use the diagonal.** The main diagonal (top-left to bottom-right): $v + x + 21 = M$.
The anti-diagonal (top-right to bottom-left): $w + x + 25 = M$.

From these: $v + x + 21 = w + x + 25$, so $v = w + 4$.

**Use columns.**
- Left column: $v + 18 + 25 = M \implies v + 43 = M$.
- Right column: $w + y + 21 = M$.
- Middle column: $24 + x + z = M$.

From the left column: $M = v + 43$.
From the bottom row: $M = 46 + z$.

So $v + 43 = 46 + z \implies v = z + 3$.

From main diagonal: $v + x + 21 = M = v + 43 \implies x + 21 = 43 \implies x = 22$.

From middle column: $24 + 22 + z = M = 46 + z$. This gives $46 + z = 46 + z$. ✓ (consistent but no new info).

From middle row: $18 + 22 + y = M = 46 + z \implies 40 + y = 46 + z \implies y = z + 6$.

From right column: $w + y + 21 = 46 + z$.

Since $v = w + 4$ and $v = z + 3$: $w = v - 4 = z + 3 - 4 = z - 1$.

Substitute: $(z - 1) + (z + 6) + 21 = 46 + z$.
$$2z + 26 = 46 + z \implies z = 20.$$

Therefore $y = z + 6 = 26$, and:
$$y + z = 26 + 20 = 46.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
