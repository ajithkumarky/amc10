<Problem>
Tyrone had $97$ marbles and Eric had $11$ marbles. Tyrone then gave some of his marbles to Eric so that Tyrone ended with twice as many marbles as Eric. How many marbles did Tyrone give to Eric?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The total number of marbles is $97 + 11 = 108$, and this stays constant. After the exchange, Tyrone has twice what Eric has, so Eric has $\dfrac{108}{3} = 36$ marbles and Tyrone has $72$.

Tyrone gave away $97 - 72 = 25$ marbles.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
