<Problem>
Marvin had a birthday on Tuesday, May 27 in the leap year $2008$. In what year will his birthday next fall on a Saturday?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The day of the week for May 27 advances by 1 in a normal year and by 2 in a leap year (when Feb 29 of that year falls between the two May 27 dates, which happens when the destination year is a leap year).

Starting from Tuesday in 2008:

| Year | Shift | Day |
|------|-------|-----|
| 2009 | +1 (not leap) | Wednesday |
| 2010 | +1 | Thursday |
| 2011 | +1 | Friday |
| 2012 | +2 (2012 is leap) | Sunday |
| 2013 | +1 | Monday |
| 2014 | +1 | Tuesday |
| 2015 | +1 | Wednesday |
| 2016 | +2 (2016 is leap) | Friday |
| 2017 | +1 | **Saturday** |

May 27, 2017 falls on a Saturday.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
