<Problem>
Angelina drove at an average rate of $80$ km/h and then stopped $20$ minutes for gas. After the stop, she drove at an average rate of $100$ km/h. Altogether she drove $250$ km in a total trip time of $3$ hours including the stop. Which equation could be used to solve for the time $t$ in hours that she drove before her stop?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $t$ be the driving time before the stop (in hours). The stop is $20$ minutes $= \dfrac{1}{3}$ hour, so the driving time after the stop is $3 - \dfrac{1}{3} - t = \dfrac{8}{3} - t$ hours.

Distance before stop: $80t$. Distance after stop: $100\!\left(\dfrac{8}{3} - t\right)$. Total distance:
$$80t + 100\!\left(\dfrac{8}{3} - t\right) = 250.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
