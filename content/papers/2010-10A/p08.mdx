<Problem>
Tony works $2$ hours a day and is paid $\$0.50$ per hour for each full year of his age. During a six month period Tony worked $50$ days and earned $\$630$. How old was Tony at the end of the six month period?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Tony's daily earnings are $2 \times 0.50 \times (\text{age}) = \text{age}$ dollars per day (in dollars, with age in years).

Over $50$ days he earned $50 \times \text{age} = 630$, so his age was $12.6$ full years. Since age must be a whole number and is measured in full years, Tony must have turned $13$ during the six-month period — he was $12$ for part of it and $13$ for the rest, with a weighted average of $12.6$. At the end of the period his age was $13$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
