<Problem>
For positive numbers $x$ and $y$ the operation $x \spadesuit y$ is defined as
$$x \spadesuit y = x - \dfrac{1}{y}$$
What is $2 \spadesuit (2 \spadesuit 2)$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First compute the inner expression: $2 \spadesuit 2 = 2 - \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{2}$.

Then $2 \spadesuit \dfrac{3}{2} = 2 - \dfrac{1}{3/2} = 2 - \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{4}{3}$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
