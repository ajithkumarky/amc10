<Problem>
The area of a circle whose circumference is $24\pi$ is $k\pi$. What is the value of $k$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
From the circumference $2\pi r = 24\pi$, we get $r = 12$. The area is $\pi r^2 = 144\pi$, so $k = 144$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
