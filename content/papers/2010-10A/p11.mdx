<Problem>
The length of the interval of solutions of the inequality $a \le 2x + 3 \le b$ is $10$. What is $b - a$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Subtracting $3$ from all parts: $a - 3 \le 2x \le b - 3$. Dividing by $2$: $\dfrac{a-3}{2} \le x \le \dfrac{b-3}{2}$.

The length of this interval is $\dfrac{b-3}{2} - \dfrac{a-3}{2} = \dfrac{b-a}{2} = 10$, so $b - a = 20$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
