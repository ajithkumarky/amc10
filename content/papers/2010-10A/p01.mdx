<Problem>
Mary's top book shelf holds five books with the following widths, in centimeters: $6$, $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $1$, $2.5$, and $10$. What is the average book width, in centimeters?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The sum of the widths is $6 + \dfrac{1}{2} + 1 + 2.5 + 10 = 20$. Dividing by $5$ books gives an average of $\dfrac{20}{5} = 4$ centimeters.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
