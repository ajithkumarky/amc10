<Problem>
Eight points are chosen on a circle, and chords are drawn connecting every pair of points. No three chords intersect in a single point inside the circle. How many triangles with all three vertices in the interior of the circle are created?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Each interior intersection point of chords is formed by exactly one pair of crossing chords, which corresponds to exactly one set of 4 points on the circle (the 4 endpoints of the two chords). Conversely, any 4 points on the circle determine exactly one interior intersection.

A triangle in the interior has its three vertices at three interior intersection points. Each interior intersection point is determined by a 4-element subset of the 8 circle-points. Three intersection points form a triangle if and only if their three corresponding 4-element subsets together use exactly 6 distinct circle-points (since the three chords of the triangle must mutually intersect, which requires the 6 endpoints to be arranged so each pair of chords crosses).

In fact, any 6 points on the circle determine exactly one interior triangle: label the 6 points in order around the circle; the three chords connecting opposite points in the hexagon they form create one interior triangle. More precisely, a set of 6 circle-points yields $\binom{6}{2}/3 = 5$ ... 

The key fact: any $6$ points on the circle produce exactly one triangle with all three vertices in the interior. This is because choosing 6 points and pairing them as three crossing chords uniquely determines a triangle (the number of ways to partition 6 points into 3 crossing pairs is 15, but only 1 of those 15 pairings creates a triangle where all three intersection points form a triangle — actually it's exactly $\binom{6}{2\,2\,2}/\text{symmetry}$ ...).

The correct combinatorial fact: the number of interior triangles equals $\binom{8}{6} = 28$, since each set of 6 points on the circle determines exactly one such triangle (the intersections of the three "long" chords of the convex hexagon they form).

To verify: from 6 points in convex position, label them $1,2,3,4,5,6$ around the circle. The three chords $13$, $25$, $46$... do these pairwise cross? Chord $13$ and chord $25$: is one endpoint of each chord on each arc defined by the other? Points around circle: $1,2,3,4,5,6$. Chord $13$ separates $\{2\}$ from $\{4,5,6\}$. Chord $25$ has $2$ and $5$ on opposite arcs of chord $13$: $2$ is between $1$ and $3$, $5$ is not. Yes, they cross. Similarly all three pairs cross, giving one interior triangle per 6-point set.

Therefore the answer is $\binom{8}{6} = \boxed{28}$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
