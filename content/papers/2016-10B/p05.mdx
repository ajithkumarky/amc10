<Problem>
The mean age of Amanda's $4$ cousins is $8$, and their median age is $5$. What is the sum of the ages of Amanda's youngest and oldest cousins?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Since the mean of 4 ages is 8, their total is $4 \times 8 = 32$.

The median of 4 values is the average of the 2nd and 3rd values when sorted. Let the ages in order be $a \le b \le c \le d$. The median is $\dfrac{b+c}{2} = 5$, so $b + c = 10$.

Therefore $a + d = 32 - (b + c) = 32 - 10 = 22$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
