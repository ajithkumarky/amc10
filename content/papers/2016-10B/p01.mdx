<Problem>
What is the value of $\dfrac{2a^{-1}+\dfrac{a^{-1}}{2}}{a}$ when $a= \tfrac{1}{2}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Substitute $a = \tfrac{1}{2}$, so $a^{-1} = 2$.

The numerator is $2 \cdot 2 + \dfrac{2}{2} = 4 + 1 = 5$.

The denominator is $a = \tfrac{1}{2}$.

So the expression equals $\dfrac{5}{1/2} = 5 \cdot 2 = 10$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
