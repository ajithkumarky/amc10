<Problem>
The ratio of the measures of two acute angles is $5:4$, and the complement of one of these two angles is twice as large as the complement of the other. What is the sum of the degree measures of the two angles?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the two acute angles be $5k$ and $4k$ for some positive value $k$.

Their complements are $90 - 5k$ and $90 - 4k$.

We're told one complement is twice the other. Since $90 - 4k > 90 - 5k$ (the smaller angle has the larger complement), we set:

$$90 - 4k = 2(90 - 5k).$$

$$90 - 4k = 180 - 10k$$

$$6k = 90$$

$$k = 15.$$

The two angles are $5(15) = 75°$ and $4(15) = 60°$. Both are acute. Their sum is $75 + 60 = 135°$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
