<Problem>
If $n \heartsuit m = n^3 m^2$, what is $\dfrac{2 \heartsuit 4}{4 \heartsuit 2}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Compute each part using the definition $n \heartsuit m = n^3 m^2$.

$2 \heartsuit 4 = 2^3 \cdot 4^2 = 8 \cdot 16 = 128$.

$4 \heartsuit 2 = 4^3 \cdot 2^2 = 64 \cdot 4 = 256$.

So $\dfrac{2 \heartsuit 4}{4 \heartsuit 2} = \dfrac{128}{256} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
