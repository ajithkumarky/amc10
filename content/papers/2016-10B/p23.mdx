<Problem>
In regular hexagon $ABCDEF$, points $W$, $X$, $Y$, and $Z$ are chosen on sides $\overline{BC}$, $\overline{CD}$, $\overline{EF}$, and $\overline{FA}$ respectively, so lines $AB$, $ZW$, $YX$, and $ED$ are parallel and equally spaced. What is the ratio of the area of hexagon $WCXYF Z$ to the area of hexagon $ABCDEF$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Place the regular hexagon with side length $1$ so that $AB$ is the top edge and $ED$ is the bottom edge, both horizontal. Using standard coordinates:
$$A = \!\left(-\tfrac{1}{2},\, \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right),\; B = \!\left(\tfrac{1}{2},\, \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right),\; C = (1, 0),\; D = \!\left(\tfrac{1}{2},\, -\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right),\; E = \!\left(-\tfrac{1}{2},\, -\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right),\; F = (-1, 0).$$

The four equally spaced parallel lines are at heights $y = \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ (line $AB$), $y = \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{6}$ (line $ZW$), $y = -\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{6}$ (line $YX$), and $y = -\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ (line $ED$), dividing the total height $\sqrt{3}$ into three equal strips.

**Finding $W$:** $W$ lies on $BC$ from $B = (\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})$ to $C = (1, 0)$. Parametrize: $y = \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}(1-t)$. Setting $y = \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{6}$ gives $t = \tfrac{2}{3}$, so $W = (\tfrac{5}{6}, \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{6})$. By symmetry $Z = (-\tfrac{5}{6}, \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{6})$, giving $ZW = \tfrac{5}{3}$.

**Area of trapezoid $ABWZ$** (the region cut off at the top):
$$\text{Area} = \tfrac{1}{2}(AB + ZW) \cdot h = \tfrac{1}{2}\!\left(1 + \tfrac{5}{3}\right)\cdot\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3} = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfrac{8}{3}\cdot\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3} = \tfrac{4\sqrt{3}}{9}.$$

By symmetry, the bottom trapezoid $YEDX$ has the same area $\tfrac{4\sqrt{3}}{9}$.

The total hexagon area is $\tfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}$. The ratio of each trapezoid to the hexagon is:
$$\frac{4\sqrt{3}/9}{3\sqrt{3}/2} = \frac{4}{9}\cdot\frac{2}{3} = \frac{8}{27}.$$

The two trapezoids together account for $\tfrac{16}{27}$ of the hexagon, so:
$$\frac{[WCXYFZ]}{[ABCDEF]} = 1 - \frac{16}{27} = \frac{11}{27}.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
