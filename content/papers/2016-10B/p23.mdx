<Problem>
In regular hexagon $ABCDEF$, points $W$, $X$, $Y$, and $Z$ are chosen on sides $\overline{BC}$, $\overline{CD}$, $\overline{EF}$, and $\overline{FA}$ respectively, so lines $AB$, $ZW$, $YX$, and $ED$ are parallel and equally spaced. What is the ratio of the area of hexagon $WCXYF Z$ to the area of hexagon $ABCDEF$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Place the regular hexagon with center at the origin, with vertices at angles $0°, 60°, 120°, \ldots$ Let the side length be $3$ (chosen for convenient arithmetic).

Set up coordinates with $AB$ and $ED$ horizontal. Label:

Let the hexagon have vertices at unit distances with $A$ at top-left. For a regular hexagon of side $s$, the distance between parallel sides is $s\sqrt{3}$.

The four parallel lines $AB$, $ZW$, $YX$, $ED$ divide the hexagon into three equal strips (equally spaced). The total height (distance from $AB$ to $ED$) spans 3 units if we set the strip width to $1$ unit.

Place the hexagon with $AB$ at height $y = 3$ and $ED$ at height $y = 0$. The four lines are at heights $y = 3, 2, 1, 0$.

For a regular hexagon with $AB$ at top and $ED$ at bottom (both horizontal), the left and right sides slope. Using side length $s$, set the hexagon with $AB$ from $(-s/2, s\sqrt{3})$ to $(s/2, s\sqrt{3})$, with $E = (-s, 0)$, $D = (s, 0)$.

Let $s = 1$. Total height $= \sqrt{3}$. Three equal strips of height $\sqrt{3}/3 = 1/\sqrt{3}$.

Trapezoid $ABWZ$: $AB = 3$, $ZW = ?$ At $1/3$ of the way from $AB$ to $ED$, the parallel strip width in a regular hexagon scales linearly: at fraction $t$ from the top, width $= 3 + 3t$ (for $t \in [0, 1/2]$, the hexagon widens from $3$ to $6$; for $t \in [1/2, 1]$ it narrows back).
