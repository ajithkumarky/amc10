<Problem>
What is the tens digit of $2015^{2016}-2017$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We need the tens digit, so we work modulo $100$.

First, find $2015^{2016} \pmod{100}$. Since $2015 \equiv 15 \pmod{100}$, we need $15^{2016} \pmod{100}$.

Notice $15^2 = 225 \equiv 25 \pmod{100}$, and $15^3 = 15 \cdot 25 = 375 \equiv 75 \pmod{100}$... wait, let's be more careful.

$15^1 \equiv 15$, $15^2 = 225 \equiv 25$, $15^3 = 15 \cdot 25 = 375 \equiv 75$, $15^4 = 15 \cdot 75 = 1125 \equiv 25 \pmod{100}$.

So for $n \ge 2$: $15^n \equiv 25 \pmod{100}$ when $n$ is even, and $15^n \equiv 75 \pmod{100}$ when $n$ is odd.

Since $2016$ is even, $15^{2016} \equiv 25 \pmod{100}$.

Thus $2015^{2016} \equiv 25 \pmod{100}$.

Now $2017 \equiv 17 \pmod{100}$.

$2015^{2016} - 2017 \equiv 25 - 17 = 8 \pmod{100}$.

So the last two digits are $08$, meaning the tens digit is $\mathbf{0}$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
