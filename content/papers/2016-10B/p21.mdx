<Problem>
What is the area of the region enclosed by the graph of the equation $x^2 + y^2 = |x| + |y|$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Rewrite the equation by completing the square. Consider the first quadrant ($x \ge 0$, $y \ge 0$) where $|x| = x$ and $|y| = y$:

$$x^2 - x + y^2 - y = 0 \implies \left(x - \tfrac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \left(y - \tfrac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \tfrac{1}{2}.$$

This is a circle centered at $\left(\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{1}{2}\right)$ with radius $r = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$.

By symmetry, we get four such circles in the four quadrants:
- Q1: center $\left(\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{1}{2}\right)$
- Q2: center $\left(-\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{1}{2}\right)$
- Q3: center $\left(-\tfrac{1}{2}, -\tfrac{1}{2}\right)$
- Q4: center $\left(\tfrac{1}{2}, -\tfrac{1}{2}\right)$

Each circle has radius $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ and is centered at distance $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ from the origin.

Since the center of each circle is at distance equal to its radius from the origin, each circle passes through the origin.

Adjacent circles (e.g., Q1 and Q2) have centers at distance $\sqrt{(1/2+1/2)^2 + 0} = 1$ apart. Since each radius is $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \approx 0.707$, and $1 < 2 \times \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} = \sqrt{2}$, adjacent circles overlap.

**Area computation:** The total region is the union of the four circles. Each circle has area $\pi r^2 = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Four circles: total $2\pi$.

Adjacent circles overlap: two circles of radius $r = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ with centers distance $d = 1$ apart. The intersection area of two circles is $2r^2 \cos^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{d}{2r}\right) - \dfrac{d}{2}\sqrt{4r^2 - d^2}$.

Here $\dfrac{d}{2r} = \dfrac{1}{2 \cdot (1/\sqrt{2})} = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$, so $\cos^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right) = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

$4r^2 - d^2 = 2 - 1 = 1$, so $\sqrt{4r^2 - d^2} = 1$.

Intersection area $= 2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{\pi}{4} - \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 1 = \dfrac{\pi}{4} - \dfrac{1}{2}$.

There are $4$ pairs of adjacent circles (arranged in a ring), each contributing this overlap. But at the center (where all four meet), we also need to check overlap — all four circles pass through the origin but do not create a quadruple overlap region beyond a single point.

By inclusion-exclusion (4 circles in a symmetric ring, with each pair of adjacent circles overlapping and no triple overlaps except at origin):

Area $= 4 \cdot \dfrac{\pi}{2} - 4 \cdot \left(\dfrac{\pi}{4} - \dfrac{1}{2}\right) = 2\pi - \pi + 2 = \pi + 2$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
