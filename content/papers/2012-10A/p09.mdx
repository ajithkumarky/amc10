<Problem>
A pair of six-sided dice are labeled so that one die has only even numbers (two each of $2$, $4$, and $6$), and the other die has only odd numbers (two each of $1$, $3$, and $5$). The pair of dice is rolled. What is the probability that the sum of the numbers on the tops of the two dice is $7$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Each die has 6 faces. The total number of equally likely outcomes is $6 \times 6 = 36$.

The even die shows 2, 2, 4, 4, 6, 6 and the odd die shows 1, 1, 3, 3, 5, 5. For a sum of 7, the pairs (even, odd) that work are: $(2,5)$, $(4,3)$, $(6,1)$.

- $(2, 5)$: even die shows 2 in 2 ways, odd die shows 5 in 2 ways $\Rightarrow 4$ outcomes.
- $(4, 3)$: even die shows 4 in 2 ways, odd die shows 3 in 2 ways $\Rightarrow 4$ outcomes.
- $(6, 1)$: even die shows 6 in 2 ways, odd die shows 1 in 2 ways $\Rightarrow 4$ outcomes.

Total favorable outcomes: $4 + 4 + 4 = 12$.

Probability $= \dfrac{12}{36} = \dfrac{1}{3}$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
