<Problem>
A square with side length $8$ is cut in half, creating two congruent rectangles. What are the dimensions of one of these rectangles?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Cutting a square with side length 8 in half (parallel to one side) produces two rectangles each with one dimension equal to 8 and the other equal to $8 \div 2 = 4$. The dimensions are $4$ by $8$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
