<Problem>
Chubby makes nonstandard checkerboards that have $31$ squares on each side. The checkerboards have a black square in every corner and alternate red and black squares along every row and column. How many black squares are there on such a checkerboard?
</Problem>

<Solution>
On a standard checkerboard with an odd side length $n$, the coloring alternates with black in the corners. With $n = 31$ (odd):

- In odd-numbered rows (1, 3, 5, …, 31): the row starts and ends with black, so it contains $\lceil 31/2 \rceil = 16$ black squares.
- In even-numbered rows (2, 4, 6, …, 30): the row starts and ends with red, so it contains $\lfloor 31/2 \rfloor = 15$ black squares.

There are $\lceil 31/2 \rceil = 16$ odd rows and $\lfloor 31/2 \rfloor = 15$ even rows.

Total black squares $= 16 \times 16 + 15 \times 15 = 256 + 225 = 481$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
