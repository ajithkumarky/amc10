<Problem>
Cagney can frost a cupcake every 20 seconds and Lacey can frost a cupcake every $30$ seconds. Working together, how many cupcakes can they frost in $5$ minutes?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Cagney's rate is $\frac{1}{20}$ cupcakes per second and Lacey's rate is $\frac{1}{30}$ cupcakes per second. Together their combined rate is:
$$\frac{1}{20} + \frac{1}{30} = \frac{3}{60} + \frac{2}{60} = \frac{5}{60} = \frac{1}{12} \text{ cupcakes per second.}$$

In 5 minutes = 300 seconds, they frost $300 \times \frac{1}{12} = 25$ cupcakes.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
