<Problem>
In a bag of marbles, $\frac{3}{5}$ of the marbles are blue and the rest are red. If the number of red marbles is doubled and the number of blue marbles stays the same, what fraction of the marbles will be red?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Suppose there are 5 marbles total: 3 blue and 2 red. After doubling the red marbles, there are still 3 blue but now 4 red, for a total of 7 marbles. The fraction that are red is $\frac{4}{7}$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
