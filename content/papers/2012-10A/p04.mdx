<Problem>
Let $\angle ABC = 24^\circ$ and $\angle ABD = 20^\circ$. What is the smallest possible degree measure for angle $CBD$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Ray $BD$ and ray $BC$ both emanate from point $B$. The angle between them is $|{}\angle ABC - \angle ABD{}| = |24° - 20°| = 4°$ when ray $BD$ lies inside $\angle ABC$, or their sum when they are on opposite sides. The minimum is achieved when one ray is between the other and ray $BA$, giving $\angle CBD = 4°$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
