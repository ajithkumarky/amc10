<Problem>
The product of two positive numbers is $9$. The reciprocal of one of these numbers is $4$ times the reciprocal of the other number. What is the sum of the two numbers?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the two positive numbers be $a$ and $b$. We have:
$$ab = 9 \quad \text{and} \quad \frac{1}{a} = \frac{4}{b}.$$

From the second equation: $b = 4a$. Substituting into the first:
$$a(4a) = 9 \implies 4a^2 = 9 \implies a^2 = \frac{9}{4} \implies a = \frac{3}{2}.$$

Then $b = 4 \cdot \frac{3}{2} = 6$, and the sum is $a + b = \frac{3}{2} + 6 = \frac{15}{2}$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
