<Problem>
The sums of three whole numbers taken in pairs are $12$, $17$, and $19$. What is the middle number?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the three numbers be $x \le y \le z$. The three pairwise sums are $x+y$, $x+z$, and $y+z$. Their total is $2(x+y+z) = 12+17+19 = 48$, so $x+y+z = 24$.

The largest pairwise sum is $y + z = 19$, so $x = 24 - 19 = 5$.
The smallest pairwise sum is $x + y = 12$, so $z = 24 - 12 = 12$.
Then $y = 24 - 5 - 12 = 7$.

The middle number is $y = 7$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
