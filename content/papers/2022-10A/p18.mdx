<Problem>
Let $T_k$ be the transformation of the coordinate plane that first rotates the plane $k$ degrees counterclockwise around the origin and then reflects the plane across the $y$-axis. What is the least positive integer $n$ such that performing the sequence of transformations $T_1, T_2, T_3, \ldots, T_n$ returns the point $(1,0)$ back to itself?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Track the angle.** Represent a point by its angle $\theta$ from the positive $x$-axis (at unit distance from the origin, which is preserved by both operations). Let $\theta_0 = 0$ (starting angle of $(1,0)$).

Each transformation $T_k$ does:
1. Rotate by $k°$: $\theta \mapsto \theta + k$.
2. Reflect across the $y$-axis: $\theta \mapsto 180° - \theta$.

So $T_k$ sends $\theta \mapsto 180° - (\theta + k) = 180° - k - \theta$.

**Compose the transformations.** Let $\theta_j$ be the angle after applying $T_1, T_2, \ldots, T_j$. We have:

$$\theta_j = 180° - j - \theta_{j-1}.$$

This recurrence alternates the "flip" behavior. Let's unroll it:

$$\theta_j = (180° - j) - \theta_{j-1}.$$

Let $S_j = 1+2+\cdots+j = \frac{j(j+1)}{2}$.

For odd $j$: since each step flips the sign of the accumulated angle, we get

$$\theta_j = 180°(1-1+1-\cdots) - (j - (j-1) + (j-2) - \cdots) + \text{(sign flip of }\theta_0).$$

Let's just compute directly. $\theta_0 = 0$.

$$\theta_1 = 180 - 1 - 0 = 179.$$
$$\theta_2 = 180 - 2 - 179 = -1.$$
$$\theta_3 = 180 - 3 - (-1) = 178.$$
$$\theta_4 = 180 - 4 - 178 = -2.$$

Pattern: $\theta_{2m-1} = 180 - m$ and $\theta_{2m} = -m$ (in degrees, mod 360).

Check: $\theta_1 = 179 = 180-1$, $\theta_2 = -1$, $\theta_3 = 178 = 180-2$, $\theta_4 = -2$. Yes!

**Verify the pattern.** Assume $\theta_{2m} = -m$.
$$\theta_{2m+1} = 180 - (2m+1) - (-m) = 180 - 2m - 1 + m = 179 - m = 180-(m+1).$$
$$\theta_{2m+2} = 180 - (2m+2) - (179-m) = 180 - 2m - 2 - 179 + m = -m - 1 = -(m+1). \checkmark$$

**Find when the point returns to $(1,0)$, i.e., $\theta \equiv 0 \pmod{360}$.**

- Odd case: $\theta_{2m-1} = 180-m \equiv 0 \pmod{360}$ means $m \equiv 180 \pmod{360}$. Smallest $m = 180$, giving $n = 2(180)-1 = 359$.
- Even case: $\theta_{2m} = -m \equiv 0 \pmod{360}$ means $m \equiv 0 \pmod{360}$. Smallest $m = 360$, giving $n = 720$.

The smallest is $n = 359$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
