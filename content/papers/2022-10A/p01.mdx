<Problem>
What is the value of
$$3+\frac{1}{3+\frac{1}{3+\frac{1}{3}}}?$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
Work from the inside out.

The innermost fraction: $3 + \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{10}{3}$.

Next level: $3 + \dfrac{1}{\frac{10}{3}} = 3 + \dfrac{3}{10} = \dfrac{33}{10}$.

Outermost: $3 + \dfrac{1}{\frac{33}{10}} = 3 + \dfrac{10}{33} = \dfrac{99}{33} + \dfrac{10}{33} = \dfrac{109}{33}$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
