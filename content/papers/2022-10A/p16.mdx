<Problem>
The roots of the polynomial $10x^3 - 39x^2 + 29x - 6$ are the height, length, and width of a rectangular box (right rectangular prism). A new rectangular box is formed by lengthening each edge of the original box by $2$ units. What is the volume of the new box?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the roots be $p$, $q$, $r$ (the dimensions of the original box). By Vieta's formulas for $10x^3 - 39x^2 + 29x - 6$:

$$p + q + r = \frac{39}{10}, \quad pq + pr + qr = \frac{29}{10}, \quad pqr = \frac{6}{10} = \frac{3}{5}.$$

The new box has dimensions $p+2$, $q+2$, $r+2$, so its volume is:

$$(p+2)(q+2)(r+2).$$

Expand:

$$(p+2)(q+2)(r+2) = pqr + 2(pq+pr+qr) + 4(p+q+r) + 8.$$

Substituting the Vieta values:

$$= \frac{3}{5} + 2 \cdot \frac{29}{10} + 4 \cdot \frac{39}{10} + 8$$
$$= \frac{3}{5} + \frac{58}{10} + \frac{156}{10} + 8$$
$$= \frac{6}{10} + \frac{58}{10} + \frac{156}{10} + \frac{80}{10}$$
$$= \frac{6 + 58 + 156 + 80}{10} = \frac{300}{10} = 30.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
