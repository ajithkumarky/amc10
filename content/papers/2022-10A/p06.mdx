<Problem>
Which expression is equal to
$$\left|a-2-\sqrt{(a-1)^2}\right|$$
for $a < 0$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
First simplify $\sqrt{(a-1)^2} = |a-1|$.

Since $a < 0$, we have $a - 1 < 0$, so $|a-1| = -(a-1) = 1-a$.

The expression inside the outer absolute value becomes:
$$a - 2 - (1-a) = a - 2 - 1 + a = 2a - 3.$$

Since $a < 0$, we have $2a - 3 < 0$, so:
$$|2a-3| = -(2a-3) = 3 - 2a.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
