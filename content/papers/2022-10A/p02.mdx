<Problem>
Mike cycled $15$ laps in $57$ minutes. Assume he cycled at a constant speed throughout. Approximately how many laps did he complete in the first $27$ minutes?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Mike's rate is $\dfrac{15}{57}$ laps per minute. In $27$ minutes he completes

$$27 \times \frac{15}{57} = \frac{405}{57} = \frac{45}{6.333\ldots} \approx 7.1 \text{ laps.}$$

More precisely, $\dfrac{27 \times 15}{57} = \dfrac{405}{57} = \dfrac{45}{6.\overline{3}} \approx 7.1$, which is closest to $7$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
