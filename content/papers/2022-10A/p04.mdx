<Problem>
In some countries, automobile fuel efficiency is measured in liters per $100$ kilometers while other countries use miles per gallon. Suppose that $1$ kilometer equals $m$ miles, and $1$ gallon equals $l$ liters. Which of the following gives the fuel efficiency in liters per $100$ kilometers for a car that gets $x$ miles per gallon?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We want to convert $x$ miles per gallon into liters per 100 km.

Start with $x$ miles per gallon. Convert miles to kilometers: $1$ mile $= \dfrac{1}{m}$ km, so $x$ miles $= \dfrac{x}{m}$ km. Thus the car travels $\dfrac{x}{m}$ km per gallon.

Convert gallons to liters: $1$ gallon $= l$ liters, so the car travels $\dfrac{x}{m}$ km per $l$ liters, i.e., $\dfrac{x}{ml}$ km per liter, i.e., $\dfrac{ml}{x}$ liters per km.

For 100 km, that is $\dfrac{100ml}{x} = \dfrac{100lm}{x}$ liters.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
