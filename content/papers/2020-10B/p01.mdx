<Problem>
What is the value of $1 - (-2) - 3 - (-4) - 5 - (-6)$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Work left to right, keeping track of each sign change:

$$1 - (-2) - 3 - (-4) - 5 - (-6)$$
$$= 1 + 2 - 3 + 4 - 5 + 6$$
$$= (1 + 2 + 4 + 6) - (3 + 5)$$
$$= 13 - 8 = 5.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
