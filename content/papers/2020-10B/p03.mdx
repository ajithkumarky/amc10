<Problem>
The ratio of $w$ to $x$ is $4:3$, the ratio of $y$ to $z$ is $3:2$, and the ratio of $z$ to $x$ is $1:6$. What is the ratio of $w$ to $y$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Express everything in terms of $x$.

From $w:x = 4:3$, we get $w = \dfrac{4}{3}x$.

From $z:x = 1:6$, we get $z = \dfrac{1}{6}x$.

From $y:z = 3:2$, we get $y = \dfrac{3}{2}z = \dfrac{3}{2} \cdot \dfrac{1}{6}x = \dfrac{1}{4}x$.

Therefore:
$$\frac{w}{y} = \frac{\frac{4}{3}x}{\frac{1}{4}x} = \frac{4}{3} \cdot 4 = \frac{16}{3}.$$

So $w:y = 16:3$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
