<Problem>
How many positive even multiples of $3$ less than $2020$ are perfect squares?
</Problem>

<Solution>
We need positive integers $n$ such that:
1. $n$ is a perfect square,
2. $n$ is a multiple of $3$,
3. $n$ is even (a multiple of $2$),
4. $n < 2020$.

Conditions 2 and 3 together mean $n$ is a multiple of $\text{lcm}(2,3) = 6$. Since $n$ is also a perfect square, $n$ must be divisible by $6^2 = 36$ (every prime in the factorization must appear to an even power). So $n$ must be a multiple of $36$ that is a perfect square.

The perfect squares that are multiples of $36$ are exactly $(6k)^2 = 36k^2$ for positive integers $k$. We need:
$$36k^2 < 2020 \implies k^2 < 56.1\ldots \implies k \le 7.$$

The values are $k = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7$, giving $7$ numbers.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
