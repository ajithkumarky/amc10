<Problem>
How many ordered pairs of integers $(x, y)$ satisfy the equation
$$x^{2020} + y^2 = 2y?$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
Rearrange by completing the square in $y$:

$$x^{2020} + y^2 - 2y = 0$$
$$x^{2020} + (y - 1)^2 = 1.$$

Both $x^{2020}$ and $(y-1)^2$ are non-negative. Their sum equals $1$, so we need two non-negative values that add to $1$.

**Case A: $x^{2020} = 0$ and $(y-1)^2 = 1$.**
$x = 0$, and $y - 1 = \pm 1$, giving $y = 0$ or $y = 2$. That's $2$ solutions: $(0, 0)$ and $(0, 2)$.

**Case B: $x^{2020} = 1$ and $(y-1)^2 = 0$.**
$y = 1$, and $x^{2020} = 1$. Since the exponent $2020$ is even, $x = 1$ or $x = -1$. That's $2$ solutions: $(1, 1)$ and $(-1, 1)$.

In total there are $2 + 2 = 4$ ordered pairs.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
