<Problem>
As shown in the figure below, six semicircles lie in the interior of a regular hexagon with side length $2$ so that the diameter of each semicircle coincides with one side of the hexagon. What is the area of the shaded region — inside the hexagon but outside all of the semicircles?

(Each semicircle bulges inward; the diameter lies along one side of the hexagon.)
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Step 1: Area of the hexagon.**

A regular hexagon with side length $s = 2$ has area $\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}s^2 = 6\sqrt{3}$.

**Step 2: Each semicircle has radius 1, area $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.**

The six semicircles each have their diameter on one side (length 2), so radius 1.

**Step 3: Adjacent semicircles overlap.**

Place the hexagon with vertices at $(1,0),(3,0),(4,\sqrt{3}),(3,2\sqrt{3}),(1,2\sqrt{3}),(0,\sqrt{3})$ and center $(2,\sqrt{3})$. The bottom semicircle has center $M_1 = (2,0)$ and the adjacent lower-left semicircle has center $M_2 = (0.5, \sqrt{3}/2)$. Their center distance is $\sqrt{(1.5)^2+({\sqrt{3}}/{2})^2} = \sqrt{3} < 2$, so the two circles intersect.

Moreover, each hexagon vertex lies on both adjacent semicircles: the vertex $(1,0)$ is at distance $1$ from both $M_1$ and $M_2$. The two circles therefore intersect exactly at the shared hexagon vertex (and one interior point). The lens-shaped overlap region of the two full unit circles at distance $\sqrt{3}$ has area

$$A_{\text{lens}} = 2\arccos\!\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cdot\sqrt{4-3} = 2\cdot\frac{\pi}{6} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{\pi}{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}.$$

By the symmetry of the configuration, this entire lens lies inside both inward-pointing semicircles, so the overlap area of each adjacent pair of semicircles equals $\dfrac{\pi}{3} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

Opposite semicircles (centers distance $2\sqrt{3}$ apart) do not overlap. There are 6 adjacent pairs and no triple overlaps.

**Step 4: Inclusion-exclusion for the union of semicircles.**

$$\text{Union} = 6\cdot\frac{\pi}{2} - 6\left(\frac{\pi}{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = 3\pi - 2\pi + 3\sqrt{3} = \pi + 3\sqrt{3}.$$

**Step 5: Shaded area.**

$$\text{Shaded} = 6\sqrt{3} - (\pi + 3\sqrt{3}) = 3\sqrt{3} - \pi.$$

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
