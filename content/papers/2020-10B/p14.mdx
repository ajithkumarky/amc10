<Solution>
**Step 1: Area of the regular hexagon.**

A regular hexagon with side length $s$ has area $\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}s^2$. With $s = 2$:

$$\text{Area}_{\text{hex}} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\cdot 4 = 6\sqrt{3}.$$

**Step 2: Area of each semicircle.**

Each semicircle has diameter equal to the side length $2$, so radius $1$. The area of one semicircle is $\dfrac{\pi(1)^2}{2} = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

**Step 3: Do the semicircles overlap?**

We need to check whether any two semicircles overlap inside the hexagon. Each semicircle bulges inward from one side. The center of the hexagon is at distance $\sqrt{3}$ from each side (the apothem equals $\sqrt{3}$ when $s=2$). Since each semicircle has radius $1 < \sqrt{3}$, no semicircle reaches the center. Two semicircles from adjacent sides: the closest points of their arcs are separated by a positive distance. One can verify (by coordinates) that adjacent semicircles do not overlap each other. Thus the six semicircles are pairwise non-overlapping inside the hexagon.

**Step 4: Shaded area.**

$$\text{Shaded} = \text{Area}_{\text{hex}} - 6\cdot\frac{\pi}{2} = 6\sqrt{3} - 3\pi.$$
