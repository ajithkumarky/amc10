<Problem>
Carl has $5$ cubes each having side length $1$, and Kate has $5$ cubes each having side length $2$. What is the total volume of these $10$ cubes?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The volume of a cube with side length $s$ is $s^3$.

Carl's 5 unit cubes each have volume $1^3 = 1$, so Carl's total volume is $5 \times 1 = 5$.

Kate's 5 cubes each have volume $2^3 = 8$, so Kate's total volume is $5 \times 8 = 40$.

The grand total is $5 + 40 = 45$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
