<Problem>
A three-quarter sector of a circle of radius $4$ inches together with its interior can be rolled up to form the lateral surface area of a right circular cone by taping together along the two radii shown. What is the volume of the cone in cubic inches?

(The sector has radius $4$ and central angle $270°$.)
</Problem>

<Solution>
When the sector is rolled into a cone, the radius of the sector ($4$ inches) becomes the **slant height** $\ell$ of the cone, and the arc length of the sector becomes the **circumference of the base** of the cone.

**Finding the base radius $r$:**

Arc length of a $270°$ sector of radius $4$:
$$\text{arc} = \frac{270}{360} \cdot 2\pi \cdot 4 = \frac{3}{4} \cdot 8\pi = 6\pi.$$

This equals the circumference of the cone's base: $2\pi r = 6\pi$, so $r = 3$.

**Finding the height $h$:**

Using the Pythagorean theorem with $\ell = 4$ and $r = 3$:
$$h = \sqrt{\ell^2 - r^2} = \sqrt{16 - 9} = \sqrt{7}.$$

**Volume of the cone:**

$$V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h = \frac{1}{3}\pi (9)\sqrt{7} = 3\pi\sqrt{7}.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
