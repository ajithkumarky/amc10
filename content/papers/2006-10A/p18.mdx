<Problem>
A license plate in a certain state consists of 4 digits, not necessarily distinct, and 2 letters, also not necessarily distinct. These six characters may appear in any order, except that the two letters must appear next to each other. How many distinct license plates are possible?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Treat the two letters as a single block. Then the plate has 5 positions: 4 digit slots and 1 letter-block slot. The letter-block can occupy any of 5 positions (positions 1–2, 2–3, 3–4, 4–5, or 5–6 in the 6-character plate), giving **5** arrangements for where the letter pair sits.

For each arrangement:
- $10^4$ ways to choose the 4 digits.
- $26^2$ ways to choose the 2 letters (ordered, since the plate shows them in a specific order).

Total: $5 \times 10^4 \times 26^2$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
