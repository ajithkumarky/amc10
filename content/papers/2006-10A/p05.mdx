<Problem>
Doug and Dave shared a pizza with 8 equally-sized slices. Doug wanted a plain pizza, but Dave wanted anchovies on half of the pizza. The cost of a plain pizza was $\textdollar 8$, and there was an additional cost of $\textdollar 2$ for putting anchovies on one half. Dave ate all of the slices of anchovy pizza and one plain slice. Doug ate the remainder. Each then paid for what he had eaten. How many more dollars did Dave pay than Doug?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The pizza has 8 slices. Anchovies go on 4 slices (half). Dave ate 4 anchovy slices + 1 plain slice = 5 slices. Doug ate 3 plain slices.

**Cost per slice:** The plain pizza costs $\textdollar 8$ for 8 slices, so each plain slice costs $\textdollar 1$. The anchovy half costs an extra $\textdollar 2$ on top of its $\textdollar 4$ base cost, so each anchovy slice costs $\dfrac{4+2}{4} = \textdollar 1.50$.

**Dave's payment:** $4 \times 1.50 + 1 \times 1.00 = 6.00 + 1.00 = \textdollar 7$.

**Doug's payment:** $3 \times 1.00 = \textdollar 3$.

**Difference:** $7 - 3 = 4$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
