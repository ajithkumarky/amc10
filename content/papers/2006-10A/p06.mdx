<Problem>
What non-zero real value for $x$ satisfies $(7x)^{14} = (14x)^7$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Expand both sides:
$$7^{14} x^{14} = 14^7 x^7.$$

Since $x \neq 0$, divide both sides by $x^7$:
$$7^{14} x^7 = 14^7.$$

Solve for $x^7$:
$$x^7 = \frac{14^7}{7^{14}} = \frac{14^7}{7^{14}} = \frac{(2 \cdot 7)^7}{7^{14}} = \frac{2^7 \cdot 7^7}{7^{14}} = \frac{2^7}{7^7} = \left(\frac{2}{7}\right)^7.$$

Therefore $x = \dfrac{2}{7}$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
