<Problem>
The ratio of Mary's age to Alice's age is $3:5$. Alice is $30$ years old. How many years old is Mary?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Since Mary's age to Alice's age is $3:5$, Mary's age equals $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of Alice's age:
$$\frac{3}{5} \times 30 = 18.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
