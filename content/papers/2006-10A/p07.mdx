<Problem>
The $8 \times 18$ rectangle $ABCD$ is cut into two congruent hexagons, as shown, in such a way that the two hexagons can be repositioned without overlap to form a square. What is $y$?

The rectangle has $A$ at top-left, $B$ at top-right, $C$ at bottom-right, $D$ at bottom-left. The width is 18 and the height is 8. A staircase cut goes from a point on the top edge (distance $y$ from $A$) straight down partway, then horizontally to the midpoint of the rectangle, then straight down to the bottom edge. A label $y$ appears at the top-left portion and again at the bottom-right portion, showing the cut creates two congruent hexagons.

<Solution>
The rectangle has area $8 \times 18 = 144$. The two hexagons rearrange to form a square, so the square has area $144$ and side length $\sqrt{144} = 12$.

When the two hexagons slide together to form a $12 \times 12$ square, the staircase cut allows one piece to shift horizontally by some amount $s$. The rectangle has height 8 and width 18; the square has side 12.

The cut runs: from a point $y$ from the left on the top edge, down to the midheight (height 4), across to a point $y$ from the right on the bottom edge (by symmetry/congruence), then down. The horizontal segment of the cut is at height 4 (the midpoint vertically), spanning from $x = y$ to $x = 18 - y$.

When the pieces are repositioned into a 12×12 square, the shift is $18 - 12 = 6$ horizontally. This shift equals $y$ (the overlap created by sliding). So $y = 6$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
