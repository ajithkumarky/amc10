<Problem>
How many four-digit positive integers have at least one digit that is a $2$ or a $3$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Use complementary counting. Count the four-digit integers with **no** digit equal to 2 or 3, then subtract from the total.

**Total four-digit integers:** $9000$ (from 1000 to 9999).

**Four-digit integers with no digit being 2 or 3:**
- The thousands digit can be 1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 — that is 7 choices (not 0, 2, or 3).
- Each of the remaining three digits can be 0, 1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 — that is 8 choices each.

Count: $7 \times 8 \times 8 \times 8 = 7 \times 512 = 3584$.

**Answer:** $9000 - 3584 = 5416$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
