<Problem>
Which of the following describes the graph of the equation $(x + y)^2 = x^2 + y^2$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Expand the left side: $(x+y)^2 = x^2 + 2xy + y^2$.

Set equal to the right side: $x^2 + 2xy + y^2 = x^2 + y^2$.

Simplify: $2xy = 0$, so $xy = 0$.

This means $x = 0$ or $y = 0$, which is exactly the two coordinate axes — two lines.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
