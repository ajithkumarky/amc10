<Problem>
The interior of a quadrilateral is bounded by the graphs of $(x + ay)^2 = 4a^2$ and $(ax - y)^2 = a^2$, where $a$ is a positive real number. What is the area of this region in terms of $a$, valid for all $a > 0$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Rewrite the equations.** Each equation represents two parallel lines:

$(x + ay)^2 = 4a^2 \Rightarrow x + ay = 2a$ or $x + ay = -2a$.

$(ax - y)^2 = a^2 \Rightarrow ax - y = a$ or $ax - y = -a$.

So the quadrilateral is bounded by four lines:
$$L_1: x + ay = 2a, \quad L_2: x + ay = -2a,$$
$$L_3: ax - y = a, \quad L_4: ax - y = -a.$$

**Identify the quadrilateral.** Lines $L_1$ and $L_2$ are parallel (direction vector $(a, -1)$, normal $(1, a)$). Lines $L_3$ and $L_4$ are parallel (direction vector $(1, a)$, normal $(a, -1)$). The two pairs of parallel lines form a parallelogram.

**Find the vertices** (intersections of $L_1$ or $L_2$ with $L_3$ or $L_4$).

$L_1 \cap L_3$: $x + ay = 2a$ and $ax - y = a$.
From $ax - y = a$: $y = ax - a$. Substitute: $x + a(ax-a) = 2a \Rightarrow x + a^2x - a^2 = 2a \Rightarrow x(1+a^2) = 2a + a^2 \Rightarrow x = \dfrac{a^2+2a}{1+a^2} = \dfrac{a(a+2)}{a^2+1}$.
$y = ax - a = \dfrac{a^2(a+2)}{a^2+1} - a = \dfrac{a^3+2a^2 - a^2 - a}{a^2+1} = \dfrac{a^3+a^2-a}{a^2+1} = \dfrac{a(a^2+a-1)}{a^2+1}$.

This is getting complex. Use the area formula for a parallelogram directly.

**Parallelogram area.** For a parallelogram bounded by $\mathbf{n_1} \cdot \mathbf{x} = \pm c_1$ and $\mathbf{n_2} \cdot \mathbf{x} = \pm c_2$ (where $\mathbf{n_1}, \mathbf{n_2}$ are the normal vectors), the area is:
$$\text{Area} = \frac{(2c_1)(2c_2)}{|\mathbf{n_1} \times \mathbf{n_2}|} = \frac{4c_1 c_2}{|\sin\theta| \cdot |\mathbf{n_1}||\mathbf{n_2}|},$$
where $\theta$ is the angle between the normal vectors.

Here: $\mathbf{n_1} = (1, a)$ with $|\mathbf{n_1}| = \sqrt{1+a^2}$, $c_1 = 2a$. $\mathbf{n_2} = (a, -1)$ with $|\mathbf{n_2}| = \sqrt{a^2+1}$, $c_2 = a$.

The cross product (2D): $\mathbf{n_1} \times \mathbf{n_2} = 1\cdot(-1) - a\cdot a = -1 - a^2$.
$|\mathbf{n_1} \times \mathbf{n_2}| = 1 + a^2$.

Area $= \dfrac{4 \cdot 2a \cdot a}{1+a^2} = \dfrac{8a^2}{a^2+1}$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
