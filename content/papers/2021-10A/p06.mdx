<Problem>
Chantal and Jean start hiking from a trailhead toward a fire tower. Jean is wearing a heavy backpack and walks slower. Chantal starts walking at $4$ miles per hour. Halfway to the tower, the trail becomes really steep, and Chantal slows down to $2$ miles per hour. After reaching the tower, she immediately turns around and descends the steep part of the trail at $3$ miles per hour. She meets Jean at the halfway point. What was Jean's average speed, in miles per hour, until they meet?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the total distance from the trailhead to the fire tower be $2d$ miles (so the halfway point is $d$ miles from the trailhead).

**Chantal's time:**
- First half (flat): distance $d$ at $4$ mph takes $d/4$ hours.
- Second half (steep, going up): distance $d$ at $2$ mph takes $d/2$ hours.
- Second half (steep, coming down): distance $d$ at $3$ mph takes $d/3$ hours.

Chantal's total time to meet Jean at the halfway point is:
$$\frac{d}{4} + \frac{d}{2} + \frac{d}{3} = d\left(\frac{3 + 6 + 4}{12}\right) = \frac{13d}{12} \text{ hours.}$$

**Jean's distance and speed:**
Jean walks from the trailhead to the halfway point, a distance of $d$ miles, in the same time $\dfrac{13d}{12}$ hours.

Jean's average speed is:
$$\frac{d}{\dfrac{13d}{12}} = \frac{12}{13} \text{ mph.}$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
