<Problem>
The quiz scores of a class with $k > 12$ students have a mean of $8$. The mean of a collection of $12$ of these quiz scores is $14$. What is the mean of the remaining quiz scores in terms of $k$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The total sum of all $k$ scores is $8k$.

The sum of the 12 selected scores is $12 \cdot 14 = 168$.

The sum of the remaining $k - 12$ scores is $8k - 168$.

The mean of the remaining scores is:
$$\frac{8k - 168}{k - 12}.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
