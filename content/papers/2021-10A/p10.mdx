<Problem>
Which of the following is equivalent to
$$(2+3)(2^2+3^2)(2^4+3^4)(2^8+3^8)(2^{16}+3^{16})(2^{32}+3^{32})(2^{64}+3^{64})?$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
The key insight is the difference-of-squares identity: $(a - b)(a + b) = a^2 - b^2$.

Multiply the product by $(3 - 2) = 1$ (which doesn't change the value) and telescoping:

$$(3 - 2)(3 + 2)(3^2 + 2^2)(3^4 + 2^4) \cdots (3^{64} + 2^{64})$$

Step by step:
- $(3-2)(3+2) = 3^2 - 2^2$
- $(3^2 - 2^2)(3^2 + 2^2) = 3^4 - 2^4$
- $(3^4 - 2^4)(3^4 + 2^4) = 3^8 - 2^8$
- Continuing: each step doubles the exponent.
- After all 7 factors: $3^{128} - 2^{128}$.

Since we multiplied by $(3-2) = 1$, the original product equals $3^{128} - 2^{128}$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
