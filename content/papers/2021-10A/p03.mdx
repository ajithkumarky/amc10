<Problem>
The sum of two natural numbers is $17{,}402$. One of the two numbers is divisible by $10$. If the units digit of that number is erased, the other number is obtained. What is the difference of these two numbers?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the smaller number be $n$. Then the larger number is $10n$ (appending a zero multiplies by 10, which is the same as erasing the units digit of $10n$ to get $n$).

Their sum is $n + 10n = 11n = 17{,}402$, so $n = 1{,}582$.

The larger number is $10n = 15{,}820$.

Their difference is $15{,}820 - 1{,}582 = 14{,}238$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
