<Problem>
Portia's high school has $3$ times as many students as Lara's high school. The two high schools have a total of $2600$ students. How many students does Portia's high school have?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $L$ be the number of students at Lara's school. Then Portia's school has $3L$ students, and together they have $L + 3L = 4L = 2600$, so $L = 650$.

Portia's school has $3 \times 650 = 1950$ students.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
