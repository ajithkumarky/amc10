<Problem>
What is the value of $(2^2-2)-(3^2-3)+(4^2-4)$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Compute each term directly:

- $2^2 - 2 = 4 - 2 = 2$
- $3^2 - 3 = 9 - 3 = 6$
- $4^2 - 4 = 16 - 4 = 12$

So the expression equals $2 - 6 + 12 = 8$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
