<Problem>
Let $ABCDEF$ be an equiangular hexagon. The lines $AB$, $CD$, and $EF$ determine a triangle with area $192\sqrt{3}$, and the lines $BC$, $DE$, and $FA$ determine a triangle with area $324\sqrt{3}$. The perimeter of hexagon $ABCDEF$ can be expressed as $m + n\sqrt{p}$, where $m$, $n$, and $p$ are positive integers and $p$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. What is $m + n + p$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Label the sides $a = AB,\, b = BC,\, c = CD,\, d = DE,\, e = EF,\, f = FA$. An equiangular hexagon (all interior angles $120°$) satisfies the closure relations $a+b = d+e$, $b+c = e+f$, $c+d = f+a$, from which $e = a+b-d$ and $f = c+d-a$.

Extending alternate pairs of sides of an equiangular hexagon produces two equilateral triangles. The triangle from lines $AB, CD, EF$ has side $s_1 = a+b+c$, and the triangle from lines $BC, DE, FA$ has side $s_2 = b+c+d$.

For an equilateral triangle with side $s$, area $= \dfrac{s^2\sqrt{3}}{4}$:

$$\frac{s_1^2\sqrt{3}}{4} = 192\sqrt{3} \implies s_1^2 = 768 \implies s_1 = 16\sqrt{3},$$
$$\frac{s_2^2\sqrt{3}}{4} = 324\sqrt{3} \implies s_2^2 = 1296 \implies s_2 = 36.$$

The perimeter is:
$$P = a+b+c+d+e+f = (a+b+c) + d + (a+b-d) + (c+d-a) = (a+b+c) + (b+c+d) = s_1 + s_2 - (b+c) + (b+c).$$

Computing directly:
$$P = (a+b+c) + d + e + f = s_1 + d + (a+b-d) + (c+d-a) = s_1 + b+c+d = 16\sqrt{3} + 36.$$

Thus $m = 36$, $n = 16$, $p = 3$, and $m+n+p = 36+16+3 = 55$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
