<Problem>
The Dunbar family consists of a mother, a father, and some children. The average age of the members of the family is $20$, the father is $48$ years old, and the average age of the mother and children is $16$. How many children are in the family?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let $n$ be the number of children, so the family has $n + 2$ members total.

The total age of all members: $(n + 2) \times 20$.

The father is 48, so the combined age of the mother and children is $(n+2) \times 20 - 48$.

The average age of the mother and children (there are $n + 1$ of them) is 16, so their combined age is $16(n + 1)$.

Setting these equal:
$$(n+2)(20) - 48 = 16(n+1)$$
$$20n + 40 - 48 = 16n + 16$$
$$4n = 24$$
$$n = 6.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
