<Problem>
Last year Mr. Jon Q. Public received an inheritance. He paid $20\%$ in federal taxes on the inheritance, and paid $10\%$ of what he had left in state taxes. He paid a total of $\textdollar 10500$ for both taxes. How many dollars was his inheritance?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the inheritance be $I$ dollars. Federal taxes: $0.20I$. After federal taxes, he has $0.80I$ left. State taxes: $0.10 \times 0.80I = 0.08I$.

Total taxes: $0.20I + 0.08I = 0.28I = 10500$.

Therefore $I = \dfrac{10500}{0.28} = 37500$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
