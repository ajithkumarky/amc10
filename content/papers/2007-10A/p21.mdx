<Problem>
A sphere is inscribed in a cube that has a surface area of $24$ square meters. A second cube is then inscribed within the sphere. What is the surface area in square meters of the inner cube?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The outer cube has surface area 24, so each of its 6 faces has area $24/6 = 4$, giving side length $2$ meters. The inscribed sphere has diameter equal to the side length, so radius $r = 1$ meter.

The inner cube is inscribed in the sphere of radius 1, meaning all 8 vertices of the inner cube lie on the sphere. If the inner cube has side length $s$, its space diagonal has length $s\sqrt{3}$, which equals the sphere's diameter $2r = 2$:
$$s\sqrt{3} = 2 \implies s = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}.$$

The surface area of the inner cube is $6s^2 = 6 \cdot \dfrac{4}{3} = 8$ square meters.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
