<Problem>
At Euclid High School, the number of students taking the AMC 10 was $60$ in 2002, $66$ in 2003, $70$ in 2004, $76$ in 2005, $78$ in 2006, and is $85$ in 2007. Between what two consecutive years was there the largest percentage increase?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Compute each year-to-year percentage increase:

- 2002 to 2003: $\dfrac{66 - 60}{60} = \dfrac{6}{60} = 10\%$
- 2003 to 2004: $\dfrac{70 - 66}{66} = \dfrac{4}{66} \approx 6.06\%$
- 2004 to 2005: $\dfrac{76 - 70}{70} = \dfrac{6}{70} \approx 8.57\%$
- 2005 to 2006: $\dfrac{78 - 76}{76} = \dfrac{2}{76} \approx 2.63\%$
- 2006 to 2007: $\dfrac{85 - 78}{78} = \dfrac{7}{78} \approx 8.97\%$

The largest percentage increase is $10\%$, between 2002 and 2003.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
