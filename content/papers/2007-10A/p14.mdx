<Problem>
A triangle with side lengths in the ratio $3 : 4 : 5$ is inscribed in a circle with radius $3$. What is the area of the triangle?
</Problem>

<Solution>
A 3-4-5 triangle is a right triangle. For a right triangle inscribed in a circle, the hypotenuse is a diameter of the circumscribed circle.

The circumradius is $R = 3$, so the diameter (hypotenuse) is $2R = 6$. Since the sides are in ratio $3:4:5$, we scale so the hypotenuse equals 6:
$$3k = \text{short leg},\quad 4k = \text{long leg},\quad 5k = 6 \implies k = \frac{6}{5}.$$

The legs are $3k = \dfrac{18}{5}$ and $4k = \dfrac{24}{5}$.

The area of the triangle is:
$$\frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{18}{5} \cdot \frac{24}{5} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{432}{25} = \frac{216}{25} = 8.64.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
