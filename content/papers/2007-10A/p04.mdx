<Problem>
The larger of two consecutive odd integers is three times the smaller. What is their sum?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Let the smaller odd integer be $n$. The next consecutive odd integer is $n + 2$. The condition gives:
$$n + 2 = 3n \implies 2 = 2n \implies n = 1.$$

The two integers are $1$ and $3$, and their sum is $1 + 3 = 4$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
