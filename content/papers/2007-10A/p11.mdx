<Problem>
The numbers from $1$ to $8$ are placed at the vertices of a cube in such a manner that the sum of the four numbers on each face is the same. What is this common sum?
</Problem>

<Solution>
A cube has 6 faces, and each vertex belongs to exactly 3 faces. The total sum of all numbers is $1 + 2 + \cdots + 8 = 36$. When we add up the sums of all 6 faces, each vertex is counted 3 times, giving a total of $3 \times 36 = 108$. Since there are 6 faces with equal sums, the common sum is:
$$\frac{108}{6} = 18.$$

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
