<Problem>
Circles centered at $A$ and $B$ each have radius $2$, as shown. Point $O$ is the midpoint of $\overline{AB}$, and $OA = 2\sqrt{2}$. Segments $OC$ and $OD$ are tangent to the circles centered at $A$ and $B$, respectively, and $EF$ is a common external tangent. What is the area of the shaded region $ECODF$?

Circle $A$ is at the left with center $A$, circle $B$ is at the right with center $B$. $O$ is the midpoint of $AB$ with $OA = OB = 2\sqrt{2}$. From $O$, segment $OC$ is tangent to circle $A$ (touching it at $C$) and $OD$ is tangent to circle $B$ (touching at $D$). Line $EF$ is the common external tangent above, tangent to circle $A$ at $E$ and to circle $B$ at $F$. The shaded region $ECODF$ is bounded by segment $OC$, arc $CE$ on circle $A$, segment $EF$, arc $FD$ on circle $B$, and segment $DO$.
</Problem>

<Solution>
Place $O = (0,0)$, $A = (-2\sqrt{2}, 0)$, $B = (2\sqrt{2}, 0)$.

**Key lengths and angles.** The tangent length from $O$ to circle $A$ is $OC = \sqrt{OA^2 - r^2} = \sqrt{8-4} = 2$. Since $\sin\angle AOC = r/OA = 1/\sqrt{2}$, we get $\angle AOC = 45°$, so the radius $AC$ makes a $45°$ angle with $AO$, placing $C = (-\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{2})$. By symmetry $D = (\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{2})$.

Since the circles have equal radii, the common external tangent $EF$ is horizontal at height 2, touching circle $A$ at $E = (-2\sqrt{2}, 2)$ and circle $B$ at $F = (2\sqrt{2}, 2)$, with $EF = 4\sqrt{2}$.

**Straight-line polygon area.** Apply the shoelace formula to vertices $O(0,0)$, $C(-\sqrt{2},\sqrt{2})$, $E(-2\sqrt{2},2)$, $F(2\sqrt{2},2)$, $D(\sqrt{2},\sqrt{2})$:

$$2A = (0\cdot\sqrt{2} - (-\sqrt{2})\cdot 0) + ((-\sqrt{2})\cdot 2 - (-2\sqrt{2})\cdot\sqrt{2}) + ((-2\sqrt{2})\cdot 2 - 2\sqrt{2}\cdot 2) + (2\sqrt{2}\cdot\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{2}\cdot 2) + (\sqrt{2}\cdot 0 - 0\cdot\sqrt{2})$$
$$= 0 + (-2\sqrt{2}+4) + (-8\sqrt{2}) + (4-2\sqrt{2}) + 0 = 8 - 12\sqrt{2}.$$

Taking the absolute value, the polygon area is $\tfrac{1}{2}(12\sqrt{2}-8) = 6\sqrt{2}-4$.

**Arc correction.** Each arc $CE$ and $FD$ subtends $45° = \pi/4$ at its center (from $45°$ to $90°$ in the respective circle). The arc curves inward toward $O$, so the shaded region is the polygon minus two circular segments. Each segment has area $\tfrac{1}{2}r^2(\theta - \sin\theta) = \tfrac{1}{2}(4)\!\left(\tfrac{\pi}{4}-\tfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right) = \tfrac{\pi}{2} - \sqrt{2}$, and two segments give $\pi - 2\sqrt{2}$.

**Total shaded area:**
$$(6\sqrt{2}-4) - (\pi - 2\sqrt{2}) = 8\sqrt{2} - 4 - \pi.$$

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
