<Problem>
Define $a \mathbin{@} b = ab - b^{2}$ and $a \mathbin{\#} b = a + b - ab^{2}$. What is $\dfrac{6 \mathbin{@} 2}{6 \mathbin{\#} 2}$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Compute the numerator: $6 \mathbin{@} 2 = 6 \cdot 2 - 2^2 = 12 - 4 = 8$.

Compute the denominator: $6 \mathbin{\#} 2 = 6 + 2 - 6 \cdot 2^2 = 8 - 24 = -16$.

Therefore $\dfrac{6 \mathbin{@} 2}{6 \mathbin{\#} 2} = \dfrac{8}{-16} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
