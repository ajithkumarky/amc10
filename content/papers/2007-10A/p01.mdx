<Problem>
One ticket to a show costs $\textdollar 20$ at full price. Susan buys 4 tickets using a coupon that gives her a $25\%$ discount. Pam buys 5 tickets using a coupon that gives her a $30\%$ discount. How many more dollars does Pam pay than Susan?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Susan pays $4 \times 20 \times 0.75 = 4 \times 15 = 60$ dollars.

Pam pays $5 \times 20 \times 0.70 = 5 \times 14 = 70$ dollars.

The difference is $70 - 60 = 10$ dollars.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
