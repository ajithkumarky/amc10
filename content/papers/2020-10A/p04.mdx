<Problem>
A driver travels for $2$ hours at $60$ miles per hour, during which her car gets $30$ miles per gallon of gasoline. She is paid $\textdollar 0.50$ per mile, and her only expense is gasoline at $\textdollar 2.00$ per gallon. What is her net rate of pay, in dollars per hour, after this expense?
</Problem>

<Solution>
In $2$ hours at $60$ mph, the driver travels $2 \times 60 = 120$ miles.

**Earnings:** $120 \times \textdollar 0.50 = \textdollar 60$.

**Gasoline used:** $\dfrac{120}{30} = 4$ gallons.

**Gasoline cost:** $4 \times \textdollar 2.00 = \textdollar 8$.

**Net earnings:** $\textdollar 60 - \textdollar 8 = \textdollar 52$ over $2$ hours.

**Net rate:** $\dfrac{\textdollar 52}{2} = \textdollar 26$ per hour.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
