<Problem>
Assuming $a \neq 3$, $b \neq 4$, and $c \neq 5$, what is the value in simplest form of the following expression?

$$\frac{a-3}{5-c} \cdot \frac{b-4}{3-a} \cdot \frac{c-5}{4-b}$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
Notice that each factor in the numerator pairs with a negated version in the denominators:

$$\frac{a-3}{3-a} = \frac{a-3}{-(a-3)} = -1,$$

$$\frac{b-4}{4-b} = \frac{b-4}{-(b-4)} = -1,$$

$$\frac{c-5}{5-c} = \frac{c-5}{-(c-5)} = -1.$$

Rearranging the original expression to group these pairs:

$$\frac{a-3}{3-a} \cdot \frac{b-4}{4-b} \cdot \frac{c-5}{5-c} = (-1)(-1)(-1) = -1.$$

Answer: **A**.
</Solution>
