<Problem>
Real numbers $x$ and $y$ satisfy $x + y = 4$ and $x \cdot y = -2$. What is the value of

$$x + \frac{x^3}{y^2} + \frac{y^3}{x^2} + y?$$
</Problem>

<Solution>
We want to evaluate $x + y + \dfrac{x^3}{y^2} + \dfrac{y^3}{x^2}$.

We already know $x + y = 4$, so we need $\dfrac{x^3}{y^2} + \dfrac{y^3}{x^2}$.

Combine over the common denominator $x^2 y^2$:

$$\frac{x^3}{y^2} + \frac{y^3}{x^2} = \frac{x^5 + y^5}{x^2 y^2}.$$

**Finding $x^2 y^2$.**

$(xy)^2 = (-2)^2 = 4$, so $x^2 y^2 = 4$.

**Finding $x^5 + y^5$.**

Use Newton's identity approach. Let $p_k = x^k + y^k$, $s = x+y = 4$, $p = xy = -2$.

- $p_1 = s = 4$
- $p_2 = s \cdot p_1 - 2p = 4 \cdot 4 - 2(-2) = 16 + 4 = 20$
- $p_3 = s \cdot p_2 - p \cdot p_1 = 4 \cdot 20 - (-2)(4) = 80 + 8 = 88$
- $p_4 = s \cdot p_3 - p \cdot p_2 = 4 \cdot 88 - (-2)(20) = 352 + 40 = 392$
- $p_5 = s \cdot p_4 - p \cdot p_3 = 4 \cdot 392 - (-2)(88) = 1568 + 176 = 1744$

**Putting it together.**

$$\frac{x^5 + y^5}{x^2 y^2} = \frac{1744}{4} = 436.$$

Therefore the answer is $4 + 436 = 440$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
