<Problem>
The numbers $3, 5, 7, a,$ and $b$ have an average (arithmetic mean) of $15$. What is the average of $a$ and $b$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
Since the five numbers have a mean of $15$, their sum is $5 \times 15 = 75$.

The sum of the three known numbers is $3 + 5 + 7 = 15$.

So $a + b = 75 - 15 = 60$.

The average of $a$ and $b$ is therefore $\dfrac{60}{2} = 30$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
