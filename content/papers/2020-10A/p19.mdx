<Problem>
As shown in the figure below, a regular dodecahedron (the polyhedron consisting of 12 congruent regular pentagonal faces) floats in space with two horizontal faces. Note that there is a ring of five slanted faces adjacent to the top face, and a ring of five slanted faces adjacent to the bottom face. How many ways are there to move from the top face to the bottom face via a sequence of adjacent faces so that each face is visited at most once and moves are not permitted from the bottom ring to the top ring?

(The dodecahedron has: 1 top face; a top ring of 5 faces each adjacent to the top face; a bottom ring of 5 faces each adjacent to the bottom face; 1 bottom face. Each face in the top ring is adjacent to 2 faces in the bottom ring, and moves from the bottom ring back to the top ring are not allowed.)
</Problem>

<Solution>
Label the faces: $T$ (top), $T_1,\ldots,T_5$ (top ring, cyclically adjacent), $B_1,\ldots,B_5$ (bottom ring, cyclically adjacent), $B$ (bottom). The adjacency rules are: $T$ connects to every $T_i$; each $T_i$ connects to its two neighbors in the top ring and to two adjacent bottom-ring faces; each $B_j$ connects to two top-ring faces and to $B$; no move from the bottom ring back to the top ring is allowed.

Every valid path has the form $T \to T_{i_1} \to \cdots \to T_{i_s} \to B_{j_1} \to \cdots \to B_{j_t} \to B$ (with $s,t \ge 1$), where the $T$-portion is a simple directed path along the top 5-cycle and the $B$-portion is a simple directed path along the bottom 5-cycle.

**Counting top-ring paths.** The first step from $T$ is any of 5 faces; from there the path continues along the 5-cycle. From a fixed starting face, the path can have length 1 (stop immediately) or extend in either direction for 1 to 4 more steps, giving $1 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 9$ directed simple paths per starting face. Total top-ring paths: $5 \times 9 = 45$.

**Transition.** Each top-ring path ends at some $T_{i_s}$, which is adjacent to exactly 2 bottom-ring faces. Each gives 2 entry choices.

**Counting bottom-ring paths.** By the same 5-cycle argument, from any entry face $B_j$ there are 9 directed simple paths in the bottom ring (all lengths 1 through 5, in either direction), each ending at a face adjacent to $B$ (since every $B_j$ is adjacent to $B$, the path can stop at any bottom-ring face).

**Total:**
$$45 \times 2 \times 9 = 810.$$

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
