<Problem>
What is the sum of all real numbers $x$ for which $|x^2 - 12x + 34| = 2$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The absolute value equation splits into two cases.

**Case 1:** $x^2 - 12x + 34 = 2$

$$x^2 - 12x + 32 = 0$$
$$(x-4)(x-8) = 0$$

So $x = 4$ or $x = 8$.

**Case 2:** $x^2 - 12x + 34 = -2$

$$x^2 - 12x + 36 = 0$$
$$(x-6)^2 = 0$$

So $x = 6$ (a double root, but we count it once as a value).

The distinct real solutions are $x = 4, 6, 8$.

Their sum is $4 + 6 + 8 = 18$.

Answer: **C**.
</Solution>
