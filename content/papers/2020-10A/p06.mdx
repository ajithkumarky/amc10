<Problem>
How many $4$-digit positive integers (that is, integers between $1000$ and $9999$, inclusive) having only even digits are divisible by $5$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
A number is divisible by $5$ if and only if its last digit is $0$ or $5$. Since only even digits are allowed, the last digit must be $0$ (the only even digit that is also $0$ or $5$).

So the units digit is fixed as $0$.

The even digits are $0, 2, 4, 6, 8$.

- **Thousands digit:** must be even and nonzero (can't be $0$ since the number must be a 4-digit number): choices are $2, 4, 6, 8$ — that's $4$ choices.
- **Hundreds digit:** any even digit ($0, 2, 4, 6, 8$) — $5$ choices.
- **Tens digit:** any even digit — $5$ choices.
- **Units digit:** must be $0$ — $1$ choice.

Total count: $4 \times 5 \times 5 \times 1 = 100$.

Answer: **B**.
</Solution>
