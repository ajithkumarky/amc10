<Problem>
A positive integer divisor of $12!$ is chosen at random. The probability that the divisor chosen is a perfect square can be expressed as $\dfrac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. What is $m + n$?
</Problem>

<Solution>
**Step 1: Prime factorization of $12!$.**

$$12! = 2^{10} \cdot 3^5 \cdot 5^2 \cdot 7 \cdot 11.$$

Check the exponents:
- Powers of 2: $\lfloor 12/2 \rfloor + \lfloor 12/4 \rfloor + \lfloor 12/8 \rfloor = 6 + 3 + 1 = 10$
- Powers of 3: $\lfloor 12/3 \rfloor + \lfloor 12/9 \rfloor = 4 + 1 = 5$
- Powers of 5: $\lfloor 12/5 \rfloor = 2$
- Powers of 7: $\lfloor 12/7 \rfloor = 1$
- Powers of 11: $\lfloor 12/11 \rfloor = 1$

**Step 2: Count total divisors.**

$$\tau(12!) = (10+1)(5+1)(2+1)(1+1)(1+1) = 11 \cdot 6 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 792.$$

**Step 3: Count perfect-square divisors.**

A divisor $d = 2^a \cdot 3^b \cdot 5^c \cdot 7^e \cdot 11^f$ is a perfect square when all exponents are even.
- $a$: even values in $\{0,2,4,6,8,10\}$ — 6 choices
- $b$: even values in $\{0,2,4\}$ — 3 choices
- $c$: even values in $\{0,2\}$ — 2 choices
- $e$: even values in $\{0\}$ — 1 choice
- $f$: even values in $\{0\}$ — 1 choice

Total: $6 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1 \cdot 1 = 36$.

**Step 4: Compute the probability.**

$$P = \frac{36}{792} = \frac{1}{22}.$$

Since $\gcd(1, 22) = 1$, we have $m = 1$ and $n = 22$, giving $m + n = 23$.

Answer: **E**.
</Solution>
