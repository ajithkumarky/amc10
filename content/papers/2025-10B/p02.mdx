<Problem>
Jerry wrote down the ones digit of each of the first $2025$ positive squares: $1, 4, 9, 6, 5, 6, \ldots$ What is the sum of all the numbers Jerry wrote down?
</Problem>

<Solution>
The ones digit of $n^2$ depends only on the ones digit of $n$, and it repeats with period $10$. The ones digits of $1^2$ through $10^2$ are:

$$1, 4, 9, 6, 5, 6, 9, 4, 1, 0.$$

Their sum is $1+4+9+6+5+6+9+4+1+0 = 45$.

Now $2025 = 10 \cdot 202 + 5$, so there are $202$ complete cycles of $10$ and then $5$ extra terms.

- Sum from $202$ complete cycles: $202 \times 45 = 9090$.
- The first $5$ ones digits are $1, 4, 9, 6, 5$, which sum to $25$.

Total: $9090 + 25 = 9115$.

Answer: **D**.
</Solution>
